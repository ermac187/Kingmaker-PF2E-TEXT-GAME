> Each entry: stat block at L1 + full leveling map L1–20.
> After confirming ancestry, output full `.status` panel before any scene content.
>
> **SORCERER NOTE:** Bloodline determines spell tradition, granted spells, and blood magic
> effect. Spontaneous caster — no preparation, all slots flexible within known spells.
> CHA is key ability. Blood magic triggers on any bloodline-granted spell OR any spell
> using a slot to heighten a signature spell.
>
> **ANCESTRY BASE HP:** Dwarf 10 | Orc 10 | Half-Orc 10 | Human 8 | Aasimar 8 | Gnome 8 | Elf 6 | Halfling 6
> **HP CORRECTION:** New L1 HP = Listed HP − Default Ancestry HP + Chosen Ancestry HP

> **ROGUE NOTE:** Sneak Attack fires whenever the target is Off-Guard (flanked,
