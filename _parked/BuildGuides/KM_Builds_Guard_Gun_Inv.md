**Combat rotation (typical turn):** (1) Taunt the biggest threat — they're now compelled to come for you. (2) Raise Shield. (3) Strike with Flickmace from 10-ft reach (Trip if Athletics is favorable). End turn at full defense.

**Reaction priority:** Intercept Attack > Shield Block. Intercept Attack first when an ally within 10 ft takes physical damage — Step into the path if needed and absorb it yourself. Shield Block second when YOU take a hit that would drop you significantly. With Quick Shield Block at L11, you get TWO Shield Blocks per round on top of Intercept.

**Auto-level note:** STR+2 first, CON+2 second. Toughness at L1 (via Versatile Heritage's general feat slot) keeps the HP buffer wide. Bodyguard at L1 (via Natural Ambition's bonus class feat) unlocks the Aid-action +1 AC support that scales to +2 at Greater Bodyguard L8. Hampering Stance at L4 is the pivot: Taunted enemies can't easily walk away to chase your glass cannons. You are the unbreakable protector — you don't need to kill enemies, you make them regret ignoring you.
