# Conditions

> **STUB — not yet populated.** Until your condition data is added here, the GM
> must NOT state numeric condition effects from memory. If a player is, e.g.,
> *frightened 2* or *clumsy 1* and this file is empty, the GM says:
> "I don't have the conditions data loaded — paste or push `conditions.md` and
> I'll apply it exactly."

Add each PF2E condition with its **exact** mechanical effect, e.g.:

- **Frightened N**: −N status penalty to all checks and DCs; decreases by 1 at
  the end of each turn.
- **Clumsy N**: −N status penalty to Dexterity-based checks and DCs (AC, Reflex,
  attacks/ranged, etc.).
- **Off-guard**: −2 circumstance penalty to AC.

(The examples above are illustrative of the format only — replace this whole file
with your verified condition data.)
