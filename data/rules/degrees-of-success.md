# Degrees of Success

Source: PF2E core rules. This is the four-step ladder applied to every check.

## The ladder

Compare the check **total** to the **DC**:

| Result | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Critical Success** | Total ≥ DC + 10 |
| **Success** | Total ≥ DC (but less than DC + 10) |
| **Failure** | Total < DC (but more than DC − 10) |
| **Critical Failure** | Total ≤ DC − 10 |

## Natural 20 / Natural 1

After determining the degree above, adjust by the natural die:

- **Natural 20** on the d20 → improve the degree of success by **one step**.
- **Natural 1** on the d20 → worsen the degree of success by **one step**.

Steps, in order: Critical Failure → Failure → Success → Critical Success.
A natural 20 cannot push past Critical Success; a natural 1 cannot drop below
Critical Failure.

> Example: total 21 vs DC 18 is a Success. If that 21 came from a natural 20,
> it becomes a Critical Success. If the total of 16 vs DC 18 (a Failure) came
> from a natural 1, it becomes a Critical Failure.

## Why this matters here

Many PF2E effects have a different outcome for **each** of the four degrees, not
just pass/fail. The GM must read the specific effect for the degree rolled from
the relevant data file (spell, skill action, save) — never collapse it to a
generic "you succeed / you fail."
