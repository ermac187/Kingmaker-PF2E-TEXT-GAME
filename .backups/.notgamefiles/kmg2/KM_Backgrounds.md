## BORDERLANDS PIONEER
*Rare Background*

You have long lived along the southern border of Brevoy in the shadow of the Stolen Lands. Life has been hard — hunting, trapping, trading, and coaxing crops from unforgiving ground. Whether driven by personal expertise or a desire to reclaim family land, you've joined the expedition south.

**Ability Boosts:** Strength or Wisdom + one free
**Trained In:** Nature + one wilderness Lore (choose: forest, hills, mountains, plains, rivers, or swamp)
**Skill Feat:** Train Animal

---

## BREVIC NOBLE
*Rare Background*

You claim a tangential but legitimate connection to one of Brevoy's noble families. You've had a comfortable life — though nothing like what your distant cousins enjoy. An expedition into the Stolen Lands seems like the test to prove you're truly worthy of the title.

**Ability Boosts:** Constitution or Wisdom + one free
**Trained In:** Society + one Brevic noble house Lore (choose below)
**Skill Feat:** Varies by lineage (see below)

**Noble Lineages:**
| House | Motto | Trained Skill | Feat |
|-------|-------|---------------|------|
| Garess | "Strong as the Mountains" | Crafting + Architecture Lore | Specialty Crafting |
| Lebeda | "Success through Grace" | Diplomacy + Mercantile Lore | Multilingual |
| Lodovka | "The Waters, Our Fields" | Athletics + Sailing Lore | Underwater Marauder |
| Medvyed | "Endure and Grow Strong" | Survival + Hunting Lore | Terrain Expertise |
| Orlovsky | "High Above" | Diplomacy + Brevoy Lore | Group Impression |
| Surtova | "Ours Is the Right" | Deception + Politics Lore | Charming Liar |

*Additional effect:* Once you establish yourself as a true noble or secure a leadership role in a kingdom, you no longer take the usual −1 Charisma penalty with nobility that this background initially imposes.

## BREVIC OUTCAST
*Rare Background*

One of your parents was a member of one of Brevoy's great families — perhaps even the lost line of Rogarvia — but you have no proof. Claiming noble blood without evidence invites ridicule or worse. You've joined the Stolen Lands expedition hoping to make a name all your own.

**Ability Boosts:** Charisma or Constitution + one free
**Trained In:** Politics Lore
**Penalty:** −1 on all Charisma-based skill checks with nobility
**Skill Feat:** Haughty Obstinacy (gain this human ancestry feat even if not human)

*The penalty fades if you prove your heritage or establish a kingdom role.*

---

## ISSIAN PATRIOT
*Rare Background*

You grew up in northern Brevoy (Issia), loyal to the Surtova crown. The call to help reclaim holdings in the Stolen Lands has inflamed your dreams of proving your worth and protecting Issian interests. You head south with a mix of patriotism and ambition.

**Ability Boosts:** Intelligence or Strength + one free
**Trained In:** Society + Legal Lore
**Skill Feat:** Streetwise

---

## LOCAL BRIGAND
*Rare Background*

You hail from the River Kingdoms or the lawless reaches of Brevoy. You know how to ambush travelers, bully traders, avoid the law, and camp unseen. Recently you've run into trouble — with the law or rival bandits — and an expedition into the wilderness seems like a perfect way to lie low.

**Ability Boosts:** Strength or Constitution + one free
**Trained In:** Intimidation + Banditry Lore
**Skill Feat:** Group Coercion

---

## ROSTLANDER
*Rare Background*

You were raised in southern Brevoy (Rostland) — dense forests, rolling plains, crystalline rivers. You come from hardy stock: hard work earns gains, charity matters, family honor is everything. You are thoroughly Brevic, and the call to expand your land's influence has inflamed your patriotism.

**Ability Boosts:** Strength or Constitution + one free
**Trained In:** Athletics + Farming Lore
**Skill Feat:** Hefty Hauler

---

## SWORD SCION
*Rare Background*

You have lived all your life in and around Restov, growing up on tales of Baron Sirian Aldori and the swordlords. Joining the Stolen Lands expedition is the perfect way to improve your skills and begin building a legend worthy of the swordlord tradition.

**Ability Boosts:** Dexterity or Charisma + one free
**Trained In:** Warfare Lore
**Special:** Access to Aldori dueling swords; treat them as martial weapons rather than advanced. May purchase one as starting equipment.
**Skill Feat:** None (the weapon access is the bonus)

**ALDORI DUELING SWORD** *(Item 1 — Uncommon, Finesse, Versatile P)*
Price 20 gp | 1 hand | Bulk 1
Slim, single-bladed dueling sword with slight curve and reinforced point. Deals 1d8 slashing damage. Advanced weapon (treat as martial with Sword Scion background or Aldori Duelist archetype).
