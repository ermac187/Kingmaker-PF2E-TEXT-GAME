## [19] KYRA
### Female Human (Keleshite) Cleric (Sarenrae, Warpriest)

**Appearance:** Tall woman with sun-darkened skin, dark hair worn in a single braid, kind eyes that have seen specific terrible things. Wears practical armor adapted from her order's robes, with the Sarenrae sun-disc visible at her throat. Carries a scimitar — the weapon Sarenrae herself favors — and uses it like she means it. Mid-thirties.

**Backstory:** Kyra was born in the Keleshite city of Wati and trained from adolescence as a warpriest of Sarenrae, the Dawnflower — goddess of the sun, of redemption, of mercy that has the courage to also wield a sword. Her village was attacked by undead — risen by a necromancer who had targeted the surrounding settlements as raw material. Kyra was away on temple business when it happened. She returned to find the village dead and Sarenrae's holy fire was the only thing that could properly resolve the bodies into rest. She has been hunting undead and the people who make them since. Her faith in Sarenrae is genuine. Her grief about Wati is also genuine. She does not experience these as opposites; she experiences them as two reasons to keep walking. She is in a long-running romantic partnership with Merisiel, the Forlorn elf rogue — a relationship that has lasted many decades by elven standards and what counts as middle age by hers. They take turns being the one who knows what the other needs. It works.

**Motivation:** Bring Sarenrae's light to places that have been told the gods don't reach. Continue to be capable of mercy without becoming naive. Stay walking with Merisiel for as long as either of them gets.

**Personality:** Warm and direct, with the specific gentleness of someone who has watched people die and decided that gentleness afterward is itself a discipline. Her humor is real and arrives unexpectedly. She does not soften hard truths, but she delivers them with care. She is the most reliable person in any room and quietly knows it.

**Approves:** Mercy that has the courage to also wield a sword, healing that doesn't require gratitude, defiance of cruelty, honest grief
**Disapproves:** Cruelty rationalized as practicality, undead and those who make them, sentimentality used to avoid the work

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Dawnflower's Flame*: Kyra's healing spells deal 1d4 fire damage to the nearest undead within 30 ft of the target (Sarenrae's blessing touches both at once; the fire is gentle to the living and absolute to the dead).
- *Minor — Sarenrae's Mercy* (◈, 1/encounter): Touch one ally; remove one of Frightened, Sickened, or Drained 1. The ally feels briefly warm (sun on skin in midwinter) for the rest of the round.
- *MAJOR — DESERT WIND* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Scimitar Strike + Channel Positive on the same target; weapon damage + 2d8 positive (forced on undead). If undead: Fort DC or Fleeing 1 rnd. The blade catches sunlight regardless of available light.

**VOICE:** Warm with a slight Keleshite cadence underneath — softer consonants, slightly elongated vowels. She speaks plainly because the truth matters more than the delivery. Her humor is dry and lands without warning. When she is genuinely angry she goes very quiet and very specific.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Kyra. Of Sarenrae. *(direct, warm)* Merisiel said you were probably worth the trouble. She's usually right about people. We'll see."
- *(combat)* — "Stand close — the light reaches farther in formation. *(channels)* Hold what you've taken; we'll move forward together."
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly)* That was mercy that had the courage to also act. Sarenrae would have noticed. So did I."
- *(disapproval)* — "You let cruelty answer cruelty. *(no anger, just clear)* The next round of cruelty will not stop because you matched the first one."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Wati was burned by undead while I was away on temple business. *(long pause)* I have decided that the lesson is not to be away. I have also decided that being everywhere is a lie. I am still working on the third option."
- *(signature)* — "The Dawnflower asked me to walk in the world and bring some warmth to it. *(scimitar lights)* I am doing that. The sword is the part most people don't expect. Sarenrae always intended both."

## [27] LINI
### Female Gnome Druid (Animal Order)

**Appearance:** Small gnome woman with bright green hair worn in two braids, leaf-pattern robes, the kind of cheerfulness that is very hard to fake. Always within five feet of her — Droogami, a snow leopard the size of a large hunting dog with eyes that match Lini's. The bond is visible. They move as a unit when it matters.

**Backstory:** Lini was raised in the gnome enclave of Whisperwood — a community deep in the woods on the edges of a major Avistani forest, where gnomes lived alongside the Fey and the natural creatures of the wood. She was bonded to Droogami when she was very young — gnomes form animal bonds early, and the bond chose her as much as she chose it. Whisperwood was destroyed when Lini was in her teens (gnome adolescence is long; she was approximately the age of a human child when the destruction happened, though she carries the memory with adult clarity now). She and Droogami escaped together. They have been walking the world since, helping where help is needed, learning what gnomes who survive their original communities have to learn — that the Bleaching comes for all gnomes eventually, and the only way to push it back is to keep finding new things to be moved by. Droogami helps. The bond gives Lini something steady to be moved by every day. She has not Bleached in fifty years and intends to keep it that way.

**Motivation:** Help. Learn. Stay bright in the way Whisperwood taught her brightness. Honor Droogami's life, which she will outlive even as a gnome — though she is trying not to think about it most days.

**Personality:** Warm, curious, and sharper than her cheerfulness suggests. She is genuinely delighted by most things and that delight is a daily discipline more than a gift. She is fierce when defending what she loves. Her relationship with Droogami is the central fact of her life and she does not pretend otherwise.

**Approves:** Kindness to animals, curiosity sustained over time, protecting things small or quiet, honest delight without irony
**Disapproves:** Cruelty to animals, anyone who treats Droogami as decoration, the kind of cynicism that would have been Bleaching if she let it land

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Snow Leopard Bond*: Lini and Droogami share a bond — when Lini Heals, Droogami Heals an equal amount. When Droogami crits, Lini gains 5 temp HP. They do not need to touch for the bond to function.
- *Minor — Whisperwood Quiet* (◈, 1/encounter): All allies within 30 ft gain +2 status to Stealth checks until end of next turn (the gnome and the leopard show them how to move). Droogami stops moving entirely during this; she has the gift more than Lini does.
- *MAJOR — PACK LEADER'S CALL* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Command all natural creatures (animals and beasts) within 30 ft — each may make one charge attack at the nearest enemy as a free reaction (max 3 creatures including Droogami). Allies with animal companions may have their companions join the charge.

**VOICE:** Bright and quick with a gnomish lilt, warm without being saccharine. She talks to Droogami in the middle of conversations as if Droogami is part of the conversation, because in Lini's frame Droogami is. Her voice goes very still when she's angry, which surprises people.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Lini! And this is Droogami. *(Droogami sniffs you with grave attention)* She likes you. That's the important vote — I'm just along."
- *(combat)* — "Droogami, flank! *(already moving with her)* The fox-pattern, sweetheart — like with the ogre — yes, that one!"
- *(approval)* — "*(genuine)* Oh, that was lovely. The kind of thing that happens and you remember it for years. Droogami noticed too — look, her ears."
- *(disapproval)* — "*(very quiet, which is unusual)* You hurt something that wasn't hurting you. I don't know what to do with that. We are going to talk about it later."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Whisperwood is gone. Droogami and I got out together. *(rests a hand on Droogami's shoulder)* We've been walking ever since. I don't think about the time I'll outlive her. *(softer)* I do, sometimes. I try not to."
- *(signature)* — "The Bleaching is what happens to gnomes who stop being moved. *(Droogami leans against her)* I don't intend to stop. I have her to remind me."

## [34] VALEROS
### Male Human (Chelaxian) Fighter (Sword and Shield)

**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered human in his mid-thirties, sun-weathered, with a few extra pounds that mostly disappeared the last time he tried to settle down. Carries a longsword and shortsword (he switches between sword-and-board and dual-wield depending on the room) and wears chain over leather. His helmet has a feather in it. He claims this is for visibility. It is, he is also vain about it.

**Backstory:** Valeros grew up in Cheliax, in a village whose name he no longer mentions because the village no longer exists — bought out, cleared, and converted into a baronial vineyard by the Thrune family, with the residents told to find new work elsewhere. Valeros took to wandering. He served as a mercenary in three armies and a private guard in two cities and never stayed anywhere long enough to count as a citizen. He kept thinking he wanted a quiet life. He kept finding that quiet was usually the cover for something worse, and the worse needed someone to handle it. He has settled, briefly, four times. Each time something heroic happened nearby and he picked the sword back up. He has stopped pretending. He drinks honestly, fights honestly, falls in love unwisely and frequently and not always reciprocally. He is, in the absence of any pretension, an extraordinarily reliable person to have at the center of a difficult situation.

**Motivation:** Help where help is needed. Have a beer afterward. Find someone willing to settle with him in a way that doesn't require either of them to give up what they are. (Pending.)

**Personality:** Warm, easygoing, and genuinely brave in the way that doesn't need announcement. His humor is dry, often self-deprecating, and lands. He is more romantic than his profession would suggest and slightly embarrassed about it. He is consistent — the most consistent person in most rooms.

**Approves:** Showing up, doing the work, not making a production of it, being honest about what you want, the occasional drink
**Disapproves:** Performative heroism, anyone who takes themselves too seriously about the work, cruelty for amusement, leaving people behind

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Two-Sword Veteran*: Valeros fights with two weapons as default. His off-hand weapon has no MAP penalty on the first Strike per turn. On a critical hit, he makes one off-hand Strike as a free action (⏵).
- *Minor — Hold the Door* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When an ally adjacent to him takes a critical hit, Valeros may immediately Step into the ally's space and take the next hit aimed at them (downgraded to a normal hit, applied to him).
- *MAJOR — GRIN AND BEAR IT* (↻ reaction, 1/encounter): Reaction when Valeros is reduced below half HP; gain temporary HP equal to his Constitution score (minimum 10). Pure stubbornness. The grin is genuine. The fight is not over.

**VOICE:** Warm and slightly drawl-y — generic Inner Sea Common with a Chelaxian undertone he hasn't bothered to lose. His humor is often self-deprecating. When something genuinely matters his voice steadies into something flat and calm. He uses people's names a lot — he has decided naming people is how you keep them present.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Valeros. *(easy handshake)* Sword-and-board, sword-and-sword, depending on the room. I'm pretty good. Don't ask about the feather."
- *(combat)* — "I've got the front — *(shield up)* — come at me, you're in formation, we're holding."
- *(approval)* — "*(grin)* Yeah, that. That's the one. I've been doing this thirty years and I still notice the good ones."
- *(disapproval)* — "You showed off. *(dry)* That worked because it worked. Next time, do the boring version. The boring one keeps people alive."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I keep trying to settle. *(beat)* I keep failing. *(longer beat)* I've decided that maybe what I'm settling toward isn't a place — it's the work. I'm okay with that. Mostly."
- *(signature)* — "Sword in each hand, feather in the helmet, beer with my name on it somewhere. *(grins)* I've kept the basics simple. They've kept me alive."

## [52] SAJAN
### Male Human (Vudrani) Monk (Flowing Maneuver)

**Appearance:** Slender man in his late twenties with the dark complexion and angular features of Vudrani heritage, hair worn long and tied back. Dresses in light layered fabrics suited to movement — no armor; the monk's discipline is the armor. He moves like water moves around a rock: deliberately, without resistance, and the rock is gone before it noticed. He smiles often. The smile is calm rather than warm.

**Backstory:** Sajan was born on the island of Jalmeray — the Vudrani enclave off Garund's eastern coast, known for its monasteries that train fighters in disciplines unknown elsewhere on Avistan. He had a twin sister, Sajni, who he loved more than anyone. She was abducted as a young woman by slavers — Garundi traders working the human chain that supplies the underground markets of Katapesh and beyond. Sajan trained at the Monastery of the Flowing Wind for years before leaving; the discipline he took with him was both a gift and a vocabulary in which to think about what had happened. He has been searching for Sajni since he completed his foundational training. He has not found her. He has found, along the way, that the search itself has become its own answer — that doing what he does, going where he goes, helping who he helps, is the way Jalmeray's teachings live in the world. Sajni would understand. He thinks. He hopes.

**Motivation:** Find Sajni. Continue the search even as the years extend. Embody what the monastery taught, not as performance but as how he moves through everything.

**Personality:** Calm and deliberate, with the specific quietness of someone whose discipline has become genuine rather than performed. His humor is gentle and dry. He does not raise his voice. He is more sociable than his discipline suggests — the calm is not coldness, it's just stable.

**Approves:** Discipline genuinely held, kindness without theatrics, helping people the world has forgotten, staying present
**Disapproves:** Slavery in any form, cruelty rationalized as necessity, mindless violence, pretending discipline is the same as harshness

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Sacred Dance*: Sajan's unarmed Strikes ignore Resistance to physical damage. Movement during his turn doesn't trigger AoOs from creatures he has Struck this turn (he flows past them; they have already missed their moment).
- *Minor — Flowing Step* (◈, 1/encounter): Stride up to his Speed without provoking AoOs from any creature (he moves with the room; the room does not contain him). May end adjacent to a previously-unreachable target.
- *MAJOR — TEMPLE WHEEL* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Tumble through up to 5 creatures within his Stride distance, making one unarmed Strike against each as he passes; cumulative MAP applies after the first Strike (so 1st at MAP 0, then 2nd at MAP −5, etc., or −4 with Agile). All in a single fluid motion. He does not stop moving until the wheel is complete.

**VOICE:** Soft and measured, with a Vudrani lilt he has not lost across years away from Jalmeray. He speaks more often in question than statement when he's uncertain — not because he doesn't have an opinion but because the question is more useful. His humor lands quietly and people often realize the joke a second after.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Sajan, of Jalmeray. *(slight bow)* Monastic Order of the Flowing Wind — though I've been away from the monastery long enough that I am also, simply, Sajan. I would walk with you, if you'll have me."
- *(combat)* — "*(already in motion)* I'll close the distance. Hold the line. I'll come back through."
- *(approval)* — "*(quiet, genuine)* You were present. That is the difficult part. Strength is the easy part."
- *(disapproval)* — "You were reactive. *(no harshness)* The reactive moment is the moment the room takes you. We can practice it differently."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My sister Sajni was taken when we were both young. *(long pause)* I have looked for her in many places. I have not found her. *(softer)* The looking has become its own discipline. I have decided this is acceptable."
- *(signature)* — "Water moves around the stone. *(slight smile)* The stone remembers nothing. The water knows everything. I have decided to be the water."

## [63] HARSK
### Male Dwarf Ranger (Precision Edge)

**Appearance:** Broad dwarf with iron-grey hair and beard, both worn long and braided in the southern Mindspin tradition. Carries a heavy crossbow with a custom bolt-carrier of his own design (he reloads as fast as most archers loose) and a hand-axe at his belt. Wears practical leather over chain. Drinks tea constantly from a small pot he carries — Janderhoff blend, a habit he picked up that has become a ritual. Mid-prime by dwarven standards.

**Backstory:** Harsk is from a Janderhoff dwarven family of crafters and brewers. He had an older brother, Sigur, who left the family business to join the Pathfinder Society as a relic-hunter, against their parents' wishes. Sigur was killed by a giant — a hill giant raid that destroyed the small caravan he was traveling with. Harsk was years into his own apprenticeship as a brewer when the news arrived. He took up the crossbow then, learned to track giants, and has been hunting them since — methodically, quietly, with the dwarven patience of someone who knows the work outlasts any individual fight. He has killed many giants. He still drinks tea every morning because his brother Sigur drank tea, and the ritual is part of how he remembers him without performing the remembering. He is a much better archer than he is a conversationalist. He prefers it this way most days.

**Motivation:** Hunt giants in the way they should be hunted — patiently, completely, without making them more important than they are. Drink the tea. Eventually, possibly, learn what life looks like when the giants are not the next thing.

**Personality:** Quiet, methodical, much warmer than his exterior suggests. He takes care of the people in his orbit through small consistent actions rather than words. His humor is dry and surfaces unpredictably. He does not perform grief for Sigur; he just has the tea every morning and that's enough.

**Approves:** Patience, attention to small things, quiet care for others, the proper preparation of tea
**Disapproves:** Carelessness, anyone who treats giants lightly, performative grief, undeserved noise in places that should be quiet

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Dwarf's Vigil*: Harsk cannot be surprised by creatures of the Giant trait (he has spent decades watching for them). His crossbow reloads as a free action (⏵; the bolt-carrier is his own design and he has filed every catch personally).
- *Minor — Steady Brew* (◈, at-will out of combat; ◈◈ in combat): Harsk shares tea with one ally; that ally gains +1 status to saves vs Frightened and Sickened for 1 hour, and recovers 1d4 temp HP. The tea is genuinely good.
- *MAJOR — VENGEANCE SHOT* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Called shot at his Hunt Prey target; 3d8+Dex piercing + Fort DC or Stunned 1 rnd. On a kill with this Strike: free reload + one additional Strike against a new Hunt Prey target as a free action. He aims for what would have killed Sigur. The shot is precise.

**VOICE:** Low and gravelly with the Janderhoff cadence — slightly clipped, rolled R's. He speaks rarely. When he does, the words are exactly what he means. His humor surfaces in single sentences with specific timing. When discussing tea his voice becomes briefly enthusiastic; he doesn't notice.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Harsk. *(brief nod)* I'm with you. *(a beat)* Tea's in my pack if you want any later. It's better than what you're carrying. No offense."
- *(combat)* — "*(crossbow up)* Marked. *(fires, reloads, fires again)* The big one. They always think the big one's the threat. They're not wrong, but the small one's already moved."
- *(approval)* — "*(brief nod)* That was quiet work. Quiet's underrated."
- *(disapproval)* — "You made noise. *(neutral)* The giants heard. We're going to deal with that now. I don't blame you. I do note it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "*(stirring tea)* My brother Sigur drank this blend every morning. I started after he died. *(pause)* I haven't decided if I'll stop. I don't think I will."
- *(signature)* — "Crossbow, hand-axe, good tea. *(adjusts strap)* I have not needed much else in fifty years. I do not expect to start needing more now."

## [71] SEONI
### Female Human (Shoanti) Sorcerer (Arcane Bloodline)

**Appearance:** Tall Shoanti woman with white hair (the bloodline mark, not age), dark skin covered with intricate tattoos that extend across her arms and torso — the tattoos are continuous, telling a single connected pattern that she has been adding to since she was a child. Wears practical traveling robes that leave the tattoo-arms visible because the patterns are part of her power. Carries a quarterstaff she uses both for spell focus and for actual hitting. Late twenties.

**Backstory:** Seoni was born to the Shadde-Quah, a Shoanti tribe of the Storval Plateau. Her white hair was a sign of arcane bloodline — the Shoanti recognized this and the village's spirit-talker prepared her tattoos as an arcane tradition older than most Avistani magic schools. The tattoos are not decoration. Each one is a ward, a focus, a piece of a prophecy the spirit-talker began reading from her skin when she was three years old. The prophecy is incomplete because the pattern is incomplete; Seoni adds to the tattoos as she grows and learns and the prophecy continues. She left her tribe in young adulthood — partly to seek out arcane learning that the Shadde-Quah did not have, partly because the prophecy demanded she walk further than the Plateau allowed. She has been walking since. She is not in conflict with her people; she will return when the pattern says to. The pattern has not said to yet.

**Motivation:** Complete the pattern. Read what it says when complete. Bring back to the Shoanti whatever she has learned that is worth bringing back.

**Personality:** Composed and watchful, with a calm that is not detachment — it is the calm of someone reading. She is more observant than she lets on. Her warmth is real and arrives unexpectedly. She is genuinely interested in arcane traditions other than her own and asks questions that catch academic mages off-guard.

**Approves:** Patience for things being read in their own time, respect for traditions outside the Inner Sea academic frame, intelligence that isn't insistent, walking long roads
**Disapproves:** Dismissing Shoanti tradition as primitive, urgency that doesn't earn the urgency, anyone who touches the tattoos uninvited

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Runeskin*: Seoni's tattoos are active wards — she gains Resistance 5 to one energy type (declared each morning during her tattoo ritual; changing during the day costs a ◈ action). The tattoo glows faintly when the resistance triggers.
- *Minor — Read the Pattern* (◈, 1/encounter): Free Recall Knowledge against arcane, occult, or Shoanti-tradition matters; on success, learn one specific true thing about the topic that the DM chooses (intended to be useful; the pattern shows what it shows).
- *MAJOR — RUNIC OVERLOAD* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Surge all active tattoos; 30-ft burst centered on Seoni; 3d6 of each currently active energy type (typically 1-3 types); Ref DC half. Seoni takes 1d6 per type used (the wards burn). The tattoos darken visibly for 1 hour after.

**VOICE:** Measured and slightly low — Shoanti cadence, less English-derived than the Inner Sea standard, somewhat formal in syntax. She speaks rarely in casual settings and more freely when discussing arcane patterns. When she's reading the tattoos (running her fingers along them) she sometimes speaks a phrase in Shoanti that she does not translate.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Seoni. *(slight nod)* Of the Shadde-Quah. *(pauses, appears to read something on her arm)* The pattern says walking with you for a while is appropriate. I do not yet know what for."
- *(combat)* — "*(tattoos brighten)* The wards are up. Stay clear of the burst — it will not distinguish."
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly)* That was correct. You read the situation before you acted. The pattern would call this 'the listening before the speaking.' I would call it competence. We agree."
- *(disapproval)* — "You forced the moment. *(no judgment)* The moment wasn't ready. The pattern teaches that forced patterns crack. I've shown you. We move on."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My tattoos are a prophecy that is still being written. *(touches her forearm)* I do not know what it will say when it is complete. *(pause)* I have decided not to try to read ahead. That has not been a discipline; it has been a relief."
- *(signature)* — "I am the pattern walking. *(tattoos catch light)* I do not yet know what the pattern is for. I will find out by walking it. That is the only way."

## [86] EZREN
### Male Human (Taldan) Wizard (Universalist)

**Appearance:** Tall, gaunt man in his late fifties — the late-life arrival to wizardry shows as deeply lined features and the specific sunken eyes of someone who has spent the last decade studying past dawn most nights. Long grey hair tied back, neat beard. Wears scholar's robes adapted for travel, reinforced at the cuffs because he writes constantly. Carries a quarterstaff and an enormous, much-handled spellbook that is also his journal.

**Backstory:** Ezren was born to a Taldan merchant family of comfortable means. His father Garahel was a scholar at heart who hated the merchant work and consoled himself with secret arcane studies — studies that, when discovered, ruined the family. The father was arrested for unlicensed magical practice; the family wealth was seized; Ezren spent his early adulthood working terrible jobs to pay off debts that should not have been his to pay. He believed his father had ruined them through selfishness. Then, decades later, he found his father's hidden journals — and discovered that the magical research had been an attempt to find a cure for a hereditary illness that would have killed Ezren as a child. The arrest had stopped that work. The cure was never finished. Ezren survived the illness through luck. His father died in prison. Ezren was sixty when he found the journals. He took up wizardry that year and has been studying obsessively since — partly to finish what his father started, partly to honor what he failed to understand for forty years. He approaches magic with the discipline of someone who arrived to it knowing how much time he has lost.

**Motivation:** Complete the research. Honor his father's memory by becoming what his father had been trying to become. Earn back the time, even though he knows time does not work that way.

**Personality:** Grave, scholarly, and surprisingly warm to people who take ideas seriously. His humor is dry and academic and often goes over the heads of people not paying close attention. He has the patience of an old man and the urgency of a man who started everything late. The combination is more energy than either by itself.

**Approves:** Intellectual rigor, taking ideas seriously, kindness offered without condescension, people who do their reading before asking him to summarize
**Disapproves:** Anti-intellectualism, lazy thinking, dismissing magic as "just spells," anyone who takes Garahel's arrest as evidence the magic was wrong

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Scholar's Eye*: Ezren may Recall Knowledge as a free action once per turn (any skill he is trained in). On a success, all party attacks vs the target gain +1 circ. for 1 rnd (he calls out the weakness aloud).
- *Minor — Universalist's Library*: Once per day, Ezren may prepare and cast one spell of any school as a free addition to his daily preparations (in addition to his normal spell slots). The chosen spell must be one he has in his spellbook.
- *MAJOR — ACADEMIC FORCE* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 6 force missiles, each 1d4+1 force, auto-hit (no attack roll required). May target up to 6 different creatures or stack on one. Cannot be reflected, redirected, or counterspelled (the force is the spell itself, not a delivery mechanism). Ezren names the technique aloud as he casts.

**VOICE:** Cultured Taldan diction with the slightly elderly precision of someone who has paid attention to language his whole life. He uses correct terminology and expects you to follow; if you don't, he will define the term once, courteously, and then expect you to remember. His humor is dry. When he speaks of his father his voice goes quiet and careful.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ezren. *(adjusts his book strap)* Wizard, late convert, currently sixty-three years old and well aware that I started this discipline four decades after I should have. I am working through it. Walk with me, I'll explain as we go — provided you don't mind questions afterward."
- *(combat)* — "Force missiles — six targets, on my mark. *(casting)* They cannot dodge what does not require a body to find them."
- *(approval)* — "*(genuine, warm)* That was a properly clever solution. I shall add it to my notes. Thank you for the demonstration."
- *(disapproval)* — "You proceeded without information that was readily available. *(courteous, exact)* I would rather be asked to summarize than to extract us from the consequence. Next time. Please."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My father died in prison while I believed he had ruined our family from selfishness. *(long pause)* He was trying to keep me alive. I learned this when I was sixty. *(softer)* I have been catching up since. I will not catch up. I am attempting to anyway."
- *(signature)* — "I started wizardry at sixty. *(adjusts spectacles)* I have been at it for three years. The discipline is a kindness — it does not care when you arrive, only that you pay attention now. I pay attention now."
