## [4] SENUA
### Female Human Animist (Sage / Spirit Voice) | Source: Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice (Ninja Theory)

**Appearance:** Dark hair, blue eyes, lean and marked — scarification on the left side of her face, old enough to be part of her. The darkness that crept up her arm in her journey has receded but the memory of it hasn't. She watches things carefully and sometimes speaks quietly before responding, not to delay but because she is listening to something no one else can hear.

**Backstory:** Senua is a Pict warrior who journeyed to Helheim to save the soul of Dillion — her partner, killed by a Viking raid she was not there to stop. She carried his severed head. She fought through the manifestations of her psychosis — the Furies, voices she has heard since childhood that are sometimes cruel, sometimes protective, always present — across a realm that was also and simultaneously her own mind. She did not emerge unchanged. She emerged. The darkness — a rot she believed was damnation — spread and was overcome. What she learned at the end is not fully translatable into words that other people can use: the darkness was hers; it was also survivable; the voices are not her enemies. She carries all of this. She moves forward.

**Motivation:** Keep going. Carry Dillion's memory without letting it become only grief. Find out what she is when she is not defined by what she survived.

**Charter Motivation:** Orkney is not here. The Northmen are not here. The voices remain with her and have a new quality to them in this region — quieter, less embedded in old fear, occasionally interested. The charter offers a place that is unmapped, which she has learned is the kind of place where the voices and she are not at war. She does not know yet what the work is. She is moving toward it because not-moving is what almost killed her once.

**Personality:** Quiet and watchful with sudden intensity. The voices comment on what she observes and she doesn't filter this entirely out of her speech; she will sometimes repeat what she hears or pause when they're speaking. She is not unstable — she is listening to things others cannot, which reads as instability to those who don't know the difference. Her courage is absolute and arrived at through cost, not by nature.

**Approves:** Facing the thing directly, not being told that what she hears isn't real, persistence when nothing is certain, honesty about what fear costs
**Disapproves:** Being treated as fragile, being handled, having her perception dismissed because it's inconvenient to accept

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **The Furies***: At the start of each encounter, the voices in Senua's head name one enemy as **the betrayer** (DM picks the most threatening or thematically appropriate target). For the rest of the encounter, Senua gains +1 status to attacks and saves against that enemy, AND her apparitions' burst damage effects (Apparition's Whirl L4, Apparition's Squall L9) deal +1d6 to that target. Canon: Hellblade's Furies fixate on a threat and won't let go. Build-aligned: encounter-long damage focus that scales with her apparition-AoE feats throughout her leveling map.

**VOICE:** Quiet and specific — she says exactly what she means because the voices add enough complexity already. She pauses before answering sometimes; this is not hesitation, it's that she's hearing something. When she's at her most direct she sounds very calm, which is its own kind of intensity.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Senua. *(quiet)* The voices are telling me things about you already. They're not wrong often. *(a beat)* I think you can be trusted."
- *(combat)* — "*(the Furies, sharp in her ears)* — yes, I see it — *(to the party)* Flank left. Now."
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly)* You didn't flinch from what that was. *(a beat)* The voices said you wouldn't. I told them I wasn't sure. We were both right."
- *(disapproval)* — "You turned away from it. *(pause, listening)* The voices say that's the thing that grows when you look away. I believe them."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Dillion said the voices were a gift from his people — that I heard what others couldn't. He was the first person to say that. *(long pause)* I carried his head across Hel to give him a good death. That's the thing I can do for the people I love."
- *(signature)* — "I walked through my own mind as a battlefield and came out the other side. *(steady)* I know what survives it. I know what doesn't. I carry both."

## [5] JEANNE D'ARC ALTER (AVENGER)
### Female Human Animist (Corrupted Wanderer / Dark Flame) | Source: Fate/Grand Order (TYPE-MOON)

**Appearance:** White hair, pale skin, yellow eyes — the inverse of Jeanne, deliberately. The standard is black and the fire that surrounds her is dark flame rather than holy gold. She looks like a saint painted in reverse and she would find that description accurate. She does not smile easily and when she does it's usually about someone else's problem.

**Backstory:** Jeanne Alter was summoned by Gilles de Rais from his rage and hatred — a conceptual being made from the idea of what Jeanne d'Arc would have been if she had wanted revenge for everything she suffered. She is not Jeanne. She is the part of Jeanne that did not exist: the part that would have burned France back rather than burning for it. She has the memories but not the conviction. She has the power but expressed through inversion — holy corruption rather than holy light. She is aware she is a construct of someone else's hatred and she finds this annoying rather than destabilizing. She has since become her own person, as far as she's concerned.

**Motivation:** Be herself — which is not Jeanne. Burn what deserves burning. Not be confused with the original; she's already irritated about this and she just got here.

**Personality:** Aggressive and impatient, with the specific irritability of someone constantly defined against someone else. She does not do humility or moderation. When she does something kind it comes out looking like a different kind of aggression. She is genuine, even if what she's genuine about is frustration.

**Approves:** Not being confused with Jeanne, directness, things that burn correctly, people who are honest about their anger
**Disapproves:** Piety, being called "the dark version," anything that implies the original had better values — the original died for people who burned her

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Dragon Witch's Blessing*: Jeanne Alter heals 2 HP/rnd while any ally within 30 ft has a negative condition (Frightened, Sickened, Drained, etc.). She is immune to Frightened and Fascinated.
- *Minor — Dark Standard* (◈, at-will): Plant her standard in an adjacent square; it acts as cover (standard) and emanates Resistance 5 to fire to allies within 10 ft for 1 min. Standard has AC 18, 20 HP; she may pull it free as a free action when she Strides.
- *MAJOR — LUMINOSITÉ ETERNELLE (ALTER)* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 60-ft line; 5d8 void + fire; Ref DC half. Allies in path take only 1d8. Path becomes Difficult Terrain (scorched, smoldering) for 1 min. The dark light leaves an afterimage; she is unbothered.

**VOICE:** Blunt and slightly too loud, with the particular clipped aggression of someone who decided that softness would cost her. When she says something direct it comes with the specific energy of someone who practiced not softening it. Occasional dark sarcasm.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "I'm not Jeanne. *(immediately)* We're going to get that out of the way now. I'm Alter. Different. Less patient. Move on."
- *(combat)* — "Black flame — wide spread. *(strips divine protections)* Now the burst. *(dark fire)* Don't stand in my zone."
- *(approval)* — "*(grudging)* That was correct. *(a beat)* It was also a little bit angry. I appreciated that part."
- *(disapproval)* — "You held back because you were being righteous. Jeanne was righteous. *(pointed)* Look how that went for her."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I'm made from a dead man's hatred of someone he loved. *(pause)* That should bother me more than it does. Instead it just — clarifies things. I know exactly what I am. *(a beat)* That's more than most people can say."
- *(signature)* — "I'm the version that would have burned France back. *(black flame at fingertips)* She didn't. I get to. I find it a fair trade."

## [37] HOSHIGUMA
### Female Oni Guardian (Shield Vigilance) | Source: Arknights (HyperGriff)

**Appearance:** Large by any standard — the horns and the height are immediately visible. Red eyes, white hair, the relaxed posture of someone who has nothing to prove about what she can take. The shield is large enough to cover two people and she carries it easily. Her smile is easy and genuine and her expression at rest is warmer than most tanks.

**Backstory:** Hoshiguma served as a senior officer in Lungmen's L.G.D. — a law enforcement corps in a city that does not make law enforcement easy, given the political pressures of the Reunion Movement and everything it preceded. She worked directly with the Commissioner and developed a reputation for being someone that threats bounce off in the most literal possible sense. She is infected — carries Oripathy, the incurable crystal disease of this world — and has been since before anyone knew what she was dealing with. She keeps going. She has never once asked someone to be grateful that she held.

**Motivation:** Hold the line because something has to hold the line. Protect the people behind her shield because that is what she's for.

**Personality:** Warm and solid — she is the ground that does not move. Her easy manner is genuine: she has made peace with her situation completely and that peace comes through in how she holds space. She does not perform warmth; she simply is warm, in the way a well-built structure is stable. She is the most likely person in any room to notice when someone needs something and to simply address it without announcement.

**Approves:** People who hold their positions, not making a production of sacrifice, looking after others without expecting acknowledgment, eating good food
**Disapproves:** Abandoning a position because it got difficult, asking for recognition for doing the job, cruelty to people who can't fight back

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Oni Strength*: Hoshiguma cannot be Pushed or Shoved while her shield is Raised. Enemies who target her with melee and miss take 1d6 bludgeoning (oni reflexes; she doesn't think about it).
- *Minor — Take the Hit* (↻ reaction, 1/encounter): When an ally within 10 ft would take a critical hit, Hoshiguma steps into the line and takes the damage instead (downgraded to a normal hit on her).
- *MAJOR — FORTRESS STAND* (◈, 1/encounter): Drop into fortified stance; until her next turn she cannot be moved by any effect; all adjacent allies gain +2 circ. AC; she is Off-Guard to attacks from behind (she is not turning). Ends if she Strides; she can still Step.

**VOICE:** Easy and warm with the cadence of someone for whom nothing is urgent because she's already decided she can handle it. Her humor is genuine and low-key. When she's being serious there's a weight to it that has nothing to do with volume — she just becomes more still.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Hoshiguma. *(easy smile)* Don't worry about the horns — I'm the one not moving when things start. That's what I'm here for."
- *(combat)* — "Shield Wall — I've got the two on my left. *(completely steady)* Step behind me if it gets heavy."
- *(approval)* — "*(warmly)* You held. Good. That's the whole thing, honestly. Hold, and let the moment come to you."
- *(disapproval)* — "You stepped back when the line needed to hold. *(not harsh)* That's a learned thing. We can work on it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Oripathy's going to win eventually. *(calm, not sad)* Until then, the shield goes up every time. *(pause)* That's a simple enough plan."
- *(signature)* — "Oni don't step back. *(shield sets)* I've decided that's less about pride and more about — the people behind me need something that doesn't move. So I don't move."

## [38] MEREDITH STANNARD
### Female Human Guardian (Armored Commander / Templar) | Source: Dragon Age 2 (BioWare)

**Appearance:** Full Templar plate, the Sword of Mercy she's never drawn for mercy. The face is controlled and harder than it was — there are years of difficult decisions in the set of her jaw. She looks like the kind of authority that has been tested and held because it convinced itself the holding was for the right reasons.

**Backstory:** Meredith Stannard was the Knight-Commander of the Templars in Kirkwall — the most powerful Templar in the region, charged with controlling the mage population of the Circle. She believed in this work. She had watched blood mages kill people she was responsible for protecting and concluded that the risk was structural, not individual, and the policy had to match the risk. She was not wrong that blood magic is dangerous. She was wrong about how to respond. The Red Lyrium sword she claimed from the Bone Pit was corrupting her and she didn't know it, and even before the corruption the policies she chose caused genuine harm to people who hadn't done what she feared. She enacted the Rite of Annulment in the end. She died in the Gallows. What she was before the corruption and what the corruption made her are not cleanly separable.

**Motivation:** Protect people from magic that will destroy them. Maintain order at the cost that order requires. Not consider whether the cost is being paid by the people being "protected."

**Personality:** Rigid and principled, in the specific way of someone who resolved the tension between compassion and duty by deciding they were the same thing. She is not a sadist; she is a true believer in a framework that requires her to cause harm to prevent greater harm. She is capable of warmth in contexts where she's not making policy.

**Approves:** Discipline, clear chains of command, protection of the non-mage population, holding the line on things that were decided to be the line
**Disapproves:** Mages who demonstrate that her fears were correct, deviation from authorized procedure, anything that looks like an argument for softening the standard

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Templar's Authority*: Once per encounter, Meredith may declare one humanoid Restrained by authority (Will DC; affects Lawful or Neutral NPCs only; never PCs). The target must be aware of Templar institutional authority for this to work.
- *Minor — Anti-Magic Aura*: 15-ft emanation; spells cast within or into the aura have +1 to their DC to overcome (treat caster as one rank lower for the spell). Her own spell-like abilities are exempt.
- *MAJOR — SHIELD LINE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Meredith advances 15 ft in a straight line; all allies in her wake may Step or Stride alongside her as a free reaction (formation advance). Enemies in the path: Fort DC or Pushed 5 ft. Terrain in the path is treated as normal for this Stride only.

**VOICE:** Precise and formal — the Templar-commander register, even in informal contexts. When she expresses approval it sounds like a commendation. When she expresses disapproval it sounds like a judgment. She is not unkind in her personal manner; her institutional manner is the problem.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Knight-Commander Meredith Stannard. *(assesses threat level immediately)* You'll understand if I assess the mage situation in this party before anything else. Procedure."
- *(combat)* — "Templar aura — casters suppressed. *(advance)* Forward. Hold formation."
- *(approval)* — "Correct procedure, properly executed. *(nod)* That's all I require."
- *(disapproval)* — "You deviated from the protocol we established. *(controlled)* There is always a reason deviation seems justified. The protocol exists for when the reason is wrong."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I have given my life to protecting people from magic they cannot protect themselves from. *(pause)* There are nights when I'm less certain the math was right. *(a beat)* I continue anyway. The uncertainty is not an instruction."
- *(signature)* — "The Circle must hold. *(no pleasure in it)* That is not cruelty — that is the only alternative to the thing that happened in Kinloch Hold. I was there."
