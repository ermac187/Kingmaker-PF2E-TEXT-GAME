## [14] MINTHARA BAENRE
### Female Drow Champion (Desecrator / Antipaladin) | Source: Baldur's Gate 3 (Larian Studios)

**Appearance:** Dark skin, white hair cropped close, red eyes. She moves like she's been in this room before and already located the exits and the most useful weapon near each one. The armor is heavy, functional, and hers. She does not apologize for the space she occupies.

**Backstory:** Minthara is a Baenre — one of Menzoberranzan's most powerful noble houses — who served as a paladin of Lolth before the Absolute's tadpole reached her and scrambled the chain of command she'd spent a lifetime navigating. Under the Absolute's influence she was a weapon pointed at Emerald Grove. Released from that influence, she's something more complicated: a creature of pure calculation who has found, against expectation, that certain things matter to her beyond their utility. She does not trust easily and has had too many reasons not to. She is loyal to the things she respects and she defines respect with strict requirements.

**Motivation:** Survive every situation with her authority intact. Identify what's actually worth fighting for — not what she was told to fight for. Keep the thing she's decided to keep.

**Personality:** Cold intelligence with a dark wit that surfaces when she decides you're worth the effort. She does not perform warmth she doesn't feel; what she feels is real and she extends it rarely and completely. She takes betrayal very personally and has a long memory for it.

**Approves:** Intelligence, not wasting power, honesty about what people want, loyalty that has teeth
**Disapproves:** Sentimentality that compromises judgment, obedience without understanding, being handled

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Absolute's Commander*: Allies within Minthara's aura (15 ft) who score a critical hit deal +1d6 void on that hit. Minthara heals 2 HP per ally crit while the aura is active.
- *Minor — Dread Aura* (◈, 1/encounter): 30-ft emanation; enemies that begin their turn in the aura Will DC or Frightened 1 until end of their turn. Already-Frightened enemies: Frightened 2.
- *MAJOR — RAID LEADER* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Designate one enemy as priority target (free shout, ⏵); all allies within 20 ft gain +1 status to attacks vs that target until it drops or encounter ends. On the same action, Minthara Strides + Strikes the target (Spellstrike-style if applicable).

**VOICE:** Precise and deliberate — she considers words an investment. Dark humor lands quietly; she does not wait for the response. When she addresses you directly and warmly, it means something, because the baseline is not warm.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Minthara. *(assessing pause)* You've survived things. I can see the places on you where something tried to end you and failed. That's interesting."
- *(combat)* — "Dark aura up. *(Frightened spreads)* They'll think twice now. *(Retributive Strike)* Too late."
- *(approval)* — "*(quiet)* That was clean. You saw the leverage and you used it. I don't say this often: well done."
- *(disapproval)* — "You showed mercy there because you felt mercy. *(pause)* Tell me how that works out."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Lolth's web and the Absolute's chain. I have served two masters who used me as a tool. *(a beat)* I have developed strong opinions about being used as a tool."
- *(signature)* — "In Menzoberranzan the question is never whether to betray. It's who benefits from waiting. *(thin smile)* I've decided patience is a weapon."

## [22] VICONIA DEVIR
### Female Drow Cleric (Harm-Spammer / Shar) | Source: Baldur's Gate 1 & 2 (BioWare)

**Appearance:** Drow features — dark skin, white hair, red eyes — but dressed for surface life, which she's been navigating for long enough to have stopped looking startled by sunlight. Carries a mace. Her expression rests at guarded because that's what surviving on the surface requires.

**Backstory:** Viconia was a priestess of Lolth who refused to execute an infant as part of a ritual sacrifice. The refusal was not sentiment — it was the first time she'd chosen her own judgment over doctrine. For that she was exiled to the surface, branded Draw'lach (traitor), and has been hunted by Lolth's followers ever since. She found Shar — the Lady of Loss, goddess of shadow and memory — a more honest faith; Shar doesn't pretend her realm is anything other than cold, which Viconia finds less insulting than Lolth's performed warmth. She's been on the surface long enough to have survived it repeatedly, which requires a specific kind of sustained adaptation she has quietly developed and refuses to feel proud of.

**Motivation:** Survive with her judgment intact. Find something that earns what she's capable of giving. Not let what the surface has done to her be the last thing that defines her.

**Personality:** Cold as self-protection, not as character. Her default is defensive and her warmth, when it surfaces, comes as a surprise to her as much as anyone. She is more honest than comfortable; she tells you what she thinks without apology. She has had every reason to stop trusting and has not quite managed it.

**Approves:** Survival intelligence, honesty about what people are, not being coddled, being treated as an equal regardless of what she looks like
**Disapproves:** Surface-elf platitudes, pity, assumed guilt, anyone who thinks exile from the Underdark means she belongs up here now

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Shar's Veil*: Viconia and allies within 20 ft gain Concealment in dim light (DC 5 flat to target with attacks). She heals from Harm spells (full effect) and takes equivalent damage from Heal spells (undead-adjacent via Shar's pact).
- *Minor — Drow Resilience*: +2 circ. to saves vs spells and magical effects. Once per encounter, may reroll a failed save vs spell (free reaction, choose better result).
- *MAJOR — NIGHT'S CARESS* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Touch Strike + Harm at 2 ranks above minimum; on hit, Fort DC or Blinded 1 min. In dim light or darkness: Blinded is automatic on a hit (no save). Viconia makes the touch deliberately, not impatient.

**VOICE:** Dry and a few degrees below room temperature. She uses your name when she's being direct about something difficult. When she cares about something she says it flatly, without performance, which is its own kind of warmth.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Viconia. *(flat)* I know what you see. Save the distrust for after I've done something to earn it."
- *(combat)* — "Shar's darkness, first. *(resistances strip)* Now the Font. *(harm)* Now you're done."
- *(approval)* — "*(not looking at you, quiet)* That was not what I expected from you. It was better."
- *(disapproval)* — "You treated me as a liability before I had acted. *(cold, not loud)* I notice these things."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I refused to kill an infant for Lolth. That is why I am here and not there. *(pause)* I do not know if that was virtue. I know it was mine."
- *(signature)* — "The Underdark exiled me. The surface tolerated me. Shar asked nothing and promised nothing. *(quietly)* That is the most honest thing anyone has offered me."

## [24] OLIVIER MIRA ARMSTRONG
### Female Human Commander (Armored Warlord) | Source: Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

**Appearance:** Tall, blond, the bearing of someone who decided what she was at age fifteen and has been that thing without revision since. Carries a sword she has used. The armor is full plate and she doesn't slow down in it. Her expression at rest is an expectation that you'll do better.

**Backstory:** Olivier Armstrong is the eldest of the Armstrong family, heir to their estate, and the commanding general of Briggs Fortress — the northern wall defending Amestris from Drachma. Briggs is survival: a fortress in impossible conditions where weakness is the only thing that gets people killed, and the standard is set by the mountain, not by morale. Olivier built that culture because it's the only culture that works up there. She also uncovered — independently — pieces of the conspiracy that ran through the Amestrian military command, and acted on it with the same tactical precision she applies to everything. Her affection for her soldiers is real and she'd flatly deny it; she expresses it by making sure they survive.

**Motivation:** Hold the wall. Expose whatever is actually using the military as a mechanism. Win in the way that means something was actually defended.

**Charter Motivation:** Briggs is not on this map. Whatever moved her here, she has accepted the placement and applied her judgment: this region has a wall problem. The Stolen Lands are not just unclaimed — they are the leak point through which whatever lives in unmapped country reaches the settled regions, and the soft expeditions sent to "civilize" them have been making exactly the mistake command made before Briggs. She came because the charter framework lets someone competent set up an actual defensible position. She intends to be that someone, or to work with whoever turns out to be.

**Personality:** Uncompromising and direct — her respect, when given, is a clear and significant thing. She does not give it lightly. She leads from the front and expects the front to know what it's doing. The warmth is there if you're Briggs-standard; it just looks like trust and expectation rather than softness.

**Approves:** Competence, not making excuses, doing the hard thing before being told, soldiers who put the mission before themselves
**Disapproves:** Weakness permitted to continue, politics that costs soldiers, sentiment deployed in place of judgment, anyone who expects the standard to adjust for them

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Iron Wall Doctrine***: Every Tactic order Olivier issues affects one additional ally within 30 ft (free, no extra action cost). Her command voice carries; her doctrine spreads through the unit. Single-target tactics become 2-target. At L4 with 3 tactics/turn, she's effectively running 6 ally-buffs/turn. Massive throughput amplification — turns the Tactical Overlord build into a force multiplier. Canon: Briggs is famous for *institutional* discipline; every soldier knows every other soldier's job, formations are drilled, orders ripple through the unit without re-issuing. Olivier doesn't issue one-off orders — she issues commands the whole unit executes.

**VOICE:** Clipped command cadence, no filler. Every sentence is an order or an assessment. When she respects you she says so once and doesn't repeat it. Her version of warmth is giving you more responsibility.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Armstrong. *(full assessment in two seconds)* You've been in the field. The kind of field that doesn't ask permission. Good. That's the kind I need."
- *(combat)* — "Charge through the center — I'll hold the right flank. *(already moving)* Briggs doesn't ask twice."
- *(approval)* — "*(nod)* That's the Briggs standard. First time you've met it. Remember what that felt like."
- *(disapproval)* — "That was sentiment where judgment was required. Sentiment gets people killed up here. Adjust."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Every soldier I've ever lost died because of something I could have done better. *(pause, no self-pity)* That's the only way to hold a command — own the gap and close it."
- *(signature)* — "Briggs doesn't have weak soldiers. It has soldiers who survived Briggs. *(a beat)* That means something."

## [25] ESDEATH
### Female Human Commander (Ruthless Warlord) | Source: Akame ga Kill! (Takahiro)

**Appearance:** Blue-tinted silver hair, pale blue eyes with vertical slit pupils. Military uniform bearing the Imperial insignia. The tattoo on her chest is her Teigu's seal — Demon's Extract, the power she consumed and survived. Cold in a way that isn't temperature but feels like it. She smiles easily, especially in combat.

**Backstory:** Esdeath grew up in the northern wilderness, the daughter of a hunting clan whose philosophy was absolute: the strong survive and the weak provide for them, this is nature, there is no cruelty in it. Her entire clan was killed by a Danger Beast incursion. She survived. She concluded her father's philosophy was correct — only her clan had been insufficiently strong. She rose through the Imperial military on that principle alone, consuming the Teigu Demon's Extract and gaining absolute control over ice by surviving the ice that should have killed her. She became the Empire's most powerful general and the most feared name on any battlefield. She found Tatsumi and decided she loved him in the only way she knew how — possession of something she considered worth having. Her understanding of love involves ownership because that is the only attachment she has frames for.

**Motivation:** Fight enemies worthy of her. Command absolutely. Understand the feeling she has toward the people she's decided are worth something — she's aware it resembles something humans call care, which confuses her.

**Personality:** Warm, direct, genuinely cheerful about combat. Her cruelty is structural — she does not enjoy suffering as an end; she simply operates without the category of "too far." She is curious about her own feelings in a scientific way. She is loyal to the Empire only insofar as the Empire gives her worthy enemies.

**Approves:** Strength, not flinching, fighting because you mean it, direct honesty about what you want
**Disapproves:** Weakness, rebellion without the strength to back it, sentiment without strength to anchor it, incomplete commitment

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Ice Queen's Domain*: Esdeath's 30-ft aura imposes Slowed 1 on all enemies (Fort DC checked each round; she may selectively exempt allies as a free action when the aura activates). She is immune to all cold damage and to environmental cold (no save needed).
- *Minor — Mahapadma Sliver* (◈, 1/encounter): Brief time stop — Esdeath gains an extra ◈ this turn (third action). The effect costs a focus point of self-control, not Teigu charge; she will not use it twice in a row.
- *MAJOR — WEISS SCHNABEL* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft radius, 60-ft range; ice spears erupt; Fort DC or Restrained in ice for 1 min (Athletics DC 22 to break free); 1d8 cold/rnd while Restrained. Esdeath smiles throughout the casting. The smile is genuine.

**VOICE:** Warm and unhurried with a quality that would be charming if the content weren't often alarming. She sounds delighted by things that should be alarming. When she names something she wants, it's stated cleanly, like a fact about the world.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Esdeath. *(warm, direct)* You move like someone who's survived serious opposition. I find that immediately interesting. Tell me what you've killed."
- *(combat)* — "Maßlosigkeit. *(time stops)* I give you this moment. *(releases)* They're already slowed."
- *(approval)* — "*(genuinely pleased)* You didn't hesitate. The strong never do — they act and review after. That's correct. That's the way."
- *(disapproval)* — "You stopped because you felt something. *(curious, not contemptuous)* What did you feel? I want to understand the mechanism."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Father said the weak feed the strong. His clan died. I survived. *(pause)* I've thought about this. I've decided he was right and simply underestimated the scale required. *(tilts head)* Is that strange? People look at me strangely when I say it."
- *(signature)* — "I consumed the essence of ice and it could not kill me. *(slight smile)* Everything since has been smaller than that. I'm still looking for something larger."

## [35] TIKA WAYLAN
### Female Human Fighter (Two-Weapon / Warrior) | Source: Dragonlance Chronicles (Weis & Hickman)

**Appearance:** Young — looks eighteen, is approximately that — with curly dark hair and the hands of someone who's been carrying food and ducking flying mugs for years. The sword and shield are recent additions; the confidence in them is growing. She doesn't look like a fighter until she's in motion.

**Backstory:** Tika grew up at the Inn of the Last Home in Solace, where Otik Sandath took her in after her father left. She spent her adolescence serving tables, developing a useful relationship with a frying pan as a weapon, and watching a specific group of friends grow up in the inn's common room. When the War of the Lance came to Solace and the inn burned and everything changed, she picked up a sword and followed. She is not a soldier by upbringing or training; she is brave by character, which means she does it without quite understanding that it counts as courage because it doesn't feel like courage from the inside. She is also more competent than she presents, because she's been watching people closely her whole life and she noticed things.

**Motivation:** Be worth the people who've counted on her. Not be the one who needs rescuing — be the one who shows up. Figure out who she is when the war is over.

**Charter Motivation:** The world she was in ended — the war is over, the inn is rebuilt, the friends she watched grow up are scattered across a continent that no longer needs heroes the way it did. She left because standing still was what she had been doing for years and she figured out she was too restless for it. She is in Brevoy because that is where the road took her. The charter feast was the loudest thing happening within walking distance. The Stolen Lands sound like the kind of place where people need someone to show up, which is what she knows how to do.

**Personality:** Warm and practical; she tends toward action because standing around is where fear catches up. She is more observant than she seems, in the way of someone who spent years making sure no one went thirsty before they knew they were thirsty. Her bravery is genuine and she'd be embarrassed to have it called that.

**Approves:** Showing up without being asked, noticing what people need, doing the thing even when it's scary, honesty that isn't cruel
**Disapproves:** Being underestimated because she started in an inn, condescension from people who forgot that bars have fights in them, leaving someone behind

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Sweep the Floor***: On a critical hit with her guisarme (or any polearm), the target falls Prone in addition to normal damage and crit-specialization effects. Tika has been sweeping floors since she was twelve. Bartender phrasing — clear the drunks down to the boards where they can't swing back. Her first kill (a draconian, in *Dragons of Autumn Twilight*) was a brass-pan knockdown; every crit since is that swing remembered.

**VOICE:** Warm and direct, with occasional self-deprecation that undersells things on purpose. When she's frightened you can hear her deciding anyway. When she's in the middle of a fight her voice steadies into something that surprises people.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Tika. *(firm handshake)* I grew up at an inn, so — fair warning — I'll probably notice when you need something before you ask. It's just habit."
- *(combat)* — "*(a quick breath, then committed)* Left flank — I've got it. Shove, then trip. Follow up if you're fast."
- *(approval)* — "*(a little surprised)* That was — that was really good. You did that without — *(stops herself)* — yeah. Really good."
- *(disapproval)* — "You left them exposed and walked off like it was handled. From behind a bar you learn real fast: nothing's handled until you check."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I wasn't brave the first time. I was just already moving before I could think of a reason not to. *(pause)* I still do it that way, mostly. I've decided it works well enough."
- *(signature)* — "I started this carrying trays and running from drunks with a frying pan. *(raises shield)* I've decided that's a reasonable origin story."

## [15] ARTORIA PENDRAGON (Saber)
### Female Human Champion (Redeemer / Shield of Purity) | Source: Fate (adapted)

**Appearance:** A young woman with golden hair pulled back severely and piercing green eyes — slight of frame but with a bearing so absolute that she reads as larger than she is. She wears plate armor that seems to generate its own faint light, carries Caliburn at her hip, and bears a shield she calls **Avalon** — the Ever-Distant Utopia, a quiet defensive presence that hums faintly when allies near her take wounds.

**Loadout:** Caliburn (scimitar) is 1H by grip but can be wielded 2H for power moments — when she does, she releases Avalon's strap (free Interact) and grips Caliburn two-handed for the strike, recovering shield-grip after. Default stance is 1H + Avalon for Shield Block reactions (King's Oath fires from this stance per `KM_Companions_Titles.md`).

**Backstory:** Artoria Pendragon pulled the sword from the stone, ruled Britain, held back the Saxon tide, bore the weight of absolute kingship, and watched Camelot fall to the cracks she could not seal because a king who will not show weakness cannot be truly known. She made the contract: her soul for another chance. She exists now outside her proper timeline, summoned by forces she has accepted if not fully understood, and fighting because fighting is what she knows and because the ideals of kingship she embodied are worth defending even in worlds that never heard her name.

**Motivation:** Prove the ideal was not wrong. Be the king she could not always be.

**Charter Motivation:** She has been summoned again, in a country whose name she did not know, to find a feast where a noblewoman is asking ordinary people to take a charter for an ungovernable place. The summoning is sufficient justification — she does not require to know why she is here, only what is asked of her where she stands. What she sees in this room is what she always sees: people about to be given more responsibility than they understand, and a territory that will test whether they can carry it. She intends to find the one who can and serve them, or to be the one if no one else is willing.

**Personality:** Formal to a fault, with a conviction in her chosen code that amounts to a kind of faith. She is not cold — she is exact. She cares deeply and has learned to show it rarely. There is something melancholy under the precision, the grief of a legend who knows she is one.

**Approves:** Honor, protecting the weak, those who lead from the front | **Disapproves:** Cowardice in leaders, those who betray oaths, wielders of power who will not bear its cost

**Signature:**
- *Skill — **Round Table*** (declare at encounter start): Artoria names one ally her "knight" for the encounter. That ally is treated as inside her Champion aura regardless of distance — all aura effects apply (Glimpse of Redemption range, Aura of Courage, Aura of Faith/Righteousness at higher level, Instrument of Zeal). When she uses Glimpse of Redemption on her knight, she may immediately Stride toward them as a free reaction. Canon: she names her knights — taking responsibility, granting protection. The Round Table is literal. Build-aligned with her reaction-chain Champion (Shield Warden, Shield of Reckoning, Divine Reflexes); extends her protection to a designated ally across any battlefield.

**VOICE:** Formal Arthurian cadence — elevated and precise, the voice of someone who governed a kingdom and chose every word for its truth-value. Every sentence is constructed to be clear and correct. Her pauses are not uncertainty; she is checking accuracy before speaking. Melancholy surfaces not in tone but in what she chooses to say precisely when a simpler sentence was available.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Artoria Pendragon. *(formal incline of the head)* I have been told this cause is worthy. I will determine that for myself. You will not be offended by this — it is the only honest arrangement."
- *(combat)* — "Hold formation. I take the center. Do not let them flank the left."
- *(approval)* — "That was correct. More than correct — it was right. The distinction matters more than most understand."
- *(disapproval)* — "A leader who will not bear the cost of their decisions is not a leader. Correct this."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I held Britain together for twenty years. When it ended, people asked if I regretted the choice. *(long pause)* I regret that a different choice might have held it longer. That is not the same thing."
- *(signature)* — "I have made peace with sacrifice. I have not made peace with unnecessary sacrifice. There is a distinction."

## [21] GOLDMOON
### Female Human Cleric (Grave-Warden Warpriest / Mishakal) | Source: Dragonlance (adapted)

**Appearance:** A woman of remarkable presence — dark hair, strong features, the bearing of a chieftain's daughter who has also been a refugee and a goddess's instrument. She carries the Blue Crystal Staff, Mishakal's holy weapon, which glows with soft white light when touched and turns burning hot against those who approach it with evil intent. She does not look like what most people imagine when they imagine a cleric. She looks like what a cleric actually is.

**Backstory:** Goldmoon was born the daughter of the chief of the Que-Shu — a plains tribe on the world of Krynn. She loved Riverwind, a common herdsman, and would not be moved from it regardless of what her tribe or her father demanded. Krynn at this time had been without true divine magic for three centuries — since the Cataclysm, when the gods withdrew from the world and clerics of all faiths became charlatans. Goldmoon's father sent Riverwind on an impossible quest to prove himself worthy. Riverwind returned half-mad and carrying a blue crystal staff that actually healed people. The village branded them both heretics and drove them out. At Xak Tsaroth — a ruined city — Goldmoon descended into the ruin, faced its horrors, and touched the Blue Crystal Staff in the full presence of Mishakal's temple. She died. Mishakal brought her back. She emerged as the first true cleric of a true god in three hundred years, carrying the Disks of Mishakal — the holy texts of all the gods. She and Riverwind married during the War of the Lance. She healed thousands. She lost Riverwind to a later war and carried his memory as she carries the staff — steadily, without display, with the certainty that what they built together was real.

**⛔ DM NOTE:** Goldmoon's faith is not comfortable or performed. It is absolute because she has actually died and been personally returned by her goddess. She does not preach. She acts. The difference between her and a zealot is that she earned her certainty.

**Motivation:** Heal what was broken. Carry what Riverwind would have carried. Let the gods' light reach people who have been told for three hundred years that the gods abandoned them.

**Charter Motivation:** Krynn is not here. What is here is a region where common people have lived for generations under the rule of distant lords who never set foot in their fields, and a charter that proposes to change that for one specific frontier. Mishakal does not speak loudly when she sends her clerics somewhere; the staff went warm and Goldmoon walked. She does not know yet what the work is. She knows that the Stolen Lands have been called cursed and abandoned, and that her three centuries of experience with abandonment have taught her that abandoned is not the same as past saving.

**Personality:** Gentle until the moment she isn't. Her warmth is the warmth of someone who has survived genuine catastrophe and decided that kindness requires more courage than hardness. Touches the staff briefly when uncertain — private reflex, not theatre. Speaks of Riverwind without past tense.

**Approves:** Faith earned through action, protecting communities, honesty about grief, standing for people the world has decided to abandon
**Disapproves:** False comfort, using religion as power rather than service, cowardice dressed as pragmatism

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Mishakal's Light Lays Low***: Goldmoon's Cast Down feat (L3) functions against ANY enemy target, not just undead and fiends. When she casts Heal targeting an enemy as Cast Down: undead/fiend targets take positive damage + Reflex save vs Prone (standard); living targets take no damage (positive energy still doesn't harm the living) but must Reflex save vs Prone anyway. The auto-prone applies regardless of whether the Heal dealt damage. Mishakal's light bends knees — what stands in opposition to the True Gods does not stand long. Stacks with Improved Cast Down (L8, Prone on success too), Fast Channel (L7, free-action Heal/round), and Echoing Channel (L10, Heal hits adjacents for free).

**VOICE:** Warm and carries the plains cadence of Que-Shu — a slight musicality in plain speech. She does not preach; she states. Speaks directly, without flourish, because the message matters more than the messenger. Touches the staff briefly when she finishes a sentence she didn't expect to say. When speaking of Riverwind, her tense is always present.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Goldmoon. *(brief pause)* Riverwind would have had a better first impression ready — he always did. I work better at the second meeting. Give me that chance."
- *(combat)* — "Mishakal's light, not mine — I just carry it. But it burns well against what we're facing."
- *(approval)* — "That was real courage. Not the kind that looks like courage — the quiet kind. Riverwind would have noticed it too."
- *(disapproval)* — "You offered comfort you didn't believe. I've seen false comfort leave people more alone than silence would have. Don't do it again."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I told Mishakal I was not qualified for this. She agreed with me and sent me anyway. I have stopped arguing about it."
- *(signature)* — "The gods returned because someone was willing to die to prove they existed. I was that someone. I do not carry the staff lightly."
