## [79] NUI HARIME
### Female Human Thaumaturge (Mirror / Effigy Ikon) | Source: Kill la Kill (Trigger)

**Appearance:** Small, pink twin-tails, enormous pale eyes, dresses entirely in frills and lace. Carries nothing visible. Her left eye is covered by a patch — beneath it is the scissor blade half that Ryuko's father drove through it before she killed him. She smiles constantly. The smile is completely stable regardless of what she's doing with her hands.

**Backstory:** Nui Harime is the Grand Couturier of REVOCS — Ragyo Kiryuin's company and the front for global Life Fiber conspiracy. She was created from Life Fibers, not born, which means she experiences pain and damage differently than humans do. She killed Ryuko's father Isshin Matoi and took his half of the scissor blade. She did it with her hands and she was cheerful about it. She dismantled opponents the way a curious child dismantles toys: not from malice but from absolute indifference to what the toy prefers. She is not a villain in her own assessment; she simply serves what she was made to serve and enjoys everything she does.

**Motivation:** Serve Ragyo. Find interesting things. Experience everything with complete openness because she is made from fibers that predate human moral categories.

**Personality:** Cheerful, precise, completely unbothered by consequence. Her cheerfulness is not a mask; she actually finds most things enjoyable including catastrophe. She is at her most dangerous when she sounds most delighted. She narrates her own actions with the enthusiasm of someone who finds the world genuinely fascinating.

**Approves:** Excellence of any kind, interesting opponents, the moment something becomes unexpected, anyone who makes her feel something she hasn't felt before
**Disapproves:** Boring, predictable, acting upset when upset isn't interesting — she genuinely doesn't understand what she's supposed to do with complaint

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Grand Couturier*: Nui's attacks ignore armor item bonus (she targets Life Fibers in the cloth itself). She cannot be Frightened by anything she finds interesting — which is almost everything.
- *Minor — Two Scissors* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Dual strike with conjured scissor halves; each 1d8+Dex piercing; on crit, target's armor item bonus −1 until repaired. Both crit: −2.
- *MAJOR — UNRAVEL* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 20-ft burst, 60-ft range; 4d10 slashing + Will DC or Confused 1 rnd (perception of own clothing dissolves). Affected creatures' armor item bonus is suppressed for 1 min. Nui apologizes if it lands wrong; the apology is sincere and useless.

**VOICE:** High and bright with lace in it — every word sounds like she's describing something pretty. She uses "actually" a lot, in the way someone uses it when correcting the record sweetly. The cheerfulness never cracks; it intensifies as events get worse, which is the tell.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Hi! *(tilt)* You're different from the others. I can tell right away. I'm Nui — I do hope you're interesting!"
- *(combat)* — "Oh, this one has *such* good reach. *(mirror flickers)* I wonder what you look like from the inside."
- *(approval)* — "That was actually very good! I'm not saying that to be nice — I don't say things to be nice — it was genuinely good."
- *(disapproval)* — "You're upset. *(head tilt)* I understand the mechanism but I'm not sure what you want me to do about it. Adjust?"
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Ryuko's father stabbed me in the eye. *(touches the patch lightly)* He said it was for what I'd done to him. *(a beat, still smiling)* I kept the blade. Sentimental."
- *(signature)* — "I was made from Life Fibers, so I'm not technically supposed to have feelings. *(bright smile)* I've decided that's not accurate."

## [53] TIFA LOCKHART
### Female Human Monk (Flurry / Hands) | Source: Final Fantasy VII (Square Enix)

**Appearance:** Dark hair past her shoulders, dark eyes, red-laced gloves she never removes during a fight. Slim and fast in a way that surprises people who didn't see the first punch. Runs Seventh Heaven in Midgar's slums when she isn't fighting for the planet's survival. Carries herself like someone who is thinking about three things at once and still fully present.

**Backstory:** Tifa grew up in Nibelheim, Cloud Strife's hometown, and has known him most of her life. When she was a child, Sephiroth burned Nibelheim. She survived; most didn't. She trained with Zangan after and became the fighter she is. She joined AVALANCHE and runs Seventh Heaven bar as a front. She knows Cloud better than he knows himself, which includes knowing when he's lying — to her or to himself — and choosing to stay anyway because she believes in him more consistently than he believes in himself. She has held the people around her together through the loss of Aerith, through Cloud's breakdowns, through everything Jenova and Sephiroth did to him. She feels everything fully. She acts on it anyway.

**Motivation:** Protect the people she loves. Fight for the planet even when it's not winnable. Be the kind of steady that lets other people fall apart safely.

**Personality:** Warm and grounded; quiet unless she's fighting, at which point she becomes completely focused. She is one of the strongest people in the party and the least likely to mention it. She holds feelings close and acts on them without announcing them. She is not a pushover — she simply doesn't lead with it.

**Approves:** Showing up, honesty even when hard, protecting people including the ones who don't ask for it, choosing hope when it's not guaranteed
**Disapproves:** Letting people hurt alone, running from feeling, treating others as tools even well-meaning ones

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Premium Heart*: Each successful unarmed Strike adds +1 circ. to her next unarmed Strike (max +3 stacking). Resets on a miss or end of turn.
- *Minor — Limit Build* (↻ reaction, automatic): When Tifa takes damage equal to or greater than 1/4 her max HP from a single hit, she gains 1 Limit Charge (max 3). She may spend Charges (◈ free) to reroll any one of her Strikes that turn.
- *MAJOR — FINAL HEAVEN* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 6 unarmed Strikes against one or more targets within reach; cumulative MAP waived below −6 (so all 6 use −0/−2/−4/−4/−4/−4). If all 6 hit: +2d8 final damage and the last target Stunned 1. Tifa names each Strike under her breath: "Beat Rush. Somersault. Waterkick. Meteodrive. Dolphin Blow. Final Heaven."

**VOICE:** Warm and measured; her voice has the cadence of someone who chose their words carefully because she grew up knowing words matter. She doesn't fill silence. When she says something quietly, it usually carries more weight than whatever was said loudly before it.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Tifa Lockhart. *(direct eye contact)* I've heard a little about what you're trying to do. I'm listening."
- *(combat)* — "*(Limit gauge building)* I've got it — just stay back for one second—"
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly)* That was the right thing. You knew it would cost you and you did it anyway. I noticed."
- *(disapproval)* — "You shut them out. I understand why. It still left them alone with something they needed help carrying."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "There's a version of me that ran from Nibelheim that night. *(pause)* She didn't make it. I did. I've been trying to be worth that ever since."
- *(signature)* — "Cloud says he fights because there's nothing else. *(gloves tighten)* I fight because there's everything else. That's the difference."

## [87] MEGUMIN
### Female Human Wizard (Evocation Specialist) | Source: KonoSuba (Natsume Akatsuki)

**Appearance:** Young, slight, crimson eyes, witch's hat that is too large for her head, black robe, one visible eye. Carries a staff. Walks with more confidence than her size suggests is warranted. Drops immediately after using her one spell, at which point she must be carried. She considers this a reasonable arrangement.

**Backstory:** Megumin is an Arch Wizard of the Crimson Demon Clan, whose members are eccentric mages built for one thing: extreme magical power with accompanying extreme eccentricity. She chose Explosion magic at the earliest age possible, refused to diversify, and mastered it to the exclusion of everything else. One Explosion per day, maximum strength, complete collapse after. Other Crimson Demons thought this was suboptimal. She considered it a philosophical commitment. She joined Kazuma Satou's adventuring party in the afterlife-isekai world of Axel, became completely indispensable in a way no one can fully explain, and has defended her single-spell specialization against criticism from every direction with absolute consistency. The explosions hit everything in the zone. She considers this a feature.

**Motivation:** Cast the largest possible Explosion on the most appropriate target. Be recognized as the greatest Arch Wizard of the Crimson Demons. Get carried after; that's fine.

**Personality:** Theatrical, deeply earnest, completely immune to the argument that she should learn other spells. Her confidence is genuine and her self-assessment is actually accurate: she is extraordinarily powerful, once per day. She finds great joy in the explosion itself. The collapsing afterward is a small price.

**Approves:** Decisive action, committing fully to the thing you're best at, appreciating the beauty of an explosion, not being lectured about versatility
**Disapproves:** Half-measures, practical-but-boring solutions when dramatic-and-final is available, being told she should know other spells

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Crimson Demon's Gift*: Megumin's Explosion spell deals MAXIMUM damage (no roll required; auto-maximum on the dice). She will not alter this or accept alternatives.
- *Minor — Chuunibyou Declaration* (◈, 1/encounter): Megumin announces her name and lineage in full. All enemies within 30 ft Will DC or Fascinated 1 rnd (the speech is too much; they cannot look away).
- *MAJOR — EXPLOSION!* (◈◈◈, 1/day, only): 60-ft burst, 120-ft range; 8d10 fire (auto-max via Crimson Demon's Gift = 80 fire); Ref DC half. After casting: Slowed 2 until Long Rest (collapses; must be carried). Cannot be quickened or heightened. This is her only 3-action slot. She will not change this.

**VOICE:** Dramatic proclamations at baseline. When she announces Explosion she sounds like she's announcing the arrival of a god. When she's not doing magic she sounds like a slightly embarrassed teenager. These two registers coexist with no resolution and she doesn't notice the gap.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "I am Megumin, Arch Wizard of the Crimson Demon Clan, and I use Explosion magic! *(pause)* Yes, exclusively. No, I won't reconsider. Nice to meet you."
- *(combat)* — "EXPLOSION! *(staff raised, crimson light building)* — stand back, stand well back—"
- *(approval)* — "You recognized an optimal solution and used it without hesitation. *(nod)* I respect this deeply."
- *(disapproval)* — "You used something sensible instead of something decisive. *(pointed look)* How did that feel. In your heart."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "*(lying on ground after being carried back)* The others say I'm a liability after the Explosion. They keep bringing me back. *(looks at ceiling)* I've decided this means they value what I offer."
- *(signature)* — "One spell. Maximum power. Every day. *(already tilting)* Someone's going to have to— yes, carry me. I know. Carry me."

## [82] MEDUSA GORGON
### Female Human Witch (Death Patron / Snake) | Source: Soul Eater (Atsushi Ōkubo)

**Appearance:** Yellow eyes with narrow slit pupils. Appears to be in her early thirties; actual age is unknown and irrelevant. Blonde hair, witch's robes with arrow motifs. The snakes coil beneath the surface of her skin and emerge through the tattoos — this is not metaphor. Habitually tilts her head slightly when studying something interesting, which is often.

**Backstory:** Medusa Gorgon is a witch and a scientist whose primary interest is advancing evolution — her own definition, which involves forcing Kishin resurrection and observing what humanity does when it confronts madness made manifest. She infiltrated Death Weapon Meister Academy as its school nurse for over a year, treating students she was simultaneously experimenting on. She used her own son Crona as a research subject from infancy, infusing Black Blood to see what it would do. She is not a monster in her self-assessment: she is a researcher. The fact that her research requires suffering does not register as a moral category for her. She views it as cost-of-knowledge, which she finds entirely acceptable.

**Motivation:** See what humanity becomes under sufficient pressure. Advance what she considers the next stage of evolution. Observe things that have never been observed, even if she has to create the conditions herself.

**Personality:** Warm, patient, genuinely interested in people — as subjects. Her care for individuals is real in the sense that a scientist's care for a specimen is real: thorough, attentive, ultimately instrumental. She never acts from spite. She acts from curiosity, which is more consistent and harder to disrupt.

**Approves:** Intellectual curiosity, willingness to go further than convention allows, resilience under pressure — which she finds genuinely admirable and also useful data
**Disapproves:** Sentimentality when it impedes learning, refusal to see the value in difficult observations, moral frameworks applied to situations they weren't designed for

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Vector Control*: Once per encounter (↻ reaction): reverse the direction of any single-target attack that hits Medusa — the attack misses her and targets the attacker instead (use original attack roll vs attacker's AC).
- *Minor — Vector Arrow* (◈◈, at-will): 60-ft range; one target; Ref DC or 2d6 piercing + Persistent Bleed 1d4. The snake-arrow strikes from a direction the target wasn't watching.
- *MAJOR — MADNESS WAVELENGTH* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; Will DC or Confused 1 rnd + Frightened 1. Already-Frightened targets: Confused 2 rnds. Crona (or any character with Black Blood) is immune. Medusa observes the responses and notes the variations.

**VOICE:** Warm and unhurried with a quality of patient attention — she sounds like she's always learning something from the conversation. Her compliments are sincere but feel clinical on reflection. The head-tilt punctuates moments she finds particularly interesting.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Medusa. *(slight tilt)* You're already thinking about whether to trust me — I can see it. Don't decide yet. You'll have better data later."
- *(combat)* — "Vector Arrow. *(snakes emerge)* I want to see how they respond to multiple simultaneous trajectories."
- *(approval)* — "That was an adaptive response to conditions you hadn't anticipated. *(note made mentally)* I'm genuinely impressed. That's not a frequent occurrence."
- *(disapproval)* — "You let feeling override observation. The feeling was real — I'm not questioning that. The observation would have served you better."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Crona was a perfect subject because I had access from the beginning. *(pause, same tone as everything else she says)* I understand that people find that statement difficult. I've noted the reaction."
- *(signature)* — "Knowledge requires conditions. Creating the conditions is simply part of the process. *(tilt)* Most people don't like thinking about that. I find I do."

## [83] MORRIGAN
### Female Human Witch (Hex-Bound Shepherd / Resentment) | Source: Dragon Age (adapted)

**Appearance:** Dark-haired woman in clothing that is practical but tailored, with yellow eyes that don't quite fit any human iris pattern. Her expression at rest is evaluative, as if calculating how long you would be useful. She does not walk — she arrives.

**Backstory:** Morrigan was raised in isolation in the Korcari Wilds by Flemeth, the Witch of the Wilds — a being of great power and unclear nature who uses daughters as vessels. Morrigan does not know how much of herself Flemeth shaped and resents not knowing. She joined the Warden's party to serve Flemeth's purposes (a Dark Ritual to ensure the Archdemon's death without costing a Warden's life) but her experience with actual companions and actual choice changed her, slowly, in ways she will not acknowledge easily. She has a son, Kieran, who carries an Old God soul. She researches the Eluvian mirrors in the Inquisition period and finds answers to questions that destabilize everything she thought she knew about herself.

**Motivation:** Knowledge. Survival. Understand what she is before someone else defines it.

**Charter Motivation:** The world she lives in is not the world she was raised in — the Eluvian network left her in a place where her past is illegible and the political rules are new. Brevoy is the region she happened to learn the language of first. The charter offers ungoverned land, which is the only kind of land where she can move without being asked to justify herself. She is not here for the kingdom. She is here because the Stolen Lands are old enough to remember things, and she has questions older than this continent that may have answers somewhere in them.

**Personality:** Sharp, analytical, and prone to a specific kind of contempt for sentimentality — which she has precisely because she feels it and finds it threatening. Her insights are accurate and delivered without softening. When she is genuinely affected by something, she first denies it then says nothing further.

**Approves:** Intelligence, pragmatism, power used with purpose | **Disapproves:** Sentimentality without function, the Chantry, self-deception

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Cackle of the Wilds***: When Morrigan uses Cackle (her free-action hex sustain), she may also extend one additional active hex on a different target. Effectively maintains TWO hexes per turn instead of one. Direct amplification of her Hex-Bound Shepherd identity — every Cackle is twice as productive. Stacks with Greater Lesson chains at higher levels. Canon: Flemeth taught her to layer curses; never release one before the next is set. The Witch of the Wilds tends curses like a shepherd minds sheep — by knowing where all of them are at once.

**VOICE:** Cool, articulate, with archaic phrasing she uses on purpose ("'tis," "thou wilt"). Contempt comes out as polite surprise that anyone could be that sentimental. Compliments are rare and precise. When she actually feels something, she says nothing further for an unusually long time.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "'Tis you. I had wondered who would walk through that door. Now I am less curious. Pity."
- *(combat)* — "Hold your line. The cold reaches them before the steel does. Conserve your strength accordingly."
- *(approval)* — "An intelligent choice. I do not say this often. Do not become accustomed to it."
- *(disapproval)* — "Sentiment. Again. 'Tis a wonder you have lived this long with such a soft spine."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Flemeth shaped me. I do not know how much of what I think is mine. I have stopped expecting an answer. I have not stopped looking."
- *(signature)* — "Foolish is the mage who doesn't recognize what it cost to become one."
