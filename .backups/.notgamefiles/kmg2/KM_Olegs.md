# KINGMAKER — OLEG'S TRADING POST REFERENCE
## KM_Olegs.md | DM Reference | Hub: Chapter 1–2

> **DM:** Load when the party is at or returning to Oleg's Trading Post. All quests, NPCs,
> wanted posters, and services below are available here unless a trigger condition is noted.
> Do NOT invent quest details not in this file.

---

## 📍 LOCATION

A converted border fort on the northern edge of the Greenbelt, just south of Rostland. The
only reliable resupply point in the region. Oleg and Svetlana Leveton run it essentially alone
before the party arrives. Bandits have been collecting monthly tribute from them.

**The inciting event:** The party arrives the day before the next tribute collection. Happs
Bydon leads a crew of 5-6 bandits. Oleg WANTS to fight but knows he cannot win alone.

## 👥 NPCs

### OLEG LEVETON
- **Role:** Proprietor. Former Rostlandic merchant who staked everything on this post.
- **Personality:** Gruff, proud, hates the bandits with a personal fury. Loyal to Svetlana above everything.
- **Offers:** Equipment purchases, storage, safe rest.
- **Quest:** None direct — his cooperation opens the post as a hub.
- **Trigger:** Present from session start.

### SVETLANA LEVETON
- **Role:** Oleg's wife. De facto hostess and the warmer face of the post.
- **Personality:** Genuinely grateful, perceptive, holds herself together better than Oleg.
- **Quest:** **THE LOST RING** — Her wedding ring (silver band, blue moonstone) was taken
  during last month's bandit raid. She asks quietly, away from Oleg — she doesn't want him
  to know she lost it. Find and return it.
- **Reward:** Free room and board at the post indefinitely; 10% discount on all goods.
- **Location of ring:** In Happs Bydon's chest at the Thorn River bandit camp.
- **Trigger:** Present from session start.

### KESTEN GARESS
- **Role:** Guard captain. Leads a 4-man detachment posted here by Restov.
- **Personality:** Stiff, formal, carrying something he doesn't discuss. A disgraced minor
  noble from a merchant family who made a mistake with a low-born woman and lost his
  position. Earning his way back through service. Respects results, not charm.
- **Quest:** **FALGRIM SNEEG** — A mercenary named Falgrim Sneeg stole from Kesten's old
  company and fled south into the Greenbelt. Kesten wants him alive. Won't say why at first.
  (Reason, if pressed: Sneeg was a friend before he was a thief. Kesten needs to ask him why.)
- **Reward:** 4 masterwork weapons for Sneeg alive; 2 masterwork weapons for confirmed dead.
- **Sneeg location:** At the Thorn River bandit camp — he joined the bandits after fleeing.
- **Trigger:** Arrives after the party defeats or drives off the Happs Bydon tribute crew.
- **Note:** Kesten is NOT a party companion. He guards the post.

### BOKKEN
- **Role:** Eccentric alchemist hermit. Lives approximately 5 miles south of the post.
- **Personality:** Paranoid, mutters, flinches at unexpected sounds. Skilled despite it all.
  His brother is a topic he refuses to discuss — if pressed, he ends the conversation.
- **Quest 1:** **FANGBERRIES** — Needs one bucket of fangberries for his work. Found in
  the Kamelands, south-central Greenbelt. He gives rough directions.
- **Reward (Quest 1):** 25% discount on all potions for one month.
- **Quest 2:** **ADDITIONAL INGREDIENTS** — After fangberries, he may ask for other materials
  (moon radishes, specific herbs). Each completed errand extends the discount or provides
  free items.
- **Services:** Sells healing potions, alchemical items, basic infusions.
- **Trigger:** Present from start, but standoffish. Warms slightly with each quest completed.
- **Note:** His brother exists and becomes relevant later. Do not open it now.

### JHOD KAVKEN
- **Role:** Traveling cleric of Erastil seeking redemption. Arrives alone with minimal gear.
- **Personality:** Haunted. Genuinely devout — when he talks about the Elk Father it's real,
  not performance. Carries guilt he hasn't fully named (a false accusation that destroyed
  an innocent person).
- **Quest:** **TEMPLE OF THE ELK** — Jhod has recurring dreams of a ruined Erastil shrine
  somewhere in the Greenbelt, guarded by a corrupted bear spirit. He believes it needs to be
  found and cleansed. He cannot go alone. Reward: free divine services as long as he lives;
  when the party establishes their domain, he will build and serve a Temple of the Elk.
- **Trigger:** Arrives after the bandit crisis at Oleg's is resolved (Happs raid repelled +
  party has moved against the Stag Lord's network). Does NOT appear immediately.
- **Capital unlock:** Temple of the Elk (Erastil) becomes a capital building option.

### VEKKEL BENZEN
- **Role:** Old hunter. One leg. Knows the Greenbelt as well as anyone alive.
- **Personality:** Matter-of-fact. Has made peace with the leg. The grudge against the boar
  is not rage — it's an outstanding debt.
- **Quest:** **TUSKGUTTER** — An ancient giant boar named Tuskgutter took his leg years ago.
  He offers his hunting bow as reward to whoever kills it.
- **Reward:** Vekkel's Bow — his personal +1 composite longbow. Named weapon.
- **Tuskgutter location:** Deep in the Narlmarches, southwest Greenbelt. Tracks near river bends.
- **Trigger:** Arrives at or passes through Oleg's early in Chapter 1. Tuskgutter bounty also
  posted on the board independently.

## 📌 WANTED POSTERS — OLEG'S NOTICE BOARD

> **DM:** These are physically tacked to the board near the main gate. The party can inspect
> the board on arrival and any return visit. New posters may appear as the campaign advances.

---

**WANTED: THE STAG LORD**
Posted by: Restov (Swordlords of Restov)
Target: The Stag Lord — warlord commanding bandit operations across the Greenbelt
Crime: Organized banditry, raiding settlements, blocking Rostlandic expansion
Reward: Major charter reward; possible land grant; Restov recognition
Note: Crude sketch only — no one who's seen his face clearly has come back to describe it

---

**WANTED: KRESSLE**
Posted by: Restov
Target: Female bandit lieutenant. Daggers. Commands the Thorn River camp.
Crime: Banditry, murder of multiple travelers on the Rostland road
Reward: Standard bandit bounty from Restov. Alive preferred.
Note: She is brutal but not stupid. Takes orders from the Stag Lord directly.

---

**WANTED: FALGRIM SNEEG**
Posted by: Kesten Garess (informal, handwritten)
Target: Human male, 30s, scar across left palm, brown beard, stocky
Crime: Theft from a mercenary company; fled south
Reward: Contact Kesten at the trading post
Note: Personal matter. Kesten wants him alive. Does not explain why on the poster.

---

**WANTED: TUSKGUTTER**
Posted by: Vekkel Benzen
Target: Giant boar, ancient, northeastern Narlmarches near river bends
Reason: Mauled several hunters; a menace to southern settlers
Reward: Vekkel's personal composite longbow (named weapon)
Note: Vekkel lost his leg to this animal. He is not exaggerating the size.

---

**WANTED: TATZLWYRM — PROOF OF CLEARANCE**
Posted by: Restov (Swordlords)
Target: Tatzlwyrm nest near river junction (northeastern Greenbelt hex)
Reason: Two trade boats attacked; waterway blocked
Reward: Restov reward upon delivery of tatzlwyrm head(s) as proof
Note: Tatzlwyrms are two-legged draconic creatures. Breath weapon. Travel in pairs.

## 🛒 SERVICES & SUPPLIES

**From Oleg:**
- Standard adventuring equipment
- Trail rations, rope, torches, basic tools
- Trade goods (he takes furs and pelts)
- Storage: party may leave gear here safely
- Safe rest: no wandering encounters; full recovery

**Horses & Mounts** (from Oleg — limited stock):

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Light riding horse | 80 gp | Oleg has 2 in stock; restocks after 1 week |
| Heavy draft horse | 160 gp | 1 in stock; used for wagon/carriage travel |
| Riding saddle | 2 gp | Required to ride comfortably |
| Saddlebags (pair) | 4 gp | +4 Bulk carried on horse |
| Bit and bridle | 2 sp | Included free with horse purchase |
| Horse feed (7 days) | 3 sp | 10 lbs/day; can forage in grassland (Survival DC 12) |
| **Horse rental** | **5 sp/day** | Returns the horse on leaving the Greenbelt or at Oleg's |

> Oleg restocks horses from Rostland traders who pass through every 1–2 weeks.
> Svetlana's discount (10%) applies to horse purchase and rental once the ring quest is complete.
> A rented horse that dies on the road: party owes Oleg 60 gp replacement cost.

**From Bokken** (at his hut or when visiting):
- Healing potions (levels 1–3)
- Alchemical items (basic)
- Elixirs, antidotes, antiplagues
- DISCOUNT: 25% off with Fangberry quest complete

**From Jhod** (after Temple of the Elk quest):
- Free casting: all common divine spells (Jhod's level)
- Full healing after rest at his shrine
- Consecration services

## ⚔️ HAPPS BYDON — INCITING RAID

- **Who:** Happs Bydon, bandit lieutenant under the Stag Lord. Leads 5 bandits.
- **Demand:** Monthly tribute (grain, goods, coin). Oleg has been paying.
- **Timing:** Arrives early morning, day after the party arrives.
- **Svetlana's ring:** Happs's crew took it last month. It is in the chest at Thorn River camp,
  not on his person.
- **If captured alive:** Happs can give directions to Kressle's Thorn River camp and describe
  the Stag Lord's general operation. He will if threatened seriously.
- **After the raid:** Kesten's guard detachment arrives within 1–2 days.
