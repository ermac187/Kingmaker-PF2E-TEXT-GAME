>
> **Format:** POWER (passive) · MOVE (active + action cost + cadence) · LORE (canon hook)
> **Action icons:** ◈ = 1 action · ◈◈ = 2 · ◈◈◈ = 3 · ↻ = reaction · ⏵ = free
> **DC formula:** 10 + companion level + key attribute + prof
> **Use scaling:** L1–5 as listed · L6–10 +1 · L11–15 +2 · L16–20 +3 (unless fixed)
> **No fabrication.** If a companion # is not listed across A/B/C/D/E, use preset only.

## ⚗️ ALCHEMIST (#2, 3)

**[2] Sucrose** — Alchemist (Mutagenic Logistician)
  SIGNATURE — *Living Reagent* (Skill, downtime): 10 min foraging in any
    non-combat scene with natural materials → +1 Infused Reagent (max +3/day).
    Wilderness/rich biome: +2 per forage. Barren: no gain. Foraged reagents
    expire at next daily prep. Doubles+ her daily mutagen output on travel
    days. Canon: Sumeru bio-researcher.
  LORE — Plant scholar who apologizes for experiments and runs more anyway.

**[3] Excella Gionne** — Alchemist (Toxicologist)
  POWER — *Uroboros Protocol*: Excella's poisons resist antidotes — mundane
    antidotes fail; only alchemical (DC 25+) or magical antidotes work.
  MOVE — *Targeted Dose* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Precision injection; 30-ft range;
    Fort DC or Enfeebled 2 + Clumsy 2 for 1 hour. Crit fail: Doomed 1 also.
  LORE — Tricell's rising star who believed the program was entirely her own design.

## 👁️ ANIMIST (#4, 5)

**[4] Senua** — Animist (Echoing Hierophant / Witness to Ancient Battles)
  SIGNATURE — *The Furies* (Passive): At encounter start, voices name one
    enemy "betrayer." For the rest of the encounter, Senua gains +1 status to
    attacks and saves vs that enemy, AND her apparition burst feats (Whirl L4,
    Squall L9) deal +1d6 to that target. Canon: Hellblade's Furies fixate.
    Build-aligned with her apparition-AoE leveling progression.
  LORE — Pict warrior carrying her dead lover's skull and every voice that followed.

**[5] Jeanne d'Arc Alter** — Animist (Dark Order)
  POWER — *Dragon Witch's Blessing*: Jeanne Alter heals 2 HP/rnd while any ally
    has a negative condition. Immune to Frightened and Fascinated.
  MOVE — *Luminosité Eternelle (Alter)* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 60-ft line; 5d8 void +
    fire; Ref DC half. Allies in path take only 1d8. Path becomes difficult terrain
    (scorched) for 1 min.
  LORE — The dragon witch the real Jeanne refused to become; still asking why the faith survived.

  LORE — Amazon warlord who made herself the strongest thing in the room; still carrying the wound that made the tool.

## ⚔️ CHAMPION (#14, 15)

**[14] Minthara Baenre** — Champion (Desecrator/Lolth)
  POWER — *Absolute's Commander*: Allies within Minthara's aura (15 ft) who score
    a critical hit deal +1d6 void on that hit. Minthara heals 2 HP per ally crit.
  MOVE — *Raid Leader* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Designate one enemy as priority; all
    allies within 20 ft gain +1 status attack vs that target until it drops or
    encounter ends. Command shout delivered as free action (⏵).
  LORE — Drow general who served the Absolute; now examining what she'd want if the choice were hers.

**[15] Artoria Pendragon (Saber)** — Champion (Redeemer / Shield of Purity)
  SIGNATURE — *Round Table* (Skill, declare at encounter start): Name one ally
    "knight." That ally is treated as inside her Champion aura regardless of
    distance for ALL aura effects (Glimpse range, Aura of Courage/Faith/
    Righteousness, Instrument of Zeal). Using Glimpse of Redemption on her
    knight: she may Stride toward them as a free reaction. Extends reaction-
    chain protection to a designated ally across any battlefield. Canon: she
    names her knights and is responsible for them.
  LORE — Britain's Once and Future King; the sword chose duty over everything else.

## 🛡️ COMMANDER (#24, 25)

**[24] Olivier Armstrong** — Commander (Tactical Overlord, Battlecry!)
  SIGNATURE — *Iron Wall Doctrine* (Passive): Every Tactic order Olivier issues
    affects one additional ally within 30 ft (free, no extra action cost).
    Single-target tactics become 2-target. At L4 (3 tactics/turn) = 6

  LORE — General of Fort Briggs; the north is hard and she is harder; she considers that a fair trade.

**[25] Esdeath** — Commander (Conquest)

    Restrained in ice for 1 min; 1d8 cold/rnd while Restrained.
  LORE — Empire's strongest general, who found the one thing that nearly stopped her.

## ✨ EXEMPLAR (#30, 31)

**[30] Diana Prince (Wonder Woman)** — Exemplar (Amazonian Ikon)
  POWER — *Aegis Deflect*: Once per round (↻ reaction): deflect one projectile or

  MOVE — *Lasso of Truth* (◈◈, 1/encounter): 30-ft range; Will DC or Restrained

**[31] Hope (Lady Death)** — Exemplar (Death's Aspect Ikon)
  POWER — *Pale Throne's Presence*: Undead within 30 ft cannot be controlled by
    enemy necromancers while Hope (Lady Death) lives. Her Strikes deal both physical and
    void damage (target takes whichever type they're weaker to).
  MOVE — *Unholy War* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; 4d10 void; Will DC or
    Frightened 2. Undead targets Stunned 1 on crit fail. Allied Death ikon: immune.
  LORE — Born of Hell's cruelty and her own will; she rules Death because it was the only throne she wasn't denied.

## 🌊 KINETICIST (#46, 47)

**[46] Yang Xiao Long** — Kineticist (Wood + Earth Gate / Everbloom Bastion)
  SIGNATURE — *Semblance: Burn* (Passive): When Yang takes damage exceeding her
    CON modifier (4+), her next Blast or Strike within 1 round deals +1d6
    damage. Refreshes with each qualifying hit; resets per round. Direct port
    of her RWBY Semblance — damage absorbed becomes damage dealt. Build-aligned
    with CON 18 tank role (Wood reskinned as Semblance HP recovery, Earth as
    Ember Celica shockwaves).
  LORE — Beacon's brawler; rebuilt after everything broke. Some of the answers surprised her.

**[47] Caitlin Snow (Killer Frost)** — Kineticist (Water/Cold Gate)
  POWER — *Ice Absorption*: Caitlin Snow (Killer Frost) absorbs cold damage (heals equal amount).
    She is immune to Slowed from cold sources.
  MOVE — *Freeze* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Touch or 30-ft ranged; Fort DC or Slowed 2 +
    3d6 cold. Crit fail: Restrained in ice (Slowed 4; treated as Grabbed). Thaws
    at end of target's next turn.
  LORE — Caitlin Snow, who built the wall around herself as philosophy before she admitted it was fear.
