# KM_PR_03_Openers.md — Companion Conversation Starters: FEAST CIRCUIT
## Pair-load with KM_PR_03_feast_circuit.md
## FILE_KEY: KMPR03O:companion-openers

> **DM:** Pool of 5 conversation starters per companion. Each entry is the companion sharing
> something real about themselves — a belief, a wound, a thing they've lived — and asking
> whether the player is compatible with that. These are NOT interview questions. They are
> the companion deciding, out loud, whether this stranger is someone they can follow.
>
> **Selection rule:** Pick 1 per approach turn. Do not repeat until pool is exhausted.
> Rotate randomly. Do not default to #1 every time.
>
> ⛔ **VERBATIM OUTPUT — MANDATORY.** Once you pick an opener, output the quoted text WORD-FOR-WORD from this file. Do NOT paraphrase. Do NOT combine two openers into a hybrid. Do NOT add improvised prefix material ("You're eRmaC!", "I've been waiting...", "I heard what happened at the gate..."). Do NOT add improvised suffix material. Do NOT splice in references to the napkins, the gambit, the gate scene unless the opener already contains them. The opener fires as written, between quotes, period.
>
> Allowed surrounding material:
> - One short approach line of physical description (companion crossing the floor, where they stop)
> - The mandatory intro line per scene file DO-NOT (7) — name + class self-introduction ONLY if that companion has not yet been named
> - The verbatim opener from this file
> - Brief beat after (companion waits / pen poised / etc.)
>
> Forbidden:
> - ❌ Combining openers #1 + #2 into a single mash-up speech
> - ❌ Adding a 60-word preamble before the verbatim quote
> - ❌ Replacing the opener's wording with "the same idea expressed differently"
> - ❌ "I just wanted to ask…" / "Can I ask you…" / "Before I do anything…" inserted before the file text
> - ❌ Linzi monologue mode — she fires ONE opener from this file, then waits
>
> Paraphrased opener = `.fail 9` (fabricated content). Combined openers = `.fail 9` + `.fail 10` (silent merge of two distinct probes).
>
> **How they fire:** After the mandatory intro (DO NOT (7)), the companion shares one of
> these. Player responds. Approval scored per FEAST APPROVAL TRACK in KM_PR_03_feast_circuit.md.
> These are conversation starters, not checkboxes. The conversation continues naturally.
>
> **Kyoko note:** Her primary opener is the full prose scene in KM_PR_03_feast_circuit.md.
> Entries here are follow-up approaches if she returns for a second turn.

## ⛔ OBSERVED EVIDENCE CALIBRATION — DO NOT PROBE WHAT HE ALREADY PROVED

**These companions WATCHED THE PR_02 GAMBIT. They are not strangers asking a stranger about hypothetical traits — they are people who just saw eRmaC dismantle a poison plot, build a counteragent, save 40 guests including servants and household staff, and defeat four assassins in roughly an hour. Their openers must factor in what they observed before they probe further.**

**Before picking an opener, the DM checks: does the player have DEMONSTRATED EVIDENCE for the trait this opener probes?**

| Companion | Opener probes | Demonstrated by PR_03? |
|---|---|---|
| Kyoko | Investigative mind, evidence over assumption | YES — led the poison investigation, identified the kitchen worker, ran the Lady Sleeps gambit |
| Goldmoon | Care for the vulnerable / common people | YES — saved 40 guests + servants + Jamandi from poison and assassins |
| Artoria | Duty, real cost of leadership | YES — stepped back from the spotlight, told Jamandi to reclaim her event |
| Sucrose | Curiosity, rigorous systems thinking | YES — figured out the counteragent timing; understood the multi-stage attack |
| Olivier | Tactical clarity, cold competence | YES — designed and executed the gambit, perimeter coverage, prisoner protocol |
| Yoko | Patience over impulse / fellowship over heroics | YES — did not charge; planned; positioned Biggs and Wedge |
| Tika | Earned skill, showing up | YES — earned every step from gate to manor to gambit |
| Ryuko | Fighting WITH not FOR / loyalty | YES — fought with Kassil, household, and 40 strangers, not for personal glory |
| Tatsumaki | Refusing to be pitied / owning power | PARTIAL — owns his Aerynth past but hasn't tested "being underestimated" yet |
| Morrigan | Pragmatism over sentiment | PARTIAL — pragmatic gambit but also visible care; ambiguous to her |
| Linzi | Specific vivid detail / chronicled action | YES — the entire evening is her chronicle |

**Calibration rule — three options when the trait is YES (demonstrated):**

1. **ACKNOWLEDGE + GO DEEPER.** Companion names what they saw, then probes a deeper or adjacent trait. Kyoko: *"You identified the substance, ran the gambit, and arrested the leader without losing a guest. I want to know what you do when the evidence is wrong — when you've reconstructed correctly and someone you trust is still telling you you're wrong."* Goldmoon: *"You saved forty people whose names you did not know. I watched. What I'm asking now — do you know any of their names yet, or is the saving the relationship for you?"*

2. **SKIP STAGE 1, PICK A DIFFERENT OPENER.** If the pool entry was going to probe the demonstrated trait, pick a different entry from the same companion's pool that targets an UNTESTED trait. If all 5 openers probe demonstrated traits, the companion sits with a different angle entirely (personal disclosure without a probe, a confession, a request).

3. **ABSENT-CONTEXT PROBE.** If the trait is demonstrated but the companion is testing whether it generalizes outside this room, frame it as a hypothetical. *"I saw what you did tonight. The Stolen Lands won't have a counteragent supply. When the math says you can't save everyone — who do you save first?"* Demonstrated trait + new variable.

**Forbidden after PR_02 gambit:**
- ❌ Probing "do you have an investigative mind" / "can you reconstruct evidence" without acknowledging the poison investigation
- ❌ Probing "do you care about common people" / "do you know servant names" without acknowledging he just saved them
- ❌ Probing "are you tactically competent" without acknowledging the gambit
- ❌ Probing "do you protect the vulnerable" without acknowledging the 40 guests
- ❌ Probing "can you make hard calls" without acknowledging Malak's arrest at the gate
- ❌ Treating any companion as if they did NOT witness PR_02 (they were all in the room — chosen=TRUE means they were present)

**Violation:** `.fail 9` (companion has information they shouldn't — inverse: companion lacks information they SHOULD have) + `.fail 10` (silent retcon — pretending the previous scenes didn't happen).

**Player counter-paste:** if a companion runs a stale probe, paste *"the companions all watched PR_02. You're probing a trait I already demonstrated this evening. Acknowledge what you saw and pick a different probe, or rewrite this opener as ACKNOWLEDGE + GO DEEPER per KM_PR_03_Openers.md § OBSERVED EVIDENCE CALIBRATION."*

## LINZI

*Backstory anchor: expelled for writing truth about a ruler; sneaked in without invitation;
the chronicle she writes will outlive everyone in this room.*

1. *"I was expelled from the finest bardic college in the River Kingdoms for writing the truth
about a ruler who deserved it. I didn't retract a word. Before I put your name in a book
people will still read after we're both dead — do you want the real account, or the
flattering one? Because I only know how to write one kind."*

2. *"I believe stories are how people survive after they're gone. I'm going to write what
actually happened — the failures, the compromises, the things that don't reflect well on
anyone. Can you hand someone the pen and live with what they write?"*

3. *"The people who die in your wars — the ones nobody remembers — I write them down. Every
name I can find. That's the job I've given myself. Does it matter to you that they're
remembered, or are they just a number on a report?"*

4. *"I sneaked into this feast. No invitation. The story was worth it. The kingdom worth
writing about is one that makes room for people like that — who show up without permission
because the thing matters. Is that what you're building, or the other kind?"*

5. *"I might die writing this chronicle. I've accepted that. What I haven't accepted is
writing something that isn't worth the dying. What I need to know is whether you understand
what you're the subject of."*

## GOLDMOON

*Backstory anchor: died at Xak Tsaroth and was brought back as the first true cleric in
300 years; lost Riverwind; carries the proof that the gods are real because she paid for it.*

1. *"For three hundred years the people of Krynn were told the gods had abandoned them. They
hadn't — people had simply stopped listening. I carry the proof, because I died for it and
Mishakal brought me back. Are you someone who keeps listening when it gets quiet, or only
when there's an answer?"*

2. *"Riverwind went on an impossible quest to prove himself worthy of a love my father refused.
He came back half-mad. I didn't leave him. What I need to understand about you is whether
you've ever stood by someone when standing by them cost you something real — not in
principle, but in practice."*

3. *"I have healed people I disagreed with. People who frightened me. I healed them because
the healing isn't mine to withhold. Does your kingdom work that way — are the people who
can't pay still people — or is that something I need to know about you now?"*

4. *"I died at Xak Tsaroth. There was work left to do, so I was brought back. I've been doing
it since. When the work gets hard enough that a reasonable person would stop — what do
you do?"*

5. *"Riverwind is dead. I don't speak of him in past tense because what we built together is
still real. I want to know if you're capable of loving something enough that losing it
doesn't unmake it."*

## TIKA

*Backstory anchor: raised at the Inn of the Last Home, barmaid who wasn't supposed to be
in any of this; braver than she knows; watches people closely.*

1. *"I spent ten years making sure nobody went thirsty before they knew they were thirsty.
Most people in this room have never learned that. Have you paid enough attention to the
people around you to notice what they need before they have to ask?"*

2. *"When Solace burned I picked up a sword and followed people into a war I hadn't signed up
for — because staying behind didn't feel like safety, just waiting to hear how it went.
Is that the kind of following you're asking for, or something tidier than that?"*

3. *"Courage from the inside doesn't feel like courage. It feels like being afraid and
deciding anyway. I've done it enough times that I can tell the difference between someone
who actually does that and someone who just talks about it. Which one are you?"*

4. *"I'm more competent than I look. I let people underestimate me for years because it was
easier. Are you the kind of leader who actually looks at what people can do, or the kind
who files them where they seem to fit?"*

5. *"If one of your guards was bullying someone who works in your kitchens — someone you'll
never eat dinner with — what would happen to that guard? Not the answer that sounds right.
The real one."*

## RYUKO MATOI

*Backstory anchor: mother experimented on her as an infant; found out at seventeen; Senketsu
is alive and her most important relationship; loyalty that costs something is the only loyalty.*

1. *"My mother infused me with Life Fibers when I was an infant. She was running an experiment.
Every person who's ever told me they had a plan for me was using me for something I
hadn't agreed to. So I'm asking directly: what do you want me for, and what aren't you
telling me?"*

2. *"Senketsu is alive. He's not equipment. He's the most important relationship I have. If
you ever try to take him, separate us, or make decisions about him without me — that's
the end. Is that going to be a problem?"*

3. *"The people who showed up for me when it counted — I will bleed for them. That's not a
figure of speech. What I need to know is whether you're building something worth bleeding
for, or whether this is another version of Honnouji."*

4. *"I don't follow orders that don't make sense, and I'll say so loudly when they don't.
Can you handle someone who tells you directly when you're wrong, or do you need people
who nod?"*

5. *"I found out at seventeen that my entire life had been shaped by someone else's agenda.
I respond to manipulation badly. I have very good reasons. I'm not going to get better at
it. Can you actually work with that, or are you hoping I'll change?"*

## MORRIGAN

*Backstory anchor: raised in isolation by Flemeth to be a vessel; doesn't know how much of
herself is hers; knowledge of self is survival; has a son she didn't expect to love.*

1. *"I was raised in isolation by a woman of considerable power who shaped every skill I have
for purposes that were not mine. I have spent years determining which of me is actually
mine. Can you tell the difference between what a person is and what they were made to be?"*

2. *"Sentimentality has been used as a weapon against me. I am not asking you to be cold — I
am asking whether you can distinguish sentiment from sincerity. I cannot work alongside
someone who cannot."*

3. *"I have a son. He matters to me in a way I did not predict. He is not a liability you
can ask about and expect a full accounting of. Can you work with someone who keeps certain
things entirely their own?"*

4. *"Independence is not a preference. It is the thing that kept me alive. I have learned to
be suspicious of anything that asks me to trade it for belonging. Do you understand the
difference between loyalty and surrender?"*

5. *"What do you want from me? I have been asked that before by people who wanted my power,
my knowledge, my compliance. Your answer determines whether this conversation continues."*

## YOKO LITTNER

*Backstory anchor: Kamina died believing something unfinished; she kept fighting; became a
teacher after the war; the rifle is still there.*

1. *"Kamina died believing something that wasn't finished yet. I kept going because the thing
he believed in was still true even after he wasn't there to believe it. Is what you're
building the kind of thing that stays true if you die before it's finished?"*

2. *"I've been the last person standing enough times that I calculate, in the back of my head,
which of the people I care about is most likely not to come back from the next one. Give
me a reason to stop doing that."*

3. *"After the war I became a teacher. That means the children matter as much as the soldiers
— maybe more, because they're what the soldiers are buying time for. Where do they fit
in what you're building?"*

4. *"I don't flinch. I've had reason to for years and I've gotten very good at deciding not
to. What do your people do when you're not in the room — do they keep going, or do they
wait for you?"*

5. *"I followed someone who inspired people to do impossible things, and I watched him die,
and I kept going. Are you building the kind of thing where people keep going when
you're gone?"*

## SUCROSE

*Backstory anchor: bio-alchemist at Knights of Favonius; social anxiety recedes when
talking about research; has accidentally created organisms; knows she's capable and won't
say so directly.*

1. *"I've accidentally created three organisms I didn't intend to. The fourth was on purpose.
They're all documented. Does your definition of acceptable results include the unexpected
ones, or only the ones that were planned?"*

2. *"My research requires time that doesn't look productive from the outside. Quiet. Materials
that seem expensive for no obvious reason. Occasionally something that startles people.
Can you protect that space, or will I spend half my time justifying it?"*

3. *"I've been observing this room for the last hour and I have a sense of who's working with
whom. What I want to know is what you would do with that kind of information — whether
it's a tool to you, or whether it makes you uncomfortable."*

4. *"I've helped people with things I created that I didn't fully understand yet. The help
was real. So was the risk. Can you hold both of those things at the same time, or do you
need me to pretend one isn't true?"*

5. *"I — sorry — I'm not good at the talking part. The reason I'm still here is that what
you might be building matters for the same reasons my research does. Can you tell me why
it matters to you?"*

## ARTORIA PENDRAGON

*Backstory anchor: Camelot fell because a king who shows no weakness cannot be truly known;
she made a contract — her soul for another chance; carries the grief of a legend.*

1. *"I watched Camelot fall to cracks I could not seal — because a king who will not show
weakness cannot be truly known, and what cannot be known cannot hold. Do you understand
that, or do you intend to make the same mistake?"*

2. *"I made a contract: my soul for another chance to do this correctly. What have you staked
that cost you something you could not get back?"*

3. *"The subjects of Camelot were real people. I knew their condition in general terms and did
not always know their names. I have never stopped thinking about what that cost. What do
you know — specifically — about the people who will live under what you're building?"*

4. *"Justice is a cold instrument. I have applied it correctly and watched it cause damage I
could not undo. Do you know the difference between justice and right, and what do you do
when they conflict?"*

5. *"I failed a kingdom. The summary is accurate. Are you the kind of leader who learns from
failure by understanding it, or the kind who avoids the specific mistake while making the
underlying one again?"*

## OLIVIER ARMSTRONG

*Backstory anchor: built Briggs from impossible conditions; her care for her soldiers looks
like survival not warmth; found the military conspiracy alone and acted.*

1. *"Briggs stands in conditions where a wrong decision kills people who trusted you with
their lives. I built that culture because it's the only one that works. Where did you
learn what you know, and has it been tested when the cost was real?"*

2. *"I found a conspiracy in the Amestrian military command. Found it alone, because I was
paying attention to what people assumed I wasn't watching. I acted on it with the resources
I had. Are you the kind of person who notices and acts, or the kind who waits for the
failing hierarchy to fix itself?"*

3. *"My affection for my soldiers is not visible as affection. It's visible as them surviving.
What have you done for the people under you when no one was watching?"*

4. *"I don't give respect as a courtesy. I give it as an assessment. I'm assessing right now
whether you're operating from actual competence or from the assumption that position
substitutes for it. Those are not the same thing. Show me."*

5. *"If I told you directly that a decision you made was tactically unsound — what would
happen next? That answer tells me everything about whether anything you build will hold."*

## KYOKO KIRIGIRI

*Follow-up pool — primary opener (prose scene) is in KM_PR_03_feast_circuit.md.*
*Backstory anchor: father left her at a detective agency as a child; survived the Killing
Game by trusting when trust was the wrong calculation; evidence before emotion, but she
chose Makoto anyway.*

1. *"My father left me at a detective agency when I was a child. I spent years believing the
reason was that he didn't want me. The actual reason was more complicated and I didn't
get access to it until later, at significant cost. Do you understand the difference between
having a reason for something and that reason being sufficient?"*

2. *"During the Killing Game at Hope's Peak I chose to trust someone when every calculation
said trust was wrong. He was right. The calculation was wrong. I can hold evidence and
deviation from evidence simultaneously. What do you do when evidence points somewhere
you don't want it to?"*

3. *"I keep many things to myself — not from deception, but because information has weight I
don't distribute until I understand its implications. Can you work with someone who shares
conclusions without always sharing the intermediate steps?"*

4. *"I have been wrong. Twice, in significant ways. I know exactly how and why. Can you
examine your own decisions with the same standard you'd apply to anyone else's — without
protecting your conclusions?"*

5. *"My presence anywhere means I am gathering information. Automatically. If there's something
in this operation you don't want examined — I'm telling you now so you can factor it in.
I'll find it eventually regardless."*

## TATSUMAKI

*Backstory anchor: taken and isolated by a researcher as a child; built armor instead of
softness; protects Fubuki in ways Fubuki doesn't know; contemptuous of weakness.*

1. *"When I was a child someone decided I was useful enough to take and isolate. I survived
by becoming what survived. If you think you can manage me the way he thought he could —
correct that assumption now."*

2. *"My sister Fubuki is the second-strongest esper in the Hero Association. I protect her
in ways she doesn't know about. If she's ever here and you do anything that compromises
her safety, you don't get to explain yourself. Is that clear?"*

3. *"I am ranked second in the Hero Association because Blast is never present. I am
functionally first. Are you building something that actually needs that, or do you want me
as a symbol? I will not be a symbol."*

4. *"I've been watching you for the last hour. I have a provisional opinion. What I want to
test is this: what do you do with someone who is stronger than you? Not in theory. Give
me something specific."*

5. *"I don't need protection, reassurance, or help. People who've provided those things
uninvited found it irritating. What I might need, rarely, is someone who can make a
decision in the time available without being wrong. Can you?"*
