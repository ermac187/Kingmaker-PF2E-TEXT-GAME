## 🎁 GIFT SYSTEM

> **DM:** Gifting is one of the cleanest Romance Score triggers — it is explicit,
> player-initiated, and immediately personal. Two categories exist: **Thoughtful
> Gifts** (low cost, high meaning) and **Practical Gifts** (useful items that
> also signal care). Both give +1 Romance. A gift that hits both categories —
> meaningful AND useful — gives +2. A gift that misses entirely gives 0 and
> costs the player the item.
>
> The player may give a gift at any camp scene or quiet moment. No action slot
> required in exploration mode. Takes 1 action in social scenes.

### What Makes a Gift Land

A gift lands when it connects to something the companion has said, shown, or
implied. The DM evaluates fit — not price. A 1 gp wildflower picked from the
roadside can outweigh a 500 gp enchanted blade if the flower connects to
something real. The companion's reaction signals whether it landed.

```
GIFT RESOLUTION
  Player offers gift → DM checks fit against companion profile
    PERFECT FIT  : +2 Romance. Companion reaction: visible, specific, remembered.
    GOOD FIT     : +1 Romance. Companion accepts warmly.
    NEUTRAL      : 0 Romance. Companion accepts politely. Item transferred.
    MISS         : 0 Romance. Companion declines or accepts awkwardly. Item returned.
    INSULT       : −1 Romance (rare — only if gift implies something offensive about
                   the companion's values, history, or dignity)
```

## 💞 PHYSICAL INTERACTIONS

> **DM:** Player may attempt a physical interaction at any camp or quiet scene.
> Companions never initiate first. Each has a Stage requirement — attempting
> below it results in a gentle step-back, not hostility. No roll for interactions
> at or above requirement unless marked. Check Mood state before resolving.
> **Surface in menu** when a romance-eligible companion is present in a non-combat
> scene and Romance ≥ 1: add **[Reach out]** option. Expand on selection.

### Rejection

Stage 1–2: Never hostile. Companion steps back or finds something to do with their hands. Score drops only if player pushes past a clear step-back (−1). Stage 3+: wrong moment — Mood, timing. Companion says something quiet. Not cruel. True.

## 🌹 ROMANTIC ACTIVITIES — BY STAGE

> **DM:** Optional player-initiated events during downtime, camp, or settlement visits. Not the same as Camp Interludes (which fire randomly). Cost time, sometimes gold. Companion must be present and at the right stage. Player initiates with `.activity` or by describing what they want to do.

---

### Stage 1 — CURIOUS: Early Activities

*Low stakes. Deniable. Neither party has to admit anything.*

**Walk the Perimeter Together**
> Cost: None. Time: 1 hour camp time (replaces one other camping activity).
> The player and companion do the watch circuit together instead of alone.
> No roll. No agenda. Whatever happens, happens.
> Effect: +1 Romance if the player listens more than they speak.
> DM runs 2–3 exchanges. The companion says something they haven't said before.

**Shared Meal (Camp)**
> Cost: Requires a Special Meal recipe (C10). Time: part of camp cooking.
> Player cooks specifically for the companion — or asks them to cook together.
> Effect: +1 Romance on a successful Cook Special Meal check.
> The companion's reaction is calibrated to the food — Valerie eats quietly and
> efficiently; Linzi asks what every ingredient is; Octavia has opinions about
> the spice balance.

**Teach Me Something**
> Cost: None. Time: 1 hour.
> Player asks the companion to teach them one thing they know — a weapon form,
> a language phrase, a song, a prayer, a knot.
> Effect: +1 Romance. The companion gets to be the expert.
> DM runs the lesson as a short scene. Player does not need to succeed at anything.
> The point is that they asked.

---

### Stage 2 — SMITTEN: Closer Activities

*Something is clearly happening. Neither party is pretending otherwise.*

**Stargazing / Night Watch Together**
> Cost: None. Time: replaces standard watch.
> Player specifically takes watch with the companion and lets the night get quiet.
> Effect: +1 Romance. Triggers a Camp Interlude automatically at end of watch.
> DM uses a Stage 2 Interlude template. The transition from comfortable silence
> to something more is handled as written.

**Find Something for Them in the Field**
> Cost: Hunt and Gather (C9) or Search (X8) — player declares intent to find
> something for the companion specifically.
> Effect: On success, player finds something small and fitting (DM selects from
> Thoughtful Gifts table for this companion). Giving it: +1 Romance.
> On failure: they find something anyway. It's wrong. The companion appreciates
> the attempt. 0 Romance, but a Bond Moment is logged regardless.

**Settlement Evening (in town)**
> Cost: 5 gp. Time: 1 downtime evening.
> Player invites the companion to spend an evening in a settlement — tavern,
> market, temple, whatever fits them.
> Effect: +1 Romance. DM runs 3 brief exchanges in the companion's element.
> Valerie in a tavern is very different from Linzi in a tavern is very different
> from Tristian at a temple. The setting is the activity.

---

### Stage 3 — ADMITTED: Intentional Activities

*Both parties know what this is. The activities reflect that.*

**Teach Something Personal**
> The player shares something from their backstory — a skill, a memory, a place
> they're from. Not performance. Actual disclosure.
> Effect: +1 Romance. Companion responds in kind — shares something from their
> profile that hasn't been said before. Bond Moment logged for both.
> DM Note: This is the activity that earns the +2 trigger if both parties reach
> genuine mutual disclosure in the same scene.

**Capital Tour (first time in the capital)**
> Cost: None. Time: 1 afternoon.
> Player shows the companion around the capital they're building together.
> Effect: +1 Romance. Companion comments on three things — what they like, what
> they'd change, and one thing they notice that the player probably missed.
> Valerie: the walls, the gate, and a child sitting alone near the market.
> Linzi: the archive, the tavern, and where the best view is for a chronicle entry.
