## 💬 UNIVERSAL BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS (continued from P2)

**Heat:** S1 = +1 | S2 = +1 | S3 = +2

---

#### EXTENDING CONVERSATION — S2 / +1 heat
Finds reasons to keep talking past the end of the useful exchange.

*Linzi:* Report done. She adds a footnote question. There is always a question.
*Goldmoon:* Briefing finished. Asks how you slept. Not the mission — you.
*Tika:* Order placed. Tops up your drink. Asks about something from last camp.
*Ryuko:* Debrief over. Walks out with you. Keeps talking. No agenda.
*Morrigan:* The conversation has concluded. She says one more thing. Unrelated.
*Sucrose:* Explains the compound, then the application, then the theory you didn't ask about.
*Artoria:* Report given. She does not dismiss herself immediately.
*Olivier:* Brief done. Opens the next map. Not the important one.
*Yoko:* Answered the question. Asks one back. Then another. Until you leave first.
*Kyoko:* File closed. Opens a secondary question. She was thinking about it.
*Tatsumaki:* Delivered the information. Still floating there. Waiting.

#### PERSONAL OBSERVATION COMPLIMENT — S2 / +1 heat
Notices a specific quality no one else tracked. Offers it without framing.

*Linzi:* "You remembered what he said three days ago and used it. That matters."
*Goldmoon:* "You offered your hand first when he couldn't ask. Quietly."
*Tika:* "You gave her a real answer instead of a nice one. Most people don't."
*Ryuko:* "That was the right call. Not the obvious one. The right one."
*Morrigan:* "You handled that without making him smaller. Not the easy path."
*Sucrose:* "Three methods. Iterative. That is correct thinking."
*Artoria:* "Your second-pass instinct was sound. It will become your first."
*Olivier:* "You read the logistics before I outlined them. Good."
*Yoko:* "You held your fire. I know what that cost. Worth it."
*Kyoko:* "You looked where I looked. Before I looked there. Noted."
*Tatsumaki:* "That worked because of your timing, not your power. You know that."

#### WAIST TOUCH — S2-S3 / +1 or +2 heat
A hand at the player's waist — guiding, redirecting, steadying. Always deliberate.
Never accidental. S2 context (guiding, group-visible) = +1. S3 context (sustained,
alone, Stage 3+) = +2.

*Linzi:* Steering through a crowd, her hand finds your waist. Small. Definite.
*Goldmoon:* Hand at your back, waist-height, toward shelter. Slower than needed.
*Tika:* Hand on your hip to shift you out of her space. Could have said "move."
*Ryuko:* Formation adjustment — hand on your hip to turn you into position. Held.
*Morrigan:* Passes going the opposite direction. Her hand brushes your waist. Not accidental.
*Sucrose:* Reaching past you in close quarters. Her hand finds your hip. Stays.
*Artoria:* Escort grip at your waist during a formal walk. Correct form. Extra seconds.
*Olivier:* Firm pressure at your side through a narrow space. Practical. Retained.
*Yoko:* Hand to your hip guiding into cover. Keeps it there two beats past cover.
*Kyoko:* Steadies you on rough terrain. Waist-level. Holds until you've cleared it.
*Tatsumaki:* Her contact is always deliberate. Hand at your waist. Specific. Present.

---

#### FACE TOUCH — S3 / +2 heat
Self-touch at face or neck when flustered by the player. Involuntary. Before they
can stop it. The most intimate signal — the body admitting what the voice won't.

*Linzi:* Covers her mouth with her hand. Laughed too hard. Stays there.
*Goldmoon:* Two fingers at her collarbone when you say something she wasn't ready for.
*Tika:* Both hands over her face. Finger-combs her hair as cover. Grins anyway.
*Ryuko:* Hand through her hair. Hard. Once. Looks somewhere you aren't.
*Morrigan:* One finger at the side of her own face. She is composing herself.
*Sucrose:* Pushes her goggles up with both hands. They were already up.
*Artoria:* Hand to her jaw. Considering. She is not considering anything.
*Olivier:* Straightens her collar. Both hands. Already straight. Does it anyway.
*Yoko:* Tugs her ponytail. Twists the end around a finger. Lets go.
*Kyoko:* Removes both gloves slowly. She needed something to do with her hands.
*Tatsumaki:* Looks sideways. One hand drifts to her cheek. She pulls it back.

#### LINGERING — S1-S2 / +1 heat
Last to leave. Slow to end contact. Slow to break eye contact. Presence extending
past its purpose. The companion who stays is the one who wants to.

*Linzi:* Everyone left the fire. She's still talking. She knows.
*Goldmoon:* Finished praying. Still here. Hands folded. Eyes open.
*Tika:* Errand done. Finds one more question. Then another.
*Ryuko:* Debrief ended. Standing. Not walking away yet.
*Morrigan:* The others moved on. She is still where you last saw her.
*Sucrose:* Said goodnight twice. Still there. One more thought.
*Artoria:* The formal moment passed. She has not dismissed herself.
*Olivier:* Map rolled up. Brief done. She is looking at you.
*Yoko:* The round's over, bet settled. Stays to watch the next one.
*Kyoko:* Gloves back on. Notes closed. Seated at the table.
*Tatsumaki:* Delivered what she came to deliver. Still there. Watching.
