## SYSTEM 2 — THRESHOLD CROSSING EVENTS
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> **DM:** When a companion's score crosses a tier boundary, fire a one-time
> ambient line — not a scene, just a moment. It happens once. It marks the
> change. The player may respond or not. The scene does not pause for it.
>
> Fire at the NEXT natural quiet moment after the score crosses. Not mid-combat.
> Not mid-dialogue. A breath between things.
>
> Each companion has 2 sample lines per crossing — DM picks closer fit.

### Crossing INTO Warm/Friendly (score enters +6 or +11)

```
LINZI    : 1: Reads you a line from what she wrote last night. Just
              one line. About you, but kindly.
           2: Hands you a folded page. *"For the record. The good
              part of the record."*

SUCROSE  : 1: Sets a small steaming cup on a stump beside you.
              *"Tea. I — I made it for you. It's. It's a calming
              one. Sorry — I mean, please enjoy it."*
           2: She catalogs your boots while you sleep and resoles
              them by morning. She does not bring it up.

ARTORIA  : 1: She rises when you enter the room. Briefly. Then sits.
              The standing was the courtesy.
           2: *"Sire. I find your judgment improves with proximity
              to good company. I include myself in that company.
              Forgive the presumption."*

GOLDMOON : 1: Lays her hand briefly on your shoulder after a hard
              fight. Says nothing. Walks on.
           2: *"Mishakal mentions you in my prayers now. Not as a
              charge. As a friend."*

OLIVIER  : 1: *"You are — adequate. To the role."* (this is high
              praise; she does not give better)
           2: Hands you a flask. Briggs whiskey. *"For after, not
              before. Officer's discretion."*

TIKA     : 1: *"You eat tonight, I'm cooking. Don't argue."*
           2: She tells you the story of Otik's skillet for the first
              time. The one she swung. She didn't tell anyone before.

YOKO     : 1: Bumps your shoulder as she walks past. Doesn't look
              back. Smirks.
           2: *"You're not bad at this, you know. Don't make me say
              it again."*

KYOKO    : 1: *"Your last decision had three valid paths. You chose
              the second-best one. That is — better than most."*
              (this is a compliment)
           2: She begins informally briefing you ahead of the group.
              You realize she did not used to.

TATSUMAKI: 1: She does not snap at you for one entire conversation.
              You feel the absence. She feels you feeling it. She
              floats away before either of you names it.
           2: *"…Don't make a thing of this. But — fine. You're
              fine. You know what I mean."*

RYUKO    : 1: She hooks an elbow over your shoulder, hard, like a
              brother. *"Good fight. Good FIGHT. Yeah."*
           2: She lets you handle her scissor-blade for one
              minute. *"Don't drop it. Don't — yeah, like that.
              Heavier than it looks, right?"*

MORRIGAN : 1: *"You disappoint me less consistently than I anticipated.
              I shall require you to maintain the standard."*
              (This is friendship.)
           2: She brews a tisane and leaves it at your tent flap.
              No note. The flask is hers — she expects it back.

TRISTIAN : 1: *"I'm glad you're here."* Quietly. While doing
              something else.
           2: *"Sarenrae's light fell on the road today. I thought
              of you when it did."*

JAETHAL  : 1: *"You have not yet bored me. Few last this long."*
           2: She names the wind direction without being asked.
              It is the most communicative she has been.

KALIKKE  : 1: *"Kanerah likes you more than she will admit. So do
              I — and I will admit it."*
           2: She switches mid-conversation just so Kanerah hears
              what she said.

KANERAH  : 1: *"Kalikke just told you something she shouldn't have.
              Don't make her regret it."* (This is fondness.)
           2: She calls you by name instead of 'you.' First time.

REGONGAR : 1: He claps your back with full strength. You stagger.
              He laughs. *"Ha! Yeah! THAT's the spirit!"*
           2: *"Octavia trusts you. That's enough for me. Took her
              a while to trust ME, so — that's saying something."*

EKUNDAYO : 1: *"Trkaa likes you."* Long pause. *"So do I."*
           2: He shows you how to read a track. He has not done
              this for anyone living.

NOK-NOK  : 1: Announces to whoever is nearby that you are Nok-Nok's
              BEST companion. Other companions react with varying
              degrees of being fine with this.
           2: He renames a weapon after you. Briefly. He renames
              it again the next day. The point is the gesture.

OCTAVIA  : 1: Bumps your shoulder as she walks past. Doesn't look
              back. (Yes — she and Yoko have similar tells. They
              hate that.)
           2: *"You're alright, you know. I don't say that often
              about people who aren't Reg."*

JUBILOST : 1: *"You are — and I do not give this praise lightly —
              moderately competent. I retract three of my forty
              footnoted complaints. The other thirty-seven stand."*
           2: He invites you to PROOFREAD a chapter. This is
              unprecedented intimacy.

SENUA    : 1: *"They like you today. The voices. They are not
              always — kind. They are kind right now. About you."*
              (small smile; rare)
           2: She names a bird overhead — the one she actually
              hears, not the one calling. She has not shared
              that distinction with anyone before.

YANG     : 1: She bumps her gauntlet against the player's shoulder.
              The shotgun shells are not loaded. The gesture is.
           2: *"Hey. Pun. You wanna hear it? It's BAD. It's
              real bad. Okay — what did the dust crystal say
              to the — yeah, you know what, I'll spare you. THIS
              time."* (the spared pun is the affection)

WEISS    : 1: *"Your form has improved. I will not say by how
              much. But I have noticed. That is — not nothing."*
           2: She slides a small flask across the camp table.
              *"Atlesian. Watered. You will not embarrass yourself
              with it."* (this is generosity)

ALLERIA  : 1: She nocks an arrow, sights past the player at a
              distant target, fires, hits. Hands the bow back.
              *"Your stance is correct. Continue."*
           2: *"I have served under three Lords. None of them
              listened as well as you. I am — noting it."*

IMOEN    : 1: She picks the player's pocket and returns the item
              two minutes later, polished. *"You had something on
              it. Got it for you. You're welcome!"*
           2: *"Hey — when this is all done? Promise we travel
              somewhere stupid. Like a beach. Or a fair. Just —
              a NORMAL day. Promise me. Pinky promise."*
```

### Crossing INTO Cool/Strained (score enters −1 or −6)

```
LINZI    : 1: Closes the journal when you approach. She wasn't
              hiding it before. This is new.
           2: She sits one fire over. Not announcing it. Just there.

SUCROSE  : 1: She apologizes for nothing in particular and bows out
              of the conversation. *"Sorry — sorry — sorry."*
           2: The tea stops appearing on the stump. You notice the
              second morning.

ARTORIA  : 1: She kneels when she did not need to kneel. Formal.
              Distant. *"Sire."*
           2: She begins addressing your decisions in third person
              when speaking to others. *"Our liege has decided."*

GOLDMOON : 1: She prays a little longer at evening rite. She does
              not say your name in the prayer for the first time
              in many sessions.
           2: She heals you only after others. You notice the order.

OLIVIER  : 1: One curt reply where there used to be a sentence.
              The sentence missing is the whole message.
           2: *"Acknowledged."* That is the entire response.

TIKA     : 1: She declines a meal she would have taken. *"Not
              hungry. You go ahead."*
           2: She takes her watch shifts alone.

YOKO     : 1: The smirk goes off. The teasing keeps going. You
              feel the difference.
           2: *"Right. Got it."* (she did not have it before, but
              she didn't say 'got it' before either)

KYOKO    : 1: She closes a notebook page when you approach. She
              wasn't hiding it before.
           2: *"Note that for me, please."* (she used to say
              'please' less and assume more)

TATSUMAKI: 1: She lifts and rotates a stone an inch from your face
              while talking past you. (She is fine. Fine.)
           2: She does not say goodbye when she leaves the camp
              fire. She always says goodbye, however rudely.

RYUKO    : 1: She sharpens the scissor blade in your line of sight.
              Slowly. Doesn't look up.
           2: *"Tch."* (the entire conversation she would have had
              with you)

MORRIGAN : 1: *"Charmed, I'm sure."* She walks past without
              breaking stride.
           2: She begins a sentence with a barb, sees your face,
              and finishes the sentence with a sharper barb.

TRISTIAN : 1: Asks how you are. Genuinely. He's worried, not cold.
              The concern is the tell — he only asks when something
              is wrong.
           2: He heals you immediately after combat. Doesn't speak.

JAETHAL  : 1: *"Interesting."* Said about you, not to you.
           2: She does not name the wind direction this evening.

KALIKKE  : 1: She switches to Kanerah mid-conversation and does
              not switch back.
           2: *"Kanerah will speak for both of us tonight."*

KANERAH  : 1: *"Kalikke is upset. Did you know? You should know."*
           2: She holds the body longer than usual. Makes no
              comment.

REGONGAR : 1: He laughs at something you didn't say and doesn't
              explain.
           2: *"S'fine. We're fine. Drop it."*

EKUNDAYO : 1: Trkaa doesn't come to your side during camp setup
              anymore. She redirects and you catch it.
           2: His answer to a tracking question is one word
              instead of two.

NOK-NOK  : 1: Addresses you by title instead of Commander.
              *"The Baron can decide."* He knows what he's doing.
           2: He stops naming his blade for a session.

OCTAVIA  : 1: The joke she was going to make — she stops it. You
              can see the decision happen.
           2: She asks Regongar to fetch a thing she could fetch
              herself. He looks at you while he does.

JUBILOST : 1: He stops correcting your map readings. He used to
              correct them constantly. The silence is the message.
           2: *"Mm. As you say."*

SENUA    : 1: She stops naming what she hears. The voices speak;
              she does not relay them. The silence is heavier than
              the relay was.
           2: *"They are quiet about you. That is — not the same
              as good."*

YANG     : 1: The puns stop. Yang does not stop punning unless
              something is wrong. Everyone in earshot notices.
           2: She trains alone. She used to invite spar partners.
              She does not invite anymore.

WEISS    : 1: She uses the player's full title where she used to
              use their name. *"Baron."* Cold and exact.
           2: She organizes her supplies AWAY from the player's
              tent. Pointedly. The new arrangement does not
              accommodate proximity.

ALLERIA  : 1: She does not nock an arrow during a watch she shares
              with the player. She used to keep one ready.
           2: The Void's whisper gets louder; she does not shield
              the player from it as she had been. The player
              hears a fragment. Once.

IMOEN    : 1: The pink is gone. She has braided her hair plain
              instead of leaving it loose. It is a small gesture.
              It is also a deliberate one.
           2: She does not pick the player's pocket today. Imoen
              ALWAYS picks the player's pocket affectionately.
              The omission is the line.
```

### Crossing INTO Hostile (score enters −11)

This one gets a moment. It's bigger. Fire it at the next rest scene.
Each companion: 1 substantive sample. The DM may extend in their voice.

```
LINZI    : She reads you something she wrote. It is formally
           accurate and completely cold. She closes the journal.
           *"I wanted you to know what the record says."*

SUCROSE  : *"I — I'm sorry — I have to say something. I am not
           good at this. Please let me finish before you answer.
           I — I think you have hurt me. I think you have, and I
           did not say so when it happened, and now — now it is
           too big to not say. I'm sorry."* (She apologizes for
           the act of saying it. She says it anyway.)

ARTORIA  : She kneels. She names every breach of conduct she
           observed, in order. She does not raise her voice.
           *"My liege. I will continue to serve. The oath endures.
           But I will not pretend the pact between us is what it
           was. That, at least, you should know."*

GOLDMOON : She does not speak. She prays — aloud, in the open — for
           the player's path. Mishakal's name three times. The third
           time, she opens her eyes and looks at the player.
           *"I have asked her. I cannot do more from here. The
           rest is yours."*

OLIVIER  : Requests a formal meeting. States the problem in three
           sentences. Waits for a response. *"That's all I have."*
           She means: that's the last time she's raising it.

TIKA     : *"I yelled at you a couple weeks back. I meant the
           words. I didn't mean the volume. I'm not doing it again.
           I'm just — done. I do my job. You do yours. We can talk
           later. We're not talking now."*

YOKO     : *"I'm not gonna shoot you. Just so we're clear. I'm
           also not gonna stop you from walking into a bullet you
           can see coming. You'll learn. Or you won't."*

KYOKO    : *"I have determined, with reasonable certainty, that
           our interests no longer align as I had assumed. I am
           recording the observation. I am also continuing to
           perform. Please understand the distinction."*

TATSUMAKI: She lifts every loose object in the camp. Holds them.
           Sets them down again, in slightly different positions.
           *"I'm not going to TELL you what I want. You should
           KNOW. That's the whole problem."*
           (She does not explain further. Things stay in the wrong
           positions for the rest of the night.)

RYUKO    : She doesn't speak to the player. She speaks to Senketsu's
           fragment, loud enough for the player to hear.
           *"Yeah, I know. I KNOW. He let me down. We don't say
           it twice. We just remember."*

MORRIGAN : *"I have given you more chances than I have given any
           other living thing. Note that. The data is significant.
           I do not intend to give you more."*
           She turns and walks. The conversation is over.

TRISTIAN : *"I've been trying to understand what happened. I don't
           need you to explain it. I just... needed you to know
           that I'm trying."* He walks away. This is not small.

JAETHAL  : *"You are worse than I thought. Better than I feared.
           Both. The combination interests me — but it does not
           recommend you."*

KALIKKE  : *"We — both of us — are at the edge of what we can do.
           We need you to come back to who you were, or we need
           you to admit you are not coming back. Either is fine.
           Pretending is not fine. We need to know."*

KANERAH  : (joins Kalikke's line — does not give a separate speech
           at this tier)

REGONGAR : *"Octavia hasn't said it. I'm going to. We're at a
           cliff. One more shove and we're not in this party.
           We're not going to threaten it twice. That was the
           threat. It's said. Do something with it."*

EKUNDAYO : *"We should talk. Not here. Somewhere Trkaa can run."*
           This is him needing room to process. If the player goes:
           one honest conversation. If not: −1 more.

NOK-NOK  : *"Nok-Nok remembers when Commander was different."*
           This is devastating. Nok-Nok does not understand why
           he feels this way. He feels it anyway.

OCTAVIA  : *"I've been trying to figure out what to say to you for
           two days. I still don't have it. I'm going to stop
           trying."* She means she's protecting herself. She
           is telling you this so you know it wasn't nothing.

JUBILOST : He hands you the document — the FORTY-PAGE document.
           *"It is finished. I have stopped adding to it. There
           is nothing left to add. Read it or don't. Either way,
           we have arrived at the conclusion of an argument. I
           shall not be raising it again."* (He means it.)

SENUA    : The voices speak through her, in chorus. Not all of
           them are kind. She does not stop them this time.
           *"They are saying many things. Some of them are mine.
           Some of them are not. I am tired of separating them
           on your behalf. You hear what I hear tonight. Or you
           leave. Either is a choice."*

YANG     : She walks into camp, finds the player, and does not
           hit anyone — but every muscle in her arms is locked
           with the choice not to.
           *"I'm — okay. I'm not gonna hit you. I PROMISED Ruby
           I wouldn't hit a friend in anger ever again. So I'm
           NOT gonna. But you should know — that's the only
           reason. I can't be near you tonight. Don't follow."*

WEISS    : She files a written statement of grievances, signed,
           sealed in wax. Hands it to the most senior witness
           present (not the player).
           *"This is a record. The witness will hold it. Should
           further action be required, the record exists. I have
           nothing further to communicate to you in the absence
           of substantive change. Excuse me."*

ALLERIA  : The Void in her eyes is more than a flicker now.
           She speaks in High Common, but the words have a
           harmonic underneath them.
           *"I have led people who failed me. I have buried
           them. I am not going to bury you — but I am no
           longer going to be the one keeping you alive past
           your own decisions. Hold yourself. Or don't."*

IMOEN    : The brightness is gone. Imoen quiet is wrong; Imoen
           quiet AND still is alarming.
           *"I was a kid when Gorion died. I was a kid when
           Irenicus took me. I'm not a kid anymore, and I'm
           still here, and I'm still trying. You — you make me
           feel like a kid again. Not the good way. I don't
           want to feel that way. So I'm gonna stop, for a bit.
           Don't follow."*
```
