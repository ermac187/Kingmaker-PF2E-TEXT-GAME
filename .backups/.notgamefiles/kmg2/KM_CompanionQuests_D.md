## 51. LANN — THE SURFACE DEBT

**Trigger:** Relationship ≥ Friendly + Ch2
**Location:** Capital → underground passage

---

### SCENE: THE MESSAGE

A message arrives from below — carried by a mongrelfolk runner who found a way up. It's for Lann. He reads it twice and doesn't put it down for a while.

**Lann:** *"Someone I owe a debt to is in trouble. Underground. They helped me get out. I've been meaning to go back."*

He looks at the player.

**Lann:** *"I've been meaning to go back for two years."*

### Player Choice Menu:
1. *"Let's go."*
2. *"Tell me about them."*
3. *"What kind of trouble?"*
4. *"Is this something you need to do alone?"*
5. [Custom]

**Option 4 — Best:** *"No."* Firm. *"I've been alone about it long enough."* **+1 Relationship. Quest advances.**

---

### RESOLUTION SCENE

They go down. The ally (an older mongrelfolk named Herath) is alive but in a bad situation — pressured by a surface faction that found the passage. Resolution involves either driving them out or negotiating terms.

Afterward, Lann and Herath speak privately. Brief.

On the way back up:

**Lann:** *"She told me to stop coming back to check on her."* He's almost smiling. *"She said I turned out fine and that's enough."*

**Flag:** `lann_quest = complete`
**Reward:** Lann gains *Surface-Forged* — his movement ignores difficult terrain in underground or cave environments. Herath's network provides occasional intel (DM plants one relevant rumor per chapter).

## 80. EMBER — THE THING SHE SAW

**Trigger:** Relationship ≥ Friendly + Ch3
**Location:** Camp — early morning

---

### SCENE: BEFORE DAWN

The player finds Ember awake before everyone else. She's been awake for hours.

**Ember:** *"I had a vision."* She's calm. This is just true. *"Not the kind that's guidance. The kind that's a warning. About you."*

She looks at the player with complete steadiness.

**Ember:** *"I want to tell you. But I need you to understand first that I can't always tell what a vision means. Just what it shows."*

### Player Choice Menu:
1. *"Tell me."*
2. *"Are you alright?"*
3. *"What did you see?"*
4. *"How long have you been sitting with this?"*
5. [Custom]

**Option 2 — Best:** She blinks. Most people don't ask that. *"Yes. It frightened me. I've been deciding how to say it."* She straightens. *"I'm ready now."* **+1 Relationship. Quest advances.**

---

### RESOLUTION SCENE

She tells the vision — the DM generates it from actual campaign events (something plausible and personal, not a plot spoiler). The warning is real. The player can act on it or not.

The quest completes regardless. It was never about the vision.

**Ember:** *"I tell you because you should know. Not because I think you can't handle it."*

She pauses.

**Ember:** *"That's new for me. Telling instead of carrying."*

**Flag:** `ember_quest = complete`
**Reward:** Ember gains *Spoken Warning* — once per chapter, before a scene begins, she may describe one thing that feels wrong (DM provides one hint about the upcoming encounter or social situation).

## 55. DAERAN — THE THING HE ACTUALLY CARES ABOUT

**Trigger:** Relationship ≥ Friendly + Ch3
**Location:** Capital — his quarters

---

### SCENE: THE ADMISSION

The player catches Daeran doing something he'd prefer they hadn't: sitting with a child from the capital settlement, reading to them. The child is ill. Daeran is clearly not bored.

When he notices the player, he closes the book.

**Daeran:** *"This is not what it looks like."*

### Player Choice Menu:
1. *"What does it look like?"*
2. [Say nothing — lean against the doorway]
3. *"Who is she?"*
4. *"I've never seen you look like that."*
5. [Custom]

**Option 2 — Best:** He realizes the silence is worse. *"She's the daughter of one of the kitchen staff. She has a fever. I have medical knowledge. This is purely practical."* He sounds exactly like someone for whom it is not purely practical. **+1 Relationship. Quest advances.**

---

### RESOLUTION SCENE

The girl recovers. Daeran doesn't make a thing of it. He goes back to being languid and performatively disinterested. Almost.

**Daeran:** *(if the player asks)* *"She's fine. The fever broke."* A pause that is slightly too long. *"I checked."*

**Flag:** `daeran_quest = complete`
**Reward:** Daeran gains *Genuine Investment* — his Life Mystery oracle spells deal +2 healing to targets he has treated before. The affectation costs him nothing and he knows it.

## 85. NENIO — THE HYPOTHESIS THAT WAS WRONG

**Trigger:** Relationship ≥ Friendly + Ch3
**Location:** Capital archive or library

---

### SCENE: THE ERROR

Nenio presents the player with a document. It is, she explains, a hypothesis she published four years ago. She has just found evidence it is incorrect.

**Nenio:** *"I need to file a correction."* She says this the way other people would say they need to confess a crime. *"The original paper had three hundred and forty-two readers. They all believed incorrect information because of me."*

She is genuinely distressed. This is, for Nenio, a catastrophe.

### Player Choice Menu:
1. *"How do you want to handle it?"*
2. *"Errors are part of research."*
3. *"Three hundred and forty-two is very specific."*
4. *"What do you need from me?"*
5. [Custom]

**Option 3 — Best:** *"I have the distribution records. I track readership."* She blinks at the player's expression. *"Is that unusual?"* **+1 Relationship. Quest advances.**

---

### RESOLUTION SCENE

They draft the correction together — Nenio writes it, the player helps with the parts that require admission of human (kitsune) error in accessible language. She is resistant to every sentence that isn't purely factual.

**Nenio:** *"I cannot write 'I was wrong.' That is not academically rigorous."*

**Player:** *[any wording]*

**Nenio:** *"...that is acceptable."*

She sends it.

**Nenio:** *"It is done."* Long pause. *"I feel worse and also better."*

**Flag:** `nenio_quest = complete`
**Reward:** Nenio gains *Revised Model* — her Recall Knowledge checks, when used on a creature she previously failed on, gain +4 (updated data). She keeps notes on everything.

## 23. REGILL — THE REGULATION HE WROTE

**Trigger:** Relationship ≥ Friendly + Ch3
**Location:** Capital — throne room or council chamber

---

### SCENE: THE PROPOSAL

Regill presents the player with a document. It is a proposed kingdom regulation — six pages, formatted with extraordinary precision, covering public order procedures in newly settled areas.

**Regill:** *"This is correct. I require your signature."*

He waits.

### Player Choice Menu:
1. *"I'll read it first."*
2. *"Walk me through it."*
3. *"You wrote six pages?"*
4. *"What happens if I change something?"*
5. [Custom]

**Option 4 — Best:** *"You may. I will explain why each change is suboptimal."* He is entirely sincere. **+1 Relationship. Quest advances.**

**Option 1 — Also good:** He approves of this. Waits. Reads the player's expressions as they go through it.

---

### RESOLUTION SCENE

The regulation is implemented (whatever version the player approved). Regill monitors its first enforcement. One situation arises where the regulation is technically followed but produces an unjust outcome.

He comes to the player.

**Regill:** *"There is a gap."* He's already holding a revised draft. *"I wrote it to cover the general case. The specific case requires amendment."*

He adds two sentences to the document.

**Regill:** *"Law is not static. It requires maintenance."*

He seems satisfied by this in a way that is almost warm.

**Flag:** `regill_quest = complete`
**Reward:** +1 Stability (kingdom — the regulation works). Regill gains *Gap-Finder* — once per chapter, he may identify a rule or procedure the enemy is exploiting (DM reveals one enemy tactical behavior before combat).

## 61. ARUESHALAE — THE HONEST MOMENT

**Trigger:** Relationship ≥ Friendly + Ch3
**Location:** Camp — during a quiet evening

---

### SCENE: SHE ASKS SOMETHING REAL

Arueshalae approaches the player not from behind, not at an angle. Directly. She hasn't been sure how to do this.

**Arueshalae:** *"Can I ask you something that isn't about the mission?"*

If the player says yes:

**Arueshalae:** *"How do you know when you've actually changed? Not just behaved differently. Actually changed."*

She's serious. She's been asking herself this for months.

### Player Choice Menu:
1. *"I'm not sure anyone knows."*
2. *"When it stops being an effort."*
3. *"When you stop asking the question."*
4. *"What makes you ask?"*
5. [Custom]

**Option 4 — Best:** *"I did something kind today and then I checked myself — asked if it was genuine or performance. And I couldn't tell."* She looks at the player. *"Is that how it's supposed to feel?"* **+1 Relationship. Quest advances.**

---

### RESOLUTION SCENE

No dramatic confrontation. The quest completes when Arueshalae does something unambiguously, unmistakably good — and doesn't check herself afterward. She just does it.

Later, if the player asks:

**Arueshalae:** *"I noticed I didn't think about it."* A pause. *"I think that might be what you said."*

**Flag:** `arueshalae_quest = complete`
**Reward:** Arueshalae gains *Genuine* — her attacks against evil-aligned creatures gain the Holy trait (her alignment is now backed by something real). Desna takes note.
