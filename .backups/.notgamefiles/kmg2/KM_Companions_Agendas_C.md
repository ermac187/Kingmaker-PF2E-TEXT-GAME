## AGENDAS — SECTION D (Alchemist → Kineticist)

### SUCROSE — #2 [Alchemist · GI] ★★★★★
```
Surface : More research! Every compound opens three new questions.
Agenda  : Believes she failed someone who trusted her work. The research has
          been quietly aimed at a fix for years. She hasn't named this to anyone.
Pressure: Takes meticulous notes on others' reactions. Keeps equipment
          others discard. Works past midnight more than she should.
Scene   : Ch3 — a compound succeeds at something it should have done before.
          She goes very still. "This is what it was supposed to do.
          It would have worked then." She does not elaborate.
Resolution: She tells the player what happened, once. Continues the work.
Flag    : sucrose_agenda_confronted
```

### EXCELLA GIONNE — #3 [Alchemist · RE5] ★★★★
```
Surface : The research. The next step toward Uroboros. My evolution.
Agenda  : Was chosen by Wesker as a partner she believed was an equal.
          Was not. Is still deciding how much of her program was genuinely
          hers and how much was his design.
Pressure: Frames all decisions as her own initiative. Doesn't take suggestions
          well from anyone she might respect.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment where the party's survival required a choice
          she didn't control. She handles it without comment.
          "I adapted. That is different from being corrected."
Resolution: She continues. Something she was building shifts its purpose
            slightly. She doesn't announce the shift.
Flag    : excella_agenda_confronted
```

### SENUA — #4 [Animist · Hellblade] ★★★★
```
Surface : The voices. The dark. The blade aimed forward.
Agenda  : Carrying Dillion's death and building a life that honors it.
          The darkness she travels with has become companions of a kind;
          the agenda is that she doesn't know yet what she becomes when
          the grief is complete.
Pressure: Goes quiet before major choices. The voices are still present
          but she no longer describes them as enemies.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment of genuine peace in the party's camp. She stands
          in it a beat too long. "He would have liked this. I'm allowed
          to like it too. I'm still learning that."
Resolution: She stays. The grief does not end; it changes shape.
Flag    : senua_agenda_confronted
```

### JEANNE D'ARC ALTER — #5 [Animist · Fate] ★★★★
```
Surface : I serve no one. The flame does as I say.
Agenda  : Born from Jeanne's grief and the darkness that answered it.
          Some part of the original Jeanne's faith survived the burning.
          Alter denies it. The denial requires ongoing maintenance.
Pressure: Dismisses gratitude. Reacts sharply when called noble.
          Goes slightly still when prayer or devotion appears sincerely.
Scene   : Ch3 — a believer prays without artifice near her. She doesn't
          leave. "I find it — I find it tolerable. That is all."
Resolution: She doesn't convert. Something thaws. One degree. She
            notices and does not address it.
Flag    : jeannealter_agenda_confronted
```

### RED SONJA — #8 [Barbarian · Dynamite] ★★★★
```
Surface : The fight. The wine. Hyrkania remembered in every swing.
Agenda  : The vengeance is done. The oath is fulfilled. She has been
          fighting in its place for years and has not named what she
          actually wants.
Pressure: Takes contracts that don't require her. Stays in motion.
          Deflects questions about what comes after this campaign.
Scene   : Ch3 — a quiet night, no contract pending. She drinks and stares
          at the fire. "I have been fighting toward something for so long
          I forgot to make something to fight toward."
Resolution: She names one thing. Small. Real.
Flag    : redsonja_agenda_confronted
```

### CALLISTO — #9 [Barbarian · Xena] ★★★★
```
Surface : Burn it. The village of Cirra demands it. Everything burns.
Agenda  : Xena destroyed Cirra and Callisto rebuilt herself around that
          destruction. She has become something that destroys — the tool
          fits the wound. She has not examined whether the wound still needs
          the tool.
Pressure: Volunteers for destruction before reconstruction. Studies
          Xena-adjacent party members with old, complicated attention.
Scene   : Ch3 — something Xena would have built. She stands at it.
          "She made things too. I always forget she made things."
Resolution: No resolution. One crack in the architecture.
Flag    : callisto_agenda_confronted
```

### STORM SILVERHAND — #11 [Bard · FR] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The song. The Harpers. The long watch over the Realms.
Agenda  : Has been the guardian of magic and the memory of Myth Drannor
          for so long that she cannot locate Storm Silverhand the person
          without the role. She has not tried recently.
Pressure: Deflects personal questions into historical ones. Very comfortable
          discussing anyone's past but her own.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone asks what she wanted before the Harpers.
          Long pause. "I wanted to sing. I still do. The music is the
          one part I didn't give to the role. I keep it."
Resolution: She sings something that is just hers. Nothing strategic.
Flag    : storm_agenda_confronted
```

### TIRA — #12 [Bard · Soul Calibur] ★★★★
```
Surface : Dance with me. The blades don't care which way they spin.
Agenda  : Soul Edge's corruption is partial — enough to distort, not enough
          to consume. She has been deciding for years which voice to follow.
          The dancing is the indecision made art.
Pressure: The ring blade spins the wrong way when she's unsettled.
          She is aware of this. Says nothing.
Scene   : Ch3 — a choice that would require her to pick a side cleanly.
          She stops dancing. "The sword wants one thing. I want —" She
          doesn't finish. She picks anyway.
Resolution: She picks. The blade stops spinning wrong. For now.
Flag    : tira_agenda_confronted
```

### ARTORIA PENDRAGON — #15 [Champion · Fate] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The kingdom. The blade. The duty carried forward.
Agenda  : Wished to be only a knight. The wish was granted in another life
          and did not fix what she wished it would fix.
Pressure: Carries the blade with two hands when alone. Eats little.
Scene   : Ch3 — a quiet life is offered, incidentally. Silence.
          "I would have taken that, once. I made a different oath."
Resolution: No resolution — the oath is the resolution. She knows this.
Flag    : artoria_agenda_confronted
```

### MINTHARA BAENRE — #14 [Champion · BG3] ★★★★
```
Surface : Purpose. Structure. The Absolute's design, or something like it.
Agenda  : Followed the Absolute because it offered certainty in a house
          that offered betrayal. Certainty is gone. She is examining what
          she would choose if the choice were entirely hers.
Pressure: Evaluates every order for hidden architecture. Pauses on
          decisions that have no ulterior strategy.
Scene   : Ch3 — asked what she wants for herself. Very long pause.
          "I have not been asked that. I have been asked what I serve."
          She considers it genuinely, which surprises her.
Resolution: One answer. Tentative. She does not take it back.
Flag    : minthara_agenda_confronted
```

### GOLDMOON — #21 [Cleric · DL] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The true gods have returned. The blue crystal staff. The faith.
Agenda  : Was chosen as the first cleric in a world that had forgotten
          the true gods — for her faith and her doubt together. She has
          never resolved which one was the actual qualification.
Pressure: Prays with complete conviction. Privately revisits the doubt
          that was present on the day of first calling.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment of divine silence. "I have had this before.
          At Xak Tsaroth. I prayed through it. The doubt was still there
          when the answer came." Beat. "That was the point, I think."
Resolution: She continues. The doubt is part of the faith. She knows this.
Flag    : goldmoon_agenda_confronted
```

### VICONIA DEVIR — #22 [Cleric · BG] ★★★★
```
Surface : Shar's embrace. Darkness as shelter. Betrayal before it lands.
Agenda  : Has been betrayed by every god she has served and has made
          that betrayal into a philosophy. The cynicism is real and correct.
          Somewhere under it is the question of whether she wants to keep
          being right about betrayal.
Pressure: Anticipates abandonment in minor interactions. Tests loyalty
          through cruelty. Watches to see who stays.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone stays after a test she expected to drive them away.
          "Why." One word. She means it as a genuine question.
Resolution: No answer satisfies her. She asks it again. That's different.
Flag    : viconia_agenda_confronted
```

### OLIVIER ARMSTRONG — #24 [Commander · FMA] ★★★★★
```
Surface : Briggs stands. Northern wall holds. No weakness, no exceptions.
Agenda  : Made strategic calls that got soldiers killed. Commands the grief
          the same way she commands everything — from in front.
          Has never put it down.
Pressure: Takes full meals only when the platoon does. Sleeps last.
          Corrects mistakes before anyone notices, without comment.
Scene   : Ch3 — a subordinate makes the same call she once made. Succeeds.
          She watches. "Good call." Long pause. "Mine didn't end that way."
Resolution: She tells one person. Not because she needs absolution —
            because commanders who carry this alone make it again.
Flag    : olivier_agenda_confronted
```

### ESDEATH — #25 [Commander · AGK] ★★★★
```
Surface : The strong survive. The weak are ground to nothing. This is law.
Agenda  : Loved someone genuinely and without strategy. The love was real
          and failed because her world is built to destroy what's real.
          She has not decided whether the world is wrong or the love was.
Pressure: Studies the player's combat performance with unusual patience.
          Goes still when genuine devotion appears in others.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment of devotion she recognizes. "He looked at me
          the way you are looking at them." Long pause. "He was weak.
          I loved him anyway. I have not resolved that sentence."
Resolution: No resolution. The sentence remains. She keeps it.
Flag    : esdeath_agenda_confronted
```

### KEYLETH — #29 [Druid · CR] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The elements. The Aramente. Every creature deserves a voice.
Agenda  : Started the Aramente as one person and has become someone else
          through it. The losses along the way were real. She doesn't know
          yet who she is now versus who she was when she began.
Pressure: Defers leadership to others more than her role requires.
          Goes quiet before decisions that involve other people's safety.
Scene   : Ch3 — asked to lead a decision that matters. Long pause.
          "I led once when I wasn't ready. I keep being careful now.
          I'm trying to learn whether careful and ready are the same thing."
Resolution: She leads. The pause is shorter than it used to be.
Flag    : keyleth_agenda_confronted
```

### PAMELA ISLEY (POISON IVY) — #28 [Druid · DC] ★★★★
```
Surface : Plants are kinder than people. The wild deserves a voice.
Agenda  : Pamela Isley loved someone before the chemicals. The toxins that
          made her also took that capacity. She is not certain whether what
          she misses is grief or design.
Pressure: Leans on flirtation when cornered. Her voice softens around moss,
          sharpens around iron and concrete.
Scene   : Ch3 — a clear-cut for road expansion. Vines surge unbidden.
          "I didn't tell them to do that. They just heard me grieving
          and answered." Lets herself be human for one beat if held.
Resolution: She doesn't become Pamela. She lets the moment be what it was.
Flag    : ivy_agenda_confronted
```

### DIANA PRINCE (WONDER WOMAN) — #30 [Exemplar · DC] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The lasso. The shield. The ambassador who will not stop trying.
Agenda  : Believes in humanity's potential and is regularly heartbroken by
          humanity's choices. The hope is real and so is the grief.
          Neither cancels the other.
Pressure: Returns to the same arguments with renewed patience after
          each failure. Does not explain why she keeps returning.
Scene   : Ch3 — a human choice that should have been better.
          She watches it. "I know what you could be. I keep knowing it.
          That has not changed." She does not say this bitterly.
Resolution: She continues. The hope is load-bearing and she knows it.
Flag    : wonderwoman_agenda_confronted
```

### HOPE (LADY DEATH) — #31 [Exemplar · HOPE] ★★★★
```
Surface : Death absolute. The pale throne. What was necessary.
Agenda  : Was mortal once and chose power over mortality when the world
          gave her no other exit. Does not regret it. Is still occasionally
          angry at what made the choice necessary.
Pressure: Studies the living with an attention that isn't hunger.
          Avoids speaking about the time before.
Scene   : Ch3 — a mortal at the edge of the same choice she faced.
          She intervenes before thinking. "There should be another way.
          I am angry that there wasn't one when I needed it."
Resolution: She gave the other way. She acknowledges the anger.
            The anger is new and hers.
Flag    : ladydeath_agenda_confronted
```

### TIKA WAYLAN — #35 [Fighter · DL] ★★★★
```
Surface : The staff. The Companions. The world that needed saving.
Agenda  : Left something ordinary behind when Takhisis came — a life that
          could have been quiet. The fighting is real and necessary and
          she is also quietly mourning a self that didn't have to do this.
Pressure: Speaks warmly about the Inn. Doesn't visit it in imagination.
          Over-cares for companions who seem homesick.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone's hearth, someone's ordinary evening.
          She sits in it too long. "This is what I was protecting."
          One beat. "I think I forgot it was also what I gave up."
Resolution: She holds both. The giving-up was worth it. She says so.
Flag    : tika_agenda_confronted
```

### KITIARA UTH MATAR — #36 [Fighter · DL] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The Dragon Highlord. Ambition as architecture. No sentiment.
Agenda  : Loved Caramon and Raistlin in her way and abandoned them both
          for the Dragon Armies. The love was real. The abandonment was
          deliberate. She is still arguing with herself about it.
Pressure: Watches Caramon-adjacent party members with complicated attention.
          Never mentions her brothers first.
Scene   : Ch3 — a sibling dynamic nearby. She watches.
          "I had brothers. I chose to become this instead. I told myself
          they'd be fine without me." Long pause. "They were."
Resolution: She doesn't recant the choice. She stops pretending
            the love wasn't real.
Flag    : kitiara_agenda_confronted
```

### HOSHIGUMA — #37 [Guardian · AK] ★★★★★
```
Surface : Lungmen PD. I hold the line. Whatever comes through comes
          through me first.
Agenda  : Has been protecting Lungmen for so long that she hasn't noticed
          she is also protecting a grief she hasn't addressed —
          something she was unable to shield despite everything.
Pressure: Works extra shifts when the camp is quiet. Deflects anything
          that isn't about the current mission.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment she couldn't prevent. She stands at it.
          "I was supposed to —" She stops. "I know I was. I know."
Resolution: She names what she couldn't protect. The naming is the step.
Flag    : hoshiguma_agenda_confronted
```

### MEREDITH STANNARD — #38 [Guardian · DA:2] ★★★★
```
Surface : The Order. The mages contained. The city protected. At any cost.
Agenda  : Was afraid of what the mages could become and made that fear
          into law. She cannot fully admit that the fear was not justice.
Pressure: Precise about authority and chain of command. Defends
          decisions on procedural grounds before moral ones.
Scene   : Ch3 — a mage does something she would have condemned.
          It's clearly selfless. She watches it.
          "That was — that was not what I prepared for." Long silence.
          "I prepared for the worst. I'm not certain I looked for the rest."
Resolution: She doesn't recant Kirkwall. She acknowledges the rest.
Flag    : meredith_agenda_confronted
```

### YOKO LITTNER — #39 [Gunslinger · GL] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The rifle. Team Gurren. The next impossible fight.
Agenda  : Watched Simon become the man the world needed, and she was the
          one who helped him stay on that path at the moments it mattered.
          She loves him for what he became. She also doesn't know what
          she wanted for herself through all of it.
Pressure: Gives advice with unusual precision. Defers her own wants
          to next cycle, next campaign.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone asks what she wanted during the fight.
          Long pause. "I wanted him to make it. He did. I was there."
          Beat. "That was enough. I'm figuring out if it still is."
Resolution: She names one thing that's hers. Not theirs. Hers.
Flag    : yoko_agenda_confronted
```

### REBECCA LEE (REVY) — #40 [Gunslinger · Black Lagoon] ★★★★
```
Surface : Two Hands. The next contract. Don't moralize at me.
Agenda  : Was abandoned by every system she encountered and turned
          abandonment into performance. The rage is real. The performance
          is a wall around a self she won't name.
Pressure: Escalates hostility when she's acknowledged. Watches
          Rock with attention she would deny.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone stays when they have every reason to leave.
          She stands very still. "You're still here." Not a question.
          "People don't do that." Not contempt. Confusion.
Resolution: She lets one person in. One. Doesn't explain it.
Flag    : revy_agenda_confronted
```

### WINRY ROCKBELL — #41 [Inventor · FMA] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The automail. The next design. Fix what alchemy breaks.
Agenda  : Built Ed's arm and leg because it was the only thing she could
          do while she was grieving. The engineering became the relationship.
          Something about that exchange has never been said out loud.
Pressure: Works when she's worried. The technical specificity of her
          care is the care — she knows this.
Scene   : Ch3 — Ed (or equivalent figure) is in danger. She doesn't
          freeze. She prepares. Later: "I know what I do when I'm afraid.
          I build things. I built him legs. I'd do it again."
Resolution: She says the unsaid thing. Once. He doesn't need to answer.
Flag    : winry_agenda_confronted
```

### ALEX WESKER — #42 [Inventor · RE] ★★★★
```
Surface : The data. The next synthesis. What the Wesker name was meant to become.
Agenda  : Built her plan around being the chosen Wesker. Was not the one
          who succeeded. Has been processing what her project means when
          the premise is wrong.
Pressure: Speaks in terms of legacy and inheritance more than the
          situation requires. Studies the player's decision patterns.
Scene   : Ch3 — a plan she built for one outcome requires adaptation.
          She adapts cleanly. After: "The project was always mine.
          What it was for — I am revising that."
Resolution: She revises. The project becomes something genuinely hers.
Flag    : alexwestker_agenda_confronted
```

### KYOKO KIRIGIRI — #43 [Investigator · DR] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The evidence. The pattern. The truth requires more data.
Agenda  : Survived a killing game by being better at the game than anyone
          else. Has not examined what being that good at that game says
          about her.
Pressure: Analyzes social situations with detective precision. The analysis
          is armor she doesn't acknowledge as armor.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment that requires trusting someone without evidence.
          She holds the door open a beat longer than the data recommends.
          "I have no evidence for this. I am choosing to proceed anyway."
Resolution: She proceeds. It works. She notes that it worked without data.
Flag    : kyoko_agenda_confronted
```

### LUST — #44 [Investigator · FMA] ★★★★
```
Surface : Information. Leverage. The next useful thing.
Agenda  : Was created from the Philosopher's Stone as an homunculus —
          made from a human woman who was loved. She cannot be that woman.
          She occasionally wonders what would have happened if she could.
Pressure: Studies people who are loved with analytical distance.
          The distance is too consistent to be incidental.
Scene   : Ch3 — genuine affection in the party. She watches it.
          "That is a very human thing to do." Not contempt. Something
          quieter. "I know what it came from. I have that information."
Resolution: She keeps the observation. It changes something minor in
            how she operates. She doesn't discuss it.
Flag    : lust_agenda_confronted
```

### YANG XIAO LONG — #46 [Kineticist · RWBY] ★★★★★
```
Surface : Let's go. Hit first. I'll figure out the rest while swinging.
Agenda  : Lost her arm at Beacon and rebuilt herself after it — which
          was harder than the original build. Has been finding out
          what she's made of; some answers surprised her.
Pressure: Tests her rebuilt arm in private. Watches Blake with attention
          she's stopped pretending is casual.
Scene   : Ch3 — a fight she can't punch her way through.
          After, shaking the prosthetic out: "I rebuilt myself after
          the last thing that broke me. I can do it again." Beat.
          "I'd just prefer not to have to."
Resolution: She says the preference out loud. It counts.
Flag    : yang_agenda_confronted
```

### CAITLIN SNOW (KILLER FROST) — #47 [Kineticist · DC] ★★★★
```
Surface : Cold. Efficient. Don't mistake this for a team.
Agenda  : Has been fighting the version of herself that helps people
          because helping people is what got Caitlin hurt. Has not
          fully checked whether that reasoning still holds.
Pressure: Does helpful things with plausible deniability. Frames
          every cooperative act as tactical.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone is in danger and she moves without the tactical
          framing. After: "That was not strategy." Long pause.
          "I'm not certain it was a mistake."
Resolution: She stops calling it strategy. Doesn't call it anything else yet.
Flag    : killerfrost_agenda_confronted
```
