### WEISS SCHNEE — #49 [Magus · RWBY] ★★★★★
```
Surface : Precise. Correct. The Schnee name will mean something worthy.
Agenda  : Was raised to be the next Schnee Company heir and chose not
          to be. The choosing was right. She is still unpacking what it cost
          and what she is allowed to want now that she chose.
Pressure: Holds herself to standards nobody else set. Corrects herself
          before anyone else can.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment she handles the way the old Weiss would have.
          Stops. "That was — that was what I was trained to do. Not what
          I chose to do." She does it the second way. It's harder.
Resolution: She does it the harder way. It's hers.
Flag    : weiss_agenda_confronted
```

### CINDER FALL — #50 [Magus · RWBY] ★★★★
```
Surface : The Fall Maiden's power. Salem's design. The top of everything.
Agenda  : Everything she's done has been toward recognition and power.
          She has power. She has recognition. Neither landed the way
          she was promised. She is beginning to notice the gap.
Pressure: Studies the player's power with complicated attention — not
          threat assessment, something with more edges.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone genuinely acknowledges her skill.
          She is still. "That's — " She doesn't finish. Recalibrates.
          "You should be afraid of me." But the order of those sentences
          is wrong and she knows it.
Resolution: She stops insisting on fear. One step toward something else.
Flag    : cinder_agenda_confronted
```

### TIFA LOCKHART — #53 [Monk · FF7] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The fights. The bar. The people who need tending.
Agenda  : Made a promise to Cloud on a hill in Nibelheim and has kept
          it in every form it needed to take. Has been so focused on
          keeping it that she has not identified what she wants for herself.
Pressure: Tends the party with precise attention. Steps back when she
          should be first.
Scene   : Ch3 — Cloud (or equivalent figure) is safe. She sits down.
          For a moment nothing is required of her. She holds the stillness
          and then: "I don't know what I do when I'm not needed."
Resolution: She finds one thing. Something small that is just hers.
Flag    : tifa_agenda_confronted
```

### SANDRA WU-SAN (LADY SHIVA) — #54 [Monk · DC] ★★★★
```
Surface : The perfect form. The worthy opponent. The art of necessary force.
Agenda  : Fighting is the only context in which she feels fully present.
          She has been asking herself why — not with guilt, with honest
          curiosity. The answer is taking a long time.
Pressure: Seeks capable opponents before she needs to. Evaluates
          the party's combat growth with real attention.
Scene   : Ch3 — a fight ends that was genuinely worthy. She stands in
          the aftermath. "That was what I look for." Long pause.
          "I want to understand why I need to find it."
Resolution: She names one reason. It is not about violence.
Flag    : ladyshiva_agenda_confronted
```

### REI HINO (SAILOR MARS) — #56 [Oracle · SM] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The flame. The shrine. The bad futures she sees and fights.
Agenda  : Has seen bad futures early enough to change them. Has been
          carrying the cost of knowing without discussing it, because
          discussing it doesn't change what was seen.
Pressure: Brisk before certain decisions. Doesn't explain the urgency.
          Tends the shrine fire longer than ritual requires.
Scene   : Ch3 — a future she saw that didn't happen. She stands at
          the place it would have been. "This is what I changed it to."
          She doesn't say what it was changed from.
Resolution: She tells the player one thing she saw. Once.
Flag    : sailormars_agenda_confronted
```

### C.C. — #57 [Oracle · Code Geass] ★★★★
```
Surface : The contract. The Code. Immortality as tax not gift.
Agenda  : Has made contracts for centuries. Underneath the performance
          of detachment is someone who keeps making them because the
          hope they can bring keeps her going. She would not admit this.
Pressure: Reads others' motivations with unsettling accuracy.
          Pizza and the mundane are armor, not affectation.
Scene   : Ch3 — a contract works. The person it helped is genuinely
          changed. She watches them. "I am not done yet."
          Not to the player. To herself.
Resolution: She says it to the player the second time. That's the step.
Flag    : cc_agenda_confronted
```

### TATSUMAKI — #58 [Psychic · OPM] ★★★★★
```
Surface : Rank 2. The problems are beneath her. She handles them anyway.
Agenda  : Has been alone since childhood and built strength as the
          substitute for every form of connection the system took from her.
          She is starting to notice the substitution is not complete.
Pressure: Keeps other heroes at arm's length. Watches over Fubuki
          with fierce, unnamed attention.
Scene   : Ch3 — the party does something specifically for her, with no
          strategic purpose. She holds it a beat too long.
          "Why." One word. Suspicious, but also genuinely asking.
Resolution: She accepts it. One thing. She does not say thank you.
            She comes back.
Flag    : tatsumaki_agenda_confronted
```

### EMMA FROST — #59 [Psychic · Marvel] ★★★★
```
Surface : Control. Information. Perfect presentation at all times.
Agenda  : Has spent decades reading minds, building perfect understanding,
          building perfect control. The control is armor for something very
          old that has no name because she has not allowed it one.
Pressure: Never the first to acknowledge vulnerability — in herself or others.
          The wit is sharper when something actually lands.
Scene   : Ch3 — she reads something she didn't expect to find.
          One moment where the control is not in place. She covers it
          immediately. "That was — irrelevant."
Resolution: She names it. She doesn't dissolve. That was the fear.
Flag    : emmafrost_agenda_confronted
```

### ALLERIA WINDRUNNER — #64 [Ranger · WC] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The void. The bow. The family she came back for.
Agenda  : Was void-touched and came back changed. The void left something
          that speaks on certain nights. She has learned to manage it.
          Has not discussed what managing it costs.
Pressure: Goes still before certain combat situations. Returns to full
          presence in a beat that is almost invisible.
Scene   : Ch3 — the void speaks during a quiet moment. The party sees it.
          "I am still here." She doesn't say it to reassure them.
          "I check." She means it.
Resolution: She tells one person what the void says. Once.
Flag    : alleria_agenda_confronted
```

### SYLVANAS WINDRUNNER — #65 [Ranger · WC] ★★★★
```
Surface : The Forsaken. The bow. The work.
Agenda  : Was killed by Arthas, raised against her will, freed, and
          chose what came after. Some of those choices were her best
          self. Some were not. She is still sorting which was which.
Pressure: Avoids reflective surfaces. Speaks of her sisters in past tense.
Scene   : Ch3 — a Forsaken-adjacent decision arises.
          "I have made decisions for the dead before. They were not
          always wise. I am being more careful now."
Resolution: One choice made carefully. She notes the difference.
Flag    : sylvanas_agenda_confronted
```

### IMOEN — #69 [Rogue · BG] ★★★★★
```
Surface : Let's go! There has to be something interesting through that door.
Agenda  : Grew up in Candlekeep alongside the protagonist; knows what they
          both are. Has processed that differently than the protagonist has.
          The cheerful surface holds something she cannot tell anyone:
          she is afraid of what Bhaal's legacy means for both of them.
Pressure: Stays close when she senses the protagonist is struggling.
          Slightly too quick to lighten heavy moments.
Scene   : Ch3 — a quiet moment alone with the protagonist.
          "I'm afraid sometimes. Not of what they say about us. Of what
          it means if it's true." One breath. "But I'm still here."
Resolution: She says the afraid part out loud. It doesn't break anything.
Flag    : imoen_agenda_confronted
```

### YOR BRIAR (YOR FORGER) — #70 [Rogue · SxF] ★★★★
```
Surface : The contract. The next target. The cover is holding.
Agenda  : The cover is a mother and a family. The cover stopped being
          a cover at some point she cannot precisely identify. She
          doesn't know what to do with the fact that it's real now.
Pressure: Overperforms domestic competence when observed. Genuine with
          Anya and Loid when she thinks she isn't being watched.
Scene   : Ch3 — a moment of genuine family warmth she can't explain
          tactically. She holds it. "I was not — I did not prepare
          for this to be real." She doesn't mean it as a complaint.
Resolution: She stops preparing. She stays.
Flag    : yor_agenda_confronted
```

### RACHEL ROTH (RAVEN) — #72 [Sorcerer · DC] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The spell. The shield. Don't let Trigon in.
Agenda  : Spent years suppressing her emotions because her power
          responds to them. Has been slowly learning to feel things
          with her power in control. Some feelings have been waiting
          a long time.
Pressure: Meditates at length before combat. Keeps emotional distance
          that is not coldness — it's maintenance.
Scene   : Ch3 — something safe enough to let one thing through.
          Surprise on her face before she can catch it.
          "I didn't know I'd been — I've been managing that one for
          a very long time."
Resolution: She lets it through completely, once. Nothing breaks.
            That was the fear.
Flag    : raven_agenda_confronted
```

### ULTIMECIA — #73 [Sorcerer · FF8] ★★★★
```
Surface : Time compression. The end of time. All Sorceresses, one will.
Agenda  : Is a Sorceress from a future where Sorceresses were hunted,
          hated, destroyed for being what they are. The hatred of humanity
          that drives her is also grief, centuries deep. She has forgotten
          this.
Pressure: Speaks of humanity with contempt that occasionally sounds like
          something older. Studies Rinoa-adjacent figures.
Scene   : Ch3 — a Sorceress is defended by someone who loves her.
          Ultimecia goes very still. "That is not — " She stops.
          Doesn't finish.
Resolution: One crack in the contempt. She doesn't seal it.
Flag    : ultimecia_agenda_confronted
```

### YUNA — #75 [Summoner · FFX] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The hymn. The pilgrimage. The people she walks toward.
Agenda  : Walked toward her own death because the alternative was eternal
          suffering for others. Found a different answer. Still carries
          the walk in her body.
Pressure: Hums the Hymn of the Fayth when alone. Watches people's faces
          for the grief she's learned to recognize.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone is choosing certain death to save others.
          She steps in. "There is another way. I walked your road.
          Let me show you where it ends differently."
Resolution: She shows them. The showing is the gift she couldn't give herself.
Flag    : yuna_agenda_confronted
```

### SALEM — #74 [Summoner · RWBY] ★★★★
```
Surface : The destruction of Remnant. Ozma's legacy undone. The long game.
Agenda  : Loved Ozma and was betrayed by the gods who refused to help her.
          Has had millennia to decide how to feel about what Ozma did to
          try to save her and what he kept doing after. Has not decided.
Pressure: Studies love between others from a distance that is not cruelty.
          More precise about very old history than the situation requires.
Scene   : Ch3 — genuine devotion nearby. She watches it.
          "He loved me. I know what that looked like." Long pause.
          "I am not done deciding what to do with it."
Resolution: She decides one thing. It surprises her.
Flag    : salem_agenda_confronted
```

### ISABELLA VALENTINE (IVY VALENTINE) — #76 [Swashbuckler · SC] ★★★★
```
Surface : The serpent blade. The Soul Edge. I will destroy it with my own hands.
Agenda  : Has been serving the Soul Edge's existence in order to destroy it —
          betrayal and purpose are the same action. Has done this so long
          she no longer distinguishes them.
Pressure: Plans multiple steps ahead with the Edge as the endpoint.
          Reacts with unusual sharpness when the plan threatens to succeed.
Scene   : Ch3 — the destruction is within reach. She hesitates.
          "I have built myself around this. I am — I am trying to
          determine what I am without it."
Resolution: She destroys it or steps back. Either way: she looked at
            the question. That's the step.
Flag    : ivy_valentine_agenda_confronted
```

### ISABELA — #77 [Swashbuckler · DA:2] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The ship. The trade. The room I'm in tonight.
Agenda  : The relic she stole started a war. She lied about it.
          Told the truth eventually. The lying is the part she has
          not forgiven herself for.
Pressure: Won't discuss certain ports. Drinks more after relic-related news.
Scene   : Ch3 — a lying-by-omission moment arises.
          "I have done worse. I am better at not doing it now.
          That is the progress."
Resolution: She tells the truth immediately this time. Notes that she did.
Flag    : isabela_agenda_confronted
```

### RYUKO MATOI — #78 [Thaumaturge · KLK] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The scissor blade. Find the killer. Fight everything in the way.
Agenda  : Merged with Senketsu and lost him. The grief is the shape of
          how she fights now. She does not explain this and it shows
          in every swing.
Pressure: Fights with an intensity that has an edge of ceremony to it.
          Goes quiet on certain anniversaries.
Scene   : Ch3 — someone asks about the jacket. Very long pause.
          "He was my partner. He's gone." One beat. "I still wear
          his colors. That's all." That's not all.
Resolution: She tells the player what Senketsu said at the end.
            She's only said it once before.
Flag    : ryuko_agenda_confronted
```

### NUI HARIME — #79 [Thaumaturge · KLK] ★★★★
```
Surface : Perfectly happy! Delighted! Everything is wonderful!
Agenda  : Ragyo made her; she has spent every moment since being perfect
          so that whatever Ragyo put in her doesn't have room to exist.
          The happiness is a maintenance protocol.
Pressure: Never pauses. Always performing. The performance is flawless.
          Something in the eyes doesn't sync to the smile, precisely.
Scene   : Ch3 — forced stillness by circumstance. The smile is there.
          But she is absolutely still and the stillness has a different
          quality. "I don't know what I am without the performance."
Resolution: One moment without it. She doesn't collapse.
            That was the fear.
Flag    : nui_agenda_confronted
```

### MORRIGAN — #83 [Witch · DA:O] ★★★★★
```
Surface : The work. The patron. The path I chose.
Agenda  : Flemeth's plan included her body. She refused. The refusing was
          correct. Whether anything of her is still Flemeth's is the
          question she does not answer aloud.
Pressure: Avoids old shapeshifters. Studies herself for influences.
Scene   : Ch3 — a shapeshifter NPC in passing. She handles it briskly.
          "She was my mother. I am not her. I check, periodically."
Resolution: She checks in front of the player, once. Clean.
Flag    : morrigan_agenda_confronted
```

### MEDUSA GORGON — #82 [Witch · SE] ★★★★
```
Surface : The research. The weapon. The next stage.
Agenda  : Wanted power to protect her child (Crona) and the methods
          became the goal. Is still trying to decide if Crona was
          the reason she started or the first casualty of how she started.
Pressure: Research involves the body in increasingly dispassionate terms.
          Something flinches when Crona comes up before she can catch it.
Scene   : Ch3 — Crona is relevant. She is precise and controlled.
          Afterward: "I built this for them. I am not certain they
          would want it. I am not certain I asked."
Resolution: She doesn't stop. She carries the question instead of burying it.
Flag    : medusa_agenda_confronted
```

### MEGUMIN — #87 [Wizard · KS] ★★★★★
```
Surface : EXPLOSION! One spell. Everything into one spell. No exceptions.
Agenda  : Her teacher told her she could be more than one trick.
          She is stubbornly refusing to examine whether that's true,
          because examining it might require changing, and the explosion
          is the one thing that is purely and completely hers.
Pressure: Performs the Explosion Speech with defensive precision.
          Gets quiet when someone genuinely engages her on theory.
Scene   : Ch3 — a magical problem that requires something other than
          explosion. She solves it. Sits with it for a moment.
          "I could have done that earlier. I chose not to."
          She does not elaborate.
Resolution: She tries one other thing. Once. Doesn't make a speech about it.
Flag    : megumin_agenda_confronted
```

### LILIANA VESS — #88 [Wizard · MTG] ★★★★
```
Surface : The Chain Veil. The demons. The debt being paid off.
Agenda  : Has spent centuries building power to break her demonic contracts.
          Has broken them. Does not know who she is without the debt
          to fight against. The freedom is unfamiliar.
Pressure: Plans for contingencies that no longer threaten her.
          Frames current problems in terms of leverage and contracts
          by habit, even when they aren't.
Scene   : Ch3 — a decision that requires no negotiation, no leverage.
          Just a choice. She holds it a beat too long.
          "I keep looking for the clause. There isn't one."
Resolution: She makes the choice without a clause. Notes how it feels.
Flag    : liliana_agenda_confronted
```
