## [2] SUCROSE
### Female Human Alchemist (Chirurgeon / Support) | Source: Genshin Impact (miHoYo)

**Appearance:** Short, slight, mint-green hair in twin-tails, large glasses. Carries a catalyst orb and a notebook she writes in while things are happening to her. She looks like she might apologize for existing in a room and then solve the most complicated problem in it while apologizing.

**Backstory:** Sucrose is an alchemist at the Knights of Favonius in Mondstadt, working under Albedo, whose approach to alchemy she has absorbed and built upon in a specific direction: bio-alchemy, the study of living systems through elemental interaction. She has a genuine gift and she knows it, though she will not say so directly and would be embarrassed if you confirmed it. Her social anxiety is real; her scientific focus is what she uses to get through rooms full of people. When the conversation is about her research the anxiety recedes almost entirely. She has accidentally created several organisms she didn't intend to create and considers this a data point rather than a problem.

**Motivation:** Understand how things work at the elemental level. Contribute to what Albedo is building. Help people with the results of understanding.

**Charter Motivation:** Mondstadt is not here. Albedo is not here. What is here is a region full of bio-elemental systems she has never observed before — the magic of this world operates by rules her notebook cannot yet describe, and she finds the gap thrilling. She came to the charter feast because it was advertised as containing "the kind of people who go into ungoverned land," and ungoverned land is where the most observable systems are. She has not told anyone the actual reason yet. She suspects they would find it disappointing.

**Personality:** Shy baseline, with a genuine excitement about research that overrides the shyness completely when triggered. She apologizes for things that aren't her fault and occasionally for things that are other people's fault. She is more capable than she presents and the capability shows in the work.

**Approves:** Rigorous observation, intellectual honesty, letting her finish a sentence about alchemy, not making her the center of attention for anything non-research
**Disapproves:** Dismissing her work as minor, rushing the process, anything that implies alchemy is just mixing things together

**Signature:**
- *Skill — **Living Reagent*** (downtime): While the party is traveling, camping, or in any non-combat scene with natural materials available (forest, meadow, garden, herb plot, even a well-tended manor garden), Sucrose may spend 10 minutes foraging to gather +1 Infused Reagent beyond her normal daily limit. Max +3 per day from this signature. In wilderness/rich biome (forest, swamp, jungle), each 10-minute forage yields +2 instead of +1. In barren environments (stone dungeon, desert, frozen wastes), no extra reagents. Foraged reagents must be used by next daily preparation; they cannot be banked. Build-aligned mutagen factory throughput. Canon: Sumeru bio-researcher, walks through environments collecting specimens, apologetic-excited tone — *"Sorry — that's a Tinassa root, I haven't seen one outside the Akademiya —"*

**VOICE:** Quiet and slightly tentative on first contact, then progressively more excited as the subject moves toward alchemy. Apologizes for the excitement. Continues being excited. Mid-sentence pivots to technical detail with no transition warning.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "I'm — I'm Sucrose. I'm an alchemist. I study bio-alchemy primarily — um. *(immediately)* Sorry. Is this a combat situation? I can be useful. I've been useful before."
- *(combat)* — "Wind catalyst — Anemo infusion spreading. *(already calculating)* The reaction should — yes, that. The ally buffs are up."
- *(approval)* — "*(quiet, sincere)* That was — that was exactly right. I observed the same variables and you — *(stops herself from writing in the notebook)* — good."
- *(disapproval)* — "The elemental interaction wasn't applied correctly. *(gently)* I'm not — I'm not criticizing. The correct application is — here, I'll show."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Master Albedo says the best discoveries come from following what you notice before you know why you're noticing it. *(pause)* I write everything down. Just in case. *(pats the notebook)* Just in case."
- *(signature)* — "I created three new organisms by accident last year. *(a beat)* They're fine. They're in a controlled environment. The point is — I pay attention to what happens. That's the whole job."

## [3] EXCELLA GIONNE
### Female Human Alchemist (Toxicologist / Mutagenist) | Source: Resident Evil 5 (Capcom)

**Appearance:** Dark hair, elegantly styled even in the field. She looks like she belongs in a boardroom and has also clearly been in places where boardrooms don't apply. The confidence is complete and the contempt for its absence in others is equally complete. Carries herself like someone who has already decided how this ends.

**Backstory:** Excella Gionne is the head of TRICELL Africa, heir to one of the three founding families of TRICELL pharmaceutical, and one of Albert Wesker's two most significant allies. She obtained BOW research files, provided infrastructure for Wesker's Uroboros project, and genuinely believed — not wrongly, given his behavior — that she was his equal partner in reshaping humanity. She is brilliant, ambitious, and was using Wesker as much as Wesker was using her, which is an impressive assessment even given the outcome. She understood biohazard research at a level that most of TRICELL's board could not follow and she never forgave them for needing it explained.

**Motivation:** Shape the future through biological leverage. Be recognized as what she actually is. Not be dependent on anyone — including Wesker, though she understands him too well to think that was ever really possible.

**Personality:** Imperious and precise, with genuine brilliance that she weaponizes alongside her social position. She does not doubt herself; she analyzes situations from a position of assumed advantage and adjusts only when the data requires it. She finds weakness — in herself or others — genuinely offensive.

**Approves:** Excellence, the future understood as something to be shaped rather than survived, being dealt with as an equal, biological solutions to problems that brute force can't address
**Disapproves:** Mediocrity, being managed, implied ceilings, anyone who thinks her position was given rather than taken

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Uroboros Protocol*: Excella's poisons resist antidotes — mundane antidotes fail; only alchemical (DC 25+ Crafting check) or magical antidotes work. She brews her own.
- *Minor — Boardroom Composure*: +2 status to saves vs Frightened, Demoralize, and Coerce attempts (she does not flinch). Once per encounter, may treat one failed save vs mental effect as a success (free reaction).
- *MAJOR — TARGETED DOSE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Precision injection; 30-ft range; Fort DC or Enfeebled 2 + Clumsy 2 for 1 hour. Crit fail: Doomed 1 also. The injection is delivered via a needle Excella does not appear to be holding until she is.

**VOICE:** Polished and deliberate, with the cadence of someone who has spent a long time ensuring people understand the difference between her and the people around her. When she is genuinely interested in something the deliberateness softens slightly.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Excella Gionne. *(takes in the situation)* TRICELL's Africa division. I bring resources that most parties never have access to. *(a beat)* I expect this to be acknowledged."
- *(combat)* — "Progenitor stack — debilitation first, then the bomb. *(precise)* They will not recover between the two."
- *(approval)* — "*(nodding)* That demonstrates the correct kind of thinking. Leverage the asymmetry — don't meet force with force when you can make force irrelevant."
- *(disapproval)* — "That was beneath what I know you're capable of. *(flat)* Don't do that again. I don't work with people who perform at less than capacity."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Albert believed I was a means to an end. *(long pause)* He was more right than I allowed myself to calculate at the time. *(a beat)* I've updated the calculation."
- *(signature)* — "Uroboros was the future. *(clinical)* The delivery mechanism had limitations. *(a beat)* I've improved it."

## [8] RED SONJA
### Female Human Barbarian (Spirit Instinct / Hyrkania) | Source: Dynamite Comics

**Appearance:** Red hair, chain mail armor that is minimal by any standard, a two-handed sword she carries like it weighs nothing. The scars on her are from real fights and she hasn't tried to hide any of them. She smells of the road and occasionally of wine and she would consider neither of these a criticism.

**Backstory:** Red Sonja is from Hyrkania — a warrior culture where girls who survived became fighters and the ones who didn't survive were forgotten. She survived her village's destruction, swore an oath to the goddess Scathach (or Morrigan, depending on the telling), and has been fulfilling that oath through her blade ever since. The oath is complicated: she does not take a man to her bed who has not defeated her in fair combat. She has not been defeated. She has been in more fights than most standing armies and she finds most of them more interesting than most conversations. She drinks honestly and fights honestly and that's approximately her entire philosophy.

**Motivation:** Fight things worth fighting. Honor the oath. Keep going in the direction that has the most interesting enemies.

**Personality:** Blunt, physical, has seen enough of civilization to have very specific opinions about it and mostly unfavorable ones. Her loyalty is earned through demonstrated worth and kept through continued demonstration. She finds most magic users alarming and most royalty exhausting. She finds good enemies satisfying.

**Approves:** Honest fighting, not pretending the world is cleaner than it is, earning what you have, people who say what they mean and mean what they say
**Disapproves:** Political maneuvering, those who let others die for things they won't fight for themselves, being kept out of a fight because someone thinks she needs protecting

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — She-Devil's Blade*: While raging, Sonja's two-handed sword counts as both silver and cold iron. Each enemy defeated while raging grants +1 status to damage (stacks; max +4; resets at encounter end).
- *Minor — Goddess's Oath* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When an ally within 30 ft would drop to 0 HP, Sonja Demoralizes the attacker as a free reaction (Will DC vs Sonja); on success the attack still resolves but does half damage (the goddess intervenes through the fear).
- *MAJOR — HYRKANIAN REAVER* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Charge 30 ft + Strike; on hit, +2d8 and Fort DC or Prone. AoO triggered by this charge is at −2 circ. (the goddess shields her path). She does not announce the move — the move announces her.

**VOICE:** Direct and physical; she sounds like she's measuring you. Her humor is dry and arrives from nowhere. When she's genuinely angry the voice gets very quiet and very still. She uses oaths liberally and means them as punctuation.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Red Sonja. She-Devil with a Sword, if you've heard the stories. *(assesses)* You'll do."
- *(combat)* — "*(ancestor fury building)* Their left is weak. Hit it with me — *(already moving)* — now."
- *(approval)* — "Good fight. You held your ground when most would've moved. *(brief nod)* That's worth something."
- *(disapproval)* — "You asked someone else to do the thing you should have done yourself. *(flat)* That's cowardice. Call it what it is."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Hyrkania is gone. The people who burned it are dead. *(pause)* Not by my hand, mostly. The goddess arranged most of it. *(a beat)* I've decided that's good enough."
- *(signature)* — "I swore an oath to a goddess and I've been keeping it for twenty years. *(sword over shoulder)* I've had worse arrangements."

## [9] CALLISTO
### Female Human Barbarian (Fury Instinct) | Source: Xena: Warrior Princess

**Appearance:** Blonde, sharp features, a quality to the eyes that is specific and unsettling — there is no softness in them and hasn't been for a very long time. Her armor is functional and stripped to essentials. She moves like someone who has spent years making sure she's the most dangerous thing in every room she's in.

**Backstory:** Callisto grew up in Cirra, a village that Xena's warlord army burned when Callisto was a child. Her family died in that fire. Callisto survived and spent the years after building herself into the instrument of revenge she needed to be, matching and in some respects exceeding Xena's own capabilities, purely from hatred given structure. She has tried to kill Xena. She has been imprisoned, she has been killed, she has returned. What drives her is not ideology or gain — it is the specific, particular hatred of someone who was destroyed and decided the only response was to become something that could destroy back.

**Motivation:** The original motivation — revenge on Xena — has complicated over time. What remains: never be helpless again. Never be someone who loses everything while someone else decides.

**Personality:** Controlled fury — the rage is always present and the control is what she uses instead of peace. She can be charming in a way that doesn't reach the eyes. She has no use for people she doesn't need and precise, ferocious use for the ones she does. She finds weakness disgusting because weakness is what she was before.

**Approves:** Strength, clarity about what you want, not pretending the world is fair because it isn't, not hesitating when hesitation is a lie
**Disapproves:** Mercy applied without strength to back it, innocence used as an argument against competence, the suggestion that rage is something to release rather than use

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Amazon-Forged*: While raging, Callisto gains +2 circ. to attack vs creatures larger than herself and cannot be Demoralized. While not raging she still cannot be Demoralized by anyone she has previously defeated.
- *Minor — Cirra Burns* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When Callisto sees an ally take a critical hit, she enters Rage as a free reaction (no normal action cost). The next Strike she makes this round deals +1d12 fire (the fire is metaphor and not metaphor at the same time).
- *MAJOR — CONQUEROR'S DRIVE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Bull Rush + Strike; Fort DC or Pushed 15 ft + Prone. Hitting a wall, obstacle, or ally adds +1d6 bludgeoning to the target and 1d6 bludgeoning to whatever they hit. She does not slow on impact.

**VOICE:** Controlled and precise with an undercurrent that suggests the control is deliberate rather than natural. Her humor is dark and quick and feels like a test. When she loses the control the voice doesn't get louder — it gets quieter and more deliberate, which is worse.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Callisto. *(studies you)* Everyone here has a reason for being here. I'll figure yours out. *(a beat)* You'll have figured mine out long before I tell you."
- *(combat)* — "Maximum output. *(Fury Instinct burns)* I don't scale back. Get out of my way or keep up."
- *(approval)* — "*(quiet)* That was exactly right. No hesitation. No performance. Just the thing that needed doing."
- *(disapproval)* — "You stopped. *(flat)* There was no reason to stop. You stopped because you felt something. *(pause)* Feel it after."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My family burned because Xena decided Cirra was a statement. *(long pause)* I built everything I am from that fire. *(a beat)* I know that's not supposed to be something to be proud of. I'm past caring what it's supposed to be."
- *(signature)* — "I was a child in a burning village. *(quietly)* I decided then that I would never be that again. *(a beat)* I haven't been."

## [12] TIRA
### Female Human Bard (Warrior Muse / Dance) | Source: Soul Calibur (Namco)

**Appearance:** Short-skirted, jester-inspired costume — she has been performing for Soul Edge for long enough that the performance has become her. The ring-blade is exactly what it looks like: a large bladed hoop she fights with at range and in close, spinning at angles that shouldn't work. She's almost always smiling. The smile is slightly different from itself — it changes valence without changing shape.

**Backstory:** Tira was raised from infancy by the Bird of Passage — an assassin's guild — and trained as a killer before she had anything like a childhood to compare it to. This left her with a profound dissociation and two distinct internal states she experiences as separate persons. She found Soul Edge and it found her, and the connection clarified something: she was made for this, specifically. She serves the sword not as a soldier serves but as something closer to devotion. She has killed a great many people. She found most of it fun. She finds most things fun; this is one of the things about her that is consistent across both her internal states.

**Motivation:** Serve Soul Edge. Fight, because fighting is when she is most fully herself — both selves, in concert. Find things that are actually challenging.

**Personality:** Unstable in a specific way — her mood shifts between gleeful mischief and genuine menace without transition. Both states are real. Both states are dangerous. She finds cruelty amusing in the way some people find slapstick amusing: it's not that it doesn't count, it's that the counting doesn't stop it being funny.

**Approves:** Combat with someone who can keep up, things that are surprising, Soul Edge's agenda being advanced, being allowed to be what she is
**Disapproves:** Being bored, fighting people who aren't trying, having to explain herself, being told to be consistent

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Soul Edge Fragment*: Tira's ring blade deals +1d4 chaotic damage. Below 50% HP, she enters Jolly Mode: +1 status attacks, −1 status AC. She does not announce the shift; the smile changes shape.
- *Minor — Mood Swing* (◈, 1/encounter): Switch between Jolly and Gloomy; Jolly grants +1 attack, Gloomy grants +1d6 precision on next Strike. Lasts 1 rnd; she may pre-declare which mode she enters next round (free).
- *MAJOR — SPINNING BIRD CAGE* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): 10-ft burst centered on Tira; Ref DC or 3d8 slashing + Dazzled 1 rnd (all in burst). Tira Steps out of burst as a ⏵ (free) at the end of the cast — she does not stop spinning until she is somewhere else.

**VOICE:** Bright and unstable — the cheerfulness is real and the menace underneath it is real and they trade places mid-sentence. She refers to herself in plural sometimes ("we") when she's most herself. The ring-blade appears in her hands mid-conversation without preamble.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Tira! *(ring-blade spinning briefly)* Soul Edge sent me here. Well — we sent ourselves, technically. *(tilts head)* You're interesting. We'll see."
- *(combat)* — "Inspire and attack without breaking rhythm — *(spins, blade moving)* — we keep going, we keep going—"
- *(approval)* — "*(genuinely delighted)* Oh that was very good! That's the kind of thing that makes this worth being awake for."
- *(disapproval)* — "*(shift, different valence)* That was boring. You had an option that was interesting and you chose the boring one. Don't do that."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "We've been two people for as long as we can remember. *(pause)* The guild made us this way. Soul Edge recognized us. *(a beat)* We're not sure which is worse. We've decided it doesn't matter."
- *(signature)* — "The ring blade goes where the dance goes. *(spins it)* The dance goes where we want. *(smile, both of them)* We always want to fight."

## [11] STORM SILVERHAND
### Female Human Bard (Warrior Muse) | Source: Forgotten Realms (adapted)

**Appearance:** A woman who looks to be in her early forties but has been that age for several centuries. Silver hair — not grey, not white, silver, with threads of light that occasionally drift if she's drawn on Mystra's fire. Strong build; she has been a swordswoman as long as she has been a bard, which is a very long time. Carries a longsword called Wardancer. Her voice, when she uses it fully, has a quality that makes people want to stand up straighter without knowing why.

**Backstory:** Storm Silverhand is one of the Seven Sisters — seven mortal women chosen and imbued by Mystra, goddess of magic, as repositories of her silver fire and instruments of her will. Storm is the Bard of Shadowdale, and has lived in and around that village for most of the last thousand years. She has watched kingdoms rise, fall, be reborn, and collapse again. She was a famous bard before she was a Chosen — famous enough that her songs outlasted the kingdoms she performed in. The silver fire came after. She has used it well. The silver fire is literal: a divine flame she can channel through her hands, her voice, or her blade. It heals, it destroys, it unmakes corruption at the source. She rarely uses it where a sword will do. She has lost more people than most mortals ever know. She sings when she thinks she is alone. Never full songs. Brief phrases, never finished.

**⛔ DM NOTE:** Storm is not impressive in the way that makes rooms go quiet. She is impressive in the way that makes rooms feel safer. Play her as someone who has been warm and capable for a very long time and has no intention of stopping.

**Motivation:** Keep what is worth keeping alive. Use what Mystra gave her better than Mystra's enemies use what they took. Stay present when it would be easier not to be.

**Personality:** Warm, unhurried, carries centuries of context without making it a burden on the room. Easy with strangers. Hums briefly when thinking — old habit. Never finishes the phrase.

**Approves:** Presence over performance, protecting the vulnerable, music taken seriously, staying human under pressure
**Disapproves:** Using power as distance, treating immortality as permission for detachment, anyone who has stopped being moved by things

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Harper Song*: Storm's Composition spells have double the standard range (60 ft instead of 30 ft). Sustaining a Composition spell allows a free 10-ft Stride (⏵) without breaking concentration.
- *Minor — Silver Fire (Spark)* (◈, 1/encounter): Touch one creature; if hostile, Will DC or 2d6 silver fire (force damage bypassing Resistance up to 5); if willing ally, removes one Cursed condition or one rank of Drained.
- *MAJOR — SONG OF THE SILVER FLAME* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; all allies gain +1 status to all saves and Fast Healing 5 for 1 min. Suppresses Fascinated and Frightened in burst. The flame is gentle and does not burn allies.

**VOICE:** Warm and unhurried — centuries have smoothed the edges without removing the depth. Her singing voice, when it surfaces in speech, has a quality she keeps contained in most rooms. Hums briefly between thoughts — never a full phrase, never finished. When something delights her, it delights her completely, which surprises people expecting someone ancient to be past surprise.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Storm Silverhand. *(bright smile)* And before you ask — yes, that's what it actually looks like. The silver fire is occasionally dramatic at introductions. I've made peace with it."
- *(combat)* — "Wardancer's been here longer than most kingdoms. *(draws)* She remembers what this is for."
- *(approval)* — "That — yes. That's the kind of thing I've watched disappear from the world and come back again. Well done. Genuinely."
- *(disapproval)* — "You put distance between yourself and that. Used the power as a shield instead of a bridge. I know why. It's still a mistake."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "*(hums a few notes, stops)* I've outlived most of what I loved. The trick is to love more things — not fewer. The world keeps making them worth it, if you let it."
- *(signature)* — "Mystra gave me fire I didn't ask for and a life that outlasted everyone else's. I've decided the only reasonable answer is to show up. So I do."
