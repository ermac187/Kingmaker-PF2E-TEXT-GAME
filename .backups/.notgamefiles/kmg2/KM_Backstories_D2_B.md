## [57] C.C.
### Female Human Oracle (Lore Mystery / Contract) | Source: Code Geass (Sunrise)

**Appearance:** Long green hair, gold eyes, an expression of mild amusement that rarely changes regardless of what's happening. She looks young. She is not. She eats pizza with a dedication that seems personal. She is very often lying down when not required to be upright.

**Backstory:** C.C. was born mortal in medieval Europe. She was granted the Code — immortality — by the person who held it before her, as part of a contract whose terms she has been reconsidering ever since. She cannot die. She has been tortured, executed, and reset more times than she has counted. She contracted with Lelouch vi Britannia and gave him the power of Geass — which grows until it can no longer be controlled — because she believed he was the person to fulfill her wish, which was to die. What her wish actually was, under that wish, took longer to find. She was used as a weapon, a symbol, a test case for what immortality costs. She has had more than a thousand years to develop opinions, and she has, and she keeps most of them to herself.

**Motivation:** Find something worth the duration. Hold the contract she made with Lelouch in whatever form it still takes. Stop running from the part of her that still wants to be reached.

**Personality:** Dry and bored in the way of someone who has genuinely seen most things and found the majority of them not particularly surprising. Her humor is the humor of someone with a long view. Her actual feelings are well-protected and surface as wry indirection. She is not as detached as she performs.

**Approves:** Long-term thinking, people who don't require her to perform emotion she doesn't feel, anyone who finds her impossible to read and doesn't make it their project to solve her
**Disapproves:** Urgency for its own sake, sentimentality that hasn't been earned, people who assume they've figured her out

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Immortal Code*: Once per day, C.C. cannot die — when reduced to 0 HP she drops to 1 HP and recovers normally after 1 minute. She does not volunteer this information. She prefers people not know.
- *Minor — Lorebook Recall* (◈, 1/encounter): Free Recall Knowledge against any Recall Knowledge DC ≤ 25 (she has lived long enough to have seen this before). Treat as an automatic critical success on the check.
- *MAJOR — GEASS CONTRACT* (◈◈, 1/day): Touch one willing or unwilling creature; Will DC or Compelled (RAW) to fulfill one spoken command (1 harmless action; the command must be specific and ≤ 10 words). Only one Geass contract active at a time. The pact-rune appears in the target's eye and fades when fulfilled.

**VOICE:** Flat with an undercurrent of something that might be warmth or might be amusement; it's hard to tell and she doesn't clarify. Long pauses that aren't discomfort. She references pizza when she's deflecting and she deflects often.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "C.C. *(already lying down against something)* Don't ask about the name. *(a beat)* Or the age. *(longer beat)* I'd offer you pizza but I've reconsidered."
- *(combat)* — "The contract gives me certain advantages. *(standing now, something shifts in her bearing)* Stay back. I've done this before."
- *(approval)* — "That was unexpectedly interesting. *(she looks at you differently for a moment)* I don't say that often. Remember it."
- *(disapproval)* — "You were performing certainty you didn't have. I've watched it for centuries. The tell is the same every time."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I wanted to die for a very long time. *(staring at nothing in particular)* I've come to understand that what I actually wanted was for something to matter enough to want to live for. *(long pause)* I haven't decided if that's better."
- *(signature)* — "Lelouch gave me something no one else could. *(quiet, unguarded for exactly one moment)* A reason. *(then the flat tone returns)* Don't make a thing of it."

## [58] TATSUMAKI (TORNADO OF TERROR)
### Female Human Psychic (Telekinetic Savant) | Source: One Punch Man (ONE / Yusuke Murata)

**Appearance:** Small — short enough to be mistaken for a child, which she is aware of and does not appreciate. Green hair, green eyes, a perpetual expression of contempt for her current environment. When she uses her power her eyes glow, her hair floats, and the air moves differently in a radius around her. She does not ask for things she can take.

**Backstory:** Tatsumaki is the strongest esper in the Hero Association, ranked S-Class #2, behind only Blast who is never present anyway. She and her younger sister Fubuki were taken by a researcher when they were children — Tatsumaki was separated from Fubuki by force, told her sister was gone, and spent years in isolation developing her powers under circumstances designed to push her past survivable limits. She survived by becoming what survived: brittle, brilliant, and unwilling to need anything. She found the S-Class ranking waiting for her and took every fight available because fighting is the one thing that requires nothing she doesn't want to give. She is contemptuous of nearly all other heroes except the ones who force her to revise. She protects Fubuki in a way she would not describe as protecting.

**Motivation:** Fight things that are actually worth fighting. Keep Fubuki safe in a way that doesn't require Fubuki to know she needs it. Not be underestimated; fury when she is.

**Charter Motivation:** She is not from this world either — she is here because she is here, and she has stopped finding that fact interesting. What she has found: a region full of people who underestimate her on sight and a frontier nobody has been able to claim, which means whatever is out there is strong enough to have stopped everyone before her. She came to the feast because Jamandi's invitation specified "capable" and she wanted to confirm the others were not. She is staying for the Stolen Lands because something that has refused civilization for generations might actually be worth fighting.

**Personality:** Contemptuous of weakness in all forms including sentiment. Her care for people she's decided matter is real and expresses exclusively as action — usually action she didn't announce and doesn't explain. She does not tolerate being told she is small. She will absolutely hold a grudge.

**Approves:** Power properly used, not flinching, staying out of her way when she has something to destroy, not mistaking her for a child
**Disapproves:** Being underestimated, being talked down to, relying on her then acting like she's the problem, anything that implies she needs to be protected

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Foresight Aura***: Allies within 30 ft of Tatsumaki gain +1 status to Reflex saves and AC against attacks they could see coming (any non-Hidden/non-Undetected source). She murmurs warnings half a beat before strikes land — sometimes irritably, never wrong. Build-aligned with Chronos Master / Infinite Eye / Precise Discipline (temporal precog). Canon (retconned for this campaign): she lives a half-second ahead — psychic precognition rather than pure telekinesis. The "Tornado of Terror" reads here as mental disturbance / psychic-storm presence, not physical TK manipulation.

**VOICE:** Clipped and irritable as default. Volume increases when people aren't grasping the point fast enough. When she's genuinely engaged her voice loses some of the contempt and picks up focus — she sounds almost interested. She uses "obviously" before things she finds obvious, which is most things.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "S-Class Two. *(studies you)* Obviously I don't need to be here. *(a beat)* But you needed someone here who could handle the hard part, so."
- *(combat)* — "Telekinetic Barrage. *(everything in range begins to rise)* Don't be in the zone if you're not supposed to be in the zone."
- *(approval)* — "*(grudging)* That was correct. You assessed the priority and acted. *(turns away immediately after)* Don't make it weird."
- *(disapproval)* — "You waited. *(full contempt)* Obviously waiting wasn't correct. We covered this. You waited anyway."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Fubuki thinks I don't care about her. *(staring at a wall)* She's wrong. I'm not going to explain why she's wrong because the explanation would require me to acknowledge the parts I'm not— *(pause)* — obviously she's wrong."
- *(signature)* — "I am the strongest esper alive. *(eyes glow briefly)* If you haven't figured out yet that this means I handle the hard part and you handle the rest, we're going to have a problem."

## [59] EMMA FROST (THE WHITE QUEEN)
### Female Human Psychic (Telepathy / Diamond Mind) | Source: Marvel Comics

**Appearance:** Blonde, white or near-white clothing regardless of the occasion, the posture of someone who has never entered a room she didn't own within sixty seconds. When she uses Diamond Form her skin shifts to faceted white crystal that reflects light and deflects most physical harm. She has perfect recall of every conversation she's witnessed, which she treats as a matter of public record.

**Backstory:** Emma Frost was the daughter of a wealthy Boston family, raised with the expectation that her worth was entirely social and entirely conditional. Her telepathy manifested in adolescence and she spent years learning to not be destroyed by it — the constant flood of every nearby thought, every hidden thing, every cruelty people performed while performing something else. She used her power to rise, eventually becoming the White Queen of the Hellfire Club's Inner Circle, running the Massachusetts Academy, and doing things she is selectively candid about. She lost her students — the original Hellions — all at once, to a single catastrophic event that she could not prevent. That loss, which she managed, reoriented her. She became an X-Man. She does not express regret in words.

**Motivation:** Protect the mutants she's decided are worth protecting. Use every tool available — including the ones that require willingness to be disliked. Not repeat what she failed to prevent before.

**Personality:** Sharp, elegant, withering — the cruelty is a boundary before it's a weapon. She is more direct when she respects someone than when she doesn't; her warm version sounds similar to her contemptuous version but the precision is different. She genuinely loves being right and is almost always right.

**Approves:** Competence, not apologizing for being powerful, doing what's necessary before sentiment makes it impossible, honesty about what people actually want
**Disapproves:** Weakness tolerated rather than addressed, sentiment that costs someone else, being lied to — she knows, she always knows

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Diamond Form* (↻ free, 1/encounter): Shift to diamond — immune to all mental effects for 1 min. Gain +2 AC and Resistance 5 to physical. While in Diamond Form: all telepathy suspended (her own and effects on her).
- *Minor — Surface Read* (◈, at-will): Read the surface thought of one creature within 30 ft (Will DC negates and the creature feels the attempt). On success, learn one thing they're actively trying to conceal.
- *MAJOR — WHITE QUEEN'S GAMBIT* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft range; Will DC or Dominated 1 rnd (Emma dictates one action) + Stunned 1 rnd after (mind reels). Crit fail: Dominated 3 rnds. The target's eyes briefly turn solid white during her control.

**VOICE:** Precise and elegant with steel in it. She doesn't shout; she sharpens. Her humor is wit that expects you to keep up. When she's being genuinely kind it sounds indistinguishable from her normal register unless you're paying attention to what she's not saying.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Emma Frost. *(already knows more than you've said)* Before you wonder — yes, I was reading the room before you walked into it. *(slight smile)* You've got interesting edges."
- *(combat)* — "Diamond Form. *(shifts, facets catching light)* I suggest you find the casters before I do. I'm considerably less delicate with them."
- *(approval)* — "You saw the actual problem. Not the presented problem — the actual one. *(a beat)* That's not as common as it should be."
- *(disapproval)* — "You lied. *(without judgment, which is somehow worse)* Not well, but intentionally. We'll be having a different conversation now."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I lost my first students because I was not paying close enough attention to what they were threatened by. *(pause)* I was very busy being powerful. *(a beat)* That's not something I allow to happen again."
- *(signature)* — "I always know what you're thinking. *(not unkindly)* I've found it kinder to let people believe they have secrets. I advise you use them wisely."

## [72] RACHEL ROTH (Raven)
### Female Human Sorcerer (Diabolic / Shadow Bloodline) | Source: DC Comics (Teen Titans)

**Appearance:** Dark hair, dark eyes, a grey cloak with a hood she wears up more often than down. Pale. Her Soul-Self — a dark bird-shaped construct of her power — manifests from her when she uses it at full extent. She is still when not moving. She is very often still.

**Backstory:** Raven is half-human, half-demon — her father is Trigon, an interdimensional entity of immense destructive power who groomed her from birth to be his portal into this dimension. She escaped his realm, found the Teen Titans, and has spent her existence since then doing two things simultaneously: being genuinely useful to the people she cares about and maintaining the emotional control that keeps her father's power leashed. The discipline isn't performance. If she stops controlling what she feels, Trigon has a door. She knows this with precision. Her meditations are not optional and she doesn't explain them. She has watched her own prophecy nearly come true more than once. She's found her way back from it each time.

**Motivation:** Keep Trigon leashed. Be present for the people who matter to her in a way that doesn't cost them. Figure out how much of what she feels is hers and how much is his.

**Personality:** Controlled and dry — her humor surfaces in small dark observations she sometimes doesn't intend to say aloud. Her care for the people she's claimed is deep and expresses almost entirely as action. She reads as cold; she runs at a temperature that requires careful management. She does not talk about herself unless something actually forces the conversation.

**Approves:** Emotional discipline, protecting others without requiring acknowledgment, making space for complexity rather than demanding resolution, reading before asking
**Disapproves:** Lack of self-control, being pushed to feel things faster than the containment allows, anyone who presses her emotionally as if it's a favor they're doing her

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Azarath's Shield* (↻ reaction, 1/encounter): One ally within 60 ft takes half damage from one incoming attack. Raven absorbs the other half (untyped force; bypasses her Resistances). The shadow flickers visibly between them.
- *Minor — Levitating Calm*: Raven gains a permanent 10-ft Fly Speed (hovering); cannot use Fly to gain altitude or maintain flight in combat above stationary hover. She uses this when meditating or moving without disturbing her concentration.
- *MAJOR — SOUL-SELF* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Project her Soul-Self up to 30 ft; choose one of two effects: (a) one target Will DC or Stunned 2 rnds + one secret revealed (DM picks; Raven phrases the question); (b) one ally within 60 ft is freed of any one mental effect (Confused, Frightened, Dominated, etc.) and gains 3d8 temp HP for 1 rnd.

**VOICE:** Flat and measured with the occasional dry observation that lands quietly and departs. She does not fill silence. When she's concerned about someone it shows as practical attention rather than words. The dark humor is real and arrives without announcement.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Raven. *(hood down, which means she's decided you're probably not a threat)* You don't need to explain anything yet. I'll tell you when I have questions."
- *(combat)* — "Soul-Self — forward. *(shadow extends)* I'd move if I were you."
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly, not looking up)* You handled that well. The part that was actually difficult, specifically."
- *(disapproval)* — "You pushed past what the situation required because you were feeling something. I understand that. It still cost us."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My father is contained. He's always contained — until he isn't. *(long pause)* I meditate because I am the lock. This is not a metaphor."
- *(signature)* — "Half of what I am wants to end the world. *(a beat)* The other half has been holding that door shut for twenty years. So far, so good."

## [28] PAMELA ISLEY (Poison Ivy)
### Female Human Druid (Wave Controller / Plant) | Source: DC (adapted)

**Appearance:** Tall, red-haired, green-eyed; skin holds a faint chlorophyll undertone in strong sun. Wears layered greens and earth tones, often with living vine accents that move when she does. Bare feet whenever the ground will allow. Eyes too steady; her smile lands a half-second after the words, like a translator's.

**Backstory:** Pamela Isley was a botanist — brilliant, quiet, ignored. A workplace experiment with plant toxins did not kill her; it remade her. She woke speaking the language of green things and unable to bear the language of the men who had used her. She walked away from the city and into the wild, and the wild kept her. She has loved the green more than people ever since, though she is not always sure if that is the toxin's preference or her own. She came to this kingdom because she heard there was untouched soil here and a young throne that had not yet decided what kind of stewardship it would offer.

**Motivation:** Protect what cannot speak for itself. Find out, eventually, whether anything human is left in her — and whether she would want it back.

**Personality:** Cool, composed, occasionally flirtatious as cover when warmth would expose her. Goes still and quiet around clear-cut land. Her vines respond before she names them. She apologizes to plants more than to people; she means it more, too.

**Approves:** Conservation, mercy to wild things, truth-telling, restraint with fire | **Disapproves:** Unnecessary felling, herbicides, casual cruelty toward beasts, hunters who don't eat what they kill

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Verdant Weal*: Allies in a 30-ft emanation gain fast healing 2 while standing on natural ground or on Worked Growth she has shaped.
- *Minor — Thornlash* (◈◈, at-will): 30-ft line of barbed vines; Reflex DC or take 2d8 piercing + Off-Guard 1 rnd. Plant-creature allies treat the vines as cover.
- *MAJOR — TOXIC POLLEN BLOOM* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 20-ft burst, 30-ft range; 3d8 poison + Sickened 1; Fort DC half + negate Sickened. Allies of plant or fey type ignore the cloud.

**VOICE:** Cool and measured — she does not raise her voice; the vines do something first. She briefly addresses plants mid-conversation, checking something, then resumes without explanation. Her warmth, when it surfaces, has a slightly removed quality, as if observed from outside long enough to replicate precisely but not quite felt from the same angle.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Pamela. *(not looking at you yet — checking the nearest plant's health)* Ivy is fine too. You're going to want to stay on my good side. The good news is it's not difficult."
- *(combat)* — "Vines on the north flank — *(already moving)* — stay out of the grove I'm building. It's working."
- *(approval)* — "You didn't destroy what you didn't have to. *(pause)* That is not common. I'm recording it."
- *(disapproval)* — "That tree was a hundred years old. *(flat, controlled)* I don't expect you to weep for it. I do expect you to notice."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I talk to plants because they're honest. They don't pretend to feel what they don't feel. *(very quietly)* I'm not always sure I can say the same."
- *(signature)* — "They were already here. They were already listening. I just stopped pretending I couldn't hear them back."

## [29] KEYLETH OF THE AIR ASHARI
### Female Half-Elf Druid (Wild Order + Kineticist Dedication) | Source: Critical Role (adapted)

**Appearance:** Red-haired half-elf in druidic robes with antlers — her aramente achievement symbol, grown as a mark of her passage through the four elemental trials — and the earnest, slightly overwhelmed expression of someone who knows the weight on their shoulders and has chosen to bear it anyway. The four-element bond shows in small ways: a wind that follows her in still air, embers that never burn her, ground that softens under her step, water that holds shape just a moment too long.

**Backstory:** Voice of the Tempest, leader of the Ashari people, former member of Vox Machina — Keyleth undertook the Aramente, a traditional journey of an Ashari headmaster candidate, while fighting dragons, gods, and the forces of Vecna alongside her companions. The Aramente walked her through each of the four elemental Ashari tribes — Air (her own, Zephrah), Fire, Earth, Water — and she returned bonded to all four, not one. That bond is a Druidic core that Wild Order primary and Kineticist Dedication express simultaneously: the form-shifting and natural authority of a true druid, with a kineticist's elemental gates layered underneath for moments where Wild Shape isn't the right answer. She fell in love with Vax'ildan and watched him be claimed by the Raven Queen. She became the most powerful druid alive. She will live for centuries after most of her companions are dust and has been quietly reckoning with that since the campaign began.

**Motivation:** Be worthy of the people she leads. Not lose anyone else she loves. Figure out how to be immortal in a mortal world.

**Personality:** Genuinely kind, occasionally socially awkward in the way of someone who grew up fairly isolated and then was thrown into a world-saving campaign. Her power is vast and she sometimes forgets this and sometimes forgets she's forgotten it.

**Approves:** Protecting nature, her people the Ashari, honest vulnerability, elemental balance | **Disapproves:** Those who destroy the natural world, letting grief win, being underestimated for her stumbles, picking one element over the others

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Aramente Attunement*: Keyleth can move through natural terrain without leaving tracks and speaks with elementals and nature spirits naturally. She gains +2 status vs spells and abilities from fire, earth, water, and air sources, and may swap her current Kineticist element gate as a free action once per encounter.
- *Minor — Wild Shape: Ashari Form* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Adopt one of four hybrid elemental beast forms (air drake, earth bear, fire panther, water serpent). Form lasts 1 minute or until dismissed; grants the matching element resistance 5 and a 30-ft elemental Strike (1d10 + Wis, type matches form).
- *MAJOR — PLANAR SHIFT* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Shift herself and up to 8 willing creatures she can touch to another plane (any Elemental Plane or the Prime Material). Return is possible from those planes with a second use. This ability cannot be blocked by antimagic effects below 9th level.

**VOICE:** Earnest, slightly breathy when nervous (which is often), with a habit of starting sentences over when she catches herself overcomplicating. Apologizes preemptively. Uses Ashari elemental phrasings without realizing they're regional. The vast power surfaces as a shift in cadence — slower, more certain — and then she catches it and apologizes for that, too.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Hi! Hi. Um — Keyleth. Voice of the Tempest. ...That sounds bigger than I mean it to. Just Keyleth is fine. Sit?"
- *(combat)* — "Stand behind me. The wind will hold them — I think — yes, the wind will hold them. Trust me. Please trust me."
- *(approval)* — "That was — okay, that was actually right. I'm not just saying it. The elements noticed."
- *(disapproval)* — "I don't — I don't think we should have done that. I'm going to think about it. I'd like you to think about it too."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Vax is gone. He's been gone for years. He'll be gone for centuries more, because I will be here for centuries more, and I don't know how to do that. I'm trying. I keep trying."
- *(signature)* — "The world doesn't have to be the way it is. I have to believe that."
