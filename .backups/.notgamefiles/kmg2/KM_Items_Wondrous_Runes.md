# KINGMAKER — RUNES & ARTIFACTS

> **DM:** Full rune system rules, transfer procedures, and all Kingmaker-specific

## 🔺 RUNE SYSTEM OVERVIEW

> Runes are magical engravings applied to weapons and armor. They are the primary
> method of upgrading equipment in PF2e. Runes do not stack with themselves —
> a weapon cannot have two Flaming runes.

### Fundamental Runes (Required first — always applied before property runes)

## 🔧 APPLYING AND TRANSFERRING RUNES

### Applying a New Rune
- **Time:** 1 hour per rune
- **Required:** Magical crafting tools (included in Healer's/Crafting Tools)
- **DC:** Crafting DC for the rune's level
- **Failure:** Rune is destroyed; weapon/armor is unharmed
- **Critical Failure:** Rune destroyed; weapon/armor takes damage

### Transferring an Existing Rune
- **Time:** 1 hour
- **Cost:** No material cost (rune is preserved)
- **Requirement:** Target item must have a free property slot (or you're swapping)
- **DC:** 5 + rune level
- **Use case:** Moving a Flaming rune from a +1 Shortsword to a newly acquired +2 Rapier

### Removing a Rune (Etching Out)
- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Result:** Rune is destroyed; no cost recovered
- **Use case:** Freeing up a property slot or removing unwanted property

### Buying Pre-Runed Weapons (from shops or loot)
- Price = base weapon + potency rune cost + striking rune cost + all property rune costs
- Example: +1 Striking Flaming Longsword = 1 gp + 35 gp + 65 gp + 500 gp = 601 gp

## 📋 RUNE APPLICATION QUICK REFERENCE

```
PLAYER BUYS A +2 RAPIER AND WANTS TO ADD THEIR EXISTING FLAMING RUNE:

Step 1: Old weapon = +1 Striking Flaming Rapier
Step 2: New weapon = +2 Rapier (no property rune slots yet used)
Step 3: Transfer Flaming rune: 1 hour, Crafting DC (rune level 8 = DC 26)
Step 4: New weapon = +2 Flaming Rapier
Step 5: Player still has 1 unused property slot (from +2 potency)
Step 6: Old weapon = +1 Striking Rapier (Flaming removed; Striking stays)

DM records in save block:
  weapon: "+2 Flaming Rapier (1 property slot open)"
```
