## 🧬 RARE ANCESTRIES

### KOBOLD — Ability Boosts: +DEX, +CHA, free
```
[1] Caveclimber Kobold  — Combat Climber feat; climb with feet/tail leaving hands free; auto-crit on Athletics climb.
[2] Cavernstalker Kobold — Climb natural stone at half speed (full on crit success); auto-crit on Squeeze.
[3] Dragonscaled Kobold  — Ancestry HP becomes 10 (was 6); +1 saves vs dragon breath, sleep, paralysis.
[4] Elementheart Kobold  — Choose an element; resist 1/2 level to that damage type.
[5] Heavenscribe Kobold  — Speak Draconic; crit-fails on Diplomacy Make an Impression/Request become just fails.
[6] Mightyfall Kobold    — Ancestry HP becomes 10 (was 6); optionally boost STR+CHA instead of standard.
[7] Spellhorn Kobold     — Cast 1 common arcane cantrip at will as innate (heightened to half level).
[8] Strongjaw Kobold     — Jaws unarmed attack (1d6 piercing, finesse, brawling group).
[9] Three Kobolds in a Trench Coat — Medium size; 2/day reaction to negate permanent body-part removal.
[10] Tunnelflood Kobold  — Gain swim speed 15 ft.
[11] Venomtail Kobold    — 1/day apply tail venom to a weapon: deal persistent poison = level on hit.
```
**Best for:** Dragonscaled = Sorcerer (Draconic) + extra HP. Spellhorn = Wizard/Magus (free cantrip). Strongjaw = Monk/Rogue (natural weapon). Caveclimber / Cavernstalker = Rogue/Ranger (terrain god). Venomtail = Alchemist/Swashbuckler (off-turn CC). Elementheart = thematic caster. Mightyfall = frontline Kobold Champion. Trench Coat = RP comedy build (legitimately crunchy).

*Medium fox-spirit shapeshifters; HP 8, Speed 25, low-light vision. Change Shape ability (humanoid ↔ fox/tailless) baseline. Player Core 2 / Tian Xia.*

## 🧬 VERSATILE HERITAGES (any ancestry may take one instead of ancestry-specific)

```
[V1] Nephilim (Celestial)  — [legacy: Aasimar] Low-light vision (→ darkvision if you have low-light); nephilim trait; access to celestial nephilim feats alongside your ancestry feats.
[V2] Nephilim (Fiendish)   — [legacy: Tiefling] Low-light vision (→ darkvision if low-light); nephilim trait; access to fiendish nephilim feats alongside your ancestry feats.
[V3] Changeling            — Hag-touched. Claws unarmed (1d4 slashing, agile + finesse); trained in Occultism; pick hag lineage: Brine, Dream, Green, Moon, Night, Sea, Storm, Winter.
[V4] Dhampir               — Darkvision (→ from low-light); void healing (healed by void damage & negative energy, harmed by vitality & positive healing); +1 circumstance to disease saves.
[V5] Duskwalker            — Low-light vision (→ darkvision if low-light); cannot become undead; access to duskwalker feats (L1 Ancestral Insights → psychopomp sight; L5 Damning Strikes +1d4 void vs undead).
[V6] Beastkin              — Pick 1 animal aspect (bat, eagle, shark, spider, wolf, wolverine, others). Cosmetic at-will shift; full hybrid form via L5+ ancestry feat (1d4 jaws on hybrid Strike).
[V7] Aiuvarin              — [legacy: Half-Elf] Low-light vision; access to Elf ancestry feats at their normal level.
[V8] Dromaar               — [legacy: Half-Orc] Low-light vision; Orc Ferocity 1/day (drop to 1 HP instead of 0); access to Orc ancestry feats.
```

**Play notes — which versatile fits which build:**

- **Nephilim (Celestial)** best on: Champion, Cleric (any good deity), Oracle, Cavalier. Celestial nephilim feats include at-will light, celestial wings (L5+), and glimpse-of-redemption support abilities.
- **Nephilim (Fiendish)** best on: Sorcerer (Diabolic), warlock-feel Thaumaturge, fire-themed builds. Fiendish nephilim feats grant at-will *produce flame*, fiendish claws, hellfire scaling.
- **Changeling** best on: Witch (hag-lineage flavor match), Sorcerer (Hag bloodline), Occult casters. Free Occultism training + unarmed claws.
- **Dhampir** best on: Vordakai hunter builds (Ch3), Rogue ambusher (darkvision always-on). ⛔ **WARNING — Dhampir cannot benefit from most healing.** Tristian/Harrim Heal spells HURT you. See KM_Loot_Items_Ref.md Poor Fit table.
- **Duskwalker** best on: Cleric (undead slayer), Ranger (ghost sight + tracks undead), Champion (Grave Knight hunter). Damning-strikes scale is massive by Ch3.
- **Beastkin** best on: Druid (Wild Shape synergy), Ranger, Barbarian (Animal Instinct support). Cosmetic shift doubles as disguise.
- **Aiuvarin** best on: Any build wanting an Elf ancestry feat without giving up native ancestry (e.g., Human Aiuvarin = Human boosts + Elf feats).
- **Dromaar** best on: Any build wanting Orc Ferocity without giving up native ancestry. Most popular on Bard (Warrior Muse), Commander, Cleric (Warpriest).

**Choice rule:** You pick ancestry-specific heritage OR versatile — NOT both. Versatile heritage does NOT change ancestry ability boosts or flaw.

**Legacy names still accepted:**
- Aasimar → Nephilim (Celestial)
- Tiefling → Nephilim (Fiendish)
- Half-Elf → Aiuvarin
- Half-Orc → Dromaar

**Rule:** Player picks ancestry first, versatile heritage second. Versatile heritage LAYERS ON TOP — does not replace the ancestry's stat bonuses. You cannot take BOTH an ancestry-specific heritage AND a versatile heritage — pick one.

**Kingmaker setting note:** Brevoy nobility views Nephilim (Celestial), Dhampir, and Duskwalker with suspicion — "other-touched" commoners. Aldori Scholars treat Aiuvarin/Dromaar as standard. Restov court: any heritage accepted but noted. Stolen Lands wilderness: no prejudice.

## 📋 DM NOTES (additional to KM_Heritages.md § DM Notes)

**Rare ancestry socialization.** NPCs react to rare ancestries with curiosity (Restov court) or neutrality (Stolen Lands wilderness). No mechanical penalty. If the player picks Conrasu, Nagaji, Vanara, Kashrishi, Ghoran, Goloma, or Strix — flag in save block: `"ancestry_rarity": "rare"`, and surface 1 NPC reaction line in the Pre-Prologue opening scene ("strangers linger a beat longer to look at you").

**Subterranean Kashrishi (Trogloski) vs Conrasu (Rite of Reinforcement):** Both grant automatic ancestry feats. Do NOT let the player double-dip — the granted feat is ON TOP of their L1 free ancestry feat.

**Goloma Watchful + initiative boosters:** Goloma's Watchful (+2 status to Perception for initiative) does NOT stack with class-initiative bonuses (they're different bonus types — circumstance and status can stack, same-type does not). Check `KM_CinematicCombat.md` initiative rules before compounding.

**Fleshwarp two free boosts.** Fleshwarp's two free ability boosts make it a top pick for MAD classes (Magus, Champion, Investigator). Flag as `"mad_friendly": true` in roster guidance.
