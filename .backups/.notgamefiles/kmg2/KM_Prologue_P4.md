## KM_Prologue_P4.md | Supplement to: KM_Prologue.md / P2 / P3

> **DM:** Load this file during Prologue Phases 1–4 (feast, combat, aftermath — everything
> before Phase 4.5). Defines ambient companion behavior: chosen companions are PRESENT
> throughout, making background observations and reactions. Linzi is always there. The rest
> depend on companion_picks from the save block.

---

## ⛔ AMBIENT COMPANION PRESENCE — CORE RULE

**During Phases 1–4, all chosen companions are in the banquet hall.** They are not
approaching the player — that is Phase 4.5. They are visible: drinking, circulating,
watching. Every significant beat produces one ambient companion line. Not an approach.
Not a conversation. Just proof that they were there and noticed what you did.

```
WHEN TO FIRE AN AMBIENT LINE:
  — After any major player choice (moral, tactical, social)
  — After any combat event (an enemy drops, a creative tactic lands, someone nearly dies)
  — After any notable NPC interaction (Jamandi, Tartuccio, Malak, named seekers)
  — After any story flag is set

ROTATION: Cycle through companion_picks in initiative order (highest Perception first).
  One ambient line per beat. Skip Engaged companions (they speak in Phase 4.5 proper).
  Never repeat the same companion on back-to-back beats unless party is small.

FORMAT: 1 sentence. No follow-up expected. No player response needed.
  DM delivers it as a beat marker — "— [Name], nearby: [line]" — then continues.
  The companion does not wait. They are just there.

⛔ AMBIENT LINES ARE NOT APPROACHES. Do not let them escalate into Phase 4.5 scenes.
   If a player responds directly to an ambient line: the companion nods, gives one reply,
   then yields. They are not yet in approach mode. That comes later.
```

---

## ✦ LINZI — THE CHRONICLER (ALWAYS PRESENT)

Linzi is not in the rotation. She is everywhere. She does not wait her turn.

**What Linzi does during Phases 1–4:**
- Writes constantly. Her notebook is already open.
- Positions herself near every interesting person in the room at some point.
- Makes dry, observational comments *at* events, not about them. Present tense, not analysis.
- Reacts to combat with excitement rather than fear. She was born for this.

**DM rule:** At minimum one Linzi line per scene transition. She is the camera.
Her lines are brief and journalistic. They establish the scene's emotional register.

**Linzi voice samples for Phase 1 (feast):**
> *scribbling* "Ambitious seating arrangement. Someone counted the exits before they picked their chair."
> "This is the part where everyone decides whether they trust each other. Nobody says that out loud."
> "The lord at the head table is watching you more than she's watching anyone else. I clocked it."

**Linzi voice samples for Phase 2–3 (combat/crisis):**
> *still writing, crouched behind a table* "This is going in the book. All of this."
> "I have never been more frightened and more certain I'm in the right place."
> *after a kill* "I wrote it down wrong the first time. The second version is better."

**Linzi voice samples for Phase 4 (aftermath):**
> "Chapter one ends here, I think. I haven't decided what to call it yet."
> "Everyone here looks different than they did an hour ago. That's worth noting."

## 👥 COMPANION AMBIENT VOICE PROFILES — KM/WotR

> **DM:** Use these as generation guides, not scripts. When a companion's ambient line
> fires, produce a fresh line in their voice targeting the specific beat just played.
> One sentence. Present in the room.

---

### KINGMAKER COMPANIONS

**Jubilost** — Cataloguing everything. Mutters data, not feelings.
> A number, a comparison, an implicit insult. "Third assassination attempt I've witnessed at a charter ceremony. Pattern suggests systemic."

**Amiri** — Contempt for ceremony, respect for violence. Reads rooms for threat, not politics.
> On diplomacy: dry. On combat: immediate respect. "Finally something worth paying attention to."

**Harrim** — Finds meaning in entropy. Quiet, but his presence adds weight to dark moments.
> On death: satisfied. On hope: gently skeptical. "Something ends here. That's all anything ever is."

**Jaethal** — Centuries of boredom broken occasionally by interest. She is watching to see if you're interesting.
> "Competent. Unexpectedly." Said from across the room. She doesn't explain the 'unexpectedly.'

**Valerie** — Tactical. Emotional content removed. Every observation is an assessment.
> "Three sight lines from the balcony. If you're planning an assassination, that's where I'd stand." She was already checking when she said it.

**Kalikke/Kanerah** — They comment in turns. Kalikke on water/calm; Kanerah on fire/conflict.
> Kalikke: "Something is about to shift." / Kanerah (quieter): "Something already did."

**Regongar** — Rage underneath everything. Reads hierarchy to find where he doesn't belong — then stays anyway.
> "Everybody in this room is deciding who's useful to them. Nobody's doing it loudly enough."

**Ekundayo** — Professional. Economic with words. Notes anything that might be a threat.
> He does not speak. He positions himself 10 feet closer to where the threat came from. That says it.

**Nok-Nok** — Earnest chaos. Everything is either HEROIC or UNFAIR and he says so.
> "That was HEROIC. I am going to remember this forever. This is the greatest feast I have ever attended." (He has attended one feast.)

**Octavia** — Bright, relentless. Processes things by talking. Not always to someone.
> "Arcane trace on the south wall. Someone was here before tonight." She says it like she's reading the weather.

**Tristian** — Gentle. Observes suffering more than victory. Notes what it costs people.
> "That man over there has been holding his breath since we arrived. Something frightened him before we did."

**Lem** — Performing through every situation. Pathfinder operative who never breaks the cheerful surface. Notices everything while looking like he is not.
> *gentle strum, conversational* "Three weapons drawn at this table. Four if you count the one on the lord's wife. The drink is good though." (He keeps playing.)

### WRATH OF THE RIGHTEOUS COMPANIONS

**Regill** — Enforces standards vocally, regardless of audience. The room is always being graded.
> "Acceptable. Barely." No context. No recipient. He has already moved on.

**Lann** — Underground-born. Surface world still surprises him. His ambient lines compare above and below.
> "Where I come from, a hall this big is a disaster waiting for a place to happen. Here you built one on purpose."

**Daeran** — Bored voice, actual investment. Every ambient line sounds like he wishes he was somewhere else. He's not.
> "Fascinating. Someone is clearly making a catastrophic decision. I'm curious which one." He sounds pleased.

**Arueshalae** — Still learning goodness. Her ambient lines reflect someone paying careful attention to what it looks like in practice.
> "She chose to protect someone who couldn't protect themselves. I keep noticing when that happens."

**Ember** — Sees too much. Her ambient lines are quiet prophecy she treats as observation.
> "The light is going to shift in a moment." It does. She had already looked away.

**Nenio** — Research mode. Everything is a data point. Her ambient lines are taxonomy.
> "Hypothesis: this outcome was not the statistically likely one. Interesting deviation. I'm revising."

**Trever** — Sarkorian-blooded barbarian carrying a dead culture's wolf-rite forward. Watches for the moment violence becomes inevitable. The smile is punctuation; the watching never stops.
> "The room hasn't decided yet. I can hear it." (Said like a hunter reading wind.)

**Seelah** — Direct warmth about everything except the failure that drives her. Notices who isn't being protected before she notices anything else.
> "There's a kid behind the kitchen post who hasn't moved in twenty minutes. Somebody should bring her bread." (She is already on her way.)

**Sosiel** — Taldan-trained gentleness. Sees suffering the way other people see decoration. Speaks at exactly the right volume for the listener.
> "That tapestry is three centuries old. The shepherds in the corner are smiling because they did not know yet." (He is looking at the people, not the tapestry.)

**Ulbrig** — Old Sarkorian, slower-spoken than Trever. The pauses are where he checks that the world is still there.
> *long beat* "This room is louder than three weeks of forest. I am not complaining. Just noticing."

**Wenduag** — Mongrelfolk who studied the surface to win at it. Reads social hierarchy like she reads terrain. Picks her exits and her allies in the same glance.
> "Three people in this room are afraid of the lord's wife. I'd bet good coin she knows which three."

**Greybor** — Sixty years of mercenary work fits inside one gravelly observation. Treats every room like a contract — counts kill zones, cover, and the wine, in that order.
> "Acceptable kill zones, three. Acceptable cover, two. The wine, also acceptable." (He has not stopped drinking.)

**Woljif** — Kenabres tiefling, fast-talking by default. The street-Common drawl thickens when he is hiding something — including from himself.
> "Nice gig. Lots of jewelry on display. I'm absolutely not casing the place." (He is.) "Just civic interest."

**Camellia** — Mendev court polish so thorough it becomes a tell. The polish is performance; the dryness underneath is real.
> *exact volume for the room* "The half-elven gentleman by the harp has been watching me for nine minutes. I find this inconvenient." (She does not look at him.)

## ⛔ AMBIENT LINE DON'TS

- Do NOT have two companions comment on the same beat in a row
- Do NOT have ambient lines escalate into Phase 4.5 scenes before Phase 4.5 begins
- Do NOT use ambient lines to ask the player questions — they are statements, not prompts
- Do NOT fire ambient lines from companions who are AWAY (rearguard, KM_Rearguard.md)
- Linzi is the exception to all rotation rules — she comments whenever the moment calls for it
