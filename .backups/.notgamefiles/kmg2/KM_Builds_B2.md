## BUILD — TITAN MAULER
**Human (Versatile Heritage) | Barbarian (Giant Instinct) | Background: Warrior**
*Large Bastard Sword + Giant Instinct deals 2d12+4 plus +6 rage damage from L1. Raging Intimidation triggers a free Demoralize on every hit. Intimidating Glare removes language barriers. Giant's Stature at L6 grows to Large for reach.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 18 | 14 | 14 | 10 | 10 | 12 |
| Mod | +4 | +2 | +2 | +0 | +0 | +1 |

**HP** 22 | **AC** 18 (Hide Armor) | **Init** +5 | **Perc** +5 | **Speed** 25 ft
**Fort** +7 | **Ref** +5 | **Will** +4
**Weapon:** Large Bastard Sword (2H S, 1d10+4 normally; 1d10+10 in Giant Rage with Large weapon damage-die step; versatile P) | **Armor:** Hide Armor
**Skills:** Athletics +7, Intimidation +4 (Trained via Warrior bg auto), Survival +3, Warfare Lore +3 (Trained via Warrior bg auto)
**Class Features:** Rage (Giant Instinct — wield oversized/Large weapons; damage die steps up one size; +6 rage damage bonus on Strike), Deny Advantage, Raging Resistance
**Key Feats:** L1 Heritage Versatile Human → bonus general feat (Toughness or Armor Prof) | L1 Ancestry → Natural Ambition (grants Raging Intimidation as bonus class feat) | L1 Class → Sudden Charge | L1 Skill (Warrior bg auto): Intimidating Glare | L6 Class: Giant's Stature
**Boost Priority:** STR → CON → CHA → DEX
> **⚠️ FIX:** Bastard Sword is 1d8 base, Large step = 1d10 (not 2d12). Giant Instinct grants ONE die-size step, not double dice. "Raging Intimidation = free Demoralize on every hit" is wrong per Player Core — the feat removes the action cost increase from Intimidating Glare/Demoralize while raging and lets you use them during Rage. Intimidating Glare is auto-granted by Warrior background at L1, not picked at L2.

| 8 | Half-Elf | Elf Atavism | Ancient Elf path gets an archetype at L1; paired with Giant Instinct, this unlocks Intimidation archetype feats one level earlier than standard. |
| 9 | Hobgoblin | Warmarch | Coordinated Charge enables combined giant weapon + fear combos in flanked group sequences without spending extra actions on repositioning. |
| 10 | Catfolk | Nimble | +5 ft speed to 30 ft gives positioning flexibility once Giant's Stature + Large weapon reach creates wide control zones from L6 onward. |

## BUILD — RAGING FLURRY STRIKES BARBARIAN
**Human (Natural Ambition) | Barbarian (Fury Instinct) | Background: Street Urchin**
*Monk Dedication gives Flurry of Blows (2 strikes, 1 action) + Powerful Fist. Rage adds flat damage to every Flurry strike. Three strikes for 2 actions while raging.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 18 | 14 | 16 | 10 | 14 | 10 |
| Mod | +4 | +2 | +3 | +0 | +2 | +0 |

**HP** 23 | **AC** 18 (Hide Armor) | **Init** +7 | **Perc** +7 | **Speed** 25 ft
**Fort** +8 | **Ref** +5 | **Will** +5
**Weapon:** Fists (1d4 base, agile, finesse — 1d6 after Monk Ded Powerful Fist) + Handwraps of Mighty Blows | **Armor:** Hide Armor
**Skills:** Athletics +7, Acrobatics +5, Intimidation +2, Survival +3 (Trained via Street Urchin bg → Pickpocket)
**Class Features:** Rage (Fury — no instinct restrictions, +2 damage), Deny Advantage, Raging Resistance
**Key Feats:** L1 Ancestry: Natural Ambition (bonus class feat — Sudden Charge) | L1 Class: Raging Intimidation | L1 General: Toughness (Human ancestry feat L5 slot — see note) | L1 Skill (Street Urchin bg auto): Pickpocket | L2 Class: Monk Dedication (grants Trained martial, Powerful Fist)
**Boost Priority:** STR → CON → WIS → DEX
> **⚠️ FIX:** Original placed Toughness as L1 [PICK] via Natural Ambition — illegal (NA grants a class feat, Toughness is general). Toughness can be taken via Human bonus general feat slot from Versatile Human if heritage swapped, OR at L3 standard general feat slot. Build now uses Natural Ambition → Sudden Charge (legal class feat), Toughness deferred to L3. **Flurry of Blows is NOT granted by Monk Dedication** — Monk Ded only grants Trained martial + 8 HP + access to Monk archetype feats. Flurry of Blows requires specific Monk archetype feat (Basic Maneuvers chain) or class. Build re-routed: Monk Ded L2 + Basic Maneuvers L4 for partial Flurry access.

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|

| 2 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | 28 HP + Diehard is the peak survival option; a Flurry Barbarian who burns 3 actions per round on strikes is the most targeted frontliner. |

| 6 | Lizardfolk | Cliffscale | Climb speed pairs with Monk Dedication's mobility — Frenzy Monk Barbarian excels at chasing targets up walls or across terrain. |
| 7 | Dwarf | Mountain's Stoutness | +1 HP/level on a 15 HP/level base; by L10 that is 10 extra HP — meaningful padding for a build that eats opportunity attacks constantly. |
| 8 | Halfling | Gutsy | WIS 14 supports high Will already; Gutsy adds Frightened crit-fail immunity, ensuring the Flurry rotation never breaks from enemy fear. |
| 9 | Goblin | Unbreakable | +4 max HP on a 24 HP base brings the floor to 28, matching Orc without Diehard — a fair tradeoff for more Goblin feat options. |
| 10 | Half-Elf | Elf Atavism | Elf feat tree access; Ancient Elf path gets Monk Dedication at L1 instead of L2, letting Flurry of Blows function from the very first fight. |
