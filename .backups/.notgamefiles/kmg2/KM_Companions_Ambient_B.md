## 🌍 CAMPAIGN AMBIENT — SECTION D COMPANIONS IN THE FIELD

### Location-Reactive Lines

Fire when the party enters a named location for the first time.

```
OLEG'S TRADING POST
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Storm        : "Outposts like this hold more than goods. They hold the reason people stay." She's been in enough of them to mean it.
Tifa         : Surveys the bar immediately. "Functional layout. Whoever runs this knows what they're doing."
Morrigan     : "Crude. But the people who run it are not fools. That is a different thing entirely."
Keyleth      : "There's a vegetable garden around back. Someone's been here long enough to care." She sounds pleased.
Isabela      : Already at the counter asking about ship routes. The answer is no ships. She looks disappointed but not surprised.

STOLEN LANDS (first hex entry)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Alleria      : Scanning the horizon before the party clears the ridge. "We're already being watched. Whatever it is hasn't decided whether to engage."
Sylvanas     : "Unclaimed land makes enemies faster than claimed land. Move with intent."
Keyleth      : Places her hand against a tree. "The land isn't sick. Just unattended. Like a garden that misses someone." [pause] "The elements are restless — not hostile. Haven't decided yet."
Yang         : "Wide open, multiple approach angles, no obvious kill boxes. I like it."
Artoria Pendragon (Saber) : Hand on her sword. Not drawing it — just present. "Something watches from the forest line. We proceed."

THE CAPITAL (first arrival / founding)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Storm        : "People will write songs about this place before they understand what it is. That's how it works." She doesn't sound dismissive.
Keyleth      : "People will grow up here not knowing it didn't exist before you." [beat] "That's enormous."
Morrigan     : "Power is a foundation, not a decoration. Build accordingly or watch someone rebuild it over your bones."
Kitiara      : Already calculating defensive angles. "River access, elevation, western approach the weak point. You know your enemies yet?"
Diana Prince (Wonder Woman) : Stands in the clearing looking at what it could become. "Every city starts like this. What it becomes is the question."

VARNHOLD (Ch3 — empty city)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Alleria      : Crouches at a doorstep, hand to the ground. "No blood. No sign of flight. They didn't run — they were taken somewhere they didn't need to run from."
Rei Hino (Sailor Mars)  : [very still] "I felt this coming. I didn't know from where." She doesn't say more.
Morrigan     : "The magic here is old. Older than the town. The town was built on top of something that hasn't finished."
Tifa         : Looks at someone's half-set table inside a window. Says nothing for a long moment. "Let's find them."
Sylvanas     : "A settlement taken cleanly, no chaos, no panic. Whoever did this had total control. That part is what worries me."

VORDAKAI'S TOMB
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Artoria Pendragon (Saber) : "Don't split up. Whatever is inside has had centuries to prepare this ground."
Morrigan     : "The wards are ancient but not decayed. Whoever placed them expected to return. They have not."
Sylvanas     : Hand to the stone at the entrance. "Something in there recognizes what I am. It is not afraid."
Tifa         : "Stay in sight of each other. We go in together, we come out together."
Liliana      : "Necromantic anchor, at least three layers of binding, something at the core that's been waiting. This is someone's life's work." She sounds professionally admiring.

SOOTSCALE CAVERNS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yang         : "Tight space, minimal room for my style. I'll adapt. Just watch the ceiling."
Tifa         : "Kobold smell. Scared ones. Scared things bite fast — I'll take point."
Imoen        : Already a step ahead, checking angles with the casual ease of someone who grew up in libraries with locked doors.
Red Sonja    : [not lowering her voice] "I've fought in smaller caves than this." That's the briefest tactical assessment she's going to give.

OLD SYCAMORE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Keyleth      : Both hands to the bark. Steps back immediately. "It's in pain. The earth spirits are screaming and nobody's been listening. That kind of pain festers."
Pamela Isley (Poison Ivy) : Stops ten feet from the tree. "The roots are afraid of something deeper in. I've never seen roots afraid." She watches it.
Sailor Mars  : "Don't touch the roots. Whatever is in them — it reads intent. Keep yours clear."
Megumin      : Studying the tree with intense focus. "The structural density of this thing is remarkable. It would survive an explosion." Beat. "Not saying I'd try."

PITAX (Ch4/5)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Storm        : "This city was built by someone who wanted to be remembered as cultivated. Everything here is performance of taste, not taste."
Kitiara      : "Six visible guards, rotating schedule, blind spot at the northeast gallery. We have maybe two hours before someone wonders why we're taking notes."
Emma Frost   : "Everyone here is either performing for Irovetti or calculating against him. The ones performing badly are the most dangerous."
Isabela      : "Corrupt city with a beautiful harbor. There's always a harbor. Find a ship, learn the tide schedule — just in case."
```

### Weather-Reactive Lines

```
RAIN / HEAVY DOWNPOUR
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Keyleth      : Hood down, face up. "The elements cleanse. Walk through it." Already soaked. Completely unbothered.
Morrigan     : Raises a ward to stay dry without comment. Sole dry person within ten feet.
Yang         : "My hair's doing something interesting right now. We don't acknowledge it, we move on."
Tifa         : Checks her boots, keeps moving. "Rain in the mountains was like this. You learn to move."
Yoko         : Wraps her rifle in her coat first, then her hair. Priorities clear.
Weiss        : "Moisture affects glyphs. I've already adjusted." She didn't stop moving to say it.

FOG
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Alleria      : "I can hear past what I can see. Two contacts moving north, one not. The one standing still concerns me."
Sylvanas     : Already ahead in the grey. Reappears from a different direction. "Three contacts, moving away." Gone again.
Sailor Mars  : "The fog is clean. That's what's wrong with it — something's in there that doesn't smell like weather."
Tifa         : "Close formation. Don't reach into it — let it come to us first."

COLD SNAP / BLIZZARD
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Weiss        : "Cold is workable. I know cold." She moves through a blizzard like it's slightly inconvenient.
Caitlin Snow (Killer Frost) : [standing in the blizzard facing into it] "This is fine." She means it more than even she expects.
Red Sonja    : "Good cold. Real cold." The same register as Amiri's line. Two people who were made by weather.
Artoria Pendragon (Saber) : Adjusts her gauntlets. That is the entirety of her preparation. She's ready.

THUNDERSTORM
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yang         : One fist out into the lightning. Briefly. Then she pulls it back in. "Still got it."
Sailor Mars  : Stands with the storm. The fire in her palm burns against the rain. Neither wins. She seems satisfied.
Ryuko        : [Senketsu rising in the wind] "The storm is helping." She means it practically, not poetically.
Morrigan     : "Lightning has uses. Don't assume it's working for us — but don't assume it isn't."
```

### Post-Combat Lines

```
AFTER A HARD WIN
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Red Sonja    : [breathing hard, grinning] "THAT is what we came for."
Yang         : "Okay. Okay. That was — yeah. Good." She's still shaking slightly. She's grinning about it.
Tifa         : Moving through the aftermath, checking each person. "Who's hurt. Tell me now, not later."
Ryuko        : [Senketsu settling] "One more." She doesn't mean right now. She means in general. Always one more.
Alleria      : "Arrows: fourteen remaining. Adequate." Maximum praise from her.
Kitiara      : [surveying the field] "Messy. But effective. The flanking move on the left — remember that."

AFTER PARTY MEMBER NEARLY DIED
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tifa         : Already there before anyone called for her. "I have you. Stay with me."
Yang         : [standing over the fallen companion] "Get up. Come on. Get up." She's not angry. She's scared.
Diana Prince (Wonder Woman) : [kneeling, hand to the companion's chest] "You did not come this far to fall here."
Artoria Pendragon (Saber) : [very still, watching Tifa work] "...Hold." To the party. She means: do not celebrate until they're standing.

AFTER ENEMY SURRENDERED / SPARED
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Artoria Pendragon (Saber) : "Well done." Two words. She sheathes her sword. That is the highest endorsement.
Tika         : "It was the right call." She nods once. She means it simply.
Imoen        : "I mean — was it? I'm not saying kill them, I just —" She stops. "No, it was right. Good call."
Sylvanas     : "Your call." She sheathes her bow. No comment on whether she agrees.

AFTER A CLEAN SWEEP (no injuries)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Alleria      : Surveys the field. "Well executed. Everyone held position." Maximum praise.
Sylvanas     : Nothing. There is the faintest upward movement in her expression.
Kitiara      : "I expected two of those to be harder." Studying the positions. Already learning.
Megumin      : [slightly too enthusiastic] "That explosion at the end was perfect. Did everyone see the radius? Perfect."
```
