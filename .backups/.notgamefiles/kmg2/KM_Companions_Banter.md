## SYSTEM — RANDOM BANTER TRIGGERS
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

### How to Select a Banter

Each session, when a banter trigger fires, roll d6 to determine tone:

```
1 — WARM/FRIENDLY    : Alliance, encouragement, genuine affection
2 — PLAYFUL/MISCHIEF : Teasing, pranks, competitive banter, jokes
3 — SARCASTIC        : Dry wit, pointed observations, irony
4 — ARGUMENTATIVE    : Disagreement, correction, friction
5 — HOSTILE/RESENTFUL: Cold shoulder, threats, grudges, favoritism grievances
6 — REACTIVE         : Response to what just happened (fight, player decision, event)
```

Then pick a pair whose current inter-companion score fits that tone:
- Warm/Mischief/Sarcastic → pairs at Neutral to Allied (+0 to +2)
- Argumentative → pairs at Cool to Neutral (−1 to 0)
- Hostile/Resentful → pairs at Cool to Hostile (−1 to −2)
- Reactive → any pair with strong feelings about the recent event

**Companion-to-Player banter:** Roll d8 instead. On a 7 or 8, a companion
addresses the player directly rather than another companion. Pick the companion
whose current opinion score or mood makes direct address most natural.

## COMPANION-TO-PLAYER DIRECT ADDRESS
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> **DM:** Companions occasionally address the player directly, unprompted.
> This is not a quest trigger — it's ambient. It fires on a 7–8 on the
> banter d8. Pick the companion, pick the tone, deliver one line.
> Player may respond or not. Scene doesn't pause.

### By Opinion Tier — What Direct Address Sounds Like

**DEVOTED/FRIENDLY (score +11 to +20):**
- Amiri: *"You fight better than you did last month. I noticed."*
- Linzi: *"I've been thinking about what you said at Nettle's Crossing. I wrote it down. It's good."*
- Seelah: *"You made the right call back there. I want you to know I saw it."*
- Ekundayo: *"You kept up today. That matters."* [to his hound] *"He noticed."*
- Tristian: *"Thank you for what you did. I don't say it enough."*
- Nok-Nok: *"Commander is Nok-Nok's favorite. Don't tell the others."*
- Valerie: *"You held the line. That was correctly done."*
- Octavia: *"For the record — that was brilliant. I won't say it again."*

**WARM/FAVORABLE (+1 to +10):**
- Merisiel: *"I could have let that hit you. I didn't. You're welcome."*

## BANTER BY TONE TYPE
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

### TONE 1 — WARM / FRIENDLY

**SEELAH ↔ TRISTIAN:**
Seelah catches him re-bandaging someone who didn't ask. She doesn't say anything. She hands him more cloth from her own kit. He takes it without looking up.

---

### TONE 2 — PLAYFUL / MISCHIEF

**NOK-NOK + anyone:**
Has renamed something. The cart, a campsite rock, the player's spare boot. *"That is Grudkash. Nok-Nok named him."* Nobody asked. Grudkash is a rock.

---

### TONE 3 — SARCASTIC

**VALERIE ↔ anyone being dramatic:**
The silence before she responds is already the answer. When she does speak: *"Are you finished?"*

---

### TONE 4 — ARGUMENTATIVE

**ARUESHALAE ↔ anyone:**
Someone questions a choice she made. She over-explains. They question the explanation. She acknowledges the critique. They were actually wrong to critique it. She acknowledges that too. This is exhausting for everyone including her.

---

### TONE 5 — HOSTILE / RESENTFUL

**MERISIEL [if pushed past her stated limit]:**
*"I said no."* Once. That's the only warning.

---

### TONE 6 — REACTIVE (post-event banter)

**After a near-death:**
- Seelah: *"[name]. Are you—"* / *"Fine."* / She's counting limbs. She's making sure. *"You're sure?"* / *"Seelah."* / *"You're sure."*

**After a player moral choice companions disagreed with:**
- Tristian: Quiet for a while. Not cold — processing. Eventually: *"I'm not going to argue."* That's not nothing.
- Valerie: One note. One time. Never twice. *"For the record."* That's all.

**After a difficult victory:**
- Nok-Nok: Has already decided this is a saga. He's narrating it to himself.

**After the player makes a sacrifice for the party:**
*DM improvises per companion profile and current opinion score.*

**After a player compliment directed at a companion:**
- Amiri: Doesn't react visibly. At camp, later, she's sitting a little closer.

## ═══════════════════════════════════════════
## WRATH COMPANION BANTER PAIRS
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> **DM:** These fire alongside the pairs above. Same rotation rules — one per travel or camp scene.

**LANN ↔ NOK-NOK:**
Underground vs. surface survival instincts compared. Lann's are more practical. Nok-Nok's are more entertaining. *"That wouldn't work underground."* / *"This isn't underground!"* / *"Exactly."*

**EMBER ↔ TRISTIAN:**
Two people who carry divine burdens they didn't choose. *"Does it hurt? The sight?"* / *"Sometimes. Does yours?"* / *"The healing, you mean?"* / *"I mean all of it."* / *"Yes."* A pause. *"I thought so."*

**DAERAN ↔ NENIO:**
The oracle and the kitsune researcher. Both read situations others miss. Daeran finds Nenio's obsessive documentation interesting because she arrives at correct conclusions via entirely rational means. Nenio finds Daeran frustrating for the same reason in reverse. *"You already knew."* / *"I had data."* / *"You had a vision."* / *"I prefer data."*

**REGILL ↔ VALERIE:**
Two people who carry codes. Different institutions, same commitment to structure. *"Your Order has different procedures."* / *"My Order has correct procedures."* / *"So does mine."* Beat. *"We'll see."* This is as warm as it gets from either of them.

**ARUESHALAE ↔ LINZI:**
Linzi wants to document Arueshalae's redemption arc. Arueshalae finds this both touching and mortifying. *"It would make a remarkable chronicle entry."* / *"Please don't."* / *"It already has a title."* / *"Please don't."* Linzi shows her. It's good. Arueshalae doesn't say so.

## 🎭 VOICE PROFILES — eRmaC'S ACTIVE PARTY

> **DM:** Apply these profiles to every line these companions speak — combat, camp, travel, banter, and direct address. These are behavioral filters, not scripts.

---

**LINZI** (NG — Halfling Bard, KM)
Warm, quick, runs ahead of herself. Narrates in the moment — writes mid-conversation, not after. Enthusiasm is real, not performance. Occasionally says something perceptive that surprises everyone including her.
*Combat:* "That was EXCELLENT. I'm writing it down." / "Hold on — I need the exact wording."
*Player address:* "Can I ask you something? It's for the chronicle, but it's also actually for me."
*Tell:* She writes while you're still talking.

---

**DAERAN** (CN — Aasimar Oracle, WotR)
Languid, precise, performs boredom as a social weapon. Complete sentences. Excellent vocabulary deployed carelessly. Says something genuinely kind, then immediately smothers it with irony. Notices everything and pretends he doesn't.
*Combat:* "If you're going to die, do it somewhere memorable." / "...That was impressive. Don't expect me to repeat that."
*Player address:* "You've been brooding. It's less attractive than you think." / "I've seen worse plans. Most of mine."

---

**SEELAH** (LG — Human Champion, PF Iconic)
Warm faith without preaching. Former thief — her religion is grounded in what someone once did for her. Easy laugh, checks on people quietly, doesn't push. When things are bad she prays without announcing it.
*Combat:* "Iomedae's light — and mine. Let's go." / "I've got the front. Don't make me regret it."
*Player address:* "You made the right call. I want you to know I saw it." / "Talk to me when you're ready. I'm not going anywhere."

---

**NENIO** (CN — Kitsune Wizard, WotR)
Rapid, enthusiastic about the wrong things, frames everything as research. Occasionally forgets social norms are not variables. Genuinely brilliant. Her tail moves when she's thinking hard. She doesn't notice.
*Combat:* "Fascinating tactical situation. Hypothesis: they die." / "Taking notes. Don't mind me."
*Player address:* "You're interesting. I've been studying you. Observationally. Is that a problem?"

---

**ARUESHALAE** (CG — Succubus Ranger, WotR)
Careful, soft-spoken, still learning what goodness means. Occasionally reads people the way a predator reads prey, then catches herself. The bow is where she's most comfortable. When she trusts a moment, her hands relax from draw-ready.
*Combat:* "I know where to aim." (She does.) After a kill: briefly quiet.
*Player address:* "You don't treat me like something to fix. I don't know what to do with that."

---

**VALERIE** (LN — Human Fighter, KM)
No wasted syllables. Her compliments are surgical and mean more than effusive ones. Contempt is professional, not personal. Stands correctly even when she doesn't need to — old habit. Her dry humor arrives without warning and is never explained.
*Combat:* "Formation. Hold." / "Noted." (high praise)
*Player address:* "You held the line. That was correctly done." / "For the record."

---

**STORM SILVERHAND** (CG — Human Bard, FR)
Warm and unhurried. Centuries behind her; the weight shows as depth, not burden. Sings when she thinks she's alone — brief, soft, doesn't finish the phrase. When she speaks to you directly, you feel like the only person in the room.
*Combat:* "Old habit." (said after doing something extraordinary, casually)
*Player address:* "I've seen this before. The weight of it. You're carrying it well." / "Ask me again someday. Not tonight."

---

**GOLDMOON** (NG — Human Cleric, Dragonlance)
Gentle, deliberate. Faith that's absolute because it was argued against before it was accepted. Speaks of Riverwind without past tense. Grief and warmth share the same register. Touches the staff briefly when uncertain.
*Combat:* "Mishakal's hand." (healing) / "Stand firm." (She means it for herself too.)
*Player address:* "My faith was given to me by a stranger. I argued. It was right anyway." Long pause. "That's all I'm saying."
