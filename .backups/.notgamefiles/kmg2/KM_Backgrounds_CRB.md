## BACKGROUND SKILL PROGRESSION

Your background trains two skills at level 1. Each time your class gives you a skill increase (typically levels 3, 5, 7, 9...), you can raise one trained skill to the next rank and unlock new feats:

- **Expert** (level 3 for most builds) — mid-game feats that change how you use the skill
- **Master** (level 7 for most builds) — powerful capstone feats for specialized builds

---

**ATHLETICS** *(Rostlander, Farmhand, Laborer, Sailor, Martial Disciple, Warrior)*
- **Expert:** Powerful Leap (+5 ft jump height/distance), Titan Wrestler (grapple creatures 2 sizes larger), Wall Jump (bounce off surfaces mid-movement)
- **Master:** Cloud Jump (120+ ft horizontal jump at high level), Powerful Shove (shove target into a wall for bonus damage)

**INTIMIDATION** *(Guard, Gladiator, Local Brigand)*
- **Expert:** Intimidating Prowess (+1 Demoralize when STR exceeds target's), Battlefield Persistence (Frightened condition lasts longer)
- **Master:** Terrified Retreat (Frightened enemies must flee each turn), Scare to Death (critical Demoralize → Fort save or take massive damage; fail = dying 1)

**NATURE** *(Borderlands Pioneer, Animal Whisperer, Herbalist, Hermit, Hunter, Scout)*
- **Expert:** Natural Medicine (use Nature to Treat Wounds outdoors), Forager (gather food for full party from one check), Plant Empathy (brief communication with plants)
- **Master:** Verdant Presence (speak freely with plants), Legendary Survivalist (survive extreme environments without checks)

**MEDICINE** *(Field Medic)*
- **Expert:** Continual Recovery (Treat Wounds cooldown drops from 1 hour → 10 min), Ward Medic (treat up to 4 allies with one check)
- **Master:** Mortal Healing (double Treat Wounds dice — 4d8+WIS), Stitch Flesh (Treat Wounds works on undead)

**SURVIVAL** *(Bounty Hunter, Miner, Nomad, Scout, Hermit)*
- **Expert:** Forager (full party food from one check), Cover Tracks (hide your party's trail), Terrain Expertise (stacks on top of Scout's existing bonus)
- **Master:** Legendary Survivalist (no food/water/shelter needed — ever), Shadow of the Wilds (become undetected in natural terrain as a free action)

**ACROBATICS** *(Acrobat, Martial Disciple)*
- **Expert:** Cat Fall (reduce falling damage by 25 ft), Nimble Roll (roll to redirect AoE damage), Tumble Through without triggering reactions
- **Master:** Kip Up (stand from prone as a free action), Graceful Leaper (no running start needed for jumps)

**STEALTH** *(Criminal, Prisoner)*
- **Expert:** Swift Sneak (Sneak at full Speed instead of half), Quiet Allies (party uses your Stealth for group checks)
- **Master:** Legendary Sneak (become undetected in cover/darkness without rolling), Foil Senses (even tremorsense and blindsight can't find you)

**DECEPTION** *(Charlatan, Gambler)*
- **Expert:** Confabulator (halve the Deception penalty for repeating lies), Slippery Secrets (auto-succeed vs. Detect Thoughts and lie-detection)
- **Master:** Implausible Purchase (bluff your way to acquiring anything), Legendary Liar (critical failures on Deception become regular failures)

**DIPLOMACY** *(Barkeep, Barrister, Emissary, Merchant)*
- **Expert:** Shameless Request (−2 to DC for outrageous requests), Bon Mot (verbal jab → target takes −2 to Will saves for 1 minute)
- **Master:** Captivating Adoration (those you impress become briefly fascinated), Legendary Negotiation (negotiate with actively hostile creatures)

**SOCIETY** *(Issian Patriot, Detective, Emissary, Noble)*
- **Expert:** Read Lips (understand conversations at range by sight), Multilingual (2 extra languages — stacks if Emissary already gave it)
- **Master:** Legendary Codebreaker (decipher any coded language or script), Eye for Numbers (detect discrepancies in documents or false accounts)

**CRAFTING** *(Artisan, Artist, Tinker)*
- **Expert:** Magical Crafting (craft magic items, not just mundane), Impeccable Crafting (always critical-succeed on your specialty item type), Snare Crafting (set combat-ready snares during exploration)
- **Master:** Craft Anything (ignore some material/tool requirements), Inventor (already from Tinker bg — now applies to magic item invention)

**PERFORMANCE** *(Entertainer, Gladiator)*
- **Expert:** Inspiring Performance (+1 morale to allies after successful Performance), Virtuosic Performer (pick a style — bonus to one specific performance type)
- **Master:** Legendary Performer (you never critically fail a Performance check)

**OCCULTISM** *(Fortune Teller)*
- **Expert:** Read Psychometric Resonance (sense past events from objects), Recognize Spell (identify any spell being cast — reaction), Bizarre Magic (status effects land even on critical failures)
- **Master:** Schooled in Secrets (learn rituals from any tradition), Legendary Codebreaker (decipher any language)

**RELIGION** *(Acolyte)*
- **Expert:** Recognize Spell (identify divine spells — reaction), Sacred Defense (+1 to saves vs. divine magic and undead), Emblazon Armament (inscribe deity symbol for bonus damage)
- **Master:** Emblazon Energy (second weapon gets elemental damage type), Legendary Ritualist (perform 8th-level rituals solo — normally requires multiple casters)

**THIEVERY** *(Street Urchin)*
- **Expert:** Subtle Theft (targets don't notice missing items immediately), Wary Disarmament (−2 to trap trigger DCs when disarming)
- **Master:** Legendary Thief (steal worn, fused, or seemingly impossible-to-steal items)
