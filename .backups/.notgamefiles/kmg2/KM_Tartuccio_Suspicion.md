## WHAT THIS IS

The Prologue banquet hall holds ~50 people: the Active 11, the 5 seekers, household staff (Kassil, Kesten, Ezvanki, named servants), Jamandi herself, and unnamed guests. **Any of them may develop suspicion of Tartuccio.** That suspicion is tracked as Wariness 0–3 per individual when they have observed something or had a relevant conversation.

When Wariness gets high enough across multiple NPCs, **they begin to speak to each other about it.** They compare observations. They form working theories. They play detective in low voices, in alcoves, at the edge of the room.

**All of it is hearsay.** None of it is proof. Tartuccio's plot armor is preserved by the fact that suspicion ≠ evidence. The hall can be quietly convinced he is *something* by PR_09, and Jamandi will still not act on rumor during her own feast. Public exposure waits for PR_09's parchment / ring / specific named act.

## WARINESS TIERS

| Tier | Label | What This NPC Does |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| 0 | Neutral | Treats Tartuccio as a guest. No registration. |
| 1 | Noticed | Brief look held a beat too long. Internal note. **No spoken concern.** Will glance at Tartuccio during others' approach attempts. |
| 2 | Privately Concerned | Will mention to the player privately if asked. "Something is off." Body language tightens when he passes. May warn one trusted other NPC if circumstances allow. |
| 3 | Certain Something Is Wrong | Will compare notes with any other NPC at Wariness 2+ in low voice. Refuses his approach attempts (immune to flip). Will **not** vote for him on a charter question. Will not publicly accuse — they have no proof and they know it. |

**Hard ceiling: 3.** No NPC can exceed Wariness 3 before PR_09. Wariness 3 is the *certainty without proof* tier — it is the ceiling because the proof only exists in PR_09.

## TRIGGERS

### Discussion triggers (Tartuccio NOT in earshot)

| Player Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Player asks NPC X for their read on Tartuccio | NPC X may shift Wariness ±1 based on bias + what player frames; high-bias NPCs may volunteer specifics |
| Player shares a specific observation (wine, positioning, social skill out of proportion) with NPC X | NPC X +1 Wariness if they can confirm it from their own observation; else they note it but no shift |
| Player defends Tartuccio to NPC X | NPC X at Wariness 0–1: −1 (held at 0 minimum). NPC X at Wariness 2+: NO shift — their personal observation outweighs vouching |
| Two NPCs at Wariness 2+ are near each other and one starts the conversation | DM may run a brief quiet exchange. Both may go +1 if their observations align. |

### Evidence triggers (observable tells)

| Player Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Player publicly notes an observable tell (e.g., "His glass is still full.") with NPC X in earshot AND line of sight to verify | NPC X +1 if they can confirm. NPC X may +2 if the tell is particularly damning AND their bias is +1 or higher. |
| Player rolls Perception/Society check that exposes a tell mechanically | NPCs in earshot per same line-of-sight rule. |
| Player physically intercepts Tartuccio's action visibly (e.g., catches him moving toward the wine table) | All NPCs in line of sight: +1 |
| Player produces NO evidence but accuses publicly | NPC reactions per bias — most NPCs treat it as breach of etiquette; high-bias NPCs may register the accusation as worth considering. Tartuccio's own scales apply per existing rules. |

### Detective-mode escalation

When **three or more NPCs reach Wariness 3** and have proximity to each other, the DM may run a **detective huddle** during ambient feast time: two or three of them in an alcove, in the kitchen corridor, behind the screen at the head table — comparing what they have seen. They form a working theory. The theory is correct in broad strokes. **It still does not become evidence.**

Output format for a detective huddle (DM-facing template):

```
🕵️ DETECTIVE HUDDLE — [Location], [In-game time]
Present: [NPC A, NPC B, NPC C — all at Wariness 3]
Observations compared:
  - [NPC A's tell]
  - [NPC B's tell]
  - [NPC C's tell]
Working theory: [What they believe — must be broadly correct but unproven]
What they will do with it: [Watch him. Warn the player privately. Refuse his approach.
                            NOT: accuse publicly, alert Jamandi formally, attempt detention.]
```

The player may stumble onto a huddle by walking into the right corridor at the right time. They may also be told about one privately by a trusted companion.

## PLOT ARMOR REINFORCEMENT — what Wariness can NEVER cause pre-PR_09

Even with the entire hall at Wariness 3:
- ❌ Jamandi does NOT act on rumor during her own feast. She is the host; the feast is her event. She may *develop* Wariness 3 herself and still complete the evening without intervening. (Her plot armor: a host who acts on hearsay loses standing.)
- ❌ Kassil / Kesten do NOT detain, restrain, or escort him out. They serve Jamandi; without her order, they hold position. They may *flank a doorway* or *adjust their posture*. They do not act.
- ❌ Ezvanki does NOT bless / cast / invoke detection magic on him. He sees what he sees; he keeps it private.
- ❌ No companion physically intervenes. They will refuse his approach, vote against him, warn the player. They will not initiate combat or detention.
- ❌ No public accusation produces consequence. If the player tries to publicly out him, the room reads it as breach of etiquette without proof. Tartuccio handles it socially per his existing scales.

What CAN happen pre-PR_09:
- ✅ Companions privately tell the player they don't trust him
- ✅ Companions refuse his approach attempts (Wariness 3 = immunity to flip)
- ✅ Detective huddles produce a (correct but unprovable) working theory
- ✅ The atmosphere of the hall shifts — Tartuccio feels watched (his Confidence drops per existing scales)
- ✅ Linzi writes her suspicion into the chronicle (her prose reflects her own Wariness tier)

PR_09 is the gate. Until then, hearsay accumulates. Hearsay never converts.

## CHRONICLE INTEGRATION (Linzi)

Linzi's chronicle entries reflect her own current Wariness toward Tartuccio:
- Wariness 0: He is mentioned as another guest in the room. No emphasis.
- Wariness 1: She notes him with the chronicler's pause — "the man called Tartuccio, *who I am still working out*..."
- Wariness 2: She begins to write him with reserve. Margin notes appear. *"His glass has not moved. I do not know if this means what I think it means."*
- Wariness 3: She is openly suspicious in her own prose. She still does not name him a spy because she cannot prove it. *"I am writing this so that, if I am right, the page is the witness."*

The chronicle never publishes the spy reveal before PR_09. Her suspicion is on the page; the proof is not.
