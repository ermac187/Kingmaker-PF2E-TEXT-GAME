### #2 SUCROSE — Alchemist Mutagenist / Logistician | CG ★★★★★ | GI
*Shy, intensely curious; apologizes mid-sentence; her eyes light up when she finds a new reaction formula*
**Combat:** Mutagen factory — Combine Elixirs delivers two mutagens to allies in one action; Glib Mutagen for social dungeons; Revivifying Mutagen self-heals on each application; Miracle Worker brings allies back from death; Ultimate Mutagen apex form has all upside, no drawback.

### #3 EXCELLA GIONNE — Alchemist Toxicologist/Mutagenist | LE ★★★★ | RE5
*Impeccably polished corporate arrogance; power through biological leverage; believes Uroboros will make her a god*
**Combat:** Progenitor Virus toxins stack debilitating conditions; Mutagenist mutation boosts her own combat profile; willing to contaminate allied zones for results.

### #5 JEANNE D'ARC ALTER — Animist Corrupted Wanderer | CE ★★★★ | Fate

**Combat:** Black flame corruption spirits spread Doomed on contact; holy inversion aura strips divine protections; devastating burst on clustered enemies.
