### #4 SENUA — Listening Spirit Animist

**Passive — THE FURIES' COUNSEL:** Once per encounter, when Senua attempts a Will save against mental, fear, or emotion effects, she rolls twice and takes the higher result. The voices warn her first.

**Item — DILLION'S MIRROR-SHARD:** A small, blackened bronze fragment, smooth on one side, etched with a Pict spiral on the other. Worn on a leather cord at her throat. Once per day, Senua may consult a passed spirit she has known — the DM grants one truthful piece of information about a current threat, ally, or location, in fragmentary spirit-voice. The shard hums at memorial sites.

**+5 Reaction:**
> **SENUA** *(takes the shard, presses it briefly to her forehead, eyes closed)*: *"…He says he sees you. The voices agree, for once."* *(opens her eyes, very steady)* *"You named me what the journey made me, not what hurt me into starting. Few do that. Thank you."* *(quiet, almost to herself)* *"He says thank you too."*

**+3 Reaction:**
> **SENUA** *(turning the shard over in her palm, listening)*: *"It is warm. They are pleased."* *(small nod)* *"I am pleased. Thank you."*

**0 Reaction:**
> **SENUA** *(takes the shard, examines it, ties the cord around her neck)*: *"It will travel with me. Thank you."* *(she walks away listening — not to the player)*

### #46 YANG XIAO LONG — Burning Brawler Kineticist

**Passive — SEMBLANCE FEEDBACK:** Once per encounter, when Yang takes damage that reduces her to half HP or below, her next melee Strike that round deals +2 damage dice. The hit she took becomes the hit she gives back.

**Item — EMBER CELICA (Restored):** Yellow-gold shotgun gauntlets that fold into bracers when not firing. While worn, Yang's first unarmed Strike each round counts as a Fire kineticist Impulse for damage type, and on a critical hit ejects two spent shells with a sharp metallic *click* (cosmetic — but everyone hears it). The right gauntlet fits her prosthetic forearm precisely.

**+5 Reaction:**
> **YANG** *(slips the right gauntlet on, flexes the prosthetic, the alloy and the brass meeting like they remember each other)*: *"Oh. OH. These — these are MINE. Not — not a copy, not 'inspired by,' these are MINE."* *(grins, eyes flashing red for a half-second, then back to lilac)* *"I'm not gonna cry. I'm NOT. I'm just gonna hit something really, really hard. With both hands. Thanks. THANKS."*

**+3 Reaction:**
> **YANG** *(works the action on the left gauntlet, listens to the click)*: *"Smooth. Good weight."* *(bumps her fists together; sparks)* *"Appreciate it. Genuinely."*

**0 Reaction:**
> **YANG** *(slides the gauntlets on, checks the load)*: *"Solid. Thanks."* *(she's already scanning for something to punch — that's her version of polite)*

### #49 WEISS SCHNEE — Sparkling Targe Magus

**Passive — HEIRESS' DISCIPLINE:** Once per encounter, when Weiss casts a spell through Spellstrike, the spell counts as one rank higher for the purpose of overcoming resistances and DCs (not for damage dice). Precision is the heritage she chose to keep.

**Item — MYRTENASTER (Repaired):** A multi-action dust rapier with a rotating elemental chamber at the guard, four chambers loaded (fire, ice, lightning, force). Once per round, Weiss may rotate the chamber as a free action and her next Spellstrike deals damage of the chosen element. The hilt bears no Schnee crest — it was filed off, deliberately, by her own hand.

**+5 Reaction:**
> **WEISS** *(takes the rapier, examines the guard, finds the filed-down crest, looks up sharply)*: *"You — you found someone who would take it OFF. Most jewelers in the family's reach would not."* *(she does not smile, but the corner of her mouth shifts)* *"Thank you. This is — I don't have words that aren't smaller than the gesture. So I won't reach for them. Thank you."* *(tests the chamber rotation; it clicks perfectly)*

**+3 Reaction:**
> **WEISS** *(tests the balance, the chamber rotation, the edge — in that order, unhurried)*: *"Properly forged. Properly weighted. Chamber action is correct."* *(meets the player's gaze)* *"Thank you. I will use it well."*

**0 Reaction:**
> **WEISS** *(formal nod, rapier accepted at the guard)*: *"It is acceptable. I will keep it sharp."* *(no warmth — but no coldness either; the duty acknowledged is the response)*

### #64 ALLERIA WINDRUNNER — Void-Touched Ranger-General

**Passive — WINDRUNNER PRECISION:** Alleria's first Strike of any encounter against an enemy who has not yet acted gains a +2 status bonus to hit and ignores 5 points of resistance. The bow knows before she does.

**Item — THAS'DORAH (Returned):** A longbow of ren'dorei silver-wood, the string spun from arcane and void thread woven together. While drawn, Alleria may channel one Void cantrip per round through a Strike (DM picks: Telekinetic Strike, Void Whisper, or Distort) at no spell-slot cost. The bow whispers in Thalassian when she draws it. She is the only one who hears it.

**+5 Reaction:**
> **ALLERIA** *(takes the bow with both hands, draws it once to half-tension and lets it down — the string sings)*: *"Thas'dorah. — How."* *(briefly, the violet in her eyes deepens; she steadies herself)* *"You returned the bow my line carried for three thousand years. You did not ask why I left it. You did not ask what it cost to put it down. You simply brought it."* *(quiet, exact)* *"My thanks. Spoken in full. I do not give them lightly."*

**+3 Reaction:**
> **ALLERIA** *(runs her hand along the bow's spine, tests the draw)*: *"Properly balanced. The string is correctly wound."* *(meets the player's gaze)* *"Thank you. The bow remembers. So shall I."*

**0 Reaction:**
> **ALLERIA** *(takes the bow, slings it across her back, inclines her head once)*: *"It will serve. Thank you."* *(she walks the perimeter twice that night with it in hand — that is the extended thanks)*

### #69 IMOEN — Pink-Haired Eldritch Trickster Rogue

**Passive — CANDLEKEEP FOOTING:** Once per encounter, when Imoen succeeds at a Sneak Attack, her next move action that round does not provoke reactions and may pass through difficult terrain at full speed. She was always good at slipping out of things.

**Item — STONE OF GORION:** A small, smooth river-stone, gray with one streak of white, set in a plain silver wire wrap. Worn on a thin chain around her neck. Once per day, Imoen may treat any one failed save against Charm, Compulsion, or Possession as a success; the stone goes briefly cold against her skin and the effect breaks. It was Gorion's. He never gave it to her. She kept it anyway.

**+5 Reaction:**
> **IMOEN** *(sees the stone, reaches for it slowly, then quickly, then holds it in both hands)*: *"That — that's — okay, no, you didn't, that's HIS, I — "* *(her voice steadies; she is not crying, not quite)* *"He never said it was for me. I just took it. From the body. After. I — yeah. I always thought somebody'd take it back someday. Nobody did. And now you — "* *(closes her hand around it)* *"Thank you. Thank you THANK YOU. I'll wear it. I'll never lose it. I promise. Hey — I PROMISE."*

**+3 Reaction:**
> **IMOEN** *(takes the stone, turns it over, smiles a little)*: *"Smooth one. Good weight for the chain."* *(slips it on)* *"Thanks. Really. I — yeah. Thanks."*

**0 Reaction:**
> **IMOEN** *(pockets the stone)*: *"Cool. Thanks!"* *(brisk grin; the stone is around her neck the next morning, no comment)*
