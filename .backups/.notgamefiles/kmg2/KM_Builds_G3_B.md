## BUILD — PURE IMPULSE BLASTER
**Human (Versatile Heritage) | Kineticist (Earth + Metal Gates) | Background: Hermit**
*No dedication, all impulses invested in damage output. Earth Blast's d8 + Metal Blast's range combine for consistent elemental deletion. The Kineticist's peak single-target/AoE spike build.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 10 | 14 | 18 | 10 | 16 | 10 |
| Mod | +0 | +2 | +4 | +0 | +3 | +0 |

**HP** 20 | **AC** 16 (Leather) | **Fort** +8 | **Ref** +6 | **Will** +7 | **Speed** 30 ft
**Weapon:** Earth Blast (1d8+4 B/P, 30 ft / melee) / Metal Blast (1d6+4 P, 60 ft)
**Skills:** Nature +7, Survival +7, Athletics +4, Crafting +4
**Features:** Two-Gate (Earth+Metal), maximum Gate Junction investment, Metal Carapace (resist 10 physical, overflow), max impulse feat density
**L1 Feats:** Adopted Ancestry + Stone Wall | **Boost:** CON → WIS → DEX → STR

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

|---|----------|----------|-------------|

| 4 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | Diehard keeps the peak-DPR blaster firing after a crit that would drop any other ancestry — getting one extra Meteor Strike off after being lethal-hit is irreplaceable output. |
| 5 | Half-Orc | Orc Blood | Hold-Scarred via Orc Blood on a chassis with better speed and skill training than pure Orc — optimal for this open-field blaster that uses 30 ft speed to set up Shockwave lines. |
| 6 | Leshy | Vine | Athletics training via Vine lets the Earth-specialist grapple targets into difficult terrain zones where they provoke Earth Blast as they try to stand up from Seismic Stomp. |
| 7 | Lizardfolk | Frilled | Intimidation bonus pre-debuffs targets with frightened before Shrapnel Burst fires — frightened 1 on every enemy in the 20 ft cone means the burst hits at effectively -1 AC. |
| 8 | Halfling | Gutsy | Frightened immunity ensures Earth+Metal's 2-action Shockwave setup never gets interrupted mid-cast by a fear aura on a boss that spotted the Kineticist repositioning. |
| 9 | Catfolk | Cat's Luck | Reflex reroll on a build with DEX 14 but no armor investment means one very bad AoE Reflex save gets a second chance — pure blasters take AoE back-scatter every fight. |
| 10 | Gnome | Wellspring | Primal cantrip fills Earth+Metal's one gap (no utility outside combat) with a reliable Produce Flame or Tanglefoot option when every impulse fee is on cooldown. |

## BUILD — METAL WEAPON + FIGHTER
**Human (Natural Ambition) | Kineticist (Metal Gate) + Fighter Archetype | Background: Gladiator**
*Metal Gate provides weapon proficiency scaling and metal armor. Fighter Dedication unlocks Attack of Opportunity and weapon critical specialization. Hybrid melee/ranged versatility.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 16 | 14 | 16 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Mod | +3 | +2 | +3 | +0 | +0 | +0 |

**HP** 19 | **AC** 17 (Studded Leather) | **Fort** +7 | **Ref** +6 | **Will** +4 | **Speed** 30 ft
**Weapon:** Metal Blast (1d6+3 P, 60 ft) / Longsword (1d8+3 S, melee)
**Skills:** Athletics +7, Intimidation +3, Warfare Lore +4, Society +4
**Features:** Metal Gate, Metal Carapace (react resist 10 physical), Fighter Dedication (L2: Attack of Opportunity, weapon training)
**L1 Feats:** Natural Ambition + Metal Carapace | **Boost:** CON → STR → DEX → WIS

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Human | Natural Ambition | Two L1 feats (Metal Carapace + Natural Ambition bonus) means Attack of Opportunity is available at L2 Fighter Dedication with the full Metal opener already live. |
| 2 | Half-Orc | Orc Blood | Hold-Scarred Diehard on a melee sentinel that intentionally eats Attack of Opportunity triggers means you survive one extra counter-hit before Fighter Dedication's reaction shuts down. |
| 3 | Dwarf | Unburdened Iron | No speed penalty keeps the Metal/Fighter hybrid at 30 ft speed after heavy armor upgrades — Attack of Opportunity requires being adjacent, so mobility is everything. |
| 4 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | STR 18 Orc variant maximizes the Longsword's +STR damage, and Diehard ensures the melee sentinel stays upright through a string of Attacks of Opportunity counter-strikes. |
| 5 | Lizardfolk | Cliffscale | Climb speed lets the Metal Sentinel follow flying enemies that leave melee range, threatening Attack of Opportunity in the vertical plane without burning move actions. |
| 6 | Goblin | Unbreakable | +4 max HP on CON 16 keeps the overflow budget for Impaling Spike positive — melee range fighters take more hits than ranged, and every HP matters on AoO builds. |
| 7 | Halfling | Gutsy | Frightened immunity keeps Attack of Opportunity available — frightened enemies use actions to disengage, and a frightened Kineticist loses the key reaction on the turn it matters most. |
| 8 | Catfolk | Clawed | Natural claw attacks add an Agile melee option for Dual-Weapon Blitz turns when the Longsword's MAP is too high — claw + Metal Blast in one fluid combo action. |
| 9 | Tiefling | Pitborn | STR/Athletics bonus upgrades grapple reliability for a STR 16 melee Kineticist holding enemies adjacent for Attack of Opportunity — the combo lock that Fighter Dedication enables. |
| 10 | Tengu | Dogtooth | Natural bite adds a third Agile melee weapon type for Weapon Training proficiency, expanding critical specialization options when Fighter Mastery kicks in at L13. |
