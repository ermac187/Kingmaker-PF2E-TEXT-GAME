# KINGMAKER — COMPANION LEVELING MAPS
## KM_Companions_Leveling.md | Referenced by: KM_Companions_Iconics.md, KM_DMRules.md

> **DM:** Full leveling maps for core KM/WotR companions. Section C/D companions use compact seeds in KM_Companions_Leveling_B.md.
>
> **AUTO-LEVEL RULE:** When companion reaches a new level, apply the Auto row first. Then apply [PICK]:
> - `companion_leveling = auto` → DM silently applies the feat listed in the [PICK] column.
> - `player_leveling = manual` → DM presents class feats available at that level; marks [PICK] with ★.
>
> **HP FORMULA:** Listed per companion. Apply Con mod each level.
> **PROFICIENCY BONUS:** Trained +2 | Expert +4 | Master +6 | Legendary +8 (always add current level)
> **ABILITY BOOSTS:** At levels 5, 10, 15, 20 — +2 to four ability scores. Priorities listed per companion.
