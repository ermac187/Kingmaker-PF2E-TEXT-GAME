## 📋 DM NOTES

**Loot placement guidance:**
- Herbalism items: common in druid/ranger caches, Old Beldame/Bokken stock, Ch1+
- River/travel gear: standard stock at Oleg's after Ch1; everywhere after kingdom founded
- Mount/familiar gear: Capital stables (Ch2+), specialist breeders in Pitax and Restov
- Bandolier/quiver: basic versions everywhere; enchanted at Tier 2+ merchants
- Fey charms: Old Beldame, Bloom encounters, First World caches, Nyrissa's lair
- Noble/ceremonial: Restov tailors (Pre-Prologue), Brevoy nobility (Ch4+), Kingdom coronation
- Kingdom gear: earned through Kingdom events, not bought (unless specified)

**Kingdom Building Points (BP):** Items with BP costs can be built during a
Kingdom turn using KM_Kingdom.md rules, providing a gp alternative through
kingdom infrastructure. BP cost is payable during Economy phase.

**Pricing fit:** All prices match the PF2e value-by-level table. No item
here exceeds tier-appropriate wealth. Level-0 items (e.g., snowshoes) are
starting gear; level 20 items are artifact-adjacent.

**Tone fit:** Wilderness survival + fey magic + kingdom-building + Brevoy
nobility — the four pillars of Kingmaker. Nothing here breaks the setting.

**Cross-reference when picking items for a scene:**
- Camping scene → herbalism + river/travel
- Kingdom turn → Kingdom & Stronghold Gear
- Fey encounter → Fey Charms + Cold Iron Circlet
- Court audience → Noble/Ceremonial + Negotiator's Monocle
- Hunting trip → Mount/Familiar + Foraging Pack + Tracker's Collar
