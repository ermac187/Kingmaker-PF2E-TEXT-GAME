## COMMON ANCESTRIES

### [1] HUMAN
- **Stats:** Two free ability boosts (no flaws). Most flexible spread in the game.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Normal vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Versatile Human (bonus general feat + skilled), Skilled Human (+1 skill rank), Half-Elf (low-light + extra feat), Half-Orc (low-light + Orc Ferocity).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Natural Ambition (bonus class feat), Adapted Cantrip, Cooperative Nature, General Training (bonus general feat), Haughty Obstinacy.
- **Culture:** Humans in Brevoy fall into two camps — Issian (northern, traditional, loyal to House Surtova) and Rostlander (southern, independent, descended from Aldori swordlords). Rostland is where the player's charter originates.
- **Lifespan:** ~90 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Works for every class. Natural Ambition + Half-Elf/Half-Orc is the strongest generalist template. Never a wrong pick.

### [2] ELF
- **Stats:** +DEX, +INT, −CON, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 30 ft · Low-light vision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Ancient Elf (extra class dedication), Cavern Elf (darkvision), Seer Elf (detect magic innate), Whisper Elf (better hearing), Woodland Elf (climb vines).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Ancestral Longevity (skill-trained for a day), Elven Lore (Arcana + Nature), Elven Weapon Familiarity (longbow, longsword, etc. treated better), Nimble Elf (+5 ft speed), Otherworldly Magic (cantrip).
- **Culture:** Elves of the River Kingdoms are mostly nomadic or descended from fallen kingdoms. Not common in Brevoy proper. Often regarded as foreign, sometimes mystically.
- **Lifespan:** 600+ years.
- **Build Synergy:** Wizard, Magus, Ranger (Bow), Rogue, Investigator, Psychic. Ancient Elf heritage is the most mechanically powerful.

### [3] DWARF
- **Stats:** +CON, +WIS, −CHA, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 20 ft · Darkvision · 10 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Ancient-Blooded Dwarf (save vs magic once/day), Death Warden (saves vs negative), Forge Dwarf (fire resist), Rock Dwarf (hard to move), Strong-Blooded (poison resist + healing).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Dwarven Lore, Dwarven Weapon Familiarity (clan dagger, warhammer, battle axe, pick), Rock Runner (ignore difficult terrain on stone), Stonecunning (+2 Perception for stonework), Unburdened Iron (ignore heavy-armor speed penalty).
- **Culture:** Dwarves of Brevoy come mostly from the Golushkin Mountains or Druma. Stubborn, traditional, clan-bound. Clan daggers are passed father-to-child.
- **Lifespan:** ~350 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Fighter, Guardian, Champion, Cleric, Barbarian (Giant Instinct), Kineticist (earth/metal). Unburdened Iron makes heavy-armor classes feel better.

### [4] GNOME
- **Stats:** +CON, +CHA, −STR, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Chameleon Gnome (hair/skin color shifts, +2 stealth), Fey-Touched Gnome (innate primal cantrip), Sensate Gnome (+2 Perception for senses), Umbral Gnome (darkvision), Wellspring Gnome (innate cantrip from picked tradition).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Animal Accomplice (familiar), First World Magic (cantrip), Gnome Obsession (skill escalation), Gnome Weapon Familiarity (glaive, kukri, etc.), Illusion Sense (initiative + saves vs illusion).
- **Culture:** Gnomes flee the Bleaching — a slow fade into gray apathy — by chasing novelty, passion, and new experiences. Travelers, tinkers, performers, scholars.
- **Lifespan:** ~400 years if they keep chasing novelty; much shorter if they Bleach.
- **Build Synergy:** Wizard, Witch, Alchemist, Inventor, Investigator, Kineticist (any element). First World Magic = free cantrip for martials.

### [5] HALFLING
- **Stats:** +DEX, +WIS, −STR, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Keen Eyes · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Gutsy Halfling (save vs emotion), Hillock Halfling (rest better), Nomadic Halfling (+2 languages), Twilight Halfling (low-light vision), Wildwood Halfling (ignore plant terrain).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Distracting Shadows (hide behind larger creatures), Halfling Lore, Halfling Luck (reroll critical fail, once/day), Halfling Weapon Familiarity (sling, halfling sling staff, shortsword), Sure Feet (save vs trip and stumble).
- **Culture:** Halflings of the River Kingdoms are often servants, couriers, or wanderers. Rostland halflings run most of the inns and relay stations.
- **Lifespan:** ~150 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Rogue, Ranger, Bard, Cleric, Swashbuckler. Halfling Luck scales with every reroll feat you stack on it.

### [6] GOBLIN
- **Stats:** +DEX, +CHA, −WIS, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Darkvision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Charhide Goblin (fire resist), Irongut (eat rotten food safely), Razortooth (jaws unarmed strike), Snow Goblin (cold resist), Unbreakable Goblin (+4 HP, fall damage halved), Tailed Goblin (prehensile tail).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Burn It! (+1 fire damage per spell level), Goblin Lore, Goblin Scuttle (Step when ally ends adjacent, reaction), Goblin Song (demoralize via mockery), Goblin Weapon Familiarity (dogslicer, horsechopper).
- **Culture:** Goblins of the River Kingdoms are recently-civilized scavengers. Some speak the common tongue; others still bite. Loyal when fed.
- **Lifespan:** ~50 years (they live fast).
- **Build Synergy:** Alchemist, Gunslinger, Rogue, Inventor, Kineticist (fire). Charhide Goblin + Fire Kineticist stacks nicely.

### [7] HOBGOBLIN
- **Stats:** +CON, +INT, −WIS, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Elfbane Hobgoblin (save vs elf magic), Runtboss Hobgoblin (command goblins), Smokeworker (fire resist + smoke see-through), Warmarch Hobgoblin (endure long marches), Warrenbred Hobgoblin (tight spaces).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Alchemical Scholar (alchemical item crafting), Hobgoblin Lore, Hobgoblin Weapon Familiarity (composite longbow, glaive, longsword), Leech-Clipper (grapple proficiency), Remorseless Lash (off-guard after demoralize).
- **Culture:** Hobgoblins organize into legions. Disciplined, militaristic, hierarchical. Recently recognized as a legitimate civilization by some River Kingdom cities.
- **Lifespan:** ~80 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Fighter, Guardian, Ranger, Champion, Barbarian, Investigator. Alchemical Scholar is a real advantage for prepared-item classes.

### [8] ORC
- **Stats:** Two free boosts (no flaws). (Remastered — no fixed penalty.)
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision · 10 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Badlands Orc (hustle longer), Battle-Ready Orc (trained in Intimidate + Intimidating Glare), Deep Orc (dwarven weapons + stonecunning), Grave-Touched Orc (resist negative + void healing), Hold-Scarred Orc (diehard), Rainfall Orc (climb + swim).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Iron Fists (fist unarmed strike d6 lethal), Orc Ferocity (stay at 1 HP when dropped, 1/day), Orc Lore, Orc Superstition (save vs magic), Orc Weapon Familiarity (falchion, greataxe).
- **Culture:** Orcs of Belkzen are organized into holds and warbands. Those in the River Kingdoms are often mercenaries, exiles, or adopted children of noble houses (see Kassil Aldori).
- **Lifespan:** ~70 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Barbarian, Fighter, Guardian, Champion, Kineticist. Orc Ferocity is one of the best L1 survival feats in the game.

### [9] LESHY
- **Stats:** +CON, +WIS, −INT, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Cactus Leshy (spines unarmed strike), Fruit Leshy (edible fruit restores), Fungus Leshy (low-light → darkvision), Gourd Leshy (wise pumpkin-head), Leaf Leshy (slow fall), Lotus Leshy (water walk), Root Leshy (stay put, harder to move), Seaweed Leshy (swim speed), Vine Leshy (climb speed).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Grasping Reach (2-hand weapon reach boost), Harmlessly Cute (skill-trained), Leshy Lore, Leshy Superstition (save vs magic), Plant Nourishment (food from sun/soil).
- **Culture:** Plant-spirits given physical form by druids or the First World. Recent arrivals in most civilized lands. Curious, earnest, confused by mortal customs.
- **Lifespan:** Indefinite if cared for; decades otherwise.
- **Build Synergy:** Druid, Animist, Cleric (Gozreh, Erastil), Kineticist (wood). Plant Nourishment means never paying for food.

## UNCOMMON ANCESTRIES

### [10] LIZARDFOLK (IRUXI)
- **Stats:** +STR, +WIS, −DEX, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Cliffscale (climb), Cloudleaper (long jump + slow fall), Frilled (intimidate), Sandstrider (heat resist), Unseen (camouflage in water), Wetlander (swim).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Consume Spirit (eat fallen foe for HP), Envenom Fangs (poison bite), Iruxi Unarmed Cunning (jaws d6 → d8), Parthenogenic Hatchling (hard to poison), Razor Claws, Tail Whip.
- **Culture:** Iruxi clans are spiritual, traditional, patient. They measure time in generations. Outsiders often mistake their silence for slowness.
- **Lifespan:** ~120 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Barbarian, Monk, Champion, Fighter, Guardian, Druid.

### [11] TENGU
- **Stats:** +DEX, +free, +free (or +INT), free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Jinxed Tengu (save vs misfortune), Mountainkeeper (innate druid cantrip), Skyborn Tengu (reduce falling damage), Stormtossed Tengu (electricity resist), Taloned Tengu (talons unarmed strike), Wavediver Tengu (swim + breath).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Mariner's Fire (adapt to ships), Scavenger's Search Party, Squawk! (Diplomacy to Demoralize), Tengu Lore, Tengu Weapon Familiarity (katana, wakizashi, temple sword).
- **Culture:** Tengu are migratory scavengers and traders. Prize language collection — most speak five or more. Believed to bring luck or misfortune depending on heritage.
- **Lifespan:** ~80 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Rogue, Fighter, Monk, Swashbuckler, Magus. Katana/wakizashi access is a real mechanical win for blade classes.

### [12] CATFOLK (AMURRUN)
- **Stats:** +DEX, +CHA, −WIS, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Clawed (claws unarmed), Flexible (save vs grab), Hunting (tracking bonus), Jungle (forest climb), Liminal (sense fey/undead), Nine-Lives (extra death saves), Winter (cold resist).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Cat Nap (short rest benefit), Cat's Luck (reroll a save, 1/day), Catfolk Dance (move through enemies), Catfolk Lore, Saberteeth (jaws unarmed), Well-Met Traveler (Diplomacy bonus).
- **Culture:** Catfolk are curious, prideful, wandering. Nine-Lives culture places high value on meaningful risk.
- **Lifespan:** ~80 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Rogue, Swashbuckler, Monk, Ranger, Bard. Cat's Luck is a free extra save reroll.

### [13] KITSUNE
- **Stats:** +CHA, +free, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Celestial-Envoy (innate celestial cantrip), Dark-Fields (necromancy cantrip), Empty Sky (jumping), Frost-Fur (cold resist), Spirit-Touched (haunt/fey sense).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Foxfire (fire innate cantrip), Kitsune Lore, Kitsune Spell Familiarity (trained in spell list), Silver Tongue (Deception bonus), Spirit Touch (sense incorporeal), Two-Tailed Kitsune (+1 will save).
- **Culture:** Shapeshifters — most kitsune have a fox form and a humanoid form, with nine tails signifying age. Often trickster-scholars, diplomats, or magical emissaries.
- **Lifespan:** ~170 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Sorcerer, Bard, Rogue, Witch, Oracle. Silver Tongue is one of the best social feats at L1.

### [14] KOBOLD
- **Stats:** +DEX, +CHA, −CON, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Darkvision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Cavern Kobold (stonecunning), Dragonscaled (resist draconic element), Spellscale (innate cantrip from tradition), Strongjaw (jaws unarmed), Venomtail (venom once/day).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Cringe (debuff attacker who missed you), Dragon's Presence (intimidate bonus), Kobold Breath (cone breath 1/day), Kobold Lore, Kobold Weapon Familiarity (crossbow, spear, greatpick).
- **Culture:** Kobolds worship dragons and organize in warrens around them. Pack-minded, inventive, vengeful. Often regarded as pests by larger ancestries.
- **Lifespan:** ~120 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Alchemist, Inventor, Sorcerer (draconic), Rogue, Wizard.

### [15] RATFOLK (YSOKI)
- **Stats:** +DEX, +INT, −STR, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Deep Rat (darkvision), Desert Rat (heat resist), Longsnout Rat (+2 Perception for smell), Sewer Rat (disease resist), Shadow Rat (intimidate).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Cheek Pouches (store items in cheeks), Pack Rat (bulk bonus), Ratfolk Lore, Rat Familiar, Tinkering Fingers (Thievery check), Vicious Incisors (teeth unarmed).
- **Culture:** Ysoki are communal, inventive, traders. Entire caravans of ratfolk are common on the trade roads.
- **Lifespan:** ~60 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Alchemist, Rogue, Wizard, Investigator, Inventor.

### [16] SPRITE
- **Stats:** +DEX, +INT, −STR, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Tiny · 20 ft (no fly at L1) · Low-light vision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Draxie (fey charm), Grig (save vs emotion + cricket legs), Melixie (treat flowers as food + sting), Nyktera (echolocation), Pixie (fey magic cantrip).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Magical Experimentation (learn from cantrips), Sprite Lore, Sprite Weapon Familiarity (shortbow, shortsword — sized for Tiny), Fey Influence (fey skill), Invoke the Elements (minor elemental trick).
- **Culture:** Fey-descended, childlike but ancient. Bound to specific wilds. Not native to Brevoy — a sprite in Restov is a spectacle.
- **Lifespan:** ~500 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Witch, Wizard, Bard, Sorcerer, Rogue. Tiny size means sprites are hard to hit but HP is low.

### [17] FETCHLING
- **Stats:** +DEX, +CHA, −WIS (or +INT), free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Bright Fetchling (light adapted), Dusk Fetchling (+fast move through dim light), Liminal (resist mental), Shadow-Blessed (stealth in dim light), Wisp Fetchling (innate cantrip from Shadow plane).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Fetchling Lore, Hard to Fool (save vs Deception), Long-Term Shadow Magic (darkness spell in flares), Shadow Blending (conceal in dim light), Shrouded Magic (hide magical aura).
- **Culture:** Descended from humans trapped in the Shadow Plane. Pale gray skin, pupilless eyes, often quiet. Distrusted by those who confuse them with undead.
- **Lifespan:** ~100 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Rogue, Magus, Psychic, Sorcerer (Shadow), Swashbuckler.

### [18] GNOLL (KHOLO)
- **Stats:** +STR, +CON, −free flaw, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Ant Kholo (climb + swarm tactics), Bonuwat Kholo (swim + water breathe), Great Kholo (powerful jaws d8), Sweetbreath Kholo (persuasive), Witch Kholo (innate divination).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Bonekeeper's Bite (jaws d6), Crunch (bone bite), Distant Cackle (demoralize at range), Gnoll Lore, Pack Attack (flank bonus damage), Sensitive Nose (Perception smell).
- **Culture:** Pack-oriented, matriarchal, scavenger-hunter-warriors. Often feared in civilized lands, though some Rostland farmers hire gnoll guards for their ruthlessness.
- **Lifespan:** ~45 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Barbarian, Fighter, Ranger, Guardian, Monk.

### [19] GRIPPLI
- **Stats:** +DEX, +WIS, −STR, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Snaptongue Grippli (tongue reach), Thicktongue (disarm with tongue), Windweb (glide + short slow-fall), Poisonhide (mild poison to touch).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Grippli Lore, Grippli Weapon Familiarity (blowgun, scimitar, shortbow), Hop Up (stand from prone free), Tongue Tied (tongue grapple), Wall Jumper (climb then leap).
- **Culture:** Swamp-dwelling, tribal, secretive. The Stolen Lands have a few tribal groups in the marshes east of the Greenbelt.
- **Lifespan:** ~60 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Monk, Ranger, Druid, Rogue, Kineticist (wood or water).

### [20] NAGAJI
- **Stats:** +STR, +CON, −INT, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Low-light vision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Coiled Nagaji (tail slap unarmed), Greensting Nagaji (venom 1/day), Hooded Nagaji (intimidate display), Striped Nagaji (stealth in forest), Wyrmbled Nagaji (draconic resist).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Nagaji Lore, Nagaji Weapon Familiarity (bo staff, khopesh, spear), Scaled Skin (AC bonus), Serpent's Venom, Stolid Stare (intimidate + frighten).
- **Culture:** Descended from naga overseers. Proud, formal, reverent of ancient oaths. Rare in the River Kingdoms.
- **Lifespan:** ~110 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Champion, Fighter, Guardian, Barbarian, Oracle.

## RARE ANCESTRIES

### [21] ANADI
- **Stats:** +DEX, +WIS, −CON, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Adaptive Anadi (change-shape refined), Polychromatic Anadi (color-shift camouflage), Scuttling Anadi (climb), Snaring Anadi (web feat access), Venom Anadi (bite venom).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Anadi Lore, Dangle (drop-line from ceiling), Fangs (bite d6), Gossamer Web (hampering web), Spider Climb (short climb action), Web Walker (ignore webs).
- **Culture:** Spider-folk with human change-shape. Reclusive, contemplative, artistic. Often hidden in plain sight in human form.
- **Lifespan:** ~60 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Druid, Ranger, Rogue, Animist, Witch.

### [22] AUTOMATON
- **Stats:** +STR, +CON, −CHA, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision + Low-light · 10 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Armored Automaton (natural armor), Hammerhead (improved unarmed bludgeon), Inspired Automaton (learn faster), Primal Automaton (regen minor wounds), Void Automaton (necromantic resist).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Automaton Lore, Automaton Weapons (integrated hidden weapon), Clasping Grasp (grapple bonus), Internal Compartment (hide small item), Remote Repair, Stable Form (resist shape-change).
- **Culture:** Ancient constructs from a lost pre-divine era. Each is unique. Many are searching for their original purpose or creator.
- **Lifespan:** Indefinite. They don't age, only wear.
- **Build Synergy:** Guardian, Fighter, Champion, Barbarian, Kineticist. No sleep/food/air is a campaign-changing benefit.

### [23] AZARKETI (GILLMAN)
- **Stats:** +CON, +CHA, −WIS, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 20 ft (swim 20) · Low-light · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Hidden Gills (gills concealable), Murkeyed (clear vision in murk), Riversoul (fresh water adapted), Seabreath (breathe both), Thalassic (deep-sea pressure).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Azarketi Lore, Azarketi Weapon Familiarity (trident, spear, longspear), Hydraulic Deflection (reaction AC bonus), Surface Skimmer (surface-swim fast), Underwater Marauder (remove underwater combat penalties).
- **Culture:** Formerly enslaved by azlanti. Now free, proud, aquatic, quietly distrustful of surface empires.
- **Lifespan:** ~80 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Kineticist (water), Fighter, Guardian, Ranger, Monk.

### [24] POPPET
- **Stats:** +CON, +CHA, −STR, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Small · 25 ft · Low-light · 6 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Feather-Stuffed (slow fall), Rag Poppet (mended quick), Soft-Hearted (save emotion), Stitched Poppet (tough seams), Wooden Poppet (resist fire and slashing).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Poppet Lore, Soft Landing (fall damage halved), Ragbag (pouches inside body), Stitched-On (cling to a carrier), Uncanny Cheer (save vs fear).
- **Culture:** Constructs brought to awareness by magical accidents or deliberate creation. Child-like demeanor, surprisingly resilient. A curiosity at most tables.
- **Lifespan:** Indefinite; they don't age.
- **Build Synergy:** Bard, Sorcerer, Oracle, Witch, Cleric.

### [25] FLESHWARP
- **Stats:** +STR, +CON, −CHA, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision · 10 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Mutationist (shift traits), Reinforced (natural armor), Shapeless (sockets accept prosthetics), Sturdy Build (extra HP), Unkillable (resist death effects).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Abominable Form (frighten on sight), Fleshwarp Lore, Grafted Talons (claws unarmed), Invigorating Flesh (heal from negative), Titan Grip (grapple bonus).
- **Culture:** Living beings transformed — by drow, necromancers, alchemical accidents. Outsiders to every society they enter. Often angry, always remembered.
- **Lifespan:** Varies. Trauma aside, ~100 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Barbarian, Guardian, Fighter, Kineticist, Oracle.

### [26] VANARA
- **Stats:** +DEX, +WIS, −free flaw, free boost.
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft (climb 15) · Low-light · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Arboreal Cousin (climb improvements), Forestfolk (herbalism bonus), Prehensile (tail carry items), Seer Monkey (innate divination cantrip), Trickster (save vs illusion).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Brachiation (run across tree branches), Cheek Pouch (food storage), Prehensile Tail (extra grip), Tail Swipe (tail unarmed), Vanara Lore.
- **Culture:** Forest-dwellers from Vudra, gradually spreading. Playful, philosophical, spiritual. Most become monks, rangers, or druids.
- **Lifespan:** ~80 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Monk, Ranger, Druid, Rogue, Swashbuckler.

### [27] NEPHILIM (AASIMAR / TIEFLING base)
- **Stats:** Two free boosts. Heritage picks tradition (celestial or fiendish).
- **Size/Speed/Vision/HP:** Medium · 25 ft · Darkvision (if heritage grants) · 8 HP
- **Signature Heritages:** Celestial-Blooded Nephilim (Aasimar — resist pick one of acid/cold/electric/fire, divine cantrip), Fiend-Blooded Nephilim (Tiefling — resist fire, fiendish cantrip), Angel-Touched (healing resistance), Demon-Touched (intimidate), Pitborn (void resist), Musetouched (skill bonus).
- **Key L1 Feats:** Nephilim Lore, Nephilim Eyes (darkvision), Scion of Divinity (innate divine spell), Fiend's Mark (fiendish brand), Celestial Wings (at high level).
- **Culture:** Descended from outsiders — celestials, fiends, psychopomps. Marked physically (eyes of flame, scales, halos, horns, etc.). Often outsiders in their own families.
- **Lifespan:** ~100 years.
- **Build Synergy:** Champion, Cleric, Oracle, Sorcerer (any bloodline), Witch, Warpriest.
- **Note:** In Remastered PF2e, Aasimar and Tiefling merged into "Nephilim" ancestry with heritage picking the flavor. Legacy menu in KM_CharCreate.md still lists them separately as [27] Aasimar and [28] Tiefling for player familiarity — mechanically identical to Nephilim with celestial or fiendish heritage picked.

### [28] TIEFLING — See [27] NEPHILIM
Mechanical detail identical to Nephilim ancestry with Fiend-Blooded heritage. Kept as a separate menu entry for players who prefer the legacy name.
