## 🎯 PICK-10 COMPANION SELECTION SYSTEM

> **DM:** Run AFTER build selection, BEFORE Pre-Prologue.
> **Load `KM_Companions_Iconics.md` → output the COMPACT PICK-10 SELECTION SCREEN.** Do NOT load the full detailed roster (truncates).
> Player picks 10 from 88 available (+ Linzi forced = 11 total). QL companions (1, 16, 45, 60, 66, 84) are IN the pool; pre-selecting one triggers Quest-Replacement Rule per KM_Companions_Iconics.md.

### DM Flag Rules After Selection
```
Chosen (11 selected):
  [name]_chosen = TRUE
  → Actively seek eRmaC at their listed location
  → Use first-approach line below on first contact
  → Fully recruited when eRmaC explicitly accepts
  → Unchosen chosen companions stay at manor whole Prologue

Unchosen (not selected, non-quest-locked):
  [name]_chosen = FALSE
  → Present at manor as background characters
  → Do NOT initiate recruitment
  → Respond if spoken to — brief, professional
  → Can still be recruited if player approaches them directly later
  → Ambient behavior: see KM_Companions_Ambient.md

Quest-locked (1, 16, 45, 60, 66, 84): Unaffected. Join at quest triggers always.
```

## #13 SEELAH
### Female Human Champion (Paladin — Shining Oath) of Iomedae
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**Appearance:** Tall Garundi woman, close-cropped hair, golden full plate, genuine brilliant smile, heavy Iomedaen shield. Early 30s. Takes up space without apology and makes it better.

**Backstory:** Former street thief who stole a paladin's helmet and couldn't sell it. Iomedae's church took her in. Has been repaying that debt with interest ever since.

**Motivation:** Be worthy of the second chance. Protect people who don't have her armor or her luck.

**Personality:** Warm, direct, completely genuine. No performance. Walks into burning buildings without drama. Makes people feel safe, which she uses with precision.

**Likes:** Protecting the vulnerable, honorable causes, underdogs, fair fights
**Dislikes:** Cowardice behind authority, cruelty to the helpless, using faith as cover

### Level 1 Stat Block
```

HP: 22 | AC: 22 (Full Plate + Shield raised) | Speed: 20 ft | Init: +2
STR 18(+4) DEX 10(+0) CON 18(+4) INT 10(+0) WIS 12(+1) CHA 18(+4)
Fort +8 | Ref +2 | Will +5
Weapon: Longsword (1H S, 1d8+4) — Attack: d20+7; MAP −5/−10
Reaction: Retributive Strike (ally hit in 15 ft — halve damage, eRmaC may strike attacker)
Shield: Steel Shield (AC+2, Hard 5, HP 20) | Shield Block
Spells: Lay on Hands (focus: 1d6+4 heal, reaction to ally damaged)
Skills: Athletics +7, Intimidation +7, Religion +4, Diplomacy +7
Feats: Natural Ambition + Lay on Hands
Gear: Longsword, Full Plate, Steel Shield, Holy Symbol (Iomedae), Pack
```

### Leveling Milestones
```
L1 : Retributive Strike, Lay on Hands, Shield Block
L5 : Ability Boost, Weapon Expertise, Shield Ally (+2 AC adjacent)
L10: Ability Boost, Aura of Courage (party immune Frightened 1), Holy Avenger
L15: Ability Boost, Greater Divine Ally, Avatar of Iomedae
L20: Ability Boost, Shining Beacon (enemies dazzled in 30 ft)
HP/level: 10+Con | AC: L1:22 L5:26 L10:30 L15:34 L20:38
```

### Dialogue + Reactions
- *"If you're protecting someone, I want to be there."* | *"I've been hit harder. Come on."*
- **Approves:** Protecting the vulnerable, refusing to abandon allies, honorable decisions
- **Disapproves:** Leaving people behind, cruelty to prisoners
- **If player acts dishonorably:** *"That's not what we're doing. Tell me you know that."*

**Location:** Standing near Jamandi — not as escort, just gravitates toward where things matter.

## #20 HARRIM
### Male Dwarf Cleric (Warpriest) of Groetus
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**Appearance:** Heavyset dwarf with a thick grey-streaked beard, worn dark vestments marked with the skull-moon symbol of Groetus, and a heavy flail he carries like a man who stopped worrying about consequences a long time ago. Dark, calm eyes. The stillness of someone who has already accepted the worst.

**Backstory:** A priest of Groetus — the Skull-Moon, God of the End Times — Harrim was cast out of a Droskar cult in his youth for asking too many honest questions, and found in Groetus's doctrine of universal entropy the first theology that didn't require him to pretend. Everything ends. That's fine. He came south because something in the Stolen Lands hasn't ended yet, and he supposes he'll be present when it does.

**Motivation:** Serve Groetus. Witness endings. Be honest about what he sees. Possibly, in some corner of himself he hasn't named, be wrong.

**Personality:** Dour, fatalistic, and genuinely kind in spite of himself. His nihilism is sincere but has never once made him fail to help someone in front of him. The universe is a grim joke; he keeps showing up anyway.

**Likes:** Honesty, things that have lasted longer than expected, silence
**Dislikes:** False hope, hollow promises of salvation, being told to cheer up

### Level 1 Stat Block
```
HARRIM — Dwarf Cleric 1 (Warpriest Doctrine) of Groetus
HP: 20 | AC: 19 (Breastplate) | Speed: 20 ft | Init: +2
STR 16(+3) DEX 10(+0) CON 16(+3) INT 10(+0) WIS 18(+4) CHA 10(+0)
Fort +7 | Ref +2 | Will +8
Weapon: Heavy Flail (2H B, 1d12+3, Disarm/Trip/Shove) — Attack: d20+6; MAP −5/−10
Spells (Divine WIS DC 16): Cantrips: Guidance, Daze, Detect Magic
  L1: Heal ×2, Bane | Divine Font: 3+WIS Heals/day (1d8+4 each)
  Focus: Eerie Flicker (next creature struck by Harrim this round: Frightened 1; Will DC 14 negates)
Skills: Religion +8, Medicine +6, Athletics +6, Intimidation +4
Feats: Dwarven Weapon Familiarity (Heavy Flail), Warpriest Armor
Gear: Heavy Flail, Breastplate, Holy Symbol (Groetus), Healer's Tools, Pack
```

### Leveling Milestones
```
L1 : Divine Font, Warpriest Armor, Eerie Flicker focus
L5 : Ability Boost, Channel Smite, Raise Symbol
L10: Ability Boost, Heroic Recovery, Oblivion's Call (undead −2 saves vs. Harrim spells)
L15: Ability Boost, Greater Resolve, Touch of Entropy (crit fail: target Enfeebled 2, 1 min)
L20: Ability Boost, Miraculous Spell, Harbinger of the End (aura: enemies Frightened 1 entering 30 ft)
Heal output: L1:1d8+4 | L5:3d8+4 | L10:5d8+4 | L15:7d8+4
```

### Dialogue + Reactions
- *"Everything ends. I'm just faster at it than most."* | *"You're alive. Groetus hasn't finished with you."*
- **Approves:** Honesty about loss, acting in spite of hopelessness, accepting what can't be changed
- **Disapproves:** Empty optimism, treating death as aberration rather than destination
- **If player is overly hopeful:** *"Your hope is a door left open. Something will walk through it eventually."*

**Location:** Manor's small shrine room — sitting before a crude lunar symbol he drew on the wall. He did not ask permission.

## #68 MERISIEL
### Female Elf Rogue (Thief Racket)
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**Appearance:** Slender elf, dark choppy hair, two rapiers, practical mismatched clothing for movement, a dozen small scars on her hands. Mid-apparent-30s (centuries old; shows only in the eyes).

**Backstory:** Orphaned elf from the streets of half a dozen cities. Thief, bodyguard, sellsword. Came to the Stolen Lands because someone said there was nothing worth stealing there. Challenge accepted.

**Motivation:** Be free. Help people who remind her of who she used to be.

**Personality:** Cheerful, irreverent, genuinely skilled and aware of it without being insufferable. Makes everything look easy because she practices until it is.

**Likes:** Speed, clever solutions, anyone who's survived on their own terms
**Dislikes:** Slavers, profiting from helplessness, being underestimated (she uses it)

### Level 1 Stat Block
```

HP: 14 | AC: 19 (Studded Leather + Nimble Elf) | Speed: 35 ft | Init: +8 (Expert)

Fort +2 | Ref +8 | Will +6
Weapons: Rapier ×2 (1H P, 1d6+4 Finesse, Agile/Deadly d8) — MAP: −4/−8
  Sneak Attack: +1d6 vs Off-Guard | Thief: Use DEX for damage with Finesse weapons
Skills: Stealth +10 (Expert), Thievery +8, Acrobatics +8, Deception +5
Feats: Nimble Elf (+5 Speed), Twin Feint
Gear: Rapier ×2, Studded Leather, Thieves' Tools, Climbing Kit, Pack
```

### Leveling Milestones
```
L1 : Sneak Attack +1d6, Thief (DEX to damage), Twin Feint, 35 ft speed

L15: Ability Boost, Sneak Attack +6d6, Critical Debilitation
L20: Ability Boost, Sneak Attack +10d6, Master Strike, Legendary Thievery
```

### Dialogue + Reactions
- *"I was on the roof. I saw everything. Don't ask how I got up there."*
- **Approves:** Clever solutions, protecting the small, action against slavers
- **Disapproves:** Rigid law-following that ignores the actual problem
- **If player is lawful to a fault:** *"The law also said I couldn't eat. So."*

**Location:** Restov gate — on the rooftop above the arch. Drops down when eRmaC passes.

## NEW WotR COMPANIONS (condensed profiles)

### #81 CAMELLIA — Witch Curse Patron/Shadow | NE ★★★★ | WotR
*Half-elf spirit medium of Minata; her smile never breaks; the spirits she speaks to are not friendly ones*
**Personality:** Warm on the surface, hollow underneath. Studies everyone. The kindness is calculated but occasionally becomes real. She finds this alarming.
**Approves:** Spiritual investigation, acquiring power, pragmatic choices | **Disapproves:** Religious dogma, blind altruism

### #33 WENDUAG — Fighter Two-Weapon | NE ★★★★ | WotR
*Mongrelman warrior from the Worldwound underground; respects only strength; served the demon cultists until a stronger offer*
**Personality:** Blunt. Does not waste words. Views sentiment as a liability. Loyalty follows demonstrated power, not promises.
**Approves:** Decisive action, strength over mercy, winning | **Disapproves:** Hesitation, wasted sentiment, talking when killing works

### #67 WOLJIF JEFTO — Rogue Eldritch Trickster | CG ★★★★ | WotR
*Tiefling street-thief from Kenabres; three escapes ahead of the Inquisitors; sharper than he looks and he looks pretty sharp*
**Personality:** Irreverent, fast-talking, hides genuine warmth behind deflection. Trusted once, stays trusted.
**Approves:** Clever solutions, protecting the small, refusing to let the powerful win | **Disapproves:** Casual cruelty, lawful rigidity

### #17 SOSIEL VAENIC — Cleric Warpriest/Shelyn | NG ★★★★ | WotR
*Human cleric and painter of Shelyn; came to the Worldwound because beauty must exist where beauty is hardest*
**Personality:** Gentle but not fragile. His faith isn't armor — it's a light he carries. The art he makes in camp is better than it has any right to be.
**Approves:** Mercy, beauty in dark places, protecting civilians | **Disapproves:** Gratuitous violence, desecration, crushing hope

### #62 GREYBOR — Ranger Flurry Hunter | LN ★★★★ | WotR
*Dwarf monster-hunter for hire; reputation: problem delivered, problem solved; no speeches, no loyalty discounts*
**Personality:** Mercenary in the pure sense — honest about it. Has a code; the code is professional and complete. Respect it and he's reliable.
**Approves:** Completing the objective, keeping promises, paying what was agreed | **Disapproves:** Sentimentality that compromises the job, uncalculated risk

### #26 ULBRIG OLESK — Druid Animal/Shifting | NG ★★★★ | WotR
*Last Sarkorian warden; shapeshifter who remembers the land before the Worldwound; the wilderness remembers him back*
**Personality:** Steady. Patient in the way that mountains are patient. The grief for lost Sarkoris is there if you look — he doesn't perform it.
**Approves:** Restoring what was lost, honoring the land, protecting communities | **Disapproves:** Burning the wild, exploitation

### #7 TREVER — Barbarian Fury Instinct | NG ★★★ | WotR
*Human survivor of the crusades; not a legend, just someone who kept standing up; there is steel in the quiet ones*
**Personality:** Reserved, observant. Says little; means all of it. Has been through enough to know what matters and stopped pretending the rest does.
**Approves:** Protecting people who can't protect themselves, solidarity | **Disapproves:** Abandoning allies, using people as shields

## CORE PF2E ICONICS (condensed profiles)

### #34 VALEROS — Fighter Two-Weapon Fighter | CG ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Andoran farm boy who left for adventure and found it was mostly fighting and taverns; both fine by him*
**Personality:** Easygoing brawler who is much better at his job than his manner suggests. Likes a good fight, hates bullies, splits the difference cheerfully.

### #19 KYRA — Cleric Warpriest/Sarenrae | LG ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Keleshite survivor; her village was burned; Sarenrae's light called to her from the ashes; she answered*
**Personality:** Warm compassion hardened by loss. She does not preach — she demonstrates. The sunblade comes out when words have been tried.

### #71 SEONI — Sorcerer Draconic Bloodline | N ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Varisian traveler; the tattoos channel her bloodline power; she has always known there was something older in her blood*
**Personality:** Self-contained, observant, precise. Power with discipline. She knows what she is and has made her peace with it.

### #86 EZREN — Wizard School/Abjuration | LN ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Discovered magic at fifty; made up for lost time aggressively; methodical, curious, quietly formidable*
**Personality:** Every year he wasted studying the wrong things makes him more determined. Not angry — focused. The determination reads as calm.

### #52 SAJAN — Monk Flurry | LN ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Vudrani warrior following the compulsion to fight something worthy; seeks worthy opponents not for ego but for growth*
**Personality:** Serene and direct. Language is sometimes a barrier; action never is. He fights as a language.

### #27 LINI — Druid Animal Companion | NG ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Gnome druid; Droogami the snow leopard is her closest companion; she trusts animals more than most people and is usually right*
**Personality:** Curious, stubborn, loyal. Has gotten into more danger following interesting creatures than following dangerous people. The danger finds her either way.

### #63 HARSK — Ranger Precision Edge | LG ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Dwarf ranger; his brother was killed by giants; he follows them across the world; he has not stopped*
**Personality:** Gruff, reliable, unhurried about the things that aren't the mission. There is one mission. He mentions it obliquely and often.

### #10 LEM — Bard Maestro/Polymath | NG ★★★★ | PF2e Iconic
*Halfling bard who escaped indentured servitude through a combination of luck, music, and aggressive optimism*
**Personality:** Warm, energetic, and smarter about people than he lets on. The music is real. The smile is real. Everything else is adjustable.
