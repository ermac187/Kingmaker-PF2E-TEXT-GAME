## 🔫 GUNS & GEARS — COMPLETE FIREARM RULES

> Full rules for Build 15 (Gunslinger) and any firearm use. Supersedes any summary elsewhere.

### MISFIRE RULES

Every firearm has a Misfire value. When you roll a natural 1 on an attack with a firearm:
- Roll d20. If the result ≤ the weapon's Misfire value: the gun misfires.
- **Misfire effect:** Firearm is Broken (non-functional). Must be Cleared before use.
- **Clear Misfire:** 1A (Interact) — clears the jam. Gun can fire again.
- **Misfire values by weapon:**

| Weapon | Misfire |
|--------|---------|
| Flintlock Pistol | 1 |
| Flintlock Musket | 1 |
| Dueling Pistol | 1 |
| Arquebus | 2 |
| Coat Pistol | 1 |
| Pepperbox | 1 (each barrel) |
| Double-Barreled Pistol | 1 |

**Risky Reload feat (Build 15 L2):** Load and fire in one action. If you roll a natural 1 on the attack, the gun automatically misfires (no d20 check needed).

### CONCUSSIVE TRAIT (all firearms)

Firearms with the Concussive trait deal the higher of the target's resistance to bludgeoning or piercing damage — they don't get to apply both. Adamantine armor's Hardness does not apply to Concussive hits.

---

### KICKBACK TRAIT

Weapons with Kickback impose a −2 penalty to the next attack in the same round if you don't brace (stand still on solid ground). Build 15 ignores Kickback at L3.

## 🎭 INFLUENCE SUBSYSTEM

> Used in social encounters where the player must win over multiple NPCs
> simultaneously — court scenes, political negotiations, faction meetings.
> Referenced in KM_Actions.md (D11) but defined here.

### How It Works

An Influence encounter has:
- **Influence Points (IP):** The total needed to win over the group
- **Resistance:** Each NPC's threshold before they'll be swayed
- **Weaknesses:** Topics or skills that lower an NPC's Resistance
- **Hardliners:** NPCs who won't be swayed at all — they must be neutralized or bypassed

**Time:** Each round = one significant social exchange (5–10 minutes of in-world time)
**Actions per round:** Each party member gets 2 social actions per round

### Social Actions

| Action | Skill | Effect |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Influence | Diplomacy or relevant Lore | Reduce one NPC's Resistance by 1 (success) or 2 (crit success); +1 to own resistance on fail |
| Discover | Perception or Society | Learn one NPC's Weakness or what skills apply to them |
| Misinform | Deception | Spread false info; +1d4 IP if believed (Will DC to see through) |
| Divert | Performance or Intimidation | One NPC loses their next action (or +2 Resistance on fail) |
| Assist | Any skill | Aid another party member's Influence check (+2 circ. bonus) |

### Resistance and Winning

Each NPC has a **Resistance score** (2–6). When their Resistance reaches 0, they're swayed.
Swayed NPCs contribute their **Influence Value** (1–3 IP) to your total.
When total IP ≥ target: encounter resolved in player's favor.

**Failure conditions:**
- Any NPC's Resistance increases to 8+: they become hostile (encounter may turn to combat)
- Time runs out (DM sets number of rounds, typically 5–8)
- Player is caught Deceiving (−2 IP penalty per catch)

### Kingmaker Influence Encounters

## 🔬 RESEARCH SUBSYSTEM

> Used when investigating a mystery, studying a library, or decoding ancient texts.
> Triggered in Ch3 (Varnhold investigation) and any scene where information
> must be accumulated over multiple checks.

### How It Works

Research has a **Research Pool** — points needed to fully uncover the information.
Each research session = one extended check (1–8 hours in-world time).

**Research Check:** Roll the relevant skill vs the topic's DC.

| Result | RP Gained |
|--------|-----------|
| Critical Success | 3 RP |
| Success | 2 RP |
| Failure | 1 RP (slow progress) |
| Critical Failure | 0 RP + Setback (DM adds complication) |

When RP ≥ Pool: research complete. DM reveals all information in that topic.

## 🏃 CHASE SUBSYSTEM

> Used when the party is pursuing or being pursued. Chase replaces normal combat
> rounds with a series of skill obstacles.

### How It Works

A Chase has **Stages** — each stage is one obstacle the party must overcome.
Each party member can attempt one check per stage (or assist another).

**Movement:** Success on the stage check = advance one stage toward the goal (pursuit) or retreat one stage from pursuers (escape).
**Failure:** No movement this stage.
**Critical Failure:** Lose one stage (pursuit) or gain one stage (escape).

**Catching/Escaping:** Pursuer catches quarry when they occupy the same stage. Quarry escapes when they reach Stage 0 (pursuit) or Stage [max] (escape).

### Stage Obstacles (DM builds from this list)

| Obstacle | Skill | DC | Notes |
|----------|-------|----|-------|
| Dense crowd | Acrobatics or Deception | 18 | Acrobatics to weave through; Deception to blend in |
| Locked gate | Athletics or Thievery | 20 | Strength it open or pick the lock |
| Rooftop jump | Athletics or Acrobatics | 22 | Gap between buildings |
| River crossing | Athletics or Acrobatics | 16 | Swim or balance across stepping stones |
| Market stalls | Acrobatics | 15 | Tumble through without knocking stalls |
| Guard checkpoint | Stealth or Deception | 20 | Sneak past or talk way through |
| Narrow alley | Acrobatics | 14 | Easy for small characters; medium for large |
| Spooked horses | Nature or Athletics | 17 | Calm or dodge through panicking animals |
| Burning building | Athletics or Acrobatics | 22 | Fight through flames and debris |

## ⚔️ ARMY COMBAT (Kingdom War — Ch4)

> Used during the War of the River Kings (Ch4) when kingdom armies clash.
> Army combat is resolved at a strategic level, not individual combat.

### Army Stats

Each army has:
- **Attack** (offensive power)
- **AC** (defensive rating)
- **HP** (fighting strength — reduced by losses)
- **Morale** (will to fight; drops cause routs)
- **Commander bonus** (Leadership role fills this)

**Starting army (player kingdom):**

| Stat | Base Value | With Barracks | With Castle |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Attack | +8 | +10 | +14 |
| AC | 15 | 17 | 20 |
| HP | 50 | 65 | 90 |
| Morale | +4 | +6 | +8 |

### Army Combat Sequence

Each army combat = one strategic turn (represents hours or days of battle).

**Step 1 — Scouting:** Perception DC 18. Success = learn enemy army stats before engaging.

**Step 2 — Tactics:** Choose:
- **Assault:** Standard attack. Roll Army Attack vs enemy AC. Hit = deal 2d10 damage to enemy HP.
- **Defensive:** +2 AC this turn; −2 Attack. Enemy must attack first.
- **Flanking (2 armies):** +4 Army Attack; requires second allied army.
- **Withdraw:** Attempt to disengage. Enemy gets free Assault; if you survive, battle ends.

**Step 3 — Counterattack:** Enemy army attacks back (same procedure).

**Step 4 — Morale Check:** After taking HP damage, army must Morale Check (d20 + Morale vs DC 15). Fail = Routed (flees; loses 1d10 additional HP from desertion).

**Step 5 — Victory condition:** Enemy HP reduced to 0 = army destroyed or surrendered. Player army HP reduced to 0 = routed; player kingdom loses the hex being contested.

### Named Armies (Ch4)

| Army | Attack | AC | HP | Morale | Notes |
|------|--------|-----|-----|--------|-------|
| Pitax Royal Guard | +16 | 22 | 80 | +10 | Irovetti's elite; +2 if defending Pitax |
| Tiger Lords | +14 | 18 | 70 | +12 | Armag's barbarians; Assault only (never Defensive) |
| River Kingdom Mercs | +10 | 16 | 50 | +4 | Can be bribed to switch sides (Diplomacy DC 24) |
| Pitax Militia | +8 | 14 | 40 | +2 | Breaks on first Morale fail automatically |

### Commander Bonus (Leadership Role)

The General role adds their Warfare Lore modifier to Army Attack and Morale.
The Warden adds their modifier to Army AC.
If a role is unfilled: −2 to the relevant stat.

**Player in battle:** If the player character is present on the battlefield:
- Army gains +2 Attack and +2 Morale
- Player may spend a Hero Point to reroll one Army Attack roll
