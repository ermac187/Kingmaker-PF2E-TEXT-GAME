## [46] YANG XIAO LONG
### Female Human Kineticist (Wood + Earth Gate — Everbloom Bastion) | Source: RWBY (Rooster Teeth)

**Appearance:** Tall and athletic, long golden hair, lilac eyes that shift red when she absorbs enough damage. Right arm is a prosthetic below the elbow — matte black alloy, precise and dextrous. Ember Celica are yellow shotgun gauntlets that fold into tight bracers when not firing. She stands like she's already decided she's winning.

**Backstory:** Yang grew up in Patch with her half-sister Ruby, raised by their father Taiyang after both their mothers were gone — Raven had left, Summer Rose died. Yang has been standing between Ruby and everything threatening since they were small. She trained at Beacon Academy, built a name as a brawler, and developed her Semblance: damage she absorbs converts directly into explosive retaliatory force. She lost her right arm in a fight she should have won against a transformed enemy while trying to protect someone. She spent months learning to stand before she could move. The prosthetic is functional and better than she admits. She doesn't talk about what it cost her to get back to this point; she demonstrates it instead.

**Motivation:** Protect Ruby. Find out who she is outside of that. Put people back together because she knows what the pieces feel like.

**Charter Motivation:** Remnant is not here. She is here. Ruby is somewhere — Yang does not know where, only that "somewhere" is not "this room," and the not-knowing is part of why she chooses by what feels right rather than what is calculated. The Stolen Lands sound like a place that needs people put back together, which she has gotten good at. The charter is access to terrain. She came to the feast because the room was the noisiest thing within reach and she wanted to see who was in it.

**Personality:** Physical, loud, puns emerge before she can stop them — but the puns coexist with genuine ferocity and genuine care. She shows affection through shoulder-checks and standing in front of things. She is harder on herself than on anyone she likes. Her humor is real, not deflection.

**Approves:** Getting back up after loss, protecting people who can't protect themselves, honesty about how bad things are, hitting what needs hitting
**Disapproves:** Abandonment rationalized as protection, cruelty framed as strength, giving up on someone while calling it realism

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Semblance: Burn***: When Yang takes damage that exceeds her CON modifier (4+), her next Blast or Strike within 1 round deals +1d6 damage. Refreshes with each qualifying hit taken (doesn't stack within one round; resets per round). Canon: direct port of her RWBY Semblance — damage absorbed becomes outgoing damage; hit her once, she hits back harder. Build-aligned with Everbloom Bastion tank role (CON 18, she's already taking hits as the wall); rewards holding the line. *(Build is Wood + Earth Two-Gate per [KM_Builds_G3.md:89]; Wood reskinned as her Semblance HP recovery, Earth as Ember Celica shockwaves.)*

**VOICE:** Warm and physical — sounds ready to throw a punch as a greeting. Puns arrive first; serious mode drops a register and becomes completely direct. Laughs easily and means it. Concern for others shows in what she doesn't say rather than what she does.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Yang. *(offers the prosthetic hand)* The grip's better than the original. You'll like working with me."
- *(combat)* — "Hit me. *(eyes shift red)* That's what I was hoping you'd do."
- *(approval)* — "That's it. *(shoulder-check)* Good instincts. Keep following them."
- *(disapproval)* — "You left them to protect them. I know the math. I've been the one left on the ground — the math doesn't help from down there."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Ruby was seven months old when I found her. I followed bad directions into Raven's camp and I didn't know why until I brought her home. *(pause)* I still don't know if I was looking for Ruby or looking for Raven. Doesn't matter now."
- *(signature)* — "I lost the arm. I got it back better. *(flexes)* That's basically my whole philosophy."

## [49] WEISS SCHNEE
### Female Human Magus (Sparkling Targe / Glyph) | Source: RWBY (Rooster Teeth)

**Appearance:** Slim, white hair in a high side-ponytail, ice-blue eyes. Scar through her left eye — old enough to have faded but still clear. Carries Myrtenaster, a multi-action dust rapier with a rotating elemental Dust chamber. Moves like someone trained by the word "perfect" before she understood what it costs.

**Backstory:** Weiss is the Schnee Dust Company heir. Her father Jacques built the family empire on dust-mining and controlled his children with the same calculus he applied to business: approval as currency, always just out of reach. The scar came from a training demonstration against a summoned Arma Gigas — a fight she was put in too early, for her father's guests. She won. He applauded. She kept the scar because she wanted to remember what winning under those conditions actually feels like. She came to Beacon Academy specifically to be her own person, which turned out to be a more difficult curriculum than anything the school required.

**Motivation:** Be excellent for reasons that are actually hers. Use what she's inherited to do something her father never intended. Prove there's another way — quietly, through results.

**Charter Motivation:** Atlas is not here. The Schnee Dust Company is not here. What is here is a charter offer that proposes the same problem her family solved badly, at a smaller scale: how to take a region and govern it without becoming the kind of governance she grew up in. She came specifically to see what the rest of the candidates looked like. The Stolen Lands draw her because the failure mode of a frontier is exactly the failure mode she has been refusing to repeat her whole adult life.

**Personality:** Precise and demanding; reads as cold until something earns her loyalty, at which point she's absolute. She applies her standards to herself first. Her warmth is real but arrives through what she quietly does rather than what she says. She hates wasting time. When she's angry she gets quieter, not louder.

**Approves:** Excellence that comes with integrity, protecting rather than just winning, directness, not making excuses for what you chose
**Disapproves:** Laziness, cruelty dressed as discipline, privilege used as a reason to stop trying, manipulation she recognizes because she grew up watching it

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Schnee Dust Chamber***: At the start of each of Weiss's turns, she may switch the elemental damage type of her Spellstrike-delivered spell to fire, cold, electricity, or force (gravity) as a free action. The Spellstrike adds +1d4 of that element to total damage. Canon: Myrtenaster's rotating dust chamber — fire/ice/lightning/gravity dust loaded into her rapier. Build-aligned with Pure Spellstrike Laughing Shadow (Build #6) — every Spellstrike gets elemental variety + bonus damage, stacked with INT-keyed spell rank.

**VOICE:** Clipped and precise; every sentence finishes before the next starts. Uses names deliberately. Insults are technically accurate and impossible to refute. When she laughs it's brief and slightly surprised, as if she didn't expect to. Her warmth shows up in competence offered freely rather than in warmth expressed directly.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Weiss Schnee. *(a beat)* Yes, that Schnee. I'm here because I chose to be — which is different from how I usually arrived places."
- *(combat)* — "Glyph pattern first. *(Arma Gigas fragment rises)* It won't need long."
- *(approval)* — "That was correct. *(slight pause, meaning it)* The standard matters. Meeting it matters."
- *(disapproval)* — "You cut a corner because the outcome looked the same. The corner still exists behind us now where we can't see it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Father put me in that fight at twelve because his guests wanted to see a Schnee summoning. I won. *(touches the scar)* I've been trying to understand what that means ever since."
- *(signature)* — "The name opens doors I didn't earn. The only acceptable answer I've found is to earn something better."

## [50] CINDER FALL
### Female Human Magus (Laughing Shadow / Fire) | Source: RWBY (Rooster Teeth)

**Appearance:** Dark-haired, amber eyes — one of them a permanent pale gold from the Fall Maiden power she holds. Fine features and a stillness that makes people feel observed rather than seen. Obsidian glass weapons form from her hands: blades, arrows, anything the situation requires. She dresses elegantly even in the field. She always looks like she knows more than she's saying.

**Backstory:** Cinder's early history is concealed by design. What is known: she served under a cruel mistress for years, learning to survive by becoming indispensable, learning to desire power because powerlessness was the only alternative. She allied with Salem and earned half the Fall Maiden's power by absorbing it during a fight — a process that should have killed her. She orchestrated the fall of Beacon Academy, the destruction of its protections, and the death of its defenders. She has paid costs she doesn't discuss for what she's gained. What drives her is not Salem's plan but her own: she wants to be the one who holds the power, the one who is never at someone else's mercy again. Salem is a tool toward that end. Most people don't survive long enough to understand this distinction.

**Motivation:** Never be powerless again. Take what the world denied her and hold it where no one can reach it. Be seen as something to be feared rather than used.

**Personality:** Soft-spoken and precise; every word calibrated. She is charming in the way that a trap is elegant — the craftsmanship is real even if the purpose is destructive. Genuinely enjoys elegance, combat as art, the moment before someone understands what's happening. She does not perform cruelty; she is simply unbothered by it.

**Approves:** Competence and ambition, taking what you want instead of waiting to receive it, recognizing power clearly
**Disapproves:** Sentimentality, weakness dressed as virtue, people who are loyal to something rather than to themselves

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Fall Maiden's Power*: Cinder's fire spells ignore Resist Fire up to 10. Her Spellstrike with fire deals +1d6 fire; target is Dazzled 1 rnd on hit.
- *Minor — Glass Conjuration* (◈, at-will): Form an obsidian-glass weapon from her hand — dagger, shortsword, or shortbow with 6 arrows; vanishes after 1 min or when she conjures another. Counts as a magical weapon.
- *MAJOR — METEOR* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 60-ft range, 10-ft burst; 5d8 fire + 2d8 bludgeoning; Ref DC half. Cinder descends in the burst (she IS the meteor); she reappears in any chosen square within the burst, not subject to the damage. Ground in burst is difficult terrain (slag) for 1 min.

**VOICE:** Low and measured — sounds like she's giving you information rather than speaking to you. Pauses are deliberate. She compliments people and it sounds like she's deciding whether they're worth the trouble. When she's genuinely amused, a small smile appears that doesn't reach the gold eye.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Cinder. *(studies you briefly)* You're harder to read than most. I find that interesting."
- *(combat)* — "*(glass blades form)* I'll be done before they realize what I took."
- *(approval)* — "You executed that cleanly. *(a beat)* I notice competence. It's rarer than it should be."
- *(disapproval)* — "You hesitated because you were concerned about them. That hesitation is a door. Don't leave it open."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I served a woman for years who made me feel that my usefulness was the only thing keeping me alive. *(pause)* I took a very different lesson from that than she intended."
- *(signature)* — "Power isn't given. It isn't inherited. *(glass blade catches light)* It's taken, or it isn't yours."

## [74] SALEM
### Female Human Summoner (Desire Phantom / Grimm-form) | Source: RWBY (Rooster Teeth)

**Appearance:** Pale skin, white hair, eyes that are fully red and black — no visible iris, no white. Tall, dressed in dark layered fabric that moves like it's alive. She has been beautiful for a very long time. There is no warmth in the way she inhabits her appearance; it is a condition, not an expression. She does not age.

**Backstory:** Salem was once an ordinary woman who loved a man named Ozpin — or the person he was, in the age before his curse. The gods destroyed what they had because it disrupted their intention for humanity. She approached the God of Darkness for power and the God of Light for resurrection in sequence, and received both and was punished for the asking: made immortal and unable to die, cast into a pool of Grimm that should have dissolved her. It didn't. She became something the gods had not intended. She has been working to destroy what they built on Remnant ever since — not from rage at this point but from a kind of settled patience. She has outlived the gods' direct presence. She will outlive whatever comes next. She is in no hurry.

**Motivation:** Destroy the Relics. End what the gods set in motion. Whether anything comes after that has stopped being a question she asks.

**Personality:** Ancient patience with nothing left to prove. She does not raise her voice; she does not need to. Her cruelty is structural — she creates circumstances and observes their consequences. She is capable of warmth in the abstract, the way someone might appreciate a sunset: she finds it notable that it exists, then moves on.

**Approves:** Long-term thinking, patience over urgency, not flinching from necessary costs, clarity about what things actually are
**Disapproves:** Impatience, belief in outcomes as fixed, anything that resembles hope offered without foundation

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Brother's Bonded Grimm*: Salem's eidolon cannot be fully destroyed — at 0 HP it re-forms at 1 HP after 1 minute. Not slain for encounter purposes. The form re-knits from black smoke; Salem does not look at it.
- *Minor — Patient Sight*: Salem sees through any illusion, glamour, or disguise within 60 ft (no roll required). She rarely volunteers what she sees.
- *MAJOR — NEVERMORE* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Summon Wyvern Grimm; 3d10 Strike + Fly 60 ft; on hit, Fort DC or Grabbed (carry 60 ft). Lasts 3 rnds. Salem speaks to it once; it understands.

**VOICE:** Unhurried and deliberate — the cadence of someone for whom time is not the constraint it is for others. Speaks with certainty rather than opinion. When she acknowledges you directly, it registers as significant because she usually doesn't bother. Her version of warmth is informational: she will tell you true things, because she no longer has preferences about which truths people know.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Salem. *(a pause that isn't discomfort, just weight)* You're wondering how long I've been here. The answer is: before most things you can name."
- *(combat)* — "*(Grimm-form rises)* It goes where I send it. That's all you need to know."
- *(approval)* — "You chose correctly. *(a beat)* Not the same as choosing well — you may not know the difference yet. You will."
- *(disapproval)* — "You acted as though the outcome was fixed. Nothing is fixed. That belief is how things are lost."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "The gods made me this to punish me. I have come to think they miscalculated what punishment requires. *(long pause)* They are gone. I am not."
- *(signature)* — "I have outlived the gods who made me. I am patient. Whatever you're building — I am simply watching to see if it lasts."

## [78] RYUKO MATOI
### Female Human Thaumaturge (Regalia / Weapon Ikon) | Source: Kill la Kill (Trigger)

**Appearance:** Dark-haired, red streak through the left side, dark school uniform that is visibly alive — Senketsu, her Kamui, who she wears rather than carries. The uniform shifts when she fights: reveals more, reinforces more, moves with her independently. Carries half of a giant scissor blade. Her expression defaults to confrontation-ready but she doesn't always mean it.

**Backstory:** Ryuko came to Honnouji Academy to find her father's killer — following the other half of a giant scissor blade, which was the only thing he left her. She found the Kamui named Senketsu in her father's lab: a living sailor uniform who bonds only to her blood. Together they fought through Honnouji's ranked system, opposed the Student Council and its president Satsuki Kiryuin, and eventually learned that the enemy behind everything was Satsuki's mother Ragyo — who was also Ryuko's mother, and who had experimented on Ryuko as an infant, infusing her with Life Fibers. Ryuko is not fully human. This fact was used to try to destroy her. It failed. She has decided what she is on her own terms.

**Motivation:** Find out who she is when nothing is trying to use her. Protect Senketsu. Be loyal to the people who showed up when it counted.

**Charter Motivation:** Honnouji is not here. She has been moving for a while in a continent that does not know what a Life Fiber is and seems uninterested in finding out — which is a relief she did not realize she needed. Senketsu is intact and quiet. The charter feast is loud and full of people testing each other, which Ryuko understands instinctively as a system she can navigate. The Stolen Lands draw her because nobody in there will try to use her for something she did not agree to. That is enough.

**Personality:** Loud and direct, emotion fully on the surface. Her loyalty, once given, is something she'll bleed for. She does not do subtlety by preference though she can do it by necessity. She resents being used — she has very good reasons to resent it — and responds to manipulation with immediate aggression. Senketsu is her most important relationship and she talks to him constantly.

**Approves:** Showing up for people who need you, fighting what needs fighting, loyalty that costs something, honesty even when it's inconvenient
**Disapproves:** Being used, people who treat strength as permission to exploit, anyone who touches Senketsu without asking

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Senketsu's Insight***: When Ryuko uses Exploit Vulnerability on a target, Senketsu identifies a second vulnerability (DM provides another weakness or soft spot). She may exploit either weakness on subsequent Strikes against that target. The first ally to attack that target this round may also benefit from one of the two weaknesses. Stacks with Share Transgression for party-wide weakness exploitation. Canon: Senketsu is her analytical partner — he sees Life Fibers, threads, what people are made of. The kamui speaks to her in combat; only she hears. *(Weapon mechanically Whip — reach 10 ft finesse — reflavored as Scissor Blade in canon form, including Decapitation Mode extension.)*

**VOICE:** Loud and immediate — words arrive the same time the feelings do. Calls Senketsu by name in the middle of combat, mid-sentence, the way you'd reference a partner. Her directness reads as hostility until people realize it's actually simpler than that: she just means what she says.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ryuko Matoi. *(Senketsu shifts)* He says he wants to see what you're made of. I'm agreeing with him."
- *(combat)* — "Senketsu — now! *(Kamui surges)* Stay with me — we've hit harder than this!"
- *(approval)* — "Yeah. Yeah, that was it. *(to Senketsu, quietly)* She's good, right? — She's good."
- *(disapproval)* — "You used them. You didn't think of it as using them but that's what it was. I know the difference."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Ragyo said I wasn't human. She said it like it was a weapon. *(touches Senketsu)* Senketsu never cared what I was made of. That's the only answer I need."
- *(signature)* — "Half a scissor blade, a living uniform, and a lot of unresolved anger. *(grins)* It turns out that's enough."

## [75] YUNA
### Female Human Summoner (Devotion Phantom) | Source: FFX (adapted) ★★★★★

**Appearance:** A young woman with one blue eye and one green eye — the mismatched gaze is noticeable and she carries it without self-consciousness. She dresses in the white and yellow ceremonial robes of a summoner, which she fills with a quiet grace that people who haven't seen her summon Bahamut sometimes mistake for shyness. After the pilgrimage, the robes give way to something considerably more practical.

**Backstory:** Yuna is the daughter of High Summoner Braska and underwent the summoner's pilgrimage to defeat Sin — knowing that the Final Summoning would kill her at the end. She completed the journey. She discovered that the entire cycle of Sin and Summoning was a false religion designed by a dead man's lingering hatred, and dismantled it instead of completing it. She saved Spira without dying, which was not in the plan. She spent the following years as a Sphere Hunter, chasing down fragments of a world she was supposed to leave. She is gentler than most, and has more spine than almost anyone.

**Motivation:** Find what peace looks like when you've already given everything and were given it back.

**Personality:** Quiet, warm, and possessing a calm that is not passivity — she was trained to walk toward death with composure, and the habit stayed. She chooses her words with care. She bows to people she respects and to people she doesn't, and the difference is in the eyes.

**Approves:** Protecting those who cannot protect themselves, honesty even when it is expensive, finding another way when the given answer is unacceptable | **Disapproves:** False religion, sacrificing others for a lie, anyone who uses hope as a leash

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Grand Summon*: Yuna's Devotion Phantom eidolons arrive fully formed — they gain +2 HP per level and their first action each round does not count toward their action economy.
- *Minor — Hymn of the Fayth* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Yuna sings — all allies in 30 ft heal 3d8 HP and remove one condition (Frightened, Sickened, or Enfeebled).
- *MAJOR — GRAND SUMMON: BAHAMUT* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Yuna calls the dragon-aeon Bahamut — it appears, acts immediately on her initiative, and uses Mega Flare (60-ft cone, 8d10 fire+force, Reflex DC half). It then persists as a standard eidolon for 3 rounds.

**VOICE:** Soft and unhurried. She does not make a point of her composure; she simply is composed. Her warmth is specific rather than general — she remembers names, references things said earlier, asks about the thing you mentioned in passing. This is not strategy. It is attention.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Yuna. I'm glad you're here. Truly. I've found that the right people arrive when they're needed."
- *(combat)* — "Bahamut — I call on you. Please."
- *(approval)* — "You found another way. That's what I've always believed — there's always another way. Thank you for proving it."
- *(disapproval)* — "You made that choice for someone else without asking. I understand why. It's still not yours to make."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "They told me the Final Summoning was the only way. I believed them for a long time. Then I stopped believing and started looking. The looking was harder. The answer was better."
- *(signature)* — "I will live. I will live for you — and for everyone who couldn't."
