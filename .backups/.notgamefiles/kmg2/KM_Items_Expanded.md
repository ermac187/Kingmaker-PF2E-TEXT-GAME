## ☠️ CURSED ITEMS

> **DM:** Cursed items LOOK like ordinary magic items. Identify (Arcana/Occultism
> DC = item level + 5) may not reveal the curse on a simple success — Critical
> Success required to detect. Curses are removed by Remove Curse (caster level ≥
> item level), Wish, or a named in-world ritual (DM choice).
>
> **Placement:** Cursed items appear in loot ~1 in 8 magic drops when the chapter
> supports it (Ch3+ or haunted/evil locations). Not in friendly merchant stock.

## 📋 DM NOTES

**Drop rate guidance for loot rolls:**
- Spellhearts: 1 in 6 of magic drops L3+ (great caster loot)
- Eye slot: 1 in 10 of worn-item drops
- Cursed items: 1 in 8 of magic drops in haunted/evil locations (Ch3+)
- Utility magic: frequent — these are the "found in a chest, changes how players travel" items
- Expanded rods/staves: 1 in 12 of magic drops for L8+ loot

**Pricing is canon PF2e.** Player sell value = half listed price. Merchants in
KM_Loot_Merchants.md stock by merchant tier; rare items appear only at Tier 3+
merchants (Capital Caster's Tower, Varnhold after Ch3, Pitax Black Market).

**Cursed items NEVER appear in friendly merchant stock.** They are found in
tombs, haunted locations, evil NPCs' possessions, Nyrissa-tainted caches.

**Tone fit:** Every item here fits the Stolen Lands — no Numerian tech, no
modern weaponry, no aeon priest eldritch horror. Fey/druidic/wilderness/
noble-ceremonial themes dominate, matching the Kingmaker aesthetic.
