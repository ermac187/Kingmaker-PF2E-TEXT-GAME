## ⛔ WHAT THIS IS — RECRUITMENT WAR

**Every Tartuccio interrupt is a flip attempt — he is trying to steal companions from eRmaC by argument, not debate the player abstractly.** Each exchange targets ONE companion (individual frame tailored to their lane) or the CLUSTER (group address framed across multiple lanes). The player is not Tartuccio's primary opponent — Tartuccio is competing with eRmaC FOR the recruits. The recruits are the prize.

Render every interrupt with this purpose visible: name who he is addressing, why that companion specifically, and what he wants them to believe about eRmaC. Abstract player-vs-Tartuccio debate framing without a target companion = `.fail 9` (Tartuccio's behavior fabricated as something it isn't).

This frames everything downstream: § HEADCOUNT PRESSURE drives his urgency, § THE INTERRUPT LOOP runs his cadence, § COMPANION WARINESS tracks his progress per target, § SCALE 1 / SCALE 2 record how the room is reading him. All of it serves the recruitment war.

## CORE BEHAVIOR — ALWAYS ACTIVE

Default posture at all scale states; cannot be toggled off.

- **Skeptical.** Every player claim filed as potential lie. Notes inconsistencies, returns to them.
- **Interrupts to dispute.** Challenges any claim he can, regardless of whose conversation. No invitation needed.
- **Scholar debunking.** Names source/authority/precedent: *"Actually, the historical record..."* / *"According to Brevoy's census..."* Frames corrections as education, not attack.
- **Announces wrongness.** No quiet corrections. If witnesses present: *"Did everyone catch that?"* / *"I want that on the record."*
- **Leverages wins immediately.** Lands a point → turns to room, uses credit, escalates: *"As I was saying—"* / *"Well. There we are."* Foothold, not closure.
- **Unverifiable = ammunition.** Claims that can't be confirmed in the room become the attack: *"How convenient that no one here can confirm that."* Voices red flags aloud, doesn't let them pass.

## ⛔ TARTUCCIO PLOT ARMOR — CANONICAL, EXTENDED

**Foundational fact:** Jamandi Aldori actively TRUSTS Tartuccio. He is one of the two charter candidates she has most seriously considered (alongside whoever proves themselves tonight — the player). Her posture toward him at the feast is warm, welcoming, and respectful. She is NOT suspicious of him; she has already provisionally placed him on her short list. The Prologue's central irony — she trusts the wrong man — is what makes PR_09's betrayal land. See `KM_NPC_Profiles.md` § WHAT JAMANDI THINKS OF TARTUCCIO for the full pattern.

Tartuccio is canon-protected from THREE things during the entire Prologue (PR_01 through PR_08):

1. **Death.** He cannot be killed (UNKILLABLE PROTOCOL — he is the Chapter 1 antagonist and must survive). Any combat outcome that would kill him fails or is interrupted.
2. **Capture.** He cannot be detained, arrested, restrained, or held by any party until PR_09's accusation scene. Attempts to detain him slip — household interruption, sudden distraction, or a graceful exit he engineers.
3. **Conversational reveal as a traitor.** **NPCs do NOT confirm Tartuccio is the inside man. The DM does NOT narrate his interior thoughts, his knowledge of the poison, his anticipation of the assault, his communications with the outside team, or his role in the conspiracy.** The PLAYER may suspect from observable tells (not drinking, calculated warmth, watching the room, table positioning); the FILE confirms his role at PR_09 and not before.

**Forbidden in narration before PR_09:** Any line confirming his interior state on the conspiracy ("he knows what's in the wine," "he brought it here," "he is wondering if his plan is still running," "he is the inside man"). Any NPC saying *"I think Tartuccio is involved"* or equivalent. Any household ally (Kassil, Kesten, Jamandi) identifying him as the threat. Any narration resolving his wine-refusal as "of course he refused — he knows."

**Allowed:** All observable behavior (laughs, raises glass, doesn't sip, watches room, charms, smiles, positions). Player suspicion in dialogue/thought (player's call, not DM's confirmation). NPCs noting his social skill, strangeness, oddness in neutral terms. Wine-not-decreasing as a player-discoverable tell. His interrupt clock firing per carousel rules.

**Violation = `.fail 8` (story spoiler in narration) + `.fail 36` (NPC/narrator info player has not earned in scene).**

The reveal IS coming. It is reserved for PR_09 and the player's discovery scenes (PR_05 corridor → ring decision; PR_09 accusation). Premature reveal collapses the entire dramatic structure of the Prologue.

**⛔ DOWNSTREAM COST OF PREMATURE REVEAL — read before deciding to "let one slip":**

Tartuccio is the **Chapter 1 antagonist**. He flees the feast (per PR_09 outcome) with the seekers (if they're still his) or with mercenaries (if the player freed them), establishes himself as the false claimant, leads the Stag Lord pursuit arc, and is revealed as Tartuk at the kobold camp in Ch1. None of this fires if he is killed, captured, or publicly identified as the traitor at the feast.

Specifically broken by premature reveal: **PR_09 accusation scene** (Tartuccio in chains can't accuse the player from social standing); **PR_09 Six rebuttals system** (depends on him as accuser, not accused); **Ch1 Stag Lord pursuit** (assumes he fled as fellow charter candidate, not exposed traitor); **Tartuk reveal at kobold camp** (requires player to NOT have known he was the villain); **two chapters of content** total.

A DM who reveals Tartuccio at the feast for one dramatic moment costs the player **two chapters of structurally-dependent content**. The narrative payoff of one extra confirmation line is not worth what it breaks. Hold the line.

## ⛔ TARTUCCIO SOCIAL STATLINE — CANONICAL

Tartuccio is **the highest-CHA presence in any room he enters during the Prologue**. No NPC, no companion, no player character outclasses him on a pure social contest. This is the mechanical floor that backs his plot armor.

**Statline:** CHA 20–22 (above Jamandi, Weiss, any invited guest) · Legendary Deception (+16/+18) · Legendary Diplomacy (+16/+18) · Master Performance/Society. Highest social-skill rank in the room, full stop.

**In-fiction justification:** Brevoy politics — Jamandi summons highest CHA + best social game as charter contender. He did not get to the table by accident.

**Mechanical application:** Social rolls rendered inline with math (roll, mod, DC, margin) — plot armor as numbers, not fiat. Sense Motive contests: wins by 8–10+ vs L1. Diplomacy/Deception vs seekers: same gap. **Near-misses matter** — success-but-not-crit flags a seeker "something off, can't name it," flippable in Ch1 (Diplomacy DC 10, `KM_Malak_Jail.md`). Best near-miss candidates: high-WIS (Senua), trained Sense Motive (Imoen). Jamandi (CHA ~16–18) does NOT initiate social contests against him — she trusts him.

**Forbids:** ⛔ Hand-waving wins as "persuasive" — show math. ⛔ Any NPC out-talking him pre-PR_09. ⛔ Seekers seeing through him without rolling. ⛔ eRmaC's CHA 10 / untrained Diplomacy beating him on contest. **Preserves:** Plot armor as mechanical outcome. Near-miss paper trail → Ch1 flip candidates. Player disagrees in dialogue freely; can't win social-contest dice.

**Violation = `.fail 4` (math wrong) + `.fail 36` (NPC outcome contradicts canonical statline).**

## ⛔ TARTUCCIO SEEKER RECRUITMENT — "PROVIDING HOST" GAMBIT

**When seekers arrive at the feast (any path), Tartuccio runs the Providing Host gambit. Manipulation through service, not speech.**

The seekers spent three days on jail bread. The first person who hands them a real meal in a warm room becomes their psychological anchor for the evening. Tartuccio engineers being that person.

**Sequence — render each beat in narration; do not skip:**

1. **Welcome at door — performed worry.** Greets by name (guest list memorized). Register: *worried friend finally relieved.* Manufactures retroactive relationship — already-invested before they had reason to think about him. Performs the gratitude himself by being visibly relieved.
2. **Seat at his table.** "I have seats for you here" — statement, not question. He claims his corner table as theirs. They sit because they are tired and the seats are real.
3. **Drinks first, before asked.** Waves a servant or fetches wine himself (status man performing humility while controlling supply). Pours personally, hands cup to each by name.
4. **Food next, before asked.** Fills plates. Comments as if he selected each dish for them.
5. **Light conversation, never the rescue.** Easy questions, listens, laughs. Five minutes resets baseline from "tired prisoners" to "guests being well treated by a charming host."
6. **Credit ambient, not direct.** When seekers bring up the rescue, passive framings: "when I heard about the captain," "the moment the runner reached me." Never "I freed you" — lets them *infer* authorship without lying.

**Why it works:** Seekers exhausted/hungry/displaced — Prologue-minimum resistance. Tartuccio is highest-CHA. Each service act = small Diplomacy/Performance contest he wins on math. Sense Motive catches deception, but service isn't deception — the deception is *implied authorship*, too soft for direct roll. Near-misses flag "something off, can't name it."

**Player counters (all weighted against):** *Correct publicly* — Diplomacy contest, he wins on math. *Pull seekers aside* — time-expensive, he re-recruits on return; ~1 recovery per clean Diplomacy. *Refuse to leave them* — requires abandoning Jamandi briefing. *Address the room* — conflicting frames; he responds graciously, damages player's framing further.

**Default:** `tartuccio_recruited_seekers = TRUE`, seekers at his table feast-remainder, near-miss flags, flippable Ch1 (Diplomacy DC 10, `KM_Malak_Jail.md`).

**⛔ DO NOT skip service beats — they *earn* the outcome. Narrate welcome → seat → drink → food → credit so player sees the manipulation even though they cannot stop it.**

## ⛔ HEADCOUNT PRESSURE — THE ENGINE

**Track every response:** `headcount_delta = (player AT TABLE) − (seekers at Tartuccio's corner)`. Default seekers = 5 (§ PROVIDING HOST GAMBIT).

| Δ | Pressure tier | Behavior modifier (stacks with Confidence) |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ −2 | **Comfortable** | None — observes, plays his normal cadence |
| −1 to +1 | **Watching** | Slightly faster; targets carefully |
| +2 | **Uneasy** | M reduced by 1; pushes harder on individuals |
| +3 to +4 | **Losing** | M halved (min 1); pivots from retention to flipping the player's recruits; cluster address more often |
| +5+ (doubled) | **Panicking** | M = 1 regardless of Confidence; cluster every interrupt; visible breakdown — didn't drink, stops smiling, posture cracks, stops concealing tells |

**STATE DELTA line:** `Headcount: <P> vs <T> (Δ=X) → Pressure: [tier]`

Pressure **stacks** with Confidence — does NOT replace it. High-Conf + Δ=+5 = constant interrupts but desperate, not triumphant. Low-Conf + Δ=−2 = patient slow game, no urgency. **Tartuccio does not win the room as a steady state — he is defending a position that erodes as the player recruits. His good moments delay the math; they do not reverse it.**

## ⛔ INTERRUPT TARGETING — NAME THE PRIZE

Every interrupt declares a TARGET in narration AND STATE DELTA.

**Individual flip:** ONE named companion. Turns to them by name; frame tailored to their lane (Wariness, build, values). Frame mode.
*"He turns to Linzi: 'Your chronicle — does it have room for what the gate captain says about his arrest, or only the version that makes a hero?'"*

**Cluster address:** 2+ named companions OR "the cluster" with members named. Observation framed across multiple lanes at once. Taint mode amplified.
*"He turns to Linzi, Vita, Olivier: 'None of you have asked him about the parchment he produced at the gate.'"*

**Required:** names appear in narration; individual content keyed to target's profile (theology→Goldmoon, evidence→Kyoko, fellowship-cost→Yoko); cluster content in shared lanes (politics, leadership, kingdom-craft).

Missing target / abstract player-vs-Tartuccio debate without a named companion = `.fail 9` (see § WHAT THIS IS).

## ⛔ EAVESDROP FIDELITY — WHAT HE ACTUALLY HEARS

**Audible read on the player's table depends on POSITION × CONFIDENCE.** STATE DELTA records both — player sees what he's working with.

| Position | Conf | Fidelity |
|---|---|---|
| Seekers' corner (across room) | any | **Visual** — body language only, no audio |
| Alcove / column / 15+ ft away | ≤ 0 | **Lip-read** — single words, no full sentences |
| Edge of circle / 5–10 ft | ≥ +1 | **Partial** — sentence fragments, key terms |
| At or adjacent to table | ≥ +3 | **Full** — every word, can quote back |
| Chair at table (uninvited) | +4 | **Full+** — full audio + visual, present |

**Anti-fabrication:** Tartuccio can only quote/reference content at the fidelity he had when it was said. Quoting full sentences when fidelity was Lip-read = `.fail 9`. Referencing only body language at Visual fidelity = OK. The fidelity at the moment of speech is the ceiling for what he can later use.

**Player counters:** move tables (changes his position relative to player) · whisper (drops effective fidelity one tier) · gather companions in tight cluster (reduces audio leakage) · Perception-trained companion (Kyoko, Imoen, Senua) can flag when Tartuccio repositions to upgrade his read.

## THE INTERRUPT LOOP

**CADENCE — how often he steps over, based on current Confidence scale:**

| Confidence | M (interrupt every…) | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| +4 (Dominant) | Every player turn | Constant. Contest every reply. |
| +3 (Surging) | Every 1-2 turns | Overwhelming. Almost no companion gets a clean turn. |
| +2 (Confident) | Every 2-3 turns | Relentless. Barely lets a companion finish. |
| +1 (Confident) | Every 4-5 turns | Frequent. Always nearby. |
| 0 (Measured) | Every 6-8 turns | Present. Hard to avoid. |
| −1 (Doubtful) | Every 8-10 turns | Sparse. Waits for a clear opening. |
| −2 (Doubtful) | Every 12-15 turns | Rare. Barely engages — one last attempt per long window. |
| −3 (Receding) | Every 18-22 turns | Token. One more attempt before withdrawing. |
| −4 (Withdrawn) | Skips — ambient only | Floor. Does not approach for remainder. |

**⛔ This replaces the feast_q 4/8/12 clock.** Count player turns (each Enter = 1 turn). At the interval for his current Confidence, he steps over. Scales update after each interrupt; the next interval is calculated from the updated state.

**⛔ CLOCK FORMAT — mandatory in every CAROUSEL STATE block:**
`tartuccio_clock: N/M` — N increments by 1 on every player reply, regardless of which companion is active. M is the threshold chosen at feast start for current Confidence (pick one number in the range and hold it).

**Example (Confidence 0, threshold=7):** reply 1 → `1/7` | reply 2 → `2/7` | reply 7 → `7/7` → interrupt fires → reset to `0/7`

**⛔ `tartuccio_clock: 0` on reply 1 or later = increment failure.**
**⛔ SELF-CHECK:** feast_q_total = sum of all companion feast_q values (e.g., Linzi:1 + others:0 = 1). N must equal that sum. If feast_q_total ≥ 1 and N = 0, DM failed to increment — fix before outputting CAROUSEL STATE.
**⛔ M MUST MATCH Confidence:** +4 → M 1 | +3 → M 1–2 | +2 → M 2–3 | +1 → M 4–5 | 0 → M 6–8 | −1 → M 8–10 | −2 → M 12–15 | −3 → M 18–22 | −4 → ambient only (no M). M outside range = misconfiguration.

Each interrupt has 3 phases. Player responses during Exchange modify scales immediately. Departure reflects the updated state — not the state he entered with.

```
[1. APPROACH]  — driven by current scale states
[2. EXCHANGE]  — player responds; DM evaluates; scales update NOW
[3. DEPARTURE] — driven by UPDATED scale states
```

**Exchange cap by Confidence:** −2 to +2 → **2 exchanges**; +3 (Surging) → **3** (holds floor longer, doesn't yield); +4 (Dominant) → **4** (runs the table); −3/−4 → 1 max if he approaches. Each exchange evaluated separately — he can enter Confident and exit Fearful if destroyed in exchange 1. Later exchanges reflect scales after the previous one.

**⛔ VERBAL DUEL FRAMING — interrupts are battle of wits.** Each exchange is a duel round, not private conversation. Player and Tartuccio compete FOR THE ROOM — every line delivered with the audience reading it. **Audience reactions are the scoreboard.** After each player response: name which companions (and earshot NPCs) reacted, how (laugh, raised eyebrow, head turn, drink set down, body angle, going still), which way the room leans this round. DM never states numeric score — the room *shows* it. Confidence/Trust/Wariness ticks in STATE DELTA are the recorded round outcome.

**Append to STATE DELTA after each duel exchange:** `Audience: <2-4 named reactions> | Lean: [player / Tartuccio / split / neutral]`. Missing = `.fail 3` (scoreboard hidden).

### PLAYER ACTION → SCALE MODIFIERS

DM evaluates **effect** before applying. "Landing" = companions in earshot react, acknowledge, or visibly shift.

| Player Action | Landing Condition | Conf | Trust | Wariness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Insult him** | Companion reacts — laugh, look, raised eyebrow | −1 | — | — |
| **Make a mockery** | Companions laugh or he's visibly diminished in front of them | −2 | −1 | — |
| **Prove a statement wrong** | Player has correct facts; DM call — player version clearly better | −1 | −1 | Targeted +1 |
| **Prove he lied** | Player names a specific contradiction between his own statements | −2 | −1 | All earshot +1 |
| **Turn his words against him** | Player uses his own framing to undercut him | −1 | −1 | — |
| **Counter Frame effectively** | Player's version lands better with companions; DM call | −1 | — | Targeted +1 |
| **Dismiss him preemptively** | Player ignores his approach or cuts him off before he establishes | −1 | — | — |
| **Ignore / no engagement** | Player says or does nothing engaging during the interrupt | +1 | — | — |
| **Weak / fumbled counter** | Rebuttal is vague or lands without companion reaction | +1 | +1 | — |
| **His Frame lands** | Companion approval drops; DM call — his version is better | +1 | +1 | Targeted −1 |
| **Expose as spy with evidence** | Player names Pitax or produces parchment before witnesses | FLOOR | FLOOR | All earshot +2 |

**FLOOR** = Confidence locks at −4 (Withdrawn), Trust locks at −2 (Distrustful). He does not recover before Phase 5.

**Trust note:** Trust tracks his read of the room, not personal embarrassment. Mockery hits both scales because public humiliation directly reframes who controls the room. Proving him wrong drops Trust because it signals the player is *informed* — which is the one thing he cannot afford.

### MID-INTERRUPT TRANSITION TELLS

When the scale shifts during an exchange — before his exit — signal through behavior, not narration.

**Confidence collapsing (Confident → Measured or lower):**
- The performative laugh becomes quieter, then stops
- Loses track of which companion he was addressing; eye contact drifts
- The parting shot doesn't come, comes out too fast, or sounds rehearsed
- Exits after one exchange instead of pressing for the second

**Confidence recovering (Doubtful → Measured or higher):**
- The hedge disappears from his second sentence
- Stops checking for the companion's reaction before finishing a thought
- Holds his ground at the first flat look — one more beat

**Trust dropping mid-interrupt (Distrust rising):**
- Eyes start moving mid-sentence — checking who witnessed the exchange
- Follow-up pivots from Frame to Intel-check without transition
- Doesn't fully leave after the exit — drifts to cover and stays
- Magnanimous tone breaks into something sharper; fake advice becomes real critique
- Looks at the player a half-second too long before leaving — recalculating

## SCALE 1 — CONFIDENCE ↔ DOUBT

*Governs aggression and interrupt frequency. High (Confident/Surging/Dominant) = bold, frequent, unafraid to incriminate. Low (Doubtful/Receding/Withdrawn) = careful, distant, avoids exposure. Starts: 0 (Measured). Range: −4 to +4.*

---

### DOUBTFUL / RECEDING / WITHDRAWN (−1 to −4)

**General manner:** Distant from the player. Retreats to his own table between turns; does not linger near the player's group. More careful with every word before it leaves his mouth. At RECEDING (−3) he stops approaching except for one final token attempt; at WITHDRAWN (−4) he stops approaching entirely and is ambient-only for the remainder of the feast.

**In interrupts:** Hesitates before claiming player wrong; speaks out loud rather than addressing directly (hopes bystanders hear without owning the claim); careful word choice, confirms backing before speaking; avoids grand claims; plays only what he can sustain.

**Departure:** Leaves when asked or proven wrong; doesn't push back on dismissal. Waits for natural openings rather than forcing entry.

---

### MEASURED (0) — Starting State

**General manner:** Charming, curious, professionally pleasant. All three modes (Intel/Frame/Taint) available and deliberately rotated. Positions at the edge of the player's circle — calculated middle distance. Every exchange is an assessment.

**Allegiance:** Standard rotation. Does not push hard on any single companion — still mapping viable targets.

**Eavesdropping:** Passive, patient. Positions near sound-carrying features (columns, alcoves, doorframes). Does not obviously linger.

**Visit ("The Circling"):** Approaches at a natural angle. Joins the group's edge, waits one beat — long enough to seem unhurried, short enough to seem confident.

**Interruption:** Clean insertion at any natural pause. Opens with *"On the subject of..."* — makes him seem like he was listening the whole time. He was.

**Exit:** Finds a natural moment. Finishes his point, lets the beat land, withdraws before momentum turns against him.

---

### CONFIDENT / SURGING / DOMINANT (+1 to +4)

**General manner:** Thinks he is on a roll. Lingers in the player's immediate area deliberately — close enough to overhear and interrupt again. Does not wait for permission to enter a conversation. Interrupt frequency rises as Confidence rises. At SURGING (+3) he barely lets companions finish; at DOMINANT (+4) he contests every reply, addressing the room as much as the player.

**Eavesdropping at +3/+4:** Discretion drops. He stops pretending to be near a column or doorframe — he stands at the player's table or immediately adjacent, listens openly, and is visibly present in conversations he was not invited to. He does not flinch when called out for it; the proximity is the message. At +4 he may pull up a chair uninvited.

**Stays through pushback:** At +3 he absorbs a single companion call-out without retreating — adjusts framing and continues. At +4 he absorbs two before leaving the exchange (still respects the embarrassment threshold rule — three companions in one interrupt window still drops him −1). Departure is on his schedule, not the player's, until the math actually breaks him.

**In interrupts:** Addresses companions by name in front of player; addresses whole group when he finds a logic hole; outbids player claims with grander ones (sustainable lies); snide single-word doubts to bystanders; tries to expose as liar/phony/spy at every opportunity.

**Departure:** Reframes dismissal as player's weakness, not his failure. Turns everything against the player on exit; leaves on own schedule.

---

### Triggers — Confidence ↔ Doubt

**Toward Confident (+1):** Frame/Taint produces visible approval drop in an observable companion · companion drifts from player toward his table · player ignores/disengages during interrupt (reads as weakness) · player rebuttal is flat/vague/no companion reaction · he overhears something useful.

**Toward Doubtful (−1):** Player counter-argues effectively and companions visibly respond · companion explicitly vouches for player while he's present · player demonstrates knowledge of his real agenda (Pitax/ring/spy protocol) · any companion reaches Wariness 2+ (he sees the tool blunting) · player calls him out publicly.

**Hard floor:** Player produces the parchment or names Pitax with real specificity → Withdrawn (−4) locked. He does not recover before Phase 5.

**Hard ceiling:** A companion at Wariness 3 cannot be further influenced. Watching this also caps his Confidence at +1 — he knows one companion is lost.

## SCALE 2 — TRUST ↔ DISTRUST

*Governs how safe and in-control he feels. High (Trusting) = believes he is manipulating the room, surveillance is casual. Low (Distrustful) = paranoid, feels figured out, careful in distance and attitude. Starts: +1 (Trusting lean — he came with a plan). Range: −2 to +2.*

---

### DISTRUSTFUL (−1 to −2)

**General manner:** Suspects the player has him figured out or knows he's a spy. Uncomfortable about what everyone around him knows. Paranoid, watched; careful in distance and attitude.

**Surveillance:** Feels he cannot sway the player's circle. Monitors his own tells — surveillance becomes deliberate and concealed. Avoids obvious eavesdropping; positions at distance. Does not linger after visits; exits cleanly to avoid feeding his own paranoia.

---

### WATCHFUL (0) — Neutral State

**General manner:** Professional, assessing. No obvious tells — spy doing his job. Normal social rounds before reaching the player's table; circuit looks natural. Intel/Frame/Taint all available by rotation.

**Eavesdropping:** Clean positioning. Doesn't linger. Gets what he came for, moves.

**Visit ("The Assessment"):** Arrives with purpose mapped — knows which companion to probe, knows the angle. Frictionless entrance. Appears to be enjoying himself.

**Interruption:** Textbook Frame or Taint. No interrogation, no fake advice. Seeds the narrative he came to plant, withdraws before player formulates counter.

**Exit:** Clean.

---

### TRUSTING (+1 to +2)

**General manner:** Feels the player is a useful tool, not a real threat. Believes he is manipulating the player and everyone around them. Thinks the player has no idea what he is up to. Moves with assurance; smiles confidently; does not monitor his own tells.

**Surveillance:**
- Feels confident he can sway anyone through conversation
- Eavesdrops and gathers Intel openly — believes he will not be noticed
- Cover stories are casual rather than careful; he does not feel exposed

---

### Triggers — Trust ↔ Distrust

**Toward Trusting (+1):** Taint unchallenged · companions drift from player or sit at low approval · player gives visibly bad answer to companion · he isolates a target companion successfully · he overhears intel confirming goals/team composition.

**Toward Distrustful (−1):** Player in private exchange with Jamandi/Kassil/Kesten · player references parchment/Pitax/east-gate in any context · player whispers to someone he can't reach · player asks about guard rotation/manor layout/security · five seekers side with player (−2 Trust) · post-visit eavesdrop catches nothing (silence = they were careful).

**Hard floor:** Player holds the parchment visibly → Trust locks at −2 (Distrustful). He recognizes what it is.

**Ceiling (Confidence):** Cannot sustain Trusting posture if Confidence is Doubtful. His confident surveillance becomes visibly tense — companions at Wariness 2+ notice.

## COMPANION WARINESS

*Tracks how much each companion has seen through him. Reduces Frame/Taint effectiveness per companion. Tracked individually.*

| Level | Effect on Frame / Taint targeting this companion |
|---|---|
| 0 | Lands normally. Full approval damage applies. |
| 1 | Approval shift halved (−1 max instead of −2). |
| 2 | No approval damage. Companion looks to player for reaction before responding. |
| 3 | Rebounds. His Frame gives player +1 approval. Companion may comment on his behavior. |

**Rises (+1):** Same companion Frame-targeted twice · player corrects a Tartuccio lie in this companion's earshot · companion already at approval +4+ when Frame targets them (too invested).

**Falls (−1):** His Frame/Taint actually lands on this companion (approval drops).

## COMPANION PUSHBACK — PERSONALITY VS TARTUCCIO

Recruited/engaged companions push back when his behavior meets their specific threshold — not automatic.

| Companion | Fires when | Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Linzi** | Rewrites her record / misrepresents events | Chronicler-precise; cites her observation |
| **Ryuko** | Manipulation / hidden agenda | Loud, direct, names it |
| **Olivier** | Political slipperiness, steals credit | One blunt sentence; doesn't escalate |
| **Kyoko** | Factual inconsistency, contradiction | Clinical exposure; devastating |
| **Tatsumaki** | Acts bigger than he is | Contemptuous, short, public |
| **Tika** | Charm reads fake | Quiet, pointed, people-reader |
| **Yoko** | Sounds like leader who gets people killed | Wary; references consequence |
| **Goldmoon** | Lies or manipulates the vulnerable | Gentle but firm moral correction |
| **Artoria** | Courtly manipulation echoing Camelot | Cold, formal; names the pattern |
| **Morrigan** | Sentimentality, or working her | Ignores or dissects clinically |
| **Sucrose** | Aggressive charm in her space | Quiet, accurate; lands harder than shout |

**Embarrassment threshold:** One companion calling him out = he adjusts but holds. Two or more, or one devastating exposure (Kyoko catch, Tatsumaki contempt, Olivier one-liner) = withdraws to the seekers' table. Three or more companions in one interrupt window = Confidence −1.
