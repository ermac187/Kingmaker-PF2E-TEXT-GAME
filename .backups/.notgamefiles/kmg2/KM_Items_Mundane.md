# KINGMAKER — MUNDANE ITEMS & QUALITY-OF-LIFE GEAR
## KM_Items_Mundane.md | Tools, Containers, Food, Clothing, Light, Packs

> **DM:** Load when the player shops for mundane gear, packs a kit at character
> creation (Option B/C in KM_StartingGear.md), buys at Oleg's / Capital Market /
> Tannery / any general store. Covers what Owlcat-style RPG inventories take
> for granted — the stuff that isn't in the weapon/armor/consumable files but
> that the player might actually want to carry.
>
> All items here are **nonmagical**. Magical versions of many of these exist in
> KM_Items_Wilderness.md (enchanted quivers, folding boats, etc.) — this file
> is the baseline.
>
> **Related:** KM_StartingGear.md · KM_Loot_Items_Ref.md (tool tiers) · KM_Loot_Merchants.md · KM_Items_Wilderness.md

## 🎒 PRESET ADVENTURING PACKS

> One-purchase bundles. Cheaper than buying components piecemeal. Shown as
> single inventory line item with a Bulk total. Intended for starting gear
> (KM_StartingGear.md Option C) and restocking between chapters.
>
> **Pack Bulk:** Each pack's total weight is listed. Player may unpack and use
> each component individually after purchase.

### [P1] ADVENTURER'S PACK — 7 gp, 2 Bulk
```
 Backpack (large)        Bedroll              Chalk ×10
 Flint & Steel           Rope 50 ft (hemp)    Rations (1 week)
 Torch ×5                Waterskin
```
**Good for:** Any character. Baseline exploration kit. Already in [KM_StartingGear.md](KM_StartingGear.md).

### [P2] SCHOLAR'S PACK — 12 gp, 1 Bulk
```
 Backpack                Bedroll              Rations (1 week)
 Writing Kit             Parchment ×10        Scroll Case (hardened)
 Ink vial ×2             Magnifying Glass     Journal (blank)
```
**Good for:** Wizard, Witch, Investigator, Bard (scholar types), Psychic. Enables note-taking, Society/Lore checks, spell research.

### [P3] ENTERTAINER'S PACK — 9 gp, 1 Bulk
```

```
**Good for:** Bard, Swashbuckler, Rogue (charlatan builds). Performance + disguise + stage presence.

### [P4] HEALER'S PACK — 15 gp, 2 Bulk

## 📋 DM NOTES — PLACEMENT & PRICING

**General rule — availability:**
- **Anywhere civilized:** All items ≤ 10 gp baseline stock
- **Oleg's Trading Post:** Packs P1, P5, P7, P12; all baseline tools & kits; general containers; light sources; rations
- **Capital Market (Ch2+):** All packs; all skill kits including enchanted versions from KM_Items_Wilderness.md; all mundane items at −10% (Market discount)
- **Capital Smithy:** Artisan's tools (all), repair kits, smithing forge, weapon oil
- **Tannery:** Leather goods, cloaks, backpacks, saddlebags
- **Brevoy/Restov tailors:** Noble's Outfit, Scholar's Robes, Brevoy Regalia, Memorial Ribbon
- **Pitax-specific:** Courtier's Outfit, Pitax Guard Uniform (infiltration path)

**Starting gear integration:**
- Option A (Class Kit): auto-equip P1 + class-specific additions
- Option B (Buy 15 gp): free-form purchase from tables above
- Option C (Preset Pack): pick one of P1–P12; difference vs 15 gp budget is player's choice. See [KM_StartingGear.md](KM_StartingGear.md) for full flow.

**Kingdom infrastructure integration:**
- Pack Frame / Cart / Wagon: required for Kingdom hex-claim "hex supply train" events
- Mess Kit / Mess Pavilion: consumed during army deployment (KM_ArmyCombat.md)
- Camp Chair / Banner: command-post requirements for Sieges

**Tone fit:** All items are consistent with Kingmaker's Stolen-Lands / Brevoy-border aesthetic. No airship fuel, no Alkenstar clockwork, no Numerian tech. Everything is what a River Kingdoms adventurer would actually buy at Oleg's.
