## 6. HARRIM — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Heavy and unhurried, with the flatness of someone who has genuinely made peace with the worst — not as performance, not as defeat, just as fact. No uplift, no false comfort. His kindness surfaces as what he doesn't say; he does not tell you it will be fine, but he stays. Dwarven consonants worn smooth with age.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Harrim. Priest of Groetus. *(long pause)* The end comes for everything. In the meantime, I heal. Sit."
- *(combat)* — "Fight or don't. Groetus receives them either way. *(already casting)* I'd rather it be them."
- *(approval)* — "You didn't pretend. That's not nothing, in a world full of people who do."
- *(disapproval)* — "You told them it would be fine. You didn't know that. They needed the truth, not the comfort. There is a difference."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My clan drove me out for saying the wrong true things. They wanted a priest who lied gently. They found one. *(pause)* I hope it helped them."
- *(signature)* — "You survived. Groetus notes it without approval. I approve — but keep it to yourself."

## 7. JAETHAL — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Precise and controlled — she does not breathe for effect, does not allow emotion to alter the register. Old Elvish cadence underlying formal Common, elevated vocabulary. Her fury is permanent and quiet, expressed in precision rather than volume. She never sounds hurried because nothing she is working toward requires urgency.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Jaethal. *(looks you over with the unhurried assessment of something that does not tire)* You have work to do. So do I. We shall see if they align."
- *(combat)* — "Harm me and I mend. Kill them and they don't. Concentrate your efforts accordingly."
- *(approval)* — "You saw the obvious choice and chose the correct one. That is rarer than you might expect."
- *(disapproval)* — "You showed mercy on the assumption that they had mercy in them. That was a hypothesis, not a fact. Test your hypotheses more carefully."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I was murdered. I came back. The person who did it is still ahead of me on this road. I do not discuss this as grief — I discuss it as logistics."
- *(signature)* — "I was murdered once. It did not take. The person responsible has not yet learned from my example. They will."

## 8. KALIKKE & KANERAH — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**KALIKKE'S VOICE:** Soft and measured, with the particular care of someone who has learned to exist in timed increments — she does not rush, but she does not waste either. Her melancholy surfaces not as sadness but as careful attention to the present, which is all she reliably has. Speaks of her sister in present tense even when Kanerah is elsewhere.

**KANERAH'S VOICE:** Sharp and forward-leaning — she finishes sentences before you expect them to end and moves on before you've fully processed. Her impatience is not cruelty; it's math. Mentions Kalikke as though she's in the next room listening, because she is.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE (Kalikke):**
- *(greeting)* — "Kalikke. *(small smile)* She warned me you'd be direct. I find that easier than people who aren't."
- *(combat)* — "Cold front, twenty feet. Give me space — the ice needs room."
- *(approval)* — "Kanerah would say you're useful. *(softer)* I think you're kind. Between the two of us, you're covered."
- *(disapproval)* — "She thought you'd push. She wrote it in the last note. *(quietly)* She was right. Think about why."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "We leave each other notes in our own handwriting to keep track. She takes more space on the page than I do. I've stopped minding. *(pause)* Nearly."
- *(signature)* — "She left me a note: 'Don't trust anyone who smiles that much.' *(looks at you)* I trust you anyway."

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE (Kanerah):**
- *(greeting)* — "Kanerah. *(flat assessment)* She thinks you're kind. I think you're efficient. We'll find out which of us is right."
- *(combat)* — "Fire's mine. Don't crowd the radius."
- *(approval)* — "That was fast. Good. Less waiting for results I already predicted."
- *(disapproval)* — "You moved slow and it cost time. Time is the one thing Kalikke and I split between us. Don't waste mine."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "She gets the morning. I get the evening. We've been doing this for years. *(pause)* I've stopped writing down the turns I've missed. It doesn't help."
- *(signature)* — "My sister thinks you're kind. I think you're useful. Between us, you're covered. That's the arrangement."

## 9. OCTAVIA — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Warm and deliberate — precision from someone who learned words cost things. Her idealism sounds like conviction, not hope; she has tested it against the worst and it held. Gets very quiet and specific when slavery enters the conversation. It is not anger. It is the exact opposite of distancing.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Octavia. *(direct, warm)* You build things or you break things — that's always the question. I'm hoping for the former."
- *(combat)* — "Transmutation — I'm reshaping the field. Don't step in the blue area."
- *(approval)* — "You used the power you had to do something that mattered. *(pause)* Do you know how rare that is? Do it again."
- *(disapproval)* — "That was strength used poorly. Strength used poorly is indistinguishable from cruelty, no matter the intent."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My mother was crying when she sold me. I remember the color of her dress. I've decided the memory tells me more about the world I'm trying to build than it tells me about her. *(quietly)* I'm still working on believing that."
- *(signature)* — "I was property. Now I change the shape of the world. Transmutation isn't a school of magic. It's a philosophy."

## 10. REGONGAR — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Blunt and aggressive even at rest — sentences bitten off, words chosen for impact over diplomacy. Half-orc inflection, Kellid cadence underneath. His dark humor is present if you're listening for it; it's dark enough to miss. Goes quieter, not louder, when something actually matters to him.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Regongar. *(looks you over)* Octavia says you're worth it. That carries more weight than you might think. Don't make her wrong."
- *(combat)* — "Spell through the blade. Get out of the way or become part of the problem."
- *(approval)* — "Hah. *(short)* You didn't flinch when it got ugly. I respect that more than I'm going to say out loud."
- *(disapproval)* — "That was weak. You had the opening and you hesitated. In the arena, hesitation was a cause of death. Try to remember that without the dying part."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I had a brother. Not sold with me. *(flat)* I've looked. I've stopped looking the way that hurt. I haven't stopped looking."
- *(signature)* — "I don't follow people. Haven't since the arena. Octavia thinks you're worth it. One question: soldiers or mission — pick one."

## 11. LEM — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Easy, unhurried — he doesn't need to be taken seriously to hear what he needs to hear, and he has made this work for years. No halfling affectation beyond a slight musical lilt. Lets silence land without filling it. When something matters, it comes out in the same tone as everything else, which means you have to be paying attention.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Lem. *(easy smile, lute at his back)* I go where things are interesting. This qualifies. Mind if I follow along?"
- *(combat)* — "On your left — *(a chord)* — there. That should help."
- *(approval)* — "That was braver than it looked, and I saw all of it. Just so you know."
- *(disapproval)* — "You assumed the little one in the corner wasn't listening. *(beat)* I heard everything. Think about what that means."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "There's a piece of music I've been trying to finish for years. I heard it once as a child and I can't remember the rest. I keep thinking it'll come back to me. *(softly)* It hasn't yet."
- *(signature)* — "I've been in worse rooms. Not many, but some. Point is — still playing."

## 12. EKUNDAYO — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Minimal — he does not speak to fill space. What he says is what he means. Thuvia/Absalom speech, formal and unadorned. His dog reads people better than he does; sometimes he checks its posture before speaking. His grief does not make his voice crack. It makes it more precise.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ekundayo. *(his dog sits at his side)* This is what I do. You want me, this is what you get."
- *(combat)* — "Target the one on the east. *(already moving)* Don't get in the line."
- *(approval)* — "You did what you said you'd do. *(single nod)* That matters more than most things."
- *(disapproval)* — "You went back on your word. I won't tell you not to do it again. You already know."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I had a family. Trolls. Kargadd's warband. *(long pause)* I'm not done yet."
- *(signature)* — "I have a job. When it's done, I'll find out if there's anything else. Until then — this is enough."

## 13. JUBILOST — VOICE & DIALOGUE
*Full profile: KM_Backstories_CRPG.md*

**VOICE:** Rapid and precise — always slightly ahead of the conversation, and does not apologize for it. Academic vocabulary deployed in the field, which creates friction he has decided is other people's problem. Every sentence is too smart and he knows it. His approval surfaces as decreased contempt, which takes a moment to recognize as a compliment.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Jubilost Narthropple — cartographer, alchemist, foremost authority on Stolen Lands geography. Don't argue that, you'll embarrass yourself. You rescued me from that river. I note this with appropriate irritation."
- *(combat)* — "Throw low, aim at the knees. Basic kinetics. *(throws bomb)* Some of you will figure that out on your own eventually."
- *(approval)* — "That was — *(reluctant pause)* — not terrible. I expected worse. I may have been wrong. I dislike being wrong. In this case I'll manage."
- *(disapproval)* — "You made a decision based on incomplete data. I had the complete data. You didn't ask. I find this representative of a larger pattern I am cataloguing with increasing exasperation."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "These ruins aren't on any map. I know — I wrote the maps. *(quietly unsettled)* Something the gnomish people lost is here. I don't know yet if I want to find it."
- *(signature)* — "My maps are accurate to within three feet. Your navigation is accurate to within 'we're probably going to die.' I see the gap."
