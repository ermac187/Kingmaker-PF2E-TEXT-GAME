## 🗣️ COMPANION BANTER PAIRS

> **DM:** Add to banter rotation. One exchange per rest or travel scene.

**SEELAH ↔ TRISTIAN:** After mercy: *"She would be pleased."* / *"She would ask if we could have done more."* / *"Yes."* Neither says which she.

**AMIRI ↔ REGILL:** After a battle: *"You counted steps."* / *"Every one."* / *"Doesn't it slow you down?"* Regill doesn't answer. Amiri decides that's an answer.

**NOK-NOK ↔ ARUESHALAE:** Nok-Nok squints at her wings. *"You used to be scary?"* / *"Yes."* / *"What happened?"* Long pause. *"Someone believed in me."* Nok-Nok nods solemnly. *"Worst thing that can happen."*

**VALERIE ↔ REGILL:** Comparing formation doctrine at camp. They agree on three points. Neither will say so.

**EKUNDAYO ↔ ARUESHALAE:** On the same track, from opposite sides. Neither noticed the other first. This happens more than either admits.

**KALIKKE ↔ KANERAH (internal):** *"That was unnecessary."* / *"That was efficient."* / *"You burned the bridge."* / *"We didn't need the bridge."* / *"We might have needed the bridge."*

**DAERAN ↔ EMBER:** Daeran compliments her hexes. She knows he's mocking her. He isn't. She doesn't know what to do with that.

**LINZI (alone):** Scribbling, muttering: *"No, that's wrong — she said it better than that — where was I —"* She rewrites the same passage four times. The fifth version is perfect.
