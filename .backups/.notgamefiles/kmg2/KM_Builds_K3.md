## BUILD — ANIMAL EIDOLON + RANGER (COMPANION SYNERGY)
**Human (Natural Ambition) | Summoner (Beast Eidolon — Wolf form) + Ranger Archetype | Background: Hunter**
*Beast eidolon in animal form + Ranger Dedication Shared Prey. Both summoner and eidolon target the same prey at +2 damage. Companion synergy — two hunters, one prey, maximum focus fire.*

| Mod | +0 | +2 | +2 | +1 | +1 | +4 |

**Weapon:** Staff (1d4 B, 2-handed) | **Eidolon:** Phantom Touch 1d8+5 (ghost-touch)

| 8 | Fetchling | Resolute | Resolute Fetchling's Will save bonus is highest priority for a summoner who stays at range — enemies that bypass the eidolon target the summoner's Will first. |

## BUILD — UTILITY SUMMONER (FLEXIBLE PET CONTROL)
**Gnome (Fey-Touched) | Summoner (Fey Eidolon) | Background: Herbalist**
*No dedication — every feat enhances eidolon flexibility. Fey eidolon with illusion and charm focus. Skill coverage + social + battlefield control. Utility over raw damage.*

|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 10 | 14 | 14 | 12 | 14 | 18 |
| Mod | +0 | +2 | +2 | +1 | +2 | +4 |

**HP** 18 (shared) | **AC** 15 | **Fort** +6 | **Ref** +5 | **Will** +7 | **Speed** 25 ft
**Weapon:** Staff (1d4 B, 2-handed) | **Eidolon:** Fey Touch 1d6+4 + Stupefied 1 on crit
**Skills:** Occultism +5, Nature +6, Diplomacy +7, Deception +7, Society +5
**Features:** Fey Eidolon, Act Together, Evolution Surge focus
**Spells:** Occult tradition | DC 17, +7 attack | Cantrips: Daze, Shield, Guidance, Mage Hand, Message
**L1 Feats:** Fey-Touched (heritage, auto) + Animal Accomplice (ancestry feat) + Boost Eidolon | **Boost:** CHA → WIS → INT → CON

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Kitsune | Regal | Regal Kitsune's shape-change flexibility and CHA cantrip match the Fey Eidolon's illusion/charm focus — the summoner and eidolon both transform to fit each encounter type. |
| 2 | Gnome | Wellspring | Wellspring's free occult spell adds a non-Fey option when the eidolon's Invisibility or Charm is on cooldown; WIS 14 makes this utility build's save DCs competitive. |
| 3 | Halfling | Gutsy | Gutsy protects the utility-summoner from Frightened during social encounters where Fey charm abilities trigger hostile reactions and retaliatory Demoralizes. |
| 4 | Leshy | Leaf | Leaf Leshy Stealth complements the Fey Eidolon's Invisibility evolution — both bodies can go unseen, making this the best two-body ambush/scout combination. |

| 7 | Fetchling | Liminal | Liminal shadow step pairs with the eidolon's Teleport 30 ft — both bodies reposition freely, maximizing the "utility over raw damage" build's positional flexibility. |
