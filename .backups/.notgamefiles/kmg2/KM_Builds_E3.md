## KM_Builds_E3.md | Referenced by: KM_BuildScreen.md, KM_Companions_Builds.md

> **DM:** Load this file for Exemplar builds 7–10. Builds 1–6 are in KM_Builds_E.md.
> Each entry: stat block at L1 + full leveling map L1–20 + race options.
> After confirming ancestry, output full `.status` panel before any scene content.
>
> **EXEMPLAR NOTE:** Exemplar is a War of Immortals class. Ikons are divine implements.
> Each ikon provides a Spark (free action, 1/round on hit) + Transcendence surge 1/day.
>
> **ANCESTRY HEIGHT — NON-NEGOTIABLE:**
> Dwarf 4–4½ ft | Halfling 2½–3 ft | Gnome 3–3½ ft | Human/Half-Elf/Half-Orc 5–6 ft | Elf 5½–6½ ft | Orc 6–7 ft
>
> **ANCESTRY BASE HP:**
> Dwarf 10 | Orc 10 | Half-Orc 10 | Human 8 | Aasimar 8 | Gnome 8 | Elf 6 | Halfling 6
>
> **HP CORRECTION:** New L1 HP = Listed HP − Default Ancestry HP + Chosen Ancestry HP
>
> **SPEED:** Dwarf 20 ft | Human 25 ft | Elf 30 ft | Halfling 25 ft | Gnome 25 ft | Orc 25 ft | Half-Orc 25 ft
