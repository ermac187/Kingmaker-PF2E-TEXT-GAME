>
> **SUMMONER NOTE:** Summoner and eidolon share one HP pool. When the eidolon takes damage,
> the summoner takes it. Act Together lets both act on the same turn — effectively two
> actions each round at different locations on the battlefield. CHA is key ability.
>
> **ANCESTRY BASE HP:** Dwarf 10 | Orc 10 | Half-Orc 10 | Human 8 | Aasimar 8 | Gnome 8 | Elf 6 | Halfling 6
> **HP CORRECTION:** New L1 HP = Listed HP − Default Ancestry HP + Chosen Ancestry HP
