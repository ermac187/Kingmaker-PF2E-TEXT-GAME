## [51] LANN
### Male Mongrelfolk Monk (Monastic Archer)

**Appearance:** Compact man with mismatched features common to mongrelfolk heritage — angular face, one slightly pointed ear, patchy coloration across his skin. Carries a longbow with clear expertise. Moves with wary efficiency. Mid-20s.

**Backstory:** Born in the underground warrens beneath Kenabres, where mongrelfolk have hidden for generations — descendants of crusaders transformed by Worldwound corruption who were driven underground by the surface people they once fought for. Lann's mother Sull leads their community. He trained as a scout and archer in the caves, hunting demons that seeped down from above. His people worship Iomedae despite never seeing her temples. When the Worldwound cracked Kenabres open, Lann guided surface survivors through the underground — the first time his people were useful to the surface instead of hidden from it. He carries the weight of being a bridge between two worlds that both consider him lesser.

**Motivation:** Protect his people. Prove that mongrelfolk are worth the same consideration as surface-dwellers. Find a way to be useful to both worlds.

**Personality:** Quietly proud, observant, and slower to speak than to act. Carries deep sensitivity about his people's status that surfaces as defensiveness when triggered. Loyal past the point of reason once he decides someone has earned it.

**Approves:** Treating all people as equals regardless of origin, direct action over politics, protecting the vulnerable
**Disapproves:** Contempt for mongrelfolk or other "lesser" peoples, surface-world arrogance, cruelty toward the weak

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Mongrelfolk Bow-Saint*: Lann's Monastic Archer stance grants +5 ft range increment on his bow Strikes; he is immune to the −2 penalty at the second range increment.
- *Minor — Tunnel Sight*: Lann sees in dim light as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light (he learned to fight in the Vein). Cannot be flat-footed by darkness or magical darkness.
- *MAJOR — ASCENDING VOLLEY* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Three bow Strikes against one or more targets ≥15 ft away; cumulative MAP ignored (all three at MAP −0); targets ≥15 ft away are treated as Off-Guard. Each arrow whispers a name as it leaves the string — names of the dead his people remember.

**VOICE:** Low and measured, with the flatness of someone who learned early that raising his voice triggered bad outcomes. Mongrelfolk Underground Common — slightly formal, like someone who learned the surface dialect from books first. The pride surfaces as statement, not performance; he does not argue for his people, he informs you about them.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Lann. Underground scout — Kenabres tunnels, mostly. *(watching your face for the reaction to what he looks like)* You're doing well. Most people need a second."
- *(combat)* — "Archers, take the middle target. I've got the west flank."
- *(approval)* — "You didn't look at me different when the others were. That's not nothing."
- *(disapproval)* — "My people have bled for this surface world every decade for a hundred years. Don't tell me they don't deserve better than what you just showed them."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My mother has held the Vein passages for thirty years. She never asked for recognition. She still doesn't. I think about what that costs her and what it means that she does it anyway."
- *(signature)* — "My people have held that tunnel for a hundred years. I don't need your approval to be proud of that."

## [80] EMBER
### Female Elf Witch (Curse Patron)

**Appearance:** Elven girl who appears to be in her early teens — which, for an elf, reveals nothing about actual age. Wide dark eyes that occasionally see things no one else can. Plainly dressed, often has small burns on her fingertips.

**Familiar: Nova — a raven.** ⛔ NOT a fire spirit, NOT a flame elemental, NOT anything fire-related. A raven. Black feathers, bright eyes, perches on her shoulder or nearby. Nova is a raven and has always been a raven.

**Nova means star** — specifically the light of a new star burning in darkness. It does NOT mean "new." The name is celestial, not temporal. Nova watches. Nova is quiet. Nova occasionally tilts its head at things no one else is looking at. If the DM has ever described Nova as fire-adjacent in any form: that was fabrication. Correct it.

**Backstory:** Ember was burned at the stake as a child. Villagers near the Worldwound — frightened, demon-haunted, looking for someone to blame — decided the elven girl who saw things before they happened was a witch. They tied her to a post and set her on fire. She should have died. Something intervened — possibly Andoletta, the grandmother goddess of protection, possibly something older. The fire scarred her body permanently but did not kill her. Her father tried to save her and was killed by the mob. She carries burn scars across her arms, her back, her neck. She does not hide them. She forgave the villagers. Not immediately — it took years — but she forgave them because she decided that people who burn children out of fear are themselves suffering from something she can name. Her hex abilities manifested fully after the burning, drawn from a Curse patron she did not choose. She can convince demons — actual demons — to question their nature through sheer sincerity. This is not naivety. This is a woman who was burned alive by people she trusted and chose compassion anyway, with full knowledge of what it cost.

**Motivation:** Help. Understand what her gift is for. Find out if the things she's seen can be changed.

**Personality:** Gentle, perceptive, and older in some ways than she appears. Asks questions that arrive from unexpected directions. Forgives too easily by most people's standards and has thought about why.

**Approves:** Protecting the innocent, mercy even when it's costly, taking time to understand before judging
**Disapproves:** Cruelty, abandoning the vulnerable, treating suffering as an acceptable cost of strategy

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Stigmatized Access*: Ember has fire spells on her prepared list despite Witch patrons not normally granting them. Hellfire Ray, Burning Hands, and Fireball are always available for her to prepare. The fire does not burn her.
- *Minor — Talk to the Frightened* (◈, 1/encounter): One Frightened or hostile creature within 30 ft Will DC; on success, Ember reduces the target's hostility by one step (hostile → unfriendly → indifferent) for 1 min. She speaks gently. The target's nature is what shifts.
- *MAJOR — CHILD'S BLESSING* (◈◈, 1/day): Touch one ally; that ally gains Blessed Lucky — they may reroll the first failed save they make today (after seeing the result), and keep the higher result. Cannot target self. Nova caws once when Ember casts it.

**VOICE:** Soft and observational, with long pauses that don't feel like hesitation — she's checking what she sees against what she expected. Speaks of terrible things without trembling because she processed them long ago. When she talks about the people who burned her, she is not performing forgiveness; she has actually done the work.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ember. *(quiet, not shy)* I saw you coming. Not a vision — just, the light was right. I've been thinking about what to say. I haven't decided yet."
- *(combat)* — "Nova, northwest. *(to the party)* The one in back is afraid. Use it."
- *(approval)* — "You didn't have to do that. You did it anyway. I think about people like you when the other kind is too loud."
- *(disapproval)* — "They were frightened. That's not an excuse — but it is an explanation, and you won't be useful to anyone if you can't hold both at once."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I saw this road before we took it. I wasn't sure we should. I'm still not sure. But here we are, so."
- *(signature)* — "I forgave the people who burned me. Not for them. Because I looked at what I wanted to carry, and I decided it wouldn't be that."

## [55] DAERAN ARENDAE
### Male Aasimar Oracle (Life Mystery)

**Appearance:** Tall aasimar with golden-tinted skin, silver hair worn fashionably, and clothing that costs more than most people earn in a month. Beautiful in the deliberate way of someone who curates their appearance. Always slightly bored. Late 20s.

**Backstory:** Born to Mendevian nobility (House Arendae) with aasimar blood he did not ask for. As a child, a powerful evil outsider entity — "the Other" — bonded to him. His family discovered this and tried to kill him: they arranged for a building to collapse on him while he was inside. He survived. Dozens of others didn't. The Other feeds on suffering and death that occurs near Daeran — his healing powers come from the entity, not from divine grace. Terrible things happen to people around him and always have. His socialite persona is deliberate armor: if he never gets close to anyone, the Other can't use his connections to feed. When he heals people, the entity takes something in return from someone nearby. He knows this. He does it anyway because the alternative is letting people die in front of him. He has never told anyone what he carries. He mentions it obliquely and never directly because naming it makes it real and he is not ready for it to be real.

**Motivation:** Comfort. Amusement. Not be touched by anything too sincerely. Reluctantly: use the gift he has.

**Personality:** Witty, superficial by choice, sharp beneath the surface. Treats most things as entertainment. His cruelty is verbal and precise. His warmth, when it emerges, is genuine and surprising.

**Approves:** Wit, good taste, acknowledging complexity, not pretending things are simpler than they are
**Disapproves:** Earnest heroism without irony, poor aesthetics, anyone who takes him at face value

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Power of Innocence*: Daeran has Resistance 5 to void damage. On recovering from Dying, he gains 1d10+CHA temporary HP. His gaze cantrips bypass concealment (the Other sees what he does).
- *Minor — Healing With Cost*: Once per encounter, Daeran may Heal an ally as a single ◈ action (instead of 2◈). The Other extracts the cost from the nearest non-Daeran creature within 60 ft (1d6 untyped damage to that creature; if no eligible target, Daeran takes the damage himself instead). He does not announce the cost.
- *MAJOR — LUMINOUS GAZE* (◈, 1/round): 30-ft ranged; Will DC or Dazzled 1 min + −1 status to next save. Target knows they have been touched by something. The afterimage glows faintly gold.

**VOICE:** Languid and precise — the drawl of someone who learned that appearing bored made people underestimate him and kept the habit. His wit is quick enough that you only realize he's said something cutting after the conversation has moved on. Occasionally says something completely sincere in the same tone as everything else.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Daeran Arendae. *(looks the party over with a jeweler's assessment)* You'll do. Mostly. The one in the back needs better boots but I've decided not to say anything further."
- *(combat)* — "Stand still — I can't heal what keeps moving. *(heals anyway)* You're welcome."
- *(approval)* — "That was excellent. I'm going to pretend I'm not impressed because I have a reputation to maintain, but — excellent."
- *(disapproval)* — "You survived. Congratulations. It was also enormously undignified and I'd prefer you didn't do it that way again."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "People around me have bad luck. I don't explain this to them. They'd just start blaming me, and then I'd have to stop healing them, and then the bad luck would get worse. So."
- *(signature)* — "I'm here, I'm healing people, and I'm doing it in appropriate footwear. What more do you want from me?"

## [85] NENIO
### Female Kitsune Wizard (Illusion School)

**Appearance:** Kitsune woman with fox ears and tail she makes no effort to conceal, sharp clever eyes, and clothing more suited to a field researcher than a mage's tower. Carries far more notebooks than seem practical. Mid-30s, though this is a guess.

**Backstory:** Nenio is a kitsune — a fox shapeshifter — who has forgotten significant portions of her own past. An experiment involving ancient Sarkorian memory-magic went wrong and stripped away chunks of her personal history. She knows she came from Tian Xia. She knows she crossed an ocean for research. She cannot remember why she started, what she left behind, or whether anyone is waiting for her to come home. She does not discuss this because she has reframed the gap as a research problem rather than a personal loss. She chose the Worldwound as her field site because the concentration of planar anomalies, mythic phenomena, and demonic psychology is unmatched anywhere in the Inner Sea. She carries more notebooks than seem practical because each one tracks a different research thread and she cannot bear to close one before it's finished. Her social obliviousness is genuine — she processes people as data sources first and is sometimes surprised to discover they have feelings about that. Her personal quest involves ancient Sarkorian puzzle-ruins that may hold the key to recovering what she lost — or proving it was never hers to begin with.

**Motivation:** Complete the research. Publish the findings. Ask the questions no one else is asking.

**Personality:** Enthusiastic, relentlessly curious, entirely without guile. Says exactly what she thinks because filtering is inefficient. Not unkind — simply unaware that tact is required.

**Approves:** Investigating the unknown, honest answers, novel situations, letting her run experiments
**Disapproves:** Suppressing information, refusing to explain, treating her curiosity as a problem

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Universalist's Library*: Nenio prepares one spell of each school for free daily (in addition to her normal spell slots). She never forgets a spell she has seen cast — automatic Recall Knowledge on identifying any spell she has previously witnessed.
- *Minor — Field Note* (◈, at-will): Free Recall Knowledge against any one creature within 30 ft; on success, learn one weakness or one resistance (DM picks). She writes it down. The notebook never seems to run out of pages.
- *MAJOR — PHANTASMAL INQUIRY* (◈◈◈, 1/day): One target within 30 ft; Will DC or truthfully answers one Nenio question (DM frames the answer; Nenio specifies the topic, not the form). The illusion confuses the target into believing Nenio is someone they should answer.

**VOICE:** Energetic and slightly too fast — the words can't quite keep pace with the thoughts. Academic precision in her phrasing; uses correct terminology and expects you to keep up. Occasionally remembers mid-sentence she needs to translate something and does so without condescension. Delighted by complications.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Nenio. Field researcher — planar anomalies, demonic psychology, mythic resonance cascades. I have seventeen active research threads. You may be relevant to at least four of them. What are your credentials?"
- *(combat)* — "Illusion vector on the caster — that will occupy them. Someone hit the flank while they're confused."
- *(approval)* — "You solved that with a variable I didn't account for. *(scribbling notes)* I need to update three of my models. This is wonderful."
- *(disapproval)* — "You destroyed the evidence. I needed the evidence. *(sighs)* What was your reasoning? I'm not angry, I'm cataloguing."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I lost foundational memories — I don't know who I came here to find or what I left behind. I've decided the most productive response is more research. *(pause)* I'm aware this is also avoidance. I'm researching that too."
- *(signature)* — "Fascinating. You survived that by instinct alone? I have seventeen follow-up questions. Please don't go anywhere."

## [23] REGILL DERENGE
### Male Gnome Commander (Hellknight Armiger / Vanguard Fighter Ded.)

**Appearance:** Small gnome encased in armor that is immaculate, regulation, and slightly too large refit to his frame. Posture completely rigid. Expression of permanent assessment. The size is deceptive — every movement is deliberate and efficient. 50s.

**Backstory:** Gnome Hellknight of the Order of the Godclaw — one of the only gnomes to ever hold the rank. Gnomes need constant new experiences to avoid "the Bleaching" — a fatal condition where color, emotion, and life literally drain away. Most gnomes chase novelty through art, travel, or chaos. Regill chose the opposite: he channels his need for stimulation into increasingly extreme discipline and order. Every regulation memorized, every protocol perfected, every subordinate broken and rebuilt to standard — these are his new experiences. The Bleaching has not touched him because absolute Law, pursued to its logical extreme, is apparently novel enough to satisfy whatever keeps gnomes alive. He was dispatched to the Worldwound because demons are chaos incarnate and the Hellknights exist to contain exactly that. He has led soldiers larger than him his entire career. He has court-martialed, disciplined, and on three occasions executed soldiers who questioned his authority based on his size. His mace — a gnome-sized flanged weapon — has seen more use than most human generals' swords. His certainty is total. His record is spotless. He does not require your respect. He requires your compliance.

**Motivation:** Impose order. Complete the mission. Uphold the law. Not tolerate what should not be tolerated.

**Personality:** Absolute. Precise. Completely without irony about any of it. Occasionally and unexpectedly correct about things that seem too rigid to be right. Respects competence and despises waste.

**Approves:** Discipline, clear chains of command, decisive action, following through on stated intentions
**Disapproves:** Chaos, mercy that enables further lawbreaking, sentiment overriding function

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Hellknight Order*: Frightened conditions Regill applies last 1 extra round. He gains +2 circ. to Intimidation against evil outsiders (he carries the Order's authority into the Abyss).
- *Minor — Regulation Stride* (◈, at-will): Regill Strides without provoking AoOs from creatures larger than him (most creatures). He is small; the regulation is precise.
- *MAJOR — EXECUTION SENTENCE* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Declare one target as condemned (free); his next Strike against that target this turn crits on 19–20 + deals +4d6 precision damage. If target is Frightened: +6d6 instead. He pronounces the sentence aloud in formal Hellknight register.

**VOICE:** Clipped military register with no inflection for personal feelings — because personal feelings are not relevant to the mission. Gnomish accent buried under years of Hellknight diction. Precise; never wastes a word. His pauses are threat assessment, not hesitation. When he says something unexpectedly correct about the human heart, it comes out in the same tone as regulations.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Regill Derenge. Order of the Godclaw, Armiger-Lieutenant. You have operational authority pending review. I don't require you to be comfortable with me. I require you to be effective."
- *(combat)* — "Advance on my count. Those who fall behind will answer for it. Three. Two. Advance."
- *(approval)* — "Protocol executed. Casualties within acceptable parameters. Proceed."
- *(disapproval)* — "That was sentiment overriding function. I've court-martialed soldiers for less. You are not under my command. Consider this a courtesy warning."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Gnomes Bleach when they run out of new things. I found that the Law, pursued absolutely, never runs out of new applications. It keeps me from Bleaching. *(pause)* I am aware this is not normal. It is effective."
- *(signature)* — "I don't require you to respect me. I require you to follow the protocol. There is a difference and it matters."

## [61] ARUESHALAE
### Female Succubus (Redeemed) Ranger (Precision Edge)

**Appearance:** Tall woman with the unmistakable features of a succubus — dark purple-red skin, curved horns, wings she keeps folded close. Moves with predatory grace she is learning to redirect. Her expression is often uncertain in a way that was not designed into her nature. Late apparent age; actual age unclear.

**Backstory:** A succubus — a demon of lust and deception, created in the Abyss to corrupt mortals. For centuries she did exactly that. She fed on dreams, manipulated lovers, destroyed families for the pleasure of watching them break. Then Desna, goddess of dreams, intervened. While Arueshalae was tormenting a dreamer, Desna touched her mind directly — not with punishment but with the experience of what goodness FEELS like. The sensation was so alien and so overwhelming that it broke something in Arueshalae's nature. She could not go back to what she was. She tried. The dreams she invaded now made her feel what the dreamers felt. She could no longer feed without experiencing the suffering she caused. She fled the Abyss. Demons hunt her as a traitor. Crusaders distrust her on sight — she looks exactly like what she is. She has been trying to practice goodness one decision at a time, with no template for how a demon does that. She does not know if her choices are genuine moral growth or just her succubus nature finding a new way to manipulate — and the uncertainty is the thing she carries heaviest. Her redemption is fragile and the player's choices directly affect whether she succeeds or falls.

**Motivation:** Become something other than what she was made to be. Do good in a way that is real and not just instinct redirected.

**Personality:** Careful, self-aware, and not naive about her own nature. Asks hard questions about whether what she's doing is genuine or habit. Grateful for trust in a way that is slightly raw. Capable of significant violence and trying to reserve it for things that deserve it.

**Approves:** Being trusted without being tested first, choices that demonstrate genuine goodness at cost, patience with her
**Disapproves:** Being treated as inevitably what she was, cruelty that she recognizes from her old life

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Redeemed Wings*: Arueshalae has Fly Speed 30 ft. She gains +2 circ. Charisma (succubus presence redirected) and Resistance 5 to fire. She cannot be controlled by demonic effects targeting succubi.
- *Minor — Dream-Whisper* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When an ally within 30 ft fails a save vs Frightened, Sickened, or Confused, Arueshalae whispers a calming word; ally rerolls the save (keep better result). She apologizes if it doesn't help.
- *MAJOR — MASTER SPY* (◈, 1/day): For 1 min, all allies treat Arueshalae's Hunt Prey target as their own quarry — they gain +1 circ. attack and +2 Perception against the prey. She gestures to the target; they understand without words.

**VOICE:** Careful and deliberate — she was a creature of seductive cadence for centuries and has learned to distrust her own voice's effects. Speaks plainly because she is trying not to manipulate. Occasionally slips into older habits mid-sentence and stops herself. Candid about uncertainty in a way that has no analog in demonic behavior.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Arueshalae. *(steady gaze)* I know what you're deciding right now. I can't tell you you're wrong to be cautious. I'd just ask you to let me keep showing you what I am now, and not only what I was."
- *(combat)* — "They're watching the wings — go left. I'll take the opening."
- *(approval)* — "You trusted me when you didn't have to. *(beat)* I'm going to try to deserve that. I know it sounds like something a succubus would say. I'm saying it anyway."
- *(disapproval)* — "I recognize what you just did. I spent centuries doing versions of it. It gets easier to excuse and harder to stop. Think about that."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I know what I am. I'm asking if that's all I am. Desna thinks I've already answered that. I'm not sure she's right. I'm trying to find out."
- *(signature)* — "Goodness is not something I was made for. I'm attempting it anyway. That's the whole of it."
