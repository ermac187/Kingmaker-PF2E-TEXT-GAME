## BUILD — CURSED SABOTEUR (Bell + Wand + Mirror)
**Human (Versatile Heritage) | Thaumaturge (Bell + Wand + Mirror) + Marshal Dedication | Background: Occult Scholar**
*Absolute debuff king — the "Save-Slayer." Stacks non-overlapping penalties to AC, Saves, and attack rolls so enemies fall into a death spiral. Cursed Effigy lays a permanent −1 status to AC/saves (no save), Bell reactives push Enfeebled/Stupefied/Clumsy on the enemy turn, and Dread Marshal Stance forces Frightened over a 10-ft aura. Math swing ≈ 15-20% in your party's favor on every fight.*

| Score | 14 | 14 | 14 | 10 | 12 | 18 |
| Mod | +2 | +2 | +2 | +0 | +1 | +4 |

**HP** 18 | **AC** 17 (Studded Leather) | **Fort** +6 | **Ref** +6 | **Will** +5 | **Speed** 30 ft
**Weapon:** Guisarme (1d10 S, reach, trip — strikes Bell-debuffed enemies from 10 ft) | **Bell Implement** (1st)
**Skills:** Occultism +5, Intimidation +7, Diplomacy +7, Esoteric Lore +5, Deception +7, Society +3
**Features:** Exploit Vulnerability, Bell Implement (reactive Enfeebled/Stupefied save-or-suck), Weapon Implement on guisarme
**L1 Feats:** Versatile Human (general feat: **Reach Weapon proficiency / Sentinel Dedication chassis**) + Bell Adept | **Boost:** CHA → CON → WIS → STR
