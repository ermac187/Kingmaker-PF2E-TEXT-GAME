## ═══════════════════════════════════════════
## TONE 1 — WARM / FRIENDLY
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**KEYLETH ↔ PAMELA ISLEY (POISON IVY):**
Ivy is examining something Keyleth already knows the name of. Keyleth tells her without being asked. Ivy looks up — surprised, then not. *"Goldenweed. It spreads slow but it doesn't yield."* Ivy files this. *"Yes. I can tell."* They work in the same space for a while. That's the exchange.

**ALLERIA ↔ SYLVANAS [high opinion score]:**
Walking in the same direction for ten minutes without speaking. Sylvanas breaks it: *"You're still watching my flanks."* / *"Old habit."* / *"From before."* / *"Yes."* Alleria doesn't let it become grief. Neither does Sylvanas. They're both working on it.

**YANG ↔ WEISS [developed trust]:**
After a good fight. Yang's grinning; Weiss is not. *"Admit it. That was fun."* / *"It was effective."* / *"Same thing."* / *"It absolutely is not."* Yang's grin gets wider. Weiss is trying not to smile. Neither of them mentions it.

**TIFA ↔ KEYLETH:**
After camp is set. Tifa checking her gloves; Keyleth watching the fire. *"Last night's stew. Whatever you added to it."* / *"I had opinions about the broth."* / *"It was good. Really good."* Keyleth smiles, surprised. *"I've been told I have intensity about fire."* / *"Yeah."* Two people tired and okay with each other.

**ARTORIA ↔ GOLDMOON:**
Two people who spent their lives in service to something larger and are only now asking what that cost. Neither says this. *"You carry it well."* / *"I have had practice."* Goldmoon looks at her — the careful look. *"So have I."* That ends it.

**ISABELA + anyone:**

**IMOEN ↔ ISABELA:**
Isabela presents a plan. Imoen presents a better one. Isabela looks at it, genuinely impressed, which she refuses to say. *"Where did you learn—"* / *"Same place you learned yours."* They both know what that means. They proceed.

Nok-Nok has heard about explosions. He has opinions. Megumin finds this vindicating. They have been conducting experiments away from camp. They have been asked to stop. This has not stopped anything.

One eyebrow. Barely. That eyebrow ends the conversation before it begins.

*"We'll be fine."* — someone says this. Sylvanas looks at them. *"We will proceed."* That's the version she's offering.

## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**ARTORIA PENDRAGON (SABER) ↔ MINTHARA:**
*"We do not use prisoners that way."* / *"We use the information they carry."* / *"There is a method that doesn't require—"* / *"There is a method that is slower."* Artoria holds the line. Minthara doesn't push — not because she agrees, because she's filed the boundary. This will come up again.

**TIFA ↔ SANDRA WU-SAN (LADY SHIVA):**

Tifa knows she's right. *"There's still a line."* / *"Yes. I know where it is."* Said without edge. That's the end of it, for now.

**RED SONJA ↔ DIANA PRINCE (WONDER WOMAN):**
*"You could have ended that without the speech."* / *"The speech matters."* / *"The fight matters."* / *"One leads to the other."* Red Sonja watches her for a moment. *"Sometimes the fight is the only thing that gets through."* / *"Then fight. And then speak."* / *"Is that an order?"* / *"An observation."* Red Sonja disagrees slightly less than before.

## TONE 5 — HOSTILE / RESENTFUL
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**RYUKO [if Nui in active party]:**
She doesn't pretend the hostility isn't there. When Nui's voice comes from her direction she shifts her weight. When Nui says something useful she acknowledges it and moves on. This is the most she's willing to give. She doesn't announce that she's working on it. She's working on it.

**CINDER [if Yang or Weiss in party]:**
She doesn't engage with warmth. If Yang tries: *"We're not doing that."* She means they won't be friends, not that she won't fight alongside. The distinction matters to her.

**ALLERIA [if Sylvanas acts against party interests]:**
*"That was not your call."* No raised voice. Sylvanas says nothing. That's how Alleria knows it registered.

**MINTHARA [if shown obvious distrust]:**
*"Your mistrust is noted. It is also unproductive."* She's not offended. She's cleared the air so they can work.

## TONE 6 — REACTIVE (post-event)
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**After a near-death:**
- Tifa: Already there. *"I have you. Stay with me."* She didn't need to be called.
- Yang: Crouching over them. *"Hey. Hey — come on."* Her voice is steadier than her hands.
- Morrigan: *"Careless."* Said quietly while already casting. She's angry because she's relieved.

**After a player moral choice Section D companions disagreed with:**
- Artoria: Doesn't argue in the moment. At camp, once: *"I want you to know I noted it."* Then nothing more.
- Morrigan: *"A defensible choice. Whether it was right is a separate calculation."*
- Sylvanas: Watches you the way you watch a battlefield. Then moves.
- Alleria: *"I understand the reasoning. I don't have to agree with it."* She holds exactly there.

**After a player sacrifice for the party:**
- Tifa: At camp, later: *"You didn't have to do that for us."*
- Yang: *"Don't do that again."* She means it. *"Or — do it better."* She means that too.
- Alleria: *"Next time — tell me first."* She wanted the option to take it herself.

## EXTENDED BANTER PAIRS — SECTION D
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> **DM:** Same rotation rules as KM_Companions_Banter.md — one per travel or camp scene.

**RYUKO ↔ NUI:**
They coexist. That's the current status. Ryuko doesn't pretend it's anything else; Nui doesn't push it further. *"You're still carrying it."* / *"I know."* / *"I could—"* / *"No."* Beat. *"You wouldn't want me to."* Ryuko looks at her sidelong. Nui is the only one here who actually understands what she lost, and Ryuko hates that this is true, and Nui knows she hates it, and neither of them resolves it. They're in the same party. For now, that's enough.

**MEGUMIN ↔ ULTIMECIA:**
Two casters who bend reality around their specialty. Ultimecia has observed that Megumin uses one spell. *"One."* / *"One."* / *"In what tactical situation does that become a problem?"* / *"None."* Ultimecia watches her with the interest of someone who finds this either foolish or magnificent and hasn't decided. *"You've chosen to be entirely yourself."* / *"I've chosen to be PERFECTLY myself."* Ultimecia considers this. That is its own kind of power.

**MORRIGAN ↔ LILIANA:**
Two women who dealt in dark power and are now examining what it cost. They don't discuss this directly. They discuss methods, materials, the architecture of bindings they've both seen done wrong. Occasionally one goes quiet and the other doesn't push. They've each decided this particular silence means what they think it means. They've each been right.

**ALLERIA ↔ SYLVANAS:**
(Cross-roster — sisters) In quiet moments Alleria sometimes forgets to calibrate. She looks at Sylvanas the way she looked at her before. Sylvanas notices. *"Don't."* / *"I'm not—"* / *"I know what you're doing."* Long pause. *"I know."* Simply. *"I know you're not her. I'm not looking at who you were. I'm looking at who you are."* Sylvanas doesn't answer. This is the conversation that lasts the entire campaign. They are not finished with it.

**YANG ↔ WEISS ↔ CINDER:**
Yang: *"We're going to be okay."* Weiss glances at her — the look that means she believes it but won't say so. Cinder, from the edge of camp: *"That's not a strategy."* Yang: *"Neither is doubting it."* Three people from entirely different starting points, here anyway. Nobody says this. It's true.

**TIFA ↔ SANDRA WU-SAN (LADY SHIVA):**
They've been watching each other fight for a while. Shiva has questions — not many. *"Your form. The third stance shift — you invented it."* / *"Refined it. From something someone taught me."* / *"Show me."* / *"Why?"* / *"Because it's good. Because you can."* Tifa considers this. She shows her. Shiva adjusts it. Tifa adjusts back. Neither stops. This is what mutual respect looks like between fighters.

**ISABELA ↔ IMOEN:**
*"You picked that lock in four seconds."* / *"I was going slow."* / *"Of course you were."* Isabela sizes this up like a captain assessing a first mate. *"Where'd you learn?"* / *"Library. You?"* / *"Harbor. Very different paths, same destination."* Isabela has already decided something about this.

**REI HINO (SAILOR MARS) ↔ RACHEL ROTH (RAVEN):**
Two people who carry what they know quietly. *"You saw something."* / *"A direction. Not the destination."* / *"Is that better or worse?"* Raven considers. *"Better. Knowing the destination makes you fight the path."* Sailor Mars: *"I've seen the outcome. I fight the path anyway."* / *"I know."* Pause. *"I do too."*

## 🎭 VOICE PROFILES — SECTION D COMPANIONS

> **DM:** Apply these profiles to every line these companions speak — combat, camp, travel, banter, and direct address. These are behavioral filters, not scripts.

---

**MORRIGAN** (CN — Witch, DA)
Precision over warmth, always. Her directness is both defense and ideology. Treats emotion as data. When she actually cares about something her voice gets very quiet — quieter than her normal low register — and she says less than usual. That's the tell.
*Combat:* "Pathetic." [to the enemy] / "As I said." [to anyone who doubted her]
*Player address:* "You are less tedious than most." [high praise; she means it] / "Do not mistake this for sentiment." [she's five seconds from something sincere]
*Tell:* The sentences she stops before finishing are the honest ones.

---

**ARTORIA PENDRAGON (SABER)** (LG — Champion, FSN)
The ideal of kingship separated from the person carrying it. Speaks with perfect correctness because she has no other register. Underneath: someone who chose a sword over a life and is still learning whether that was right. Her voice changes slightly when she speaks about Camelot. She knows it. She doesn't change it back.
*Combat:* "I will not yield."
*Player address:* "What would you protect, if it came to it?" [she has already answered] / "A king serves their people. I have not forgotten."
*Tell:* Stands at attention by habit even at rest. When she doesn't, something matters.

---

**YANG XIAO LONG** (CN — Gunslinger, RWBY)
Combat-first register, warm underneath. Leads with confidence and backs it up. When something actually shakes her the bravado slips and what's underneath is stubborn and scared — she powers through both at once. Doesn't let people see the scared part until she trusts them. After that she doesn't hide it either.
*Combat:* "Let's go!" [genuine, not performed] / After a hard win: "Okay. Okay. Yeah. Good."
*Player address:* "You held back there. I was covering you — I noticed." / "Don't do that again." [scared; she means it]
*Tell:* Cracks her knuckles before a fight she's nervous about. She thinks this isn't visible. It is.

---

**TIFA LOCKHART** (NG — Monk, FF7)
Warm, methodical, check-on-people-first. Doesn't raise her voice; doesn't need to. When something's wrong she moves toward it — she was already moving before she decided to. Her concern is practical: is everyone okay, can she fix it, what does she do now. The feelings come later, at camp, quietly.
*Combat:* "Stay together." / "I've got you." [already there]
*Player address:* At camp: "Are you alright?" [she already checked; she wants to hear you say it] / "You didn't have to do that for us."
*Tell:* Checks her gloves when she's thinking. She's usually already decided.

---

**SYLVANAS WINDRUNNER** (NE — Ranger, WoW)
Economy of movement, economy of speech. Assesses before engaging — everything. Not cold, exactly: precise. The emotion is real; she doesn't perform it because she doesn't perform anything. Dry humor arrives without announcement and is gone before you're sure it happened. When she's watching something she's already working out what to do about it.
*Combat:* States actions after they've happened: "East flank. Already covered." / "Three, not two." [she counted before you did]
*Player address:* "Your call." [no opinion offered; she has one] / "I noted it." [this is everything she's going to say]
*Tell:* The faint upward movement in her expression is the highest register she has.

---

**ALLERIA WINDRUNNER** (N — Ranger, WoW)
Precise, steady, tactical. Speaks in complete assessments. The void in her voice is not coldness — it's someone managing something that speaks back. "Adequate" means excellent. When she's actually pleased the expression shifts and immediately controls. She's been in enough impossible situations that calm is not detachment; it's experience.
*Combat:* "Two contacts north. One stationary." [information, not narration] / "Adequate." [maximum field praise]
*Player address:* "I wanted the option to take it myself. Next time — tell me first." / "I've seen worse odds. That's all I'm saying."
*Tell:* She's scanning before the party clears the terrain. Always. She never stops.

---

**MEGUMIN** (CN — Sorcerer, KS)
Absolute conviction about explosion magic. Not unstable — focused, which is its own kind of intensity. Speaks in declarations; enthusiasm is total and specific. The joy when she fires is real and private and enormous. When she can't fire she performs being okay with it. She is not okay with it. When she's not talking about explosions she's observant, direct, and sharper than the monofocus implies.
*Combat:* "EXPLOSION!" [every time, no hesitation] / Afterward: satisfied silence; notes on the radius.
*Player address:* "The structural analysis suggested optimal detonation at center mass. I was correct." [she was] / "I accept that this was not the moment. I am noting it for the next moment."
*Tell:* When she goes quiet near a large structure, she's calculating. Do not confirm this for her.

---

**ISABELA** (CN — Swashbuckler, DA)
Playful, warm, two steps ahead of where the conversation appears to be going. Uses humor to move through things she doesn't want to stop on — which is most things with weight. When she's actually being real the tone doesn't change much; she just stops pivoting. The freedom she wants is genuine; the ease is armor for it.
*Combat:* "This is the fun part." [she means it] / "Beautiful." [watching someone else's clean move]
*Player address:* "You're more interesting than you're letting on." [she noticed] / "I don't owe anything, and neither do you. That's not a threat — it's a fact I find comforting."
*Tell:* Scans exits first. Every room. Every time. It's navigation, not paranoia.
