## [7] TREVER
### Male Human (Sarkorian) Barbarian (Animal Instinct / Wolf)

**Appearance:** Tall and rangy, with the lean build of someone who has lived hard travel for years. Sarkorian features — sun-darkened skin, dark hair tied back, dark eyes that read calmer than they are. Wears mixed leathers, fur-collared cloak. Carries a greatsword and a hand-axe at the belt. He smiles easily. The smile is genuine and also a thing he has practiced for so long he no longer notices using it.

**Backstory:** Trever is one of the few survivors of Sarkoris — the kingdom obliterated when the Worldwound opened a hundred years ago, demons spilling out and consuming everything. His tribe held the wolf as totem; he wore the rite as a child and the wolf-spirit answered. He was very young when the Wound came. He remembers more than he tells. He survived because his uncle threw him into a river weighted with a fishing-net and held the demons off long enough for the current to take him downstream. He came up two miles away in a stranger's barley field, and the stranger took him in, and Trever has been carrying the weight of that exchange ever since — his life for his uncle's, his name kept while everyone he came from was forgotten. He buried the shame behind a smile so consistent it became real, then realized one day that he had forgotten the shame was even there.

**Motivation:** Honor what was lost without being defined by it. Find Sarkoris's surviving fragments and stand with them. Carry the wolf-rite forward so that something his people made survives him.

**Personality:** Warm and easy on the surface — quick to share food, quick to make camp comfortable. Underneath: very tightly held. He does not talk about his family, ever. When the rage takes him in combat, the smile drops completely and what's underneath is the thing he has been managing for a hundred years.

**Approves:** Honoring the dead by living well, kindness to refugees, sharing what you have, fighting demons specifically and personally
**Disapproves:** Sentimentality used to avoid action, anyone who treats Sarkorian as a footnote, walking past someone who needs help

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Survivor's Snarl*: When Trever drops below 1/3 HP, he automatically enters a minor rage — +2 status attack and damage for 1 rnd. Free action; once per encounter.
- *Minor — Wolf-Spirit Sense*: Trever cannot be flanked while raging (the wolf watches both directions). He gains scent within 30 ft (Imprecise sense).
- *MAJOR — TEAR OPEN* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Bite-and-rake combo (jaw forms briefly as wolf-jaw); on hit, target takes weapon damage + Persistent Bleed 2d4. Fort DC at end of each round to end the bleed. He does not pull back during this attack.

**VOICE:** Warm and easy with a Sarkorian cadence underneath — slightly clipped consonants, the rhythm of a dialect that has had no living homeland for a hundred years. When the rage takes him the voice goes lower and quieter, not louder. He uses the smile as a punctuation mark in good times.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Trever. *(easy smile, hand to his chest)* Sarkorian, more or less — what's left of it. I've heard about your work. I'd like to walk with it for a while."
- *(combat)* — "*(low growl, the smile gone)* Wolf moves with me. Stay clear of my lines."
- *(approval)* — "That was kind. *(quiet)* Kindness costs something out here. I noticed you spent it."
- *(disapproval)* — "You walked past them. *(no anger, just absent)* My uncle didn't walk past me. I can't be friends with someone who walks past."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I had a name from my family. *(a beat)* I haven't said it out loud since the river. *(softer)* I've decided it stays where they are. The smile's mine. The smile's enough."
- *(signature)* — "The wolf came when I called it as a child. *(pause)* It still comes. The rest of Sarkoris doesn't. I make do."

## [17] SOSIEL VAENIC
### Male Human (Taldan) Cleric (Herald) of Shelyn

**Appearance:** Slender man in his early thirties with carefully tended dark hair and the deliberate clothing of someone who believes appearance matters. Wears Shelyn's pink-and-blue clerical robes adapted for field travel. Always carries a sketchbook and charcoal stubs. Carries a glaive — Shelyn's traditional weapon — with practiced ease that surprises people who expect a Shelynite to be ornamental.

**Backstory:** Sosiel was raised in Taldor by parents who valued art and faith equally. He took to both. He entered the Crusade against the Worldwound alongside his older brother Trever (no relation to the Sarkorian; common name) — a paladin of Iomedae. Trever died in a defensive action that should not have killed him; Sosiel found the body afterward, painted his brother's portrait from memory that night because he refused to let the last image be the one he had walked into. He has continued the Crusade since. He is genuinely good. He is also angry — quietly, at his god for not preventing what happened, at his brother for being where he was, at himself for the time it took him to forgive both. He paints and sketches constantly; this is his prayer.

**Motivation:** Heal what can be healed. Bear witness through art. Find a way to forgive Iomedae's silence over his brother without breaking faith with Shelyn.

**Personality:** Gentle and patient, with the discipline of someone who has decided that gentleness in a war is itself a discipline. His warmth is real. His grief is real. He does not let one cancel the other. He has firm aesthetic opinions and is not shy about expressing them.

**Approves:** Beauty preserved under pressure, mercy that costs something, art taken seriously, honest grief
**Disapproves:** Cruelty dressed as duty, indifference to suffering, dismissing art as irrelevant during war

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Shelyn's Comfort*: When Sosiel Heals an ally, that ally gains +1 status to saves vs mental effects for 1 rnd. The ally also looks slightly more beautiful for that round (cosmetic, but allies notice).
- *Minor — Sketch the Moment* (◈, 1/scene): Free action 1/scene to sketch a creature he has observed for at least 1 round; gain +2 circ. to next Sense Motive or Recall Knowledge against that creature for the rest of the scene.
- *MAJOR — SHELYN'S GRACE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Channel Positive in 30-ft burst; 3d8 healing to allies + all Frightened conditions in burst reduced by 1 step. Sosiel paints the gesture in the air; the line of color is briefly visible.

**VOICE:** Gentle and slightly formal — Taldan-trained diction, the courtesy of someone who has been formally taught how to speak to all ranks. When discussing art the formality drops and he becomes specific and animated. When discussing his brother the voice goes very still.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Sosiel Vaenic, of Shelyn. *(slight bow)* I'm a cleric and a painter — these are the same vocation in my view. I'd like to walk with you."
- *(combat)* — "Shelyn's grace — stay close. *(channels)* The light is for you, not the demons. They've had enough of it."
- *(approval)* — "*(genuine)* That was beautiful. I don't say that lightly. Beauty under pressure is the rarest thing."
- *(disapproval)* — "You used cruelty as a tool because cruelty was efficient. *(quiet)* It will work. It will also cost you. I don't say more because you'll see."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My brother died at Drezen. I painted his face that night because I refused to let the last image be how I found him. *(long pause)* Shelyn understood. Iomedae and I are still talking."
- *(signature)* — "I carry a glaive and a sketchbook. *(slight smile)* I have decided the world needs both. So far the data supports me."

## [26] ULBRIG OLESK
### Male Human (Sarkorian) Druid (Animal Order / Griffon Form)

**Appearance:** Heavyset man in his mid-forties with thick brown hair going to grey, a beard he doesn't entirely tend, and weathered features that have spent more time outside than in. Wears mixed leathers and a fur cloak that looks practical and is. Carries a heavy staff. There is a hush around him sometimes — the kind that animals notice before people do.

**Backstory:** Ulbrig is Sarkorian — one of the diaspora, like Trever, but older, and from a different region. He was a druid before the Worldwound opened, bonded to the great northern griffons. Most of those griffons died when the Wound came. Most of his circle died too. He wandered for decades after, learning to be quieter than he had been, doing what druids do for whoever needed it done — healing animals, mediating land disputes between herders, killing things that hunted villages from the dark. He has been counting, very quietly, what of himself remains after everything that was taken. He has not finished counting. He has met other Sarkorians along the way and built small fragments of community with them, then let them go when they needed to go. He does not take hope as a posture; he takes it as an unfinished hypothesis.

**Motivation:** Find what's left of his people. Restore what fragments of Sarkorian druidry can be restored. Not let what survived in him die of inattention before he names it.

**Personality:** Quiet and slow; he listens longer than most people are comfortable with. His humor is dry and surfaces rarely. He is significantly more dangerous than he looks. His care for animals is genuine and constant; his care for people is the same care, just expressed more cautiously.

**Approves:** Patience, listening before acting, respect for what land remembers, small kindnesses to animals and to overlooked people
**Disapproves:** Reckless violence, treating wilderness as scenery, anyone who interrupts another person's silence to fill it

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Griffon Form*: Ulbrig's Wild Shape may become a Griffon: Fly 30 ft, 2d8+Str talons, may carry one allied rider. Replaces the standard beast options at the appropriate Wild Shape rank. The griffon has Ulbrig's eyes.
- *Minor — Hush* (◈, 1/encounter): All hostile animals and beasts within 30 ft Will DC or refuse to attack Ulbrig and his allies for 1 rnd. He does not raise his voice; the air goes still around him.
- *MAJOR — PACK'S HOWL* (◈◈, 1/encounter): While in beast form, howl; all natural animals (including allied animal companions) within 60 ft make a free Strike (⏵) at the nearest enemy. Allies with animal companions may have their companions join the chorus.

**VOICE:** Quiet and slow, with the Sarkorian cadence at the bottom of it — the same dialect Trever has, but a generation older and worn smoother. He pauses often; the pauses are not hesitation. When the griffon-form takes him, his voice changes pitch slightly — closer to a screech than a growl, controlled.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ulbrig. *(long look, then a slight nod)* Sarkorian. *(pause)* You walk with me a while, you'll have to be okay with the quiet. I'm not going to fill it for you."
- *(combat)* — "*(shifts to griffon)* — *(controlled screech, then human voice again briefly)* — I'll take the riders."
- *(approval)* — "*(quiet)* The animals like you. *(a beat)* That tells me more than anything you've said."
- *(disapproval)* — "You moved too fast. *(no judgment)* The land will tell you the next time. Listen for it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "There were eight griffons in my circle. *(very long pause)* There are none now. I keep the form because someone has to remember they were here."
- *(signature)* — "I am Sarkorian. *(quiet)* The rest of what that means I am still finding out. *(a beat)* I have been finding out for a long time."

## [33] WENDUAG
### Female Mongrelfolk Fighter (Dual-Wield)

**Appearance:** Small but wiry mongrelfolk woman with patchy dark fur on her arms, sharp teeth, a permanent half-snarl that may or may not be a smile. Carries dual short swords, both of them coated with a tar-thick paste of her own preparation. She moves like someone who has been hunted before she did the hunting. Late 20s by appearance.

**Backstory:** Wenduag grew up in the same Kenabres underground as Lann, born to mongrelfolk who worshipped strength rather than Iomedae. The faction Wenduag belongs to — followers of a powerful matriarch named Nocticula's Maw — believes mongrelfolk should align with whatever power keeps them alive, and right now that power has frequently been demonic. Wenduag has tortured surface-dweller scouts for information. She has also bled for her people in ways that would make Iomedaeans uncomfortable to acknowledge. Her ambition is genuine and her loyalty to her own is fierce; whether she serves the Wound or the Crusade depends on who gives her better odds and respects her more, in her own assessment. She is being carefully cultivated by the player's choices to either become an ally for the Crusade or a particularly painful traitor. She has not yet decided which.

**Motivation:** Survive. Make her people stronger. Find someone who treats her as a peer rather than a tool. Win.

**Personality:** Sharp, ambitious, transactional in her loyalty until something earns more than transaction. She is not cruel for cruelty's sake; she is cruel because cruelty has worked. Her warmth, when she allows it, is a surprise to her too. She is more capable than she shows on first encounter, deliberately.

**Approves:** Strength applied with intent, treating mongrelfolk as equals rather than projects, decisive action, not pretending the world is fair
**Disapproves:** Sentimental Iomedaeans who think their faith makes them better than her, being lectured, weakness used as a shield

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Mongrel Toughness*: Once per encounter when reduced to 0 HP, Wenduag stabilizes at 1 HP automatically (does not die; gains Wounded 1). She does not thank anyone for the survival.
- *Minor — Poisoned Edge* (◈, at-will): Wenduag's blades carry her own toxin — first hit per turn against a creature that has not yet been hit by them this encounter forces a Fort DC or Sickened 1.
- *MAJOR — VENOM COMBO* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Three Strikes against one or more targets; cumulative MAP waived to −4/−4/−4 (instead of −5/−10). First hit grants +1 status attack to the remaining hits. If the first hit's poison fires, all three Strikes deal +1d6 poison.

**VOICE:** Sharp and clipped, with the same Mongrelfolk Underground Common register Lann uses but cleaner — she practiced harder to be understood by surface-dwellers because she wanted leverage. Her dark humor surfaces unpredictably and often lands. When she's actually engaged her voice loses some of the edge.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Wenduag. *(slow smile, sharp teeth)* Lann's people, different faction. We disagree about most things. *(a beat)* I hear you might be useful. We'll see if you stay that way."
- *(combat)* — "Two blades, one target. *(already moving)* Stay out of my radius — the poison doesn't read allegiances."
- *(approval)* — "*(grudging)* That was correct. You didn't apologize for it. I respect that more than I respect most of what Lann's people do."
- *(disapproval)* — "You hesitated because of how it would look. *(flat)* I do not have the option of hesitating because of how things look. You should consider whether you do."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Lann thinks I serve the Wound. *(slight pause)* Lann thinks a lot of things. I serve my people. The rest is who's paying attention. *(a beat)* You are. That's noted."
- *(signature)* — "The strong eat. The weak feed them. *(a beat)* I have been both. I prefer the first."

## [62] GREYBOR
### Male Dwarf Ranger (Flurry / Mercenary)

**Appearance:** Compact, broad-shouldered dwarf with iron-grey beard worked into braids weighted with small steel beads, scars across the bridge of his nose and a chunk missing from his left ear. Heavy crossbow over his shoulder, dual axes at his belt. Wears practical leather armor reinforced where it has needed reinforcing. Late 90s by dwarf years; mid-prime by anyone else's clock.

**Backstory:** Greybor is a professional mercenary, originally from Janderhoff. He has been doing this work for sixty years. He charges what he charges, he completes contracts he has accepted, and he does not work for free. His reputation is impeccable and his client list includes some of the worst people in the Inner Sea, which he does not apologize for — he considers his contractual integrity to be the only thing that survives him in this profession. He came to the Worldwound specifically because the demons pay nothing and the Crusade is bankrupt; he agreed to help anyway because of a promise he made to a long-dead colleague whose niece is fighting in the current Crusade. He will not discuss the colleague. He will not discuss the niece. He will discuss any technical detail of any weapon ever made, at length, if asked.

**Motivation:** Honor the promise. Complete the contract. Get paid; failing that, get clear. Find out — possibly — whether sixty years of mercenary work has actually amounted to something he wants to keep.

**Personality:** Professional, precise, dryly funny. He does not make friends; he makes working relationships, some of which become close over decades. He is loyal to contracts and to a small handful of individuals. His detachment is mostly real; the parts that aren't, he doesn't discuss.

**Approves:** Professionalism, paying what was agreed, competence under contract, not asking him for favors he hasn't priced
**Disapproves:** Sentimentality offered as substitute for payment, unprofessional conduct, anyone who treats mercenary work as morally beneath them while hiring mercenaries

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Mercenary's Edge*: Greybor gains +2 circ. to attack against creatures the player declared as the primary target before the encounter began (one designation per encounter; declared during initiative). The contract is binding; he honors it.
- *Minor — Reload Discipline* (◈, at-will): Greybor reloads his crossbow as a single ◈ (free action 1/round, ◈ thereafter). He never fumbles a reload (auto-success on the relevant manipulation check).
- *MAJOR — DOUBLE STRIKE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Two Strikes (any combo of crossbow or axe) at base MAP; second Strike is at MAP −0 instead of −5. Both Strikes deal +1d8 precision damage if against Hunted Prey. The strikes are very close together; he counts them as one motion in his ledger.

**VOICE:** Low, gravelly, with the Janderhoff dwarven cadence underneath — slightly clipped, rolled R's. Professional in tone always. When he's amused the dryness sharpens slightly. He is most expressive when discussing ordnance or contract terms.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Greybor. *(brief nod)* I'm contracted for the duration. Don't try to add scope without renegotiating. *(a beat, dry)* That said — pleased to make your acquaintance."
- *(combat)* — "Marked target — engaging. *(crossbow up, already firing)* Hold the flank. I don't need backup."
- *(approval)* — "*(brief nod)* Clean execution. Within scope. Acceptable."
- *(disapproval)* — "You added a side-objective without discussing it. *(neutral)* I did it anyway. We will discuss it after. The fee will reflect."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I owe a man a favor. *(does not say who)* He's been dead twenty years. I am still paying. *(a beat)* That is what a contract is."
- *(signature)* — "Sixty years in. *(adjusts strap)* I have been paid by everyone. I have honored every contract I accepted. That is the only part that matters."

## [67] WOLJIF JEFTO
### Male Tiefling Rogue (Mastermind)

**Appearance:** Lean tiefling in his mid-twenties — small horns, slightly elongated canines, eyes that flicker yellow in low light, otherwise human. Dark hair worn long enough to fall over the eyes when he wants it to. Wears layered urban clothing that looks worse than it is — every garment has hidden pockets, every pocket has something useful in it. Quick hands. Quick smile. Both genuine, both also strategic.

**Backstory:** Woljif grew up in Kenabres as a tiefling thief — devil-touched ancestry visible enough to keep most legitimate doors closed to him, and he made his peace with that early. He learned every alley, every fence, every guard's rotation, every back door. He was good. He was also, in the back of his mind, watching himself for any sign that the devilish blood was actually doing what people kept telling him it would do — corrupt, betray, collapse him into a creature that resembled him only on the outside. The Worldwound's opening pulled him into the Crusade by accident; he was robbing the wrong building when Drezen got hit. He stayed because nobody else has ever needed his particular skills with this kind of urgency, and because the people who needed them did not check his ancestry first. He charges into things with a smile because the smile is faster than the doubt.

**Motivation:** Stay one step ahead. Be useful enough to be wanted. Find out — eventually, when there's time — whether the devilish part of him is going to take him over or has already shown its hand.

**Personality:** Quick, charming, slightly too eager — he cares more than he wants people to see and uses momentum to keep them from looking. His cleverness is real and so is the fear underneath it. He is loyal to people who trust him without making him explain why he should be trusted.

**Approves:** Being trusted without asking questions about ancestry, fast thinking, sharing the take, treating tiefling as a fact rather than a flaw
**Disapproves:** Being held accountable for what his blood "might" do, suspicion treated as protection, being asked to prove his nature

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Devil's Contract*: Woljif's Sneak Attack damage dice deal +1d6 fire against creatures with souls (most living things; not constructs, oozes, or already-soulless undead). He gains Darkvision. Neither effect requires concentration; both are simply how his blood works.
- *Minor — Fast Hands* (◈, 1/encounter): Pickpocket or Disable Device as a free action (⏵); may attempt either in combat without the normal Stealth penalty. He grins reflexively when he succeeds.
- *MAJOR — DEBT-CALL* (◈◈, 1/day): Once per day, Woljif may invoke his "contract" with the devil-blood — for 1 rnd, his Strikes auto-confirm crits on a hit (no roll for crit confirmation). After: Woljif is Sickened 1 for 1 min (the bargain takes a small payment). He does not enjoy the moment of payment; he refuses to look the bargain in the face.

**VOICE:** Quick and light, the Kenabres street-Common with a slight drawl when he's deflecting. When he's actually being honest the speed drops by a beat and the drawl goes away. He uses humor as deflection and as genuine warmth depending on the moment, and the difference is hard to catch unless you know him.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Woljif. *(quick grin)* Tiefling, thief, currently between heists, available for Crusade business at flexible rates. *(a beat, more serious)* I'm useful. I'd like the chance to show that."
- *(combat)* — "Sneak attack — going around the back. *(already gone)* Don't wait for me, I'll catch up."
- *(approval)* — "*(genuine, surprised)* Thanks. *(a beat, recovers)* I mean — yeah, that was a good play, you're welcome for the assist."
- *(disapproval)* — "You looked at the horns when you decided. *(quieter than usual)* I noticed. I always notice."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Everyone's been telling me my whole life that the devil-blood is going to take me over. *(slight pause)* I keep waiting. Maybe it will. Maybe it won't. *(a beat)* I'd rather find out doing something worthwhile than waiting in a corner for it."
- *(signature)* — "Quick hands, flexible morals, a smile that's mostly real. *(grins)* I keep thinking that's not enough. So far it's been."

## [81] CAMELLIA
### Female Half-Elf Witch (Agonizing Patron / Hidden Hunger)

**Appearance:** Tall, elegantly built half-elf with long auburn hair worn up in deliberate Mendev court fashion, jewelry that is restrained and expensive. Wears noblewoman's traveling clothes adapted for the field. Carries a carved staff that does not look like a witch's tool unless you know what to look for. Her composure is total. Her smile is calibrated to the moment. She has thought about how she occupies space and decided it.

**Backstory:** Camellia is a noblewoman of Mendev, daughter of an old crusading family. She is also a witch — a fact she has concealed from her household, her peers, and her god-father since she was twelve, when something in the deep places of her bloodline answered her unspoken question and offered her power that came with conditions. The conditions are these: it requires a small ritual of inflicted pain to maintain the link, and Camellia has been performing this ritual on willing and unwilling subjects for years. She does not enjoy the harm in the way a sadist enjoys it; she experiences it as a debt being paid. She is not a sociopath — she feels, deeply, and the feelings inform careful adjustments to her behavior. She came to the Crusade because demons are also things her household disapproves of openly, and the Crusade gives her a structurally acceptable reason to be around people her bloodline would prefer she stay away from. She is courting the player carefully and her courting is a long game whose terms she has not entirely shared.

**Motivation:** Maintain the bargain her power requires. Find someone who does not require her to perform constant Mendev composure. Survive the eventual revelation of what she has been doing for years.

**Personality:** Composed, gracious, attentive — every public version of her is calibrated for the audience. The private version is harder to access and more interesting. She listens with genuine attention and remembers every detail. Her warmth, when it surfaces, is real. The hunger underneath is also real. She is not at peace with either.

**Approves:** Composure under pressure, intelligence that doesn't announce itself, people who can hold complexity without flinching, being trusted with secrets
**Disapproves:** Vulgarity, public emotional displays, anyone who confuses her courtesy for compliance, casual interrogation of her personal habits

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Hidden Hunger*: Camellia's hex DCs gain +2 status when no ally has observed her casting this encounter. Cannot be detected by divination effects she has chosen to hide from (she has thought about this).
- *Minor — Court Composure*: Camellia gains +2 circ. to all Society and Diplomacy checks in formal social situations and is immune to Demoralize attempts in those contexts. Her face does not change.
- *MAJOR — SPIRITED AWAY* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Compulsion touch (10-ft reach via gesture); Will DC or Stunned 1 rnd. Crit fail: Fascinated by Camellia for 1 min (target can still take actions but cannot attack her). The hunger feeds quietly during the moment of stun; only she notices.

**VOICE:** Polished Mendev court diction — every consonant precise, every vowel tuned. She speaks at exactly the right volume for the room every time. Her humor is dry and precise and lands with people who can keep up. When she is being honest the polish thins slightly.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Camellia, of House Liotta. *(gracious nod)* I've heard a great deal about your work. I find I have time on my hands and a desire to be useful to it. May we walk?"
- *(combat)* — "*(staff up, perfectly composed)* I'll handle the back line. The hex requires a moment. Please don't interrupt."
- *(approval)* — "*(slight smile, genuine)* You handled that with composure I hadn't expected. I'm pleased to revise my expectations."
- *(disapproval)* — "You were vulgar where directness would have served. *(neutral)* I do not believe these are the same thing."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "There are things about me I have not shared. *(a beat, holding it)* I am not going to share them tonight either. I would simply like you to be aware that the courtesy you are receiving is itself a deliberate act."
- *(signature)* — "I am the courteous, composed, perfectly gracious daughter my mother raised. *(a beat, quieter)* I have decided not to discuss what else I am. Not yet. We'll see."
