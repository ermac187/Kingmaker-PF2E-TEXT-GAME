## BUILD — GUARDIAN REDIRECT + RANGER
**Dwarf (Stone Dwarf) | Ranger (Outwit Edge) + Guardian Archetype | Background: Warrior**
*Guardian Dedication adds Intercept Strike (redirect prey's hit to self at reduced damage) + Threatened Spell. Outwit Edge's +2 AC vs prey stacks with Guardian armor + shield. Tank + hunt hybrid — mark the boss, tank its hits, punish with Hunt Prey damage.*

## BUILD — GHOST-WOLF HUNTER RANGER
**Human (Natural Ambition) | Ranger (Outwit Edge) | Background: Animal Whisperer**
*Wolf companion auto-trips prey via its Jaws attack — flanking geometry is always live.
Monster Hunter chain provides free Recall Knowledge on Hunt Prey (no action cost) and
+1 team AC vs prey. Guisarme reach 10 ft keeps the Ranger adjacent for flanking without
entering the wolf's melee zone. Outwit Edge adds +2 AC and skill bonuses vs Hunt Prey
target — this is the pack hunter: monster knowledge, wolf control, reach strikes.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 16 | 14 | 12 | 12 | 14 | 10 |
| Mod | +3 | +2 | +1 | +1 | +2 | +0 |

**HP** 19 | **AC** 17 (Studded Leather) | **Fort** +5 | **Ref** +6 | **Will** +6 | **Speed** 30 ft
**Weapon:** Guisarme (1d10+3 S, reach 10 ft, trip)
**Skills:** Nature +6, Survival +6, Athletics +7, Intimidation +4, Monster Lore +5
**Features:** Hunt Prey, Outwit Edge (+2 AC/skills vs prey), Animal Companion (Wolf — Jaws 1d6+3, Trip on hit)
**L1 Feats:** Natural Ambition + Animal Companion | **Boost:** STR → WIS → DEX → INT

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Human | Natural Ambition | Two L1 feats takes Animal Companion + Monster Hunter simultaneously — wolf is deployed and Monster Hunter free-RK is live at session 1, no other ancestry matches that. |
| 2 | Catfolk | Nimble | +5 ft speed and Cat's Luck DEX reroll: the Ghost-Wolf needs to pace the wolf's movement to maintain flanking geometry on the prey each round. |
| 3 | Elf | Nimble Elf | 35 ft speed matches the wolf companion's movement exactly — synchronized flanking is the build's entire damage model and requires both bodies adjacent to prey. |
| 4 | Half-Orc | Hold-Scarred | Hold-Scarred Diehard on a reach-fighter who stands adjacent to the boss gives the Ghost-Wolf one extra turn after a crit, keeping wolf flanking active when it matters most. |
| 5 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | STR 16 + Orc base + Diehard — the Orc Ghost-Wolf punches through the prey's AC on reach strikes and refuses to die before the wolf finishes its trip chain. |
| 6 | Halfling | Gutsy | Frightened immunity keeps Outwit Edge's skill bonuses available — fear-aura bosses specifically disable the INT/WIS bonuses Outwit relies on for Monster Hunter's RK chain. |
| 7 | Leshy | Vine | Nature-spirit Leshy + Wolf bond is thematic perfection; Vine Athletics supplements the STR 16 trip-combat without spending feat slots on Athletics investment. |
| 8 | Lizardfolk | Cliffscale | Climb speed lets the Ghost-Wolf follow flying prey that tries to escape the wolf's trip range, maintaining flanking geometry in three dimensions. |
| 9 | Goblin | Unbreakable | +4 max HP compensates the Ranger who deliberately stands at reach 10 ft inside enemy threat range every round to enable wolf flanking. |
| 10 | Dwarf | Mountain's Stoutness | +1 HP/level stacks with 12 HP/level Ranger scaling for a frontline pack-hunter who takes regular retaliatory hits while holding the flanking line. |
