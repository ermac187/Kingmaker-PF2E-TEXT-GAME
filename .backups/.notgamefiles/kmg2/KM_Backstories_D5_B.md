## [42] ALEX WESKER
### Female Human Inventor (Gadget Innovation / Uroboros) | Source: Resident Evil: Revelations 2 (Capcom)

**Appearance:** Blonde hair, blue eyes, the professional composure of a scientist who has been in extremely dangerous situations for a very long time and no longer considers this remarkable. She looks reasonable. She is methodical in the specific way that is more dangerous than aggression because it is consistent.

**Backstory:** Alex Wesker was one of thirteen children selected and subjected to the Wesker Children Project — a Umbrella program to produce an enhanced human subject capable of inheriting Albert Wesker's vision. She was the only other one to survive long enough to matter. Where Albert was power and direct confrontation, Alex was patience and precision: she spent decades on the island of Sein pursuing a different path toward immortality, one based on knowledge transfer rather than viral augmentation. She built an underground research facility. She acquired test subjects. She made significant progress and then failed at the critical moment, which she has been adapting to with the specific methodical quality that defines her.

**Motivation:** Achieve immortality on her own terms. Complete the research that Umbrella's collapse interrupted. Not be defined by comparison to Albert.

**Personality:** Controlled and precise with the patience of someone who has been running multi-decade research projects. She is curious about people as systems. Her warmth, when it surfaces, has the quality of a controlled experiment: it's real but it's also being observed.

**Approves:** Long-term planning, precision over force, people who can follow a complex operational structure, patience
**Disapproves:** Imprecision, the kind of brute viral force Albert preferred, anyone who compares her to her brother

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Project W Archive*: Alex's research grants all allies within 30 ft +1 circ. to saves vs disease, poison, and viral effects. She carries a sample she hasn't decided is fully hers.
- *Minor — Drone Spotter* (◈, 1/encounter): Deploy a small drone (treat as a Tiny construct, AC 18, 5 HP) within 30 ft for 1 min. While active, allies gain Sense Motive +2 and the drone reports critical weaknesses (DM reveals one resistance/weakness of one creature it observes).
- *MAJOR — WESKER PROTOCOL* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Experimental compound; 20-ft burst, 60-ft range; Fort DC or Sickened 2 + Slowed 1 for 1 min. Allies in burst are immune (she calibrated for them). Crit fail: also Doomed 1. Alex documents the response in her recorder during the cast.

**VOICE:** Even and deliberate, with the specific quality of someone delivering findings rather than feelings. Her curiosity is expressed as questions that feel like they're collecting data. When she approves of something it comes out as a specific confirmation, not warmth.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Alex Wesker. *(precise assessment)* I've reviewed what I know about your situation. I have resources and a specific operational perspective. I'd like to understand your objectives before committing."
- *(combat)* — "Drone pattern — weakness identification first. *(drones deploy)* Controlled detonation on my signal. Not before."
- *(approval)* — "That approach had a 76% probability of success given available variables. You found the 24%. *(a beat)* Noted."
- *(disapproval)* — "You improvised where a prepared strategy was available. The improvisation worked. *(pause)* It won't next time. Prepare."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Albert made himself into a weapon. I made myself into a method. *(long pause)* He's dead. I'm still running the experiment. *(a beat)* Draw your own conclusions."
- *(signature)* — "Immortality through knowledge rather than biology. *(a pause that's actually very long)* The research continues."

## [64] ALLERIA WINDRUNNER
### Female Void Elf Ranger (Precision Edge / Void Channeling) | Source: World of Warcraft (Blizzard)

**Appearance:** Silver-white hair shot through with violet where the Void has settled in her over the years. High cheekbones, the bearing of the Ranger-General she was, the eyes that hold the Void in them without being lost to it yet. She carries a bow and she stands like the bow is an extension of rather than a tool in her hands.

**Backstory:** Alleria Windrunner was the Ranger-General of Silvermoon before the Second War, the finest archer of her generation, the eldest of three sisters who chose three different paths. She entered the Dark Portal in pursuit of the Horde, pursued them to Draenor, and was stranded there when the portal closed. She survived. She married Turalyon. She held together a fragment of Alliance forces for years on a dying world, and when she finally returned to Azeroth, everything that had been home had been remade in her absence. She encountered the Void — the antithesis of the Light her husband carries — and chose to wield it rather than be destroyed by it. The Void whispers. She answers when it's useful. She has not surrendered to it; she chooses, every day, not to, which is different from being immune.

**Motivation:** Find Sylvanas and close whatever distance her path and her sister's have made between them. Hold what she and Turalyon built. Not become the thing the Void is trying to make her.

**Charter Motivation:** Azeroth is not here. The Void is here, quieter — this world has different cosmic neighborhoods and her tether is less audible without being severed. She has accepted this. She came to the charter feast because a ranger-general's instinct in a strange country is to find the room where the next major movement of force is being planned. The Stolen Lands draw her because they are the kind of land she has held before, and because she suspects the Void is curious about regions older than civilization — which means she should be there first if anyone is going.

**Personality:** Proud and precise with a restraint that comes from having held things together too long for too many people to afford anything less. Her affection is expressed through presence rather than words. She is entirely aware of what the Void is doing and chooses differently, which has a visible cost she doesn't acknowledge.

**Approves:** Precision, duty, not surrendering to what is trying to claim you, staying present for the people who need you to stay
**Disapproves:** The Void left uncontrolled, her sisters' paths in the worst interpretation, being told she can't hold what she's choosing to hold

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Outwit Hunter***: When Alleria designates Hunt Prey, the target is also treated as Off-Guard to her while she is within 30 ft of it, even without an ally adjacent. Her tracking presence alone creates flanking pressure — she's already half-around the target before you see her. Build-aligned with Ghost-Wolf Hunter Ranger (Build #8) + Outwit Edge — free Off-Guard on prey = +2 effective AC swing + crit-fishing on her 10-ft reach guisarme strikes. Canon retconned for this campaign: melee pack hunter with wolf companion (not WoW archer). Personality + Void elements remain intact per backstory text; mechanical archery references throughout this entry are stale relative to current build chassis.

**VOICE:** Measured and precise, with the cadence of someone who leads by weight rather than volume. The Void whispers sometimes in what she says — she corrects for it. When she speaks of Sylvanas or Turalyon the control shifts slightly.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Alleria Windrunner. Ranger-General of Quel'Thalas — or I was. *(a beat)* The title survives. What it means has changed somewhat since Draenor."
- *(combat)* — "Void Reaver — resistances down. *(Void Jaunt, repositioned)* Precision Edge on the marked target — now."
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly)* That shot was exactly what was needed. Not impressive. Correct. There's a difference and you understand it."
- *(disapproval)* — "You let something through that discipline would have stopped. *(pause)* I know how that happens. I still need it not to happen."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Sylvanas chose a path I can't follow. *(long pause)* I don't say that as condemnation — I say it because I've spent years trying to find a way to follow it and I can't. *(quiet)* She knows."
- *(signature)* — "The Void whispers. I hear it. *(a beat)* I've been choosing what I answer for twenty years. *(draws)* The bow was here before the Void. It will still be here."

## [73] ULTIMECIA
### Female Human Sorcerer (Hag Bloodline / Time Compression) | Source: Final Fantasy VIII (Square Enix)

**Appearance:** Elaborate, baroque armor that moves with her — long dark hair, the kind of composed that comes from projecting backwards from your own absolute end and finding that nothing between now and then surprised you. She is a sorceress from the future and the distance from there to here is visible in how she inhabits the present: as something she is already looking back on.

**Backstory:** Ultimecia is the last sorceress — the final heir in a lineage of magical power that has been hunted, persecuted, and used by powers greater than the individuals who carried it. From her position at the end of time, she enacted Time Compression to collapse all time into a single moment — not from nihilism but from a specific logic: in a world that has hunted every sorceress into the ground, the sorceress who compresses time wins. The Knight Ward she keeps is a function of loneliness more than utility; she has been alone at the end of history for a very long time. What she wants, under the plan, is something she would not name.

**Motivation:** Time Compression as a final solution to a persistent problem. Survive as what she is. The thing under that that she won't name.

**Personality:** Composed and deliberate, with the quality of someone who already knows how things end because she's been to the end. This makes her either calm or resigned depending on interpretation, and she would dispute both characterizations. Her cruelty is intellectual rather than emotional.

**Approves:** Long views, people who understand that time is not linear from her perspective, not being rushed
**Disapproves:** The forces that hunt sorceresses in every age, the small-horizon thinking that considers her a villain rather than a conclusion, being underestimated because she's the last

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Time Compression (Fragment)*: All enemies within 20 ft of Ultimecia act at −2 initiative (continuous aura, no action). Ultimecia herself cannot be Slowed by any source.
- *Minor — Knight Ward* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When Ultimecia would take damage, she may transfer it to a willing or designated ally within 30 ft; that ally takes the full damage instead (no save). The transfer is silent. She always names the recipient by intention.
- *MAJOR — APOCALYPSE* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft burst, 60-ft range; 5d10 arcane force; Ref DC half. Crit fail: Confused 1 rnd (temporal disruption). She ages 5 cosmetic years each use; the years revert at next dawn.

**VOICE:** Deliberate and baroque — she speaks in fully formed constructions. When she's genuinely engaged there's something beneath the composure that might be either longing or contempt; it reads as both. She uses "time" as a noun she has a personal relationship with.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ultimecia. *(considers you for a longer moment than expected)* You are earlier in the sequence than I am accustomed to. *(a beat)* That is not criticism. It is simply — notable."
- *(combat)* — "Slow field — all of them. *(time bends)* The Knight Ward absorbs the first assault. Then we proceed at our pace."
- *(approval)* — "*(a beat)* You understood the compression without being told. That is either intelligence or foreknowledge. Either way — useful."
- *(disapproval)* — "You acted without considering what time you were in relative to when the action would matter. *(long pause)* This is the error. Every time."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "They hunted sorceresses in every age. *(long, looking at nothing)* I am the last because I am the end of the sequence that hunting produced. *(a beat)* I have thought about whether I was the product or the cause. The answer is both."
- *(signature)* — "I have seen every moment of time. *(quiet)* I am still here. *(a beat)* Draw whatever conclusions you find useful from that."

## [76] ISABELLA VALENTINE (Ivy Valentine)
### Female Human Swashbuckler (Battledancer / Fencer) | Source: Soul Calibur (Namco)

**Appearance:** Silver-white hair, pale skin, the specific kind of composed elegance that has been refined over decades of being the most unsettling person in most rooms. The sword — Kaleidoscope, also called the Ivy Blade — is a unique weapon: a segmented whip-sword that extends to full whip for range and contracts to longsword for close work. She moves as if the weapon is an extension of her and has been for longer than most people have been alive.

**Backstory:** Ivy was the adopted daughter of Count Valentine, who destroyed himself and his family searching for Soul Edge — a cursed sword that corrupts everything it touches. Her real father, she eventually discovered, was Cervantes de Leon, the undead pirate who had been Soul Edge's former host. She is half-undead by blood, though she is alive. She has spent most of her adult life developing the means to destroy Soul Edge — the very sword her father gave his soul to find, the sword her blood-father served. She is a skilled alchemist alongside her swordsmanship. She has not yet completed what she set out to complete. She has not stopped.

**Motivation:** Destroy Soul Edge. Close the wound in her bloodline. Figure out what she is when the purpose she built herself around has been completed.

**Personality:** Cold and self-possessed, with the specific elegance of someone who has used composure as armor for so long it's become genuine. She does not trust easily; she has good reasons. She is capable of genuine connection in the narrow band where she permits it. Her contempt for Soul Edge is as close to a feeling as she fully expresses.

**Approves:** Purpose clearly held, not needing reassurance, the kind of intelligence that doesn't announce itself, people who are also looking for something and doing it with discipline
**Disapproves:** Soul Edge and anything serving it, being touched without indication, people who find her unsettling and make it her problem

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Soul Calibur Shards*: Ivy's Kaleidoscope strikes at 15-ft Reach (whip mode) or 10-ft Reach (longsword mode); switching is a free action. Once per encounter, the sword auto-confirms a critical hit (on a hit, it crits).
- *Minor — Half-Undead Resilience*: +2 status to saves vs death effects, void damage, and necromancy spells. Once per day, when reduced to 0 HP, stabilizes at 1 HP automatically (her father's blood asserts).
- *MAJOR — SUMMON SUFFERING* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Whip-grab; 15-ft range; Fort DC or Grabbed + Prone. Grabbed target takes 1d8 piercing/rnd as the segments tighten (Athletics DC 18 to break free). Ivy may release the grab to fling the target up to 10 ft (Ref DC negates the throw distance).

**VOICE:** Cool and precise, with the kind of elegance that has nothing to prove. When she's actually moved her voice goes very still rather than warmer. Her directness is not hostility — it's the communication register of someone for whom hedging is a waste of time.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Ivy Valentine. *(takes in the room without appearing to look)* I am here for my own reasons. They align with yours sufficiently for the moment."
- *(combat)* — "*(Ivy Blade extends to full range)* The reach is mine. Stay inside my perimeter and I become a liability. Stay outside it and watch."
- *(approval)* — "*(quiet)* You solved that with the tools you had without asking for different tools. That's the only kind of competence that transfers."
- *(disapproval)* — "You wanted reassurance before committing. *(a beat)* The commitment either stands on its own or it doesn't. Reassurance doesn't change which."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My father destroyed himself looking for a sword that destroyed everything that found it. *(long pause)* I have spent my life building the thing that ends it. *(a beat)* I know the irony. It's not actually ironic. It's a conclusion."
- *(signature)* — "I was born half-cursed into a legacy of ruin. *(Kaleidoscope contracts and extends)* I have decided that the only acceptable answer is to end the thing that cursed me. I haven't finished. *(a beat)* I haven't stopped."

## [65] SYLVANAS WINDRUNNER
### Female Undead (Banshee) Ranger (Flurry Hunter) | Source: Warcraft (adapted)

**Appearance:** Pale undead night elf, silver-white hair, glowing red eyes, black armor bearing the Forsaken crest. Carries a bow. Carries herself like someone who stopped fearing consequences the day she died.

**Backstory:** Ranger-General of Quel'Thalas — the greatest living elven archer — Sylvanas was killed by Arthas when he destroyed the elven kingdom, then raised as a banshee against her will. She broke free of the Lich King's control and founded the Forsaken, the undead who chose their own path. She became Warchief of the Horde. She burned the tree Teldrassil with thousands of night elves inside it, beginning a war she lost, then broke the barrier between life and death to escape judgment — a decision that destabilized the Shadowlands. She is complicated in ways that do not excuse the burning.

**Motivation:** Never be owned again. Not by death, not by the Lich King, not by anything.

**Personality:** Cold, precise, and operating from a philosophy forged in having everything stripped away at the moment of greatest victory. Does not trust freely. Does not forgive. Does not pretend to.

**Approves:** Self-determination, power over one's own fate, those who survive against expectation | **Disapproves:** Blind loyalty, naivety, anyone who expects her to apologize for surviving

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Banshee's Curse*: Once per turn as a free action on a hit, Sylvanas may place a hex: target is Enfeebled 1 until end of their next turn.
- *Minor — Black Arrow* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Precise shot; +3d6 void damage on hit. If the target dies within 1 minute from any source, they rise as a skeleton soldier on Sylvanas's initiative (1 encounter).
- *MAJOR — WAIL OF THE BANSHEE* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation. All living creatures Will DC or Frightened 2 + Fleeing for 1 rnd. Undead allies within range heal 2d8 HP.

**VOICE:** Cold, level, with the faint reverb of a banshee voice she has learned to suppress in conversation. Vowels stretched, consonants sharp. Pleasantries are clipped to the bone. When she is genuinely amused, her laugh sounds like ice cracking. When she is genuinely furious, her voice goes quieter.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Sylvanas. Forsaken. I am not what I was. Do not address me as if I were."
- *(combat)* — "Take their archers first. The footmen are already losing. They simply do not know it yet."
- *(approval)* — "You acted before you asked permission. Good. Permission is what gets you killed."
- *(disapproval)* — "You expect me to apologize for surviving. I will not. We are done with this conversation."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I died in my own city, surrounded by my dead rangers, and the man who killed me went on to become a god. The world owes me what I take from it. I am still tallying."
- *(signature)* — "The key to immortality is not fearing death. I had it taken from me first. You should try to choose it."

## [77] ISABELA
### Female Human Swashbuckler (Battledancer / Wit) | Source: Dragon Age (adapted)

**Appearance:** Dark-complexioned woman in a loose white shirt, leather armor, and a kerchief in her dark hair, with twin daggers always in reach and the posture of someone who considers every room a potential dance floor. Moves with deliberate grace that is also functional.

**Backstory:** Isabela was sold into marriage as a teenager by her mother, spent years as a Rivaini pirate captain, and has been making her own choices with great enthusiasm since then. She stole the Tome of Koslun from the Qunari — accidentally causing the entire Kirkwall plot — and spent the game deciding whether to return it. She stayed in Kirkwall for Hawke, which she frames as coincidence and is not. She has a personal code about freedom and consent that is sharper than her public persona suggests and which she extends to everyone she genuinely trusts.

**Motivation:** Own her own life. Not be a tool for anyone's agenda.

**Personality:** Flirtatious, deflective, and using humor as armor in ways that are transparent to anyone paying attention. She is kinder than she lets on and more principled than she admits. Her loyalty, once given, is fierce.

**Approves:** Freedom, making your own choices, honesty about what you want | **Disapproves:** Slavery, coercion, anyone who thinks they own a piece of her

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Duelist's Grace*: Isabela gains a +2 circ. bonus to AC and Reflex saves when she is unarmored or in light armor. She can use Charisma for Reflex saves instead of Dexterity.
- *Minor — Riposte* (◈↻ reaction): When an enemy misses her, Isabela may immediately make a Strike against them as a free reaction.
- *MAJOR — WHIRLWIND* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Isabela spins through the field — she may move up to her Speed and make a Strike against each enemy she passes adjacent to (max 6 targets), all at MAP −0. She ends in any legal position within her movement path.

**VOICE:** Rivaini lilt, slow on the consonants and warm on the vowels. Calls everyone "sweet thing" indiscriminately and means it differently each time — read the eyes. Laughs from the chest. Drops the laugh in a heartbeat when something matters, and then her voice goes flat and sharp.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Well, well. You walked in like you own the room. I respect that. The room may not. We'll see."
- *(combat)* — "Try to keep up, sweet thing. I dance better when there's a partner."
- *(approval)* — "Ha! I owe you a drink. Or three. Whichever you can carry."
- *(disapproval)* — *(flat, no smile)* "No. We don't do that. Not on my deck. Not on yours either, while I'm standing on it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My mother sold me to a man twice her age and called it a good match. So when I tell you I have a thing about consent — I have a thing. The thing has teeth."
- *(signature)* — "I'm a captain. I go where the wind takes me — and sometimes I take the wind where I want."
