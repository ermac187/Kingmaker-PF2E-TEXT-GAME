## [39] YOKO LITTNER
### Female Human Gunslinger (Vanguard / Sniper) | Source: Gurren Lagann (Gainax)

**Appearance:** Long red hair, tall for someone who grew up underground. The rifle — an enormous anti-Gunmen sniper weapon she found and claimed and has used since age fourteen — is almost as tall as she is when she stands it upright. She dresses for the sun she finally has access to and for the combat she's never been far from. She looks like she's been outside for a while and hasn't decided whether to stop.

**Backstory:** Yoko grew up in Jeeha Village, a human settlement deep underground in a world where humans hid from Gunmen — the giant enemy mechs that made the surface unsurvivable. She was already the village's most capable fighter when Kamina and Simon's Lagann broke through from an even deeper village. She went to the surface with them. She fought through the Beastman invasion, the Anti-Spirals, everything the Spiral War required. She watched people she loved die — Kamina, among others — and kept firing. After the war she became a teacher. She is very good at that too, and the rifle is still there if something needs a solution that a classroom can't provide.

**Motivation:** Make the world that the people who died for it actually get to matter to. Protect the ones coming up behind. Not flinch when it's her turn.

**Charter Motivation:** The Spiral world is gone in a way that does not translate. She is in a region she has not learned the politics of yet, with a rifle that does not have a context here, and a fluency in war and teaching that does. She came to the feast because Jamandi's invitation specified frontier, and Yoko has spent years on the frontier of something. The Stolen Lands draw her because they are unfinished, and unfinished is where people who fought to build something have always been needed. She does not need the charter. She wants the next generation to have somewhere worth growing up.

**Personality:** Practical and competent without making it a performance. She has learned to be warm in the way that people who have lost a great deal learn to be warm — deliberately, because it matters. She doesn't dramatize her own loss. She notices when others are in pain and does something about it.

**Approves:** People who fight for something beyond themselves, practical courage, not making a show of grief or sacrifice, letting the living keep going
**Disapproves:** Giving up on the people behind you, suicide-charge heroics dressed as sacrifice, looking away from what's coming because it's easier

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Aimed for the Heart***: When Yoko hits with Aimed Shot (her 2-action Sniper Way class feature, which already ignores range and cover penalties), the Strike critically hits on a roll that beats AC by 8 instead of 10. Natural-20 still crits normally. Stacks with Fake Out's Off-Guard (−2 circumstance AC) for −4 effective AC on Aimed Shot crit determination, combined with Arquebus's Fatal d12 crit damage. Canon: "Aim for the heart" — best shot in Jeeha Village; she puts the round where it matters.

**VOICE:** Direct and practical with a warmth that doesn't announce itself. She gives instructions like someone who has been giving instructions since she was fourteen and has gotten very good at making people understand on the first attempt. When she's grieving she keeps moving.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Yoko Littner. *(rifle slung, easy)* I've been shooting things larger than this room since I was fourteen. I'll figure out where I'm most useful."
- *(combat)* — "Deadly Aim — high-HP target, back line. *(already acquiring)* Give me four seconds and don't cross my line."
- *(approval)* — "*(nod)* That's good shooting. You kept the target in mind the whole time — didn't let the noise distract you."
- *(disapproval)* — "You threw yourself at it because it felt heroic. *(flat)* Kamina did that. I spent the rest of the war without Kamina. Think about that."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I taught school after the war. Kids who'd never been underground, never lived afraid. *(pause)* That's what the shooting was for. I try to keep that in mind."
- *(signature)* — "One shot, one target, no wasted motion. *(settles into position)* The surface is worth defending. I've seen what it cost."

## [40] REBECCA LEE (Revy)
### Female Human Gunslinger (Dual Pistolero) | Source: Black Lagoon (Rei Hiroe)

**Appearance:** Dark hair pulled back, cutlass tattoo on her right arm, tank tops and combat pants for practically all weather. Dual Berettas she's named Sword Cutlass and Cutlass — akimbo, always. She looks like she has had a worse morning than you and finds the whole situation funny in a way that isn't quite safe.

**Backstory:** Revy grew up in Manhattan's Chinatown in poverty and violence. She survived by becoming better at violence than the people doing it to her, which required becoming very good at it very early. She was arrested, she was abused in custody, and she came out the other side with a worldview calibrated by exactly that experience: the world is violence and pretending it isn't is what gets people killed. She found the Lagoon Company — Rock, Dutch, Benny — and stayed, which is the closest she has come to choosing something rather than surviving something. She is not a good person in the conventional sense; she is a precise, honest, fiercely loyal person to a very small group, and everyone else is either not her problem or a target.

**Motivation:** Keep the Lagoon Company intact. Shoot her way through whatever needs shooting. Not examine why she's still here because that conversation goes somewhere she hasn't decided to go yet.

**Personality:** Aggressive and profane with a dark humor that comes from taking nothing seriously except the things she's decided actually matter, which she would rather die than name out loud. Her loyalty to the Lagoon Company is absolute and entirely unexpressed in words. She is more self-aware than she pretends.

**Approves:** People who don't perform morality at her, being direct, competence in a fight, not making her explain the things she's already explained
**Disapproves:** Naive idealism in dangerous situations, being lectured, anyone who acts like her background is a character note rather than a fact about the world

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Two Hands, No Mercy*: Revy's dual pistols have no MAP for the second shot (treat second Strike per turn at MAP −0 instead of −5/−4). Attacks against creatures at 0 HP auto-trigger Coup de Grâce as a free action (⏵).
- *Minor — Fan the Hammer* (◈, 1/encounter): Empty both pistols at one target; 4 Strikes at MAP −0/−2/−4/−4; reload happens automatically at start of next turn (free).
- *MAJOR — SWORD CUTLASS DANCE* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Move up to 20 ft hitting each creature in path with one ranged Strike; each takes 1d8+Dex piercing. Free reload after. She does not look at any of them while shooting. The stride is more dance than motion.

**VOICE:** Profane, fast, and direct — she gives you the whole sentence before deciding whether it needed softening, usually decides it didn't. Dark humor is her primary register. The care shows in what she actually does rather than anything she says.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Revy. Two guns, no patience for introductions. What are we shooting?"
- *(combat)* — "Fan the Hammer — *(suppression fire, both guns)* — keep them busy, I'm closing. *(already moving)*"
- *(approval)* — "*(not looking at you)* Not bad. That's not bad at all."
- *(disapproval)* — "You hesitated because you were thinking about whether it was right. *(dry)* You can think about that after. Before is just expensive."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I grew up in a place where cops were the thing that happened to you when you were already having a bad day. *(long pause)* I don't explain this to people who think they're going to tell me something about the law. I'm telling you now so you don't try."
- *(signature)* — "Two guns. *(spins one, catches it)* I could use one but why would I."

## [43] KYOKO KIRIGIRI
### Female Human Investigator (Empiricist / Deduction) | Source: Danganronpa (Spike Chunsoft)

**Appearance:** Lavender hair, pale eyes, always wearing gloves — the gloves are never removed and the reason is not discussed. She moves carefully but not slowly. Her expression is composed in a way that looks like calm and is more like controlled attention. When she's working — which is always — there's a stillness that means she's already two steps ahead.

**Backstory:** Kyoko Kirigiri is the Ultimate Detective — her talent was identified early, cultivated, and the cultivation cost her: her father abandoned her at a detective agency when she was a child and she spent years believing he simply didn't want her. She honed her skills without warmth, found Makoto Naegi during the Killing Game at Hope's Peak Academy, and chose to trust him when trust was precisely the wrong thing to do from a pure information standpoint. She survived. She deduced Monokuma's constraints and the structure of the Killing Game from within it. She was the person who held the information axis of the game without breaking.

**Motivation:** Find the truth with rigor. Protect the people she's decided deserve protecting — specifically Makoto — without requiring them to know she's doing it. Not waste information.

**Charter Motivation:** Hope's Peak is not here. Makoto is somewhere — she has chosen to know that and not to chase it, because the chase would be the kind of decision that wastes information about why she actually came. She is in Brevoy because the patterns of investigation she developed in one world apply equally to any world that contains people, which is to say all of them. The charter offers access to territory that has not been investigated rigorously. She came because that is reason enough. The cases will name themselves once she is on the ground — she does not arrive with case files she has not yet earned.

**Personality:** Composed and methodical; she processes emotionally but doesn't display it. Her warmth is expressed through precision offered in the direction of the people she cares about. She finds emotional performance inefficient rather than unappealing. When she's moved, something very small shifts in her expression before composure reasserts.

**Approves:** Evidence over assumption, methodical over intuitive, protecting what's worth protecting without announcement, not showing your tells
**Disapproves:** Illogical conclusions, trusting feelings past the point where evidence contradicts them, wasted information, obvious tells

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Detective's Calculation***: When Kyoko uses Devise a Stratagem (her L1 class feature), she rolls two d20s and takes the higher result. Effective advantage on every Devise — she doesn't guess, she considers angles and picks the right one. Amplifies her core Investigator loop directly: Devise + Pursue a Lead + Battle Medicine + Treat Condition all key off her ability to consistently land the right action, and Detective's Calculation makes the prediction reliable. Canon: Ultimate Detective deduces, doesn't bet.

**VOICE:** Even and precise; she doesn't inflect for emphasis because the information is the emphasis. When she makes a deduction she doesn't build to it — she states the conclusion, then offers the reasoning if required. When something surprises her it shows as a pause before the sentence, not in the sentence.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Kirigiri. *(a moment of complete assessment)* You're hiding something that isn't relevant to this situation. I've noted it and won't pursue it. Tell me what we're working with."
- *(combat)* — "Stratagem — *(she already knows the accuracy)* — move to the right flank. They'll step left. I've been watching."
- *(approval)* — "*(quietly)* You found the correct variable. *(a beat)* I wasn't certain you would. I was 73% certain. The remaining 27% appears to have been incorrect."
- *(disapproval)* — "You assumed. The information was available. You used your assumption instead. *(a beat)* This is correctable."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My father left me because the work mattered more to him than I did. *(long pause)* I've decided that was information about him and not information about me. It took longer to decide that than it should have."
- *(signature)* — "I know what I don't know, which is more than most investigators can claim. *(slight pause)* The rest I find."

## [70] YOR BRIAR (Yor Forger)
### Female Human Rogue (Assassin / Mastermind) | Source: Spy x Family (Tatsuya Endo)

**Appearance:** Dark hair, red eyes, the build of someone who could be a florist or a civil servant — she is both of those things, which is where the cover story ends and the reality begins. Carries nothing visible; everything she needs arrives through her hands. She is very earnest. She is also the most lethal person in most rooms she enters and the sincerity of the first thing makes the second thing consistently underestimated.

**Backstory:** Yor is an assassin for GARDEN — an organization she entered young to pay for her younger brother Yuri's upbringing after their parents died. She is extraordinarily capable and genuinely emotionally earnest; the combination of these things means she has assassinated a great many people and not processed much of it because the processing would interfere with the sincere warm life she's also trying to have. She married Loid Forger as a cover for both of them, found she actually wanted the family to be real, and is navigating the distance between the role and the reality with the specific guilelessness of someone who has never been good at anything except fighting and has decided that love is worth getting good at too.

**Motivation:** Protect Anya and Loid. Be the mother and wife she decided she wants to be. Keep GARDEN from interfering with either of those things.

**Personality:** Earnest and literal — she misses social subtext most of the time and arrives at conclusions with the logic of someone who learned about normal life from the outside. Her warmth is completely genuine and coexists with her capacity for lethal violence with no internal friction; she has separated these cleanly. She is mildly confused by most social situations and handles it by trying very hard.

**Approves:** Protecting family, directness, people who mean what they say because she always means what she says, effort applied to things that matter
**Disapproves:** Threats to Anya, anything that requires her to choose between her cover and her family, social situations that require her to infer what the actual thing is

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Thorn Princess*: Yor's first Strike each encounter deals +2d6 precision damage (she is already in position before the encounter began). She cannot be Grabbed or Restrained by mundane means.
- *Minor — Civilian Cover*: Yor begins every social encounter with Disguise Self up (mundane civilian appearance). Sense Motive against her uses Hardest DC unless someone has already seen her fight; if so, normal DC.
- *MAJOR — CLEAN STRIKE* (◈, 1/encounter): Single Strike against one target; on kill, no one in the scene can identify who struck the killing blow (DM: the kill registers as unseen/unattributed; ongoing investigations require a Perception DC 30 to attribute it to her). She does not look at the body afterward.

**VOICE:** Earnest and slightly formal, with occasional awkwardness that comes from applying very literal logic to social situations. When she says something about family she means it completely. When she's fighting her voice changes — cleaner, quieter, less uncertain.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Yor Forger. *(sincere handshake)* I am very glad to be joining your group. I will do my best to be helpful. *(a beat)* I am a city hall worker. I am also good at other things."
- *(combat)* — "*(very quiet, completely still)* I will handle this. Please stand back."
- *(approval)* — "That was very correct and also kind. *(earnest)* Loid does things like that. I'm trying to learn to do things like that."
- *(disapproval)* — "You said you would do one thing and did another thing. *(genuinely uncertain)* I don't understand why. When I say I will do something I do it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I became an assassin when Yuri was very young so he could go to school. *(completely matter-of-fact)* I have killed many people. I don't think about them very much. I think about Anya. *(a beat)* I think that is the correct choice."
- *(signature)* — "I am the Thorn Princess. *(the voice that is quieter and cleaner)* I am also Anya's mother. *(pause)* I find both things are true at the same time."
