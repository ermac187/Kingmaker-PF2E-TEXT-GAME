## 📋 DM NOTES

**Overall placement rules:**

1. **Don't flood** — add one or two items from each AP per chapter, not the whole list. Keep loot aesthetic varied.

2. **Player choice on rare ancestry / companion items** — items tagged as companion-fit (Linzi, Harrim, Amiri, Ekundayo) show ★ Best Fit in loot distribution (KM_Loot_Items_Ref.md).

3. **Apex items (marked ★)** — only one per character; gate at L17+ acquisition (endgame only).

4. **Prices are canon PF2e** — use the value-by-level table. No power creep.

5. **Tone fit check:** every item above has been vetted against Kingmaker aesthetic — no Numerian tech, no Alkenstar clockwork, no Nagajor megafauna out-of-place. All fit the "Brevoy border + Stolen Lands wilderness + First World corruption" tonal triangle.

**Loot table integration:**

Add to KM_Loot_Merchants.md loot tables:
