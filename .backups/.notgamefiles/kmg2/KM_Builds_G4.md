## BUILD — PURE TANK KINETICIST
**Dwarf (Stone Dwarf) | Kineticist (Earth + Water Gates) | Background: Warrior**
*Stone Carapace + Metal Carapace stack is unavailable, but Earth + Water gives resist 10 physical AND Redirect flow — become a wall that redirects every attack. The highest-durability Kineticist.*

|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|

| Mod | +0 | +1 | +4 | +0 | +3 | -1 |

**HP** 22 | **AC** 16 (Studded Leather) | **Fort** +8 | **Ref** +5 | **Will** +7 | **Speed** 20 ft
**Weapon:** Earth Blast (1d8+4 B/P, 30 ft / melee) / Water Blast (1d6+4 B, 60 ft)
**Skills:** Nature +7, Survival +7, Athletics +4, Intimidation +3
**Features:** Earth Gate (Stone Carapace — react resist 10 physical), Water Gate (Flow Redirect — react to redirect forced movement), max defensive impulse investment
**L1 Feats:** Stonecunning + Stone Carapace | **Boost:** CON → WIS → STR → DEX

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Dwarf | Stone Dwarf | Stone terrain immunity from your own Stone Wall and Earthen Fortress impulses means you never suffer the difficult terrain you create — a pure-tank Kineticist that controls its own hazard zone. |
| 2 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | CON 18 + Hold-Scarred Diehard means this tank stays at dying 1 through two lethal hits — the Earth/Water wall build needs one extra round to close Stone Sentinel's resistance gap. |
