## 8. KALIKKE & KANERAH ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch2)
### Female Tiefling (twin) Kineticists (Dual Gate)

**Appearance:** Two tiefling women who share one body. Kalikke manifests with blue-tinged skin and an aura of cold calm; Kanerah manifests with warm orange-tinged skin and sharpened features. Only one is present on the Material Plane at a time — the other exists in the First World. The shift between them is visible: skin color changes, posture shifts, the temperature around them drops or rises.

**Backstory:** Kalikke and Kanerah are twin sisters cursed by a fey entity connected to the First World — the dimension of raw, unfiltered nature and fey magic. The curse split them across planes: only one can exist on the Material Plane while the other is trapped in the First World. They cannot both be present simultaneously. They have been living this way for years — trading places, leaving notes, managing a shared life in alternating shifts. Kalikke controls water and cold; Kanerah controls fire and heat. Their kineticist abilities are manifestations of the planar rift between them. Their quest involves finding the fey entity responsible and breaking the curse so both can exist at the same time. Kalikke is gentle, empathetic, and artistic. Kanerah is ambitious, sharp-tongued, and pragmatic. They love each other and drive each other insane. The player's interactions with one are always overheard later by the other, through notes, through shared memory, through the bond.

**Motivation:** Break the curse. Exist together. Stop taking turns being alive.

**Personality:** Kalikke: warm, thoughtful, prone to melancholy about the life they can't share. Kanerah: fierce, competitive, impatient with anyone who doesn't understand urgency. Both: fiercely protective of each other in ways that manifest completely differently.

**Approves:** (Kalikke) Compassion, patience, seeing both sides. (Kanerah) Competence, urgency, getting results.
**Disapproves:** (Kalikke) Cruelty, dismissing feelings. (Kanerah) Weakness, sentimentality that wastes time.

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Twin Gate*: One body, two gates. Kalikke = Water, Kanerah = Fire. Swap dominant soul ◈◈ out of combat (no roll), or ◈◈◈ + Will DC 20 in combat (failure: stays current; one attempt per encounter). Each soul brings her own impulses; switching mid-encounter resets impulse cooldowns for the incoming sister.
- *Minor — Sister's Echo* (↻ reaction, 1/encounter): When the active sister takes damage, the other sister "leaks through" — gain 10 temp HP and one impulse from the dormant sister's element (single use this turn).
- *MAJOR — SOUL FUSION* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Both souls channel simultaneously for 1 rnd; next impulse this turn deals water + fire (target chooses lesser resistance, applies higher); on hit, Fort DC or Stunned 1. They speak in unison during the spell. Neither remembers it cleanly afterward.

## 9. OCTAVIA ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch1)
### Female Half-Elf Wizard (Transmutation)

**Appearance:** Slender half-elf woman with auburn hair, intelligent green eyes, and the careful posture of someone who learned to make herself small before she learned she didn't have to. Carries a spellbook that doubles as a journal. Late 20s.

**Backstory:** Octavia was sold to the Technic League as a young child — by her own mother, who was crying when she did it. Octavia has few memories of before, but remembers the color of her mother's dress. She was trained as a wizard for the Technic League's magical labor purposes. She and Regongar, enslaved together, made many escape attempts before finally succeeding. She is a transmutation wizard because transformation is what saved her — the ability to change what something is, to make something better than what it was. She's idealistic in a way that has survived slavery, which makes it the dangerous kind of idealism — the kind that has been tested and held. She struggles with the gap between what she believes the world should be and what the Stolen Lands actually are. Her relationship with Regongar is romantic and complicated — she tempers his rage, he grounds her idealism, and they both know they're each other's best and worst influence.

**Motivation:** Build something that proves the world can be better than what she escaped from. Use magic for transformation, not destruction.

**Personality:** Intelligent, warm, and quietly fierce about things that matter to her. She debates ethics with genuine passion and is not afraid to be wrong in public. Her optimism is a choice, not naivety — she knows exactly what people are capable of, because she has been their property.

**Approves:** Compassion, freeing the enslaved, using power to improve lives, intellectual honesty
**Disapproves:** Slavery in all forms, cruelty rationalized as strength, wasting potential

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Escape Artist*: Octavia is trained in Thievery and Stealth at creation; gains expert proficiency in both at L7. She can pick up to a Hard lock with a hairpin if no tools are available (one attempt; on failure, the lock jams).
- *Minor — Reshape* (◈, 1/encounter): Touch one mundane object (rope, door, weapon, coin) and reshape it functionally — rope becomes ladder, door becomes wall, sword becomes spade. Lasts 10 min or until dispelled. Her thumbprint stays visible if you look closely.
- *MAJOR — TRANSMUTE THE BATTLEFIELD* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft burst centered on Octavia; ground becomes Difficult Terrain (clay, vines, or flowing water — Octavia picks); enemies in burst Ref DC or Slowed 1 for 1 min. Allies treat the area as normal terrain. The spell's name is also her thesis statement.

## 10. REGONGAR ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch1)
### Male Half-Orc Magus (Sparkling Targe)

**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered half-orc with green-tinged skin, heavy scarring from the gladiator pits, and hands that crackle with barely contained eldritch energy when he's angry — which is often. Carries a bastard sword that he channels spells through. Late 20s.

**Backstory:** Regongar was born free — the son of an orc mother and non-orc father in a Kellid tribe of Numeria — and then sold into slavery. He has some memories of before, including that he had a brother who was not sold with him. This has left him with deep resentment for his origins. He was trained for the gladiator pits. His relationship with Octavia formed during shared enslavement and many failed escape attempts before they finally got out. He is a magus because his magic and his violence are the same thing — he cannot separate them and has stopped trying. He defines loyalty through shared survival rather than shared beliefs. He has complicated feelings about freedom — he knows what it feels like to not have it, but he also knows that the rage that kept him alive in the pits doesn't turn off just because the cage opened. His relationship with Octavia is the closest thing he has to proof that he's more than what the pits made him.

**Motivation:** Never be property again. Protect Octavia. Find out if the rage has a floor.

**Personality:** Aggressive, passionate, and surprisingly perceptive about people who remind him of himself. His humor is dark and usually involves violence. His loyalty is earned through crisis, not conversation. When he's calm, he's good company. When he's not calm, clear the room.

**Approves:** Strength, decisive action, protecting your own, not apologizing for surviving
**Disapproves:** Weakness disguised as virtue, anyone who enslaves anyone, hesitation that costs lives

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Pit Memory*: When Regongar is reduced to half HP or below for the first time in an encounter, gain +2 status to attack and +1d6 damage until end of encounter. The buff ends if he leaves combat early.
- *Minor — Spellstrike Iteration* (◈, 1/encounter): After a successful Spellstrike, immediately make a second Strike at MAP −0 against the same target (single weapon attack only).
- *MAJOR — ARENA HABITS* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Stride up to Speed in a straight line; make a Spellstrike on every enemy in the path (max 4); each takes weapon damage + 2d6 force. Targets Fort DC or Off-Guard until end of next turn. Regongar's sword crackles audibly the whole way.

## 11. LEM
### Male Halfling Bard (Maestro)

**Appearance:** Short halfling man with a cheerful face, an easy smile, and a lute he plays in exactly the same relaxed manner whether the room is friendly or on fire. Practical traveling clothes in muted colors. Moves quietly for someone who talks as much as he does. 20s.

**Backstory:** Lem is a Pathfinder Society field operative who works through performance — bard's license to go anywhere, hear anything, and ask questions nobody else gets to ask. He grew up in a halfling community in Absalom and spent his early years performing in taverns and courtyards before the Society spotted his gift for ending up in the right place at the right moment. He came to the Stolen Lands as a field assignment and stayed because the kingdom-building is genuinely interesting and because Linzi's notes are better than any briefing he's ever received. He does not admit to being a Pathfinder operative in the field. He does not deny it either. He just keeps playing. His personal quest involves a piece of music he's been trying to finish for years — something he heard once as a child that he can't quite remember — and whether finding the rest of it changes anything.

**Motivation:** Go everywhere. Hear everything. Write it down in a form that outlasts all of them. Finish the song.

**Personality:** Cheerful, observant, and much more attentive than the cheerful face suggests. He laughs at the right moments. He also remembers everything. His courage is consistently larger than his body implies.

**Approves:** Stories worth telling, people who are braver than they look, music, directness over pretension
**Disapproves:** Cruelty to the small, pomposity, anyone who assumes a halfling in a corner isn't paying attention

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Halfling's Cheer*: Inspire Courage extends to allies within 60 ft (not 30 ft). Allies using his composition gain +1 circ. to saves vs. fear.
- *Minor — Bon Mot Surge* (◈, 1/encounter): Composition cantrip free + immediate Bon Mot on a target; on success, ally adjacent to target gains Inspire Courage bonus doubled for 1 rnd.
- *MAJOR — THE UNFINISHED SONG* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 60-ft emanation; allies regain 3d8 HP and one mental condition; enemies Will DC or Stupefied 1 for 1 min. Lem plays the only fragment he remembers of his lost childhood song; the magic comes from the not-knowing.

## 12. EKUNDAYO ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch2)
### Male Human Ranger (Flurry)

**Appearance:** Tall, quiet human man with deep brown skin, a longbow that is always strung, and eyes that are looking for something specific at all times. His dog — a large, silent hound — is always within ten feet of him. Mid-30s.

**Backstory:** Ekundayo's family came from Thuvia; his true motherland was Absalom. After his parents died he traveled north to Avistan as a mercenary and wanderer. He passed through the Stolen Lands often, stopping at a village called Bristlehill, where he met a local woman named Amanda. Eventually he settled there and married her. A troll warband led by Kargadd attacked Bristlehill and killed Amanda and his family while he was away. He returned to find what trolls leave behind. He has been hunting trolls ever since — methodically, patiently. He is a devout worshipper of Torag. His dog is his only companion and the only living thing he allows himself to care about. He speaks rarely. When he speaks, it matters. His personal quest is a direct confrontation with Kargadd during the Troll Trouble chapter. The player's choices determine whether Ekundayo can move past his revenge or whether it consumes him. He is not dramatic about his grief. He does not monologue. He hunts.

**Motivation:** Kill Kargadd. Kill every troll between here and Kargadd. Decide what's left after that.

**Personality:** Quiet, methodical, and present in a way that has nothing to do with peace. His silence is not calm — it's loaded. He is capable of warmth but has forgotten the habit. His dog is better at reading people than he does, and he trusts its judgment.

**Approves:** Competence, directness, protecting families, following through on commitments
**Disapproves:** Cruelty to animals, wasting time, mercy for things that eat children

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Dog Soulbound*: His animal companion (Dog) is treated as Mature at L1. Ekundayo and Dog gain +2 circ. attack within 10 ft of each other. Dog never abandons combat while Ekundayo is conscious.
- *Minor — Steady Aim* (◈, 1/round): Free reload + +1 circ. to next ranged Strike against Hunt Prey (the dog flushes the target into line).
- *MAJOR — HUNTER'S TRIPLE* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Three ranged Strikes against Hunt Prey at base MAP (no penalty); all three can critically hit. If the prey is a troll: each Strike deals +2d6 fire (he keeps fire in his quiver).

## 13. JUBILOST NARTHROPPLE ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch2)
### Male Gnome Alchemist (Bomber)

**Appearance:** Short gnome with wild copper hair, burn marks on his eyebrows (they keep growing back wrong), and the permanent squint of someone who spends too much time looking at things that are about to explode. Carries more glass vials than seem safe. 40s.

**Backstory:** Jubilost is a cartographer, explorer, and alchemist who considers himself the foremost authority on the geography of the Stolen Lands — a claim that is irritating specifically because it might be true. He has been mapping the region for years, documenting terrain, cataloguing flora and fauna, and testing alchemical compounds derived from local ingredients. He is arrogant, brilliant, and deeply irritated by incompetence. He joins the party when the player rescues him from a river crossing gone catastrophically wrong — his wagon overturned, his maps scattered, and his dignity in serious question. He does not thank the player graciously. He thanks them with the specific annoyance of a man who needed rescuing and will never fully forgive the universe for it. His personal quest involves ancient gnomish ruins in the Stolen Lands that may contain knowledge the gnomish people have lost. He throws bombs at problems. Most of his problems are solvable with bombs. The ones that aren't, he throws bombs at anyway.

**Motivation:** Map everything. Catalogue everything. Explode everything that gets in the way. Publish the definitive atlas.

**Personality:** Arrogant, precise, and secretly delighted by competence in others (he just can't admit it). His insults are specific and well-researched. His cartography is genuinely excellent.

**Approves:** Intelligence, thoroughness, respecting expertise, not wasting his time
**Disapproves:** Ignorance, carelessness with maps, anyone who says "close enough"

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Explosive Missive*: Bombs gain +1 splash per bomb tier (Lesser +1, Moderate +2, Greater +3) when thrown at creatures flanking an ally. Splash damage ignores Resistance up to 5.
- *Minor — Cartographer's Eye*: Free action 1/scene to identify the optimal throw arc — Jubilost's next bomb that round ignores cover and gains +1 to attack. He sketches the trajectory in the air with one finger first.
- *MAJOR — GNOMISH MONOLOGUE* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; all enemies Will DC vs Jubilost DC or Fascinated 1 min (he is explaining at length why their plan is bad). Crit fail: also Confused 1 rnd. Allies in burst gain +1 morale to attack rolls — they are entertained.
