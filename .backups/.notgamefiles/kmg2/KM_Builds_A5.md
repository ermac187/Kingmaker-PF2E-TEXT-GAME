## BUILD — ARCANE ARTIFICER ALCHEMIST
**Elf (Ancient Elf) | Alchemist (Bomber) | Background: Scholar**
*Ancient Elf heritage provides Wizard Dedication at L1 — spellbook and arcane spells
from the first session, one level ahead of other ancestries. Bombs + Fireball + crafted
scrolls/wands. Highest item crafting ceiling in the game with full arcane access.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 8 | 14 | 12 | 18 | 14 | 10 |
| Mod | -1 | +2 | +1 | +4 | +2 | +0 |

**HP** 15 | **AC** 17 (Wyrmskin Studded Leather) | **Init** +2 | **Perc** +5 | **Speed** 30 ft
**Fort** +5 | **Ref** +6 | **Will** +6
**Weapon:** Alchemical Bombs (1d6+4, splash, 20 ft) + Arcane spells from spellbook | **Armor:** Wyrmskin Studded Leather
**Skills:** Crafting +8, Arcana +8, Occultism +7, Alchemy Lore +8
**Class Features:** Bomber Research (+1 splash/+1 persistent), Advanced Alchemy (5/day), Perpetual Infusions (unlimited moderate bombs), Quick Alchemy
**Key Feats:** L1 Ancient Elf (Wizard Dedication built in: spellbook, 2 prepared arcane spells, 1 spell slot/day) + Calculated Splash
**Boost Priority:** INT → WIS → DEX → CON
