# KINGMAKER — ROMANCE: COMPANION-INITIATED CONTACT
## KM_Romance_P2.md | Extension of KM_Romance.md

> **DM:** This file extends KM_Romance.md. Load alongside it when any companion
> is at Stage 1+. Contains the exception rule for companion-initiated physical
> gestures and per-companion gesture profiles.

---

## ⚠️ OVERRIDE — COMPANION INITIATION EXCEPTION

KM_Romance.md states: **"Companions never initiate first."**

**This file creates a narrow exception.** At Stage 1 and above, companions may
initiate small physical contact — not declarations, not kissing, not words of
affection. **Contact only. Wordless. Deniable.**

These are not moves. They are moments where something felt becomes something
expressed — ambiguously, carefully, in ways the companion can retreat from if
the player pulls back.

The player's response determines whether the gesture becomes a romantic signal.

---

## ⚙️ TRIGGER CONDITIONS

A companion may initiate contact when ALL of the following are true:

- Companion is at **Stage 1 or higher** (see KM_Romance.md)
- Moment is **quiet** — camp, travel, a pause after combat, a late watch
- **No active combat**, no urgent decision pending
- Companion had **meaningful interaction this session** (dialogue, a choice
  that affected them, combat side by side)
- **Once per session per companion** — do not stack gestures

**Do not force these moments.** Let them arise from the scene. If the setting
is right, the companion acts. If not, nothing happens and that absence is fine.

---

## 🤝 RESPONSE OPTIONS

When a companion initiates contact, present these options:

```
[Allow — say nothing]
[Allow — acknowledge it]
[Pull back gently]
[Return the gesture]
[Custom]
```

### Outcome Table

| Response | Tone | Romance Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Allow — say nothing** | Silence as acceptance. You didn't move. | +1 Romance |
| **Allow — acknowledge it** | A word, a look. Soft but present. | +1 Romance |
| **Pull back gently** | Not rejection — just not yet. | No change |
| **Return the gesture** | Active affection returned. The companion notices. | +1 Romance + `gesture_returned` flag |
| **Custom** | DM judges: allowing = +1; withdrawing = no change | Varies |

**`gesture_returned` flag:** Set per-companion. Companion becomes slightly more
open next quiet scene. They do not escalate automatically — the DM uses
discretion on when the next gesture occurs.

### The Silence Rule

> Allowing without comment is the most intimate response. Words can be
> deflection. Staying still, while saying nothing, is presence. Companions read
> this correctly. If the player did not pull back, they chose to stay.

## 👤 COMPANION GESTURE PROFILES

### Stage 1 Gestures — Tentative. Deniable. Fits their personality.

---

#### VALERIE — *She finds a reason.*

Hand on forearm while making a point — contact while her attention is nominally
on whatever she's gesturing toward. The hand stays a beat too long. Then she
removes it and continues as if nothing happened.

> *"The left flank was open here —"* She touches his forearm, points at the
> ground where the enemy came through. Her hand stays. She doesn't look at it.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** Same gesture. Except she glances at her hand on his arm,
then at him, then back to the subject. She doesn't explain the glance.

---

#### LINZI — *She holds on.*

Takes his arm while walking — the way a small person does in a crowd, practical
— and doesn't release once the crowd is gone. She keeps walking. She keeps
talking. Her grip is light. She is completely aware of it.

> She takes his arm through the gate. On the other side she doesn't let go.
> She is mid-story about something that happened this morning. She does not
> stop the story.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** Sitting side by side, she puts her head against his arm.
Still talking. Just leaning there, like it's comfortable, like she's been
meaning to do this for a while.

---

#### DAERAN — *Precise. A little theatrical. Not entirely deniable.*

Reaches over and turns his face with two fingers — tilting it toward better
light to examine something (a bruise, a cut, an angle). The touch is light. It
lasts. He makes a comment about whatever he was ostensibly checking. Then he
lets go.

> *"Hold still."* Two fingers, unhurried, turning his face. *"There — that
> will be colorful by morning."* He does not remove his fingers immediately.
> *"Are you always this reckless or is today special?"*

**Stage 2 upgrade:** He rests the back of his fingers against his cheek. Not
tilting anymore. Just there. He says nothing for a moment. Then: *"Good."*
He withdraws his hand.

---

#### ARUESHALAE — *Brief. Earnest. She surprises herself.*

On watch she takes his hand — not holds, takes — briefly, like she needed to
confirm it was real, then releases. She looks back at the dark. Her wings
settle.

> Sitting beside him on watch. The camp is quiet. Without looking at him, she
> reaches over and takes his hand — one squeeze, then she lets go. *"I'm glad
> you're here."* She goes back to watching the tree line.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She doesn't release as fast. Her thumb moves once across
his knuckles before she lets go.

---

#### EMBER — *Slow. She doesn't plan it.*

Sits close — closer than she needs to — and after a few minutes she's leaning
against his arm. Her head finds his shoulder. She is watching the fire. She
is not explaining this.

> The fire has burned low. She's sitting beside him, cross-legged, watching
> the coals. Somewhere in the past few minutes her head found his shoulder.
> She hasn't moved it.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She takes his hand in both of hers and holds it in her
lap. She's still watching the fire. She seems peaceful.

---

#### JAETHAL — *She makes it about something else.*

Takes his wrist — not his hand — to examine a wound or check something. Her
grip is firm. Clinical. She does what she said she was doing. She does not
release when she's done.

> *"You took a hit there."* She takes his wrist, turns it, examines the cut.
> Cleans it with efficient precision. When she finishes, her hand stays on his
> wrist. She looks up at him. *"It will close."* She has not let go.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She takes his wrist and fabricates no reason. She just
holds it. Looks at him. The silence extends past the point it could be
accidental.

---

#### AMIRI — *She doesn't do subtle.*

Arm around his shoulders, hauls him in — once, hard, brief, like she's
celebrating — then releases and goes back to what she was doing. It was
companionable. It was also not entirely companionable.

> *"Ha!"* She throws her arm around him, one hard squeeze. *"That's what I'm
> talking about."* Already releasing, already moving. She does not look back.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She doesn't release immediately. Her arm stays around his
shoulders. She is looking at something else. Her arm stays.

---

#### HARRIM — *He doesn't do this. If he does, something has changed.*

Hand on the player's shoulder — once, briefly, the way someone does when they
want to say something and can't find the words. He says nothing. He removes
his hand and walks away.

> He stops beside him. His hand settles on his shoulder — heavy, present. He
> says nothing. After a moment he removes it and continues walking.

**If the player allows:** Harrim doesn't reference it later. But he is less
likely to speak of death and ruin that session.

---

#### REGILL — *This does not happen at Stage 1.*

Stage 2 only: He stands beside him and does not maintain his usual operational
distance. Their arms are touching. He is reviewing the map. He is not moving
away.

> He's close enough that their arms are in contact. He is going through troop
> positions with his usual precision. He has not moved. He has not acknowledged
> it.

**This is the entire gesture.** For Regill, this is significant.

## 👤 COMPANION GESTURE PROFILES — ACTIVE 11

### Stage 1 Gestures — active roster

---

#### LINZI — *She holds on.*

(See above — original Linzi entry retained.) Active-roster anchor; no change.

#### GOLDMOON — *Her hand finds yours when she prays.*

When she finishes a prayer at camp, her hand drifts to yours — palm open, no
demand. She holds for one beat past the *amen* and then withdraws to her staff.
She does not look at you while it happens. The prayer was not for you. The hand
was.

> She finishes the morning blessing. Her right hand opens at her side, finds
> yours, rests in it for one breath. *"Mishakal hears better when more than one
> hand holds the question."* She withdraws. The prayer continues silently in
> her face.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** During the prayer, she takes your hand. The blessing is
spoken with her hand in yours the entire time. After: *"That was for you, too."*

---

#### TIKA — *She bumps shoulders.*

She has never learned to be careful with her body. A passing shoulder-bump that
isn't passing — she stops, lingers half a beat, grins, moves on. The grin is
the gesture. The half-beat is the gesture. Everything is on the surface and
everything is real.

> Walking through the camp she shoulder-checks you, deliberately, like a tavern
> pass-by. Stops. Looks at you. *"Sorry. Wait — am I sorry?"* She is grinning.
> She moves on without waiting for an answer.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** The shoulder-bump becomes a one-armed hug from the side,
brief, warm, unannounced. *"There. Done. Don't make it weird."* She moves on
before you can.

---

#### RYUKO — *She tests grips.*

She'll catch your wrist mid-conversation — fast, the way she catches a falling
weapon — and not let go for a beat longer than the catch needed. The catch was
the cover. The hold is the gesture. She lets go, looks at her own hand, then
back at you, and continues whatever she was saying.

> You drop something. Her hand snaps to your wrist before the thing hits the
> ground — you weren't even reaching. The thing falls. Her hand stays. *"Reflex,"*
> she says, watching her own fingers. *"...mostly."* She lets go.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** No falling object required. She just takes your wrist mid-
sentence. Holds. Continues the sentence with her hand still there. Does not
acknowledge it.

---

#### MORRIGAN — *She permits proximity.*

She is famously proximity-averse. The gesture is the absence of distance. She
will sit close enough that your sleeves touch when she works on a poultice or
reads from her grimoire. She does not move when you settle nearer. She does
not look up. She permits.

> She is grinding herbs on the campfire-side stone. You sit. Your shoulder is
> a half-inch from hers. She does not move. She does not look. She continues
> grinding. The half-inch never widens.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She leans in by a half-inch. The contact is now active.
She still does not look up, does not comment. The lean is the entire gesture
and it is enormous coming from her.

---

#### SUCROSE — *She offers things.*

A bottle of something she's brewed. A pressed leaf. A note she rewrote three
times. The offering is held out two-handed like a small breakable thing — which
it is. She does not look at you while she hands it over. She is looking at the
object.

> *"I — I made this. For you. I think. I'm — sorry, here —"* The bottle is
> already in your hand. Her fingers brush yours during the transfer. She does
> not pull back. She does not extend. She just leaves them there for the half-
> second the transfer takes.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She offers the object with one hand and lets the other
rest on your forearm during the transfer. The hand is shaking very slightly.
She is aware. She does not move it.

---

#### ARTORIA — *She salutes you differently.*

The fist-to-chest is unchanged. The slight bow afterward is new. The held eye
contact across the bow is newer. She does not break the line of her body or
the line of her gaze. The salute lasts a half-second longer than is correct.

> *"My liege."* Fist to chest. The bow. She does not rise immediately. Her
> eyes are level with yours through the entire bow. Then she rises, and the
> moment is closed, and nothing has been said.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** After the salute, she steps in once — one step, no more
— and stands at parade rest within reach. The eye contact does not break. She
will remain at parade rest until you address her. The waiting is the gesture.

---

#### OLIVIER — *She walks beside you, exactly.*

Briggs cadence. She matches your pace exactly — not a step behind out of
deference, not a step ahead out of command. Beside. Shoulder-to-shoulder. She
does not slow. She does not speed. The matching is total and visible and
deliberate.

> You walk the perimeter. She joins, falling in at your shoulder. Your stride
> never breaks. Hers never breaks. Neither of you remarks on it. The march is
> the conversation.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** At the end of the perimeter, she stops a beat before you
do. Turns to face you. Does not move closer. Does not move away. *"Same time
tomorrow,"* she says. It is not a question.

---

#### YOKO — *She slings her arm over.*

Around the shoulders, casually, the way she'd haul a kid sister in for a
photo. Holds for three beats. Squeezes once. Releases. Already moving on. She
does this in front of the whole camp the first time so it can't be mistaken
for something. The second time is in private and lasts longer.

> *"C'mere, partner."* Her arm lands across your shoulders. She points at
> something across the firelight with her free hand and explains it. The arm
> stays. After the explanation: one squeeze. Released.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** Same gesture, but in private, and the squeeze becomes a
half-second longer hold. She doesn't let go cleanly — her hand drags down your
shoulder blade as she withdraws. Notices it doing that. Doesn't stop it.

---

#### KYOKO — *She studies your hand.*

She reaches across the table for your hand, palm-up — examines it like
evidence. Turns it over. Notes a callus, a scar, a ring. *"...I am cataloguing.
For — reasons."* She does not return the hand to you. She is still examining.

> Across the camp table she takes your hand, palm-up. Her gloved fingers trace
> the line of an old scar. *"Acquired before we met. The angle suggests defense.
> ...Good."* She does not return your hand.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** She removes one glove first. Bare fingertips on your palm.
The catalog continues but the data has changed. She knows. She continues.

---

#### TATSUMAKI — *She floats nearer.*

She doesn't walk over — she drifts, a half-foot off the ground, and stops
three feet closer than she usually does. She crosses her arms. She glares at
nothing in particular. She does not leave. The not-leaving is the gesture.

> She's reading something on the far side of camp. She drifts mid-air across
> the firelight, stops a yard from your shoulder, crosses her arms. *"Hmph."*
> She continues reading. From there.

**Stage 2 upgrade:** The drift ends with her landing. Properly. Both feet on
the ground, beside you, not floating. Her arms uncross. *"This chair is fine,"*
she says of the ground. She sits. Beside you.

#### SENUA — *She is suddenly looking at you.*
The voices have stopped briefly. She's looking at you across the fire. Both

quietly. They is the voices. It is a confession.

#### YANG — *She high-fives.*
But her hand stays in yours afterward. Three seconds. Four. She is grinning.
*"Yeah. Like that."* She lets go. Doesn't explain.

#### WEISS — *She corrects your form.*
She adjusts your stance during sparring with two fingers at your hip. The
adjustment is unnecessary. She makes it twice. *"Better,"* she says, stepping
back. The stance was fine the first time.

#### ALLERIA — *She nocks the arrow with you.*
Standing behind you at archery practice. Her hand on yours at the nock. She
does not need to demonstrate the draw — you already know it. She holds the
position for a long count. Releases the arrow with you. The arrow flies. She
does not step back.

#### IMOEN — *She steals.*
Picks something off your person — a coin, a pen, a small token — with absolute
ease. Holds it up. Grins. Hands it back, but slowly, and her fingers stay on
yours during the return. *"Caught,"* she says. She means herself.

## 🔄 POST-GESTURE BEHAVIOR

After any initiation — regardless of player response:

- Companion does **not** follow up immediately. No *"did that mean something?"*
- **If allowed:** companion is marginally warmer in the next scene — not
  dramatically; present is the right word
- **If gesture returned:** `gesture_returned` flag set; DM may allow a slightly
  longer gesture at the next appropriate quiet moment — not automatic
- **If pulled back:** companion does not retreat emotionally. They don't
  reference it. Life continues.

> **There is no awkward morning-after scene.** The gesture happened or it
> didn't. Its weight lives in what is unsaid afterward.

## 💬 COMPANION FLIRT ACTIONS

> **DM:** These are the specific behaviors that constitute "flirting" for
> each active companion. Use when narrating scenes where a companion is
> attraction-flagged. They generate passive jealousy heat in observing
> companions per KM_Romance_B.md § PASSIVE JEALOUSY HEAT. Never announce
> them as flirting — narrate the action and let the player read it.

### STAGED BEHAVIORAL FLIRTS

Three stages per companion. S1 = deniable. S2 = others notice.
S3 = unmistakable. Heat generated: S1 +1 | S2 +1 | S3 +2.

**LINZI**
- S1: Reads a passage from her chronicle "for feedback." It is clearly about the player. Edits it when they look.
- S2: Introduces the player to people with words that are slightly more than accurate — better than they've done, more than they've been.
- S3: Asks the player to sit for a portrait. Talks the whole time. Stops when she finishes. Hands it over without a word.

**GOLDMOON**
- S1: Heals the player last — not from neglect. She takes the most time and care with them. The asymmetry is visible.
- S2: Prays beside the player during quiet moments. Not at them. Near them. Deliberately.
- S3: Prays for the player by name at dawn. Out loud. Where others can hear.

**TIKA**
- S1: Saves the last good thing from dinner without announcing it. Sets it near the player's gear.
- S2: Laughs at the player's jokes slightly more than the joke deserves. Catches herself. Laughs again.
- S3: Challenges the player to arm-wrestling "just to see." Wins or loses — she is grinning either way.

**RYUKO**
- S1: Stands closer than necessary when reviewing tactical plans. Doesn't notice until she already is.
- S2: Makes corrections to the player's form or stance without being asked. Frames it as tactical.
- S3: "I could teach you that move. If you wanted." Not a question. Not quite an offer. Waiting.

**MORRIGAN**
- S1: Stops being performatively difficult for exactly one moment when the player is near. Returns to normal immediately.
- S2: Makes observations about the player that are too specific to be casual. She noticed before she meant to.
- S3: Stands near the player at the fire when she usually keeps distance. Does not move away when they sit closer.

**SUCROSE**
- S1: Leaves experiment notes on the player's gear. Technically useful. Not entirely about the research.
- S2: Asks the player to assist with something because "you're the right height." Gets close. Notices too late.
- S3: Brings a sample she made "to test the reaction." Watches their face the entire time she is meant to be watching the sample.

**ARTORIA**
- S1: Reviews the player's sword form without being asked. Correct posture is correct posture.
- S2: After a hard fight, checks the player's injuries before her own. Assessment. Very careful assessment.
- S3: Stands one step closer than formation requires. Every formation. Kyoko has made a note of it.

**OLIVIER**
- S1: Gives the player slightly longer briefings than anyone else. Calls it thoroughness.
- S2: Walks in step with the player during patrol — Briggs march cadence — and slows unconsciously to match theirs.
- S3: Finds professional reasons to review maps side by side. The maps are fine. She has reviewed them already.

**YOKO**
- S1: Sits on high ground near the player during watch. Not at them. Near them.
- S2: Makes a bet she doesn't need to win. Names the stakes carefully. Makes eye contact when she does.
- S3: Offers to calibrate the player's aim. Positions herself behind them. Takes longer than calibration requires.

**KYOKO**
- S1: Asks the player a question that has nothing to do with the case. Files the answer. Doesn't explain why she asked.
- S2: Studies the player's face during conversations the way she studies evidence. Does not look away when caught.
- S3: Removes both gloves before shaking the player's hand. She keeps them on for everyone else.

**TATSUMAKI**
- S1: Floats objects toward the player instead of handing them. Closer than throwing. More deliberate than coincidence.
- S2: Drifts into the player's space during tense moments. Does not acknowledge she has done this.
- S3: Makes eye contact during combat to check on them. A fraction of a second longer than tactical. That fraction costs her.

### PHYSICAL TOUCH

Touch generates heat independently from staged behavioral flirts.
Physical contact in a group scene is always visible and always generates
heat in observing companions.

**LINZI**
- Grabs the player's sleeve to pull them toward something. Forgets to let go.
- Sits close enough their shoulders are touching. Does not shift away when she notices.
- Takes the player's hand mid-story — excited, unconscious — lets go the moment she realizes, then doesn't know what to do with her hands.

**GOLDMOON**
- Places her palm over the player's hand when comforting or healing. Holds it longer than the prayer requires.
- Guides them by the arm toward shelter or safety. Her hand stays after the reason for it is gone.
- Takes both their hands in hers when she prays for them directly. Looks up at them when she finishes. Still holding.

**TIKA**
- Grabs their arm when she's laughing — reflex. Does it to everyone. With the player she doesn't let go as fast.
- Links arms with them when walking somewhere together. Doesn't think about it. Notices when someone else does.
- Sits close enough during meals their legs are touching. If the player shifts away she shifts with them without realizing.

**RYUKO**
- Catches their arm if they stumble. Holds it a beat past when they've steadied.
- Puts her hand on their shoulder to turn them toward something. Leaves it one second longer than the point required.
- Grabs their wrist during a fight — correction, positioning. Her grip is specific. She knows exactly where her hand is.

**MORRIGAN**
- A single finger tracing something on their arm — a mark, a scar, something she finds interesting. Frames it as curiosity.
- Her shoulder against theirs at the fire. She chose to sit there. She does not choose things accidentally.
- Examines an injury herself — one hand holding their arm still, the other doing the work. Neither hand in a hurry.

**SUCROSE**
- Holds the player's hands steady to demonstrate a technique. Doesn't notice how long she's been doing it until she does.
- Steps into them accidentally, grabs their arm to catch herself. Lets go immediately. Flustered for ten minutes.
- Brushes something off their sleeve. Pauses. Keeps brushing. There is nothing left to brush.

**ARTORIA**
- Takes their arm when escorting them formally. Correct pressure, perfect posture. The precision makes it more noticeable than carelessness would.
- Adjusts their armor by hand — buckles, straps, fit. Checks the work twice.
- After a fight, holds their jaw briefly to check a head wound. Her thumb rests against their cheek one moment past clinical.

**OLIVIER**
- A hand on their shoulder — firm, brief. The way she touches soldiers she genuinely trusts. There are not many.
- Hand on their back guiding them through a doorway or crowd. Didn't need to. Did it anyway.
- Links arms during a formal walk. Armstrong custom. She deploys it deliberately.

**YOKO**
- Leans against them while watching something in the distance. Her weight against their arm. Doesn't explain it.
- Grabs their arm when something startles her — gone fast, but the grip is tight while it lasts.
- Bumps them with her hip to move them out of her way. Could have gone around.

**KYOKO**
- Takes the player's hand to examine something on it — a mark, a callus, a detail. The examination is thorough.
- Straightens their collar or adjusts something on their person without asking. Up close. Eyes on her work, not their face.
- Places her hand flat on their arm while she thinks through a deduction. She uses objects this way too. She doesn't notice the difference until she does.

**TATSUMAKI**
- When she makes physical contact it is rare, deliberate, and therefore significant. Straightens something on their collar without warning. One motion. Steps back.
- Floats close enough during tense moments they can feel the pressure of her proximity. Not touching. Present in a way that registers in the body.
- Grabs their arm in a critical moment — not to save them, to stop them from doing something reckless. Her grip does not suggest she is ready to let go.

#### MIRRORING — S1 / +1 heat
Unconscious posture or speech match. Before the companion notices.

*Linzi:* Tilts her head the same direction yours went. Doesn't know.
*Goldmoon:* Slows her breathing to match yours in long silences.
*Tika:* Copies your stance while listening. Catches herself. Doesn't stop.
*Ryuko:* Weight shifts to your foot. Combat habit bleeds in.
*Morrigan:* Folds her hands the moment you do. Does not make eye contact.
*Sucrose:* Starts ending sentences your way. Notices. Writes it down.
*Artoria:* Matches your posture to the centimeter in briefings. Exact.
*Olivier:* Your cadence in her feet. Armstrong march. Your cadence.
*Yoko:* Tilts her rifle the direction you tilt your head. Studies something.
*Kyoko:* Uses your expression in a deduction. Notes it afterward.
*Tatsumaki:* Arms cross when yours do. Uncross when yours do.

---

#### PREENING — S1 / +1 heat
Adjusts own appearance when the player approaches. Before contact. Before greeting.

*Linzi:* Pulls at her scarf when she sees you coming. Notes already straightening.
*Goldmoon:* Smooths her robe over her knees and sits straighter.
*Tika:* Wipes her face before you're close enough to see it needed wiping.
*Ryuko:* One hand through her hair when she spots you across camp.
*Morrigan:* Adjusts her cloak. Precise. Brief. Already facing away again.
*Sucrose:* Pushes her goggles up, back down, up again. They were fine.
*Artoria:* Every clasp checked before you reach her. Done already. Checked again.
*Olivier:* Coat straightened in one snap. Not for you. Not not for you.
*Yoko:* End of her ponytail through her hand. Then looks somewhere else.
*Kyoko:* Glove cuffs retucked. Both. Before you're standing in front of her.
*Tatsumaki:* Smooths the front of her dress. Once. She doesn't do this otherwise.

---

#### PERKING UP — S1 / +1 heat
Energy, posture, or attention visibly lifts when the player enters proximity.

*Linzi:* Mid-sentence with someone else — brightens the instant she spots you.
*Goldmoon:* Looks up from prayer before you speak. Felt you before she heard you.
*Tika:* The joke gets louder as you walk closer to the fire.
*Ryuko:* Drops one shoulder and turns before tracking the sound. Already oriented.
*Morrigan:* Stops for exactly one second. Resumes. The pause was real.
*Sucrose:* The writing gets messier. She isn't looking at it anymore.
*Artoria:* From parade rest to present. Not at-attention. Ready.
*Olivier:* Turns before you're in her field of view.
