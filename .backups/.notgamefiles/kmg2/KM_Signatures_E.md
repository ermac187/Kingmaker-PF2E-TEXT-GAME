> **Format:** POWER (passive) · MOVE (active + action cost + cadence) · LORE (canon hook)
> **Action icons:** ◈ = 1 action · ◈◈ = 2 · ◈◈◈ = 3 · ↻ = reaction · ⏵ = free

> **Use scaling:** L1–5 as listed · L6–10 +1 · L11–15 +2 · L16–20 +3 (unless fixed)

## ⚡ MAGUS (#49, 50)

**[49] Weiss Schnee** — Magus (Pure Spellstrike — Laughing Shadow + Rapier)
  SIGNATURE — *Schnee Dust Chamber* (Passive): At start of each turn, switch
    Spellstrike spell's elemental damage to fire / cold / electricity / force
    (gravity) as free action. Spellstrike adds +1d4 of that element. Canon:
    Myrtenaster's rotating dust chamber loaded into her rapier. Build-aligned
    with Pure Spellstrike Laughing Shadow — every Spellstrike gets elemental
    variety + bonus damage on top of INT-keyed spell rank.
  LORE — Schnee heiress who left the name behind; still figuring out what she kept.

**[50] Cinder Fall** — Magus (Inexorable Iron/Flame)
  POWER — *Fall Maiden's Power*: Cinder's fire spells ignore Resist Fire up to 10.
    Spellstrike with fire deals +1d6 fire; target is Dazzled 1 rnd.
  MOVE — *Meteor* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 60-ft range, 10-ft burst; 5d8 fire + 2d8
    bludgeoning; Ref DC half. Cinder descends in the burst (she is the meteor).
  LORE — Came for recognition and found power; still asking why the landing felt hollow.

## 🥋 MONK (#53, 54)

**[53] Tifa Lockhart** — Monk (Brawler/Flurry)
  POWER — *Premium Heart*: Each successful unarmed Strike adds +1 circ. to her
    next unarmed Strike (max +3 stacking). Resets on a miss.
  MOVE — *Final Heaven* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 6 Strikes at −2 each (cumulative MAP
    waived below −6); if all 6 hit: +2d8 and target Stunned 1 rnd.
  LORE — Seventh Heaven's keeper who kept her promise; now asking what she wants for herself.

**[54] Sandra Wu-San (Lady Shiva)** — Monk (Stumbling Stance)
  POWER — *Dragon's Art*: Sandra Wu-San (Lady Shiva)'s unarmed Strikes ignore Hardness and Damage
    Reduction. On a critical hit, she chooses: Stunned 1, Prone, or Grabbed.
  MOVE — *Leopard's Claw* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Three precision Strikes, MAP waived;
    each deals +1d6 precision (she targets weak points every time).
  LORE — The world's best martial artist; she fights to find the only present moment that makes her fully alive.

## 🔮 ORACLE (#56, 57)

**[56] Rei Hino (Sailor Mars)** — Oracle (Flames Mystery)
  POWER — *Foresight*: Rei Hino (Sailor Mars) cannot be surprised. Once per encounter (before
    result known), reroll any one die roll; keep the better result.
  MOVE — *Burning Mandala* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 20-ft burst; 4d8 fire + Fort DC or
    Sickened 1 rnd. Invisible enemies in burst are revealed for 1 rnd.
  LORE — Hino Rei; fire shrine maiden whose foresight is a responsibility, not a gift.

**[57] C.C.** — Oracle (Lore Mystery, Geass-Contract)
  POWER — *Immortal Code*: Once per day, C.C. cannot die — drops to 1 HP and
    recovers after 1 minute. She does not volunteer this information.
  MOVE — *Geass Contract* (◈◈, 1/day): Touch; Will DC or Compelled to fulfill one
    spoken command (1 harmless action). Only one contract active at a time.
  LORE — Contractor of a thousand years; she keeps making contracts because hope hasn't stopped working yet.

## 🧠 PSYCHIC (#58, 59)

**[58] Tatsumaki** — Psychic (Chronos Master / Infinite Eye + Precise Discipline)
  SIGNATURE — *Foresight Aura* (Passive): Allies within 30 ft of Tatsumaki
    gain +1 status to Reflex saves and AC against attacks they could see coming
    (non-Hidden/non-Undetected sources). She murmurs warnings half a beat
    before strikes land. Canon retconned for this campaign: temporal precog
    psychic, not pure TK. The "Tornado of Terror" reads as psychic-storm
    presence, not physical telekinesis.
  LORE — Number 2 Hero; still noticing that strength and connection are not the same thing.

**[59] Emma Frost** — Psychic (Thoughtform/Telepathy)
  POWER — *Diamond Form*: Once per encounter, Emma shifts to diamond — immune to
    mental effects for 1 min. During Diamond Form: +2 AC; all telepathy suspended.
  MOVE — *White Queen's Gambit* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft range; Will DC or Dominated
    1 rnd (one action Emma dictates) + Stunned 1 rnd after. Crit fail: 3 rnds.
  LORE — Telepath who built armor out of control; still finding what the armor was for.

## 🏹 RANGER (#64, 65)

**[64] Alleria Windrunner** — Ranger (Ghost-Wolf Hunter / Outwit Edge, Guisarme + Wolf)
  SIGNATURE — *Outwit Hunter* (Passive): When Alleria designates Hunt Prey,
    the prey is also treated as Off-Guard to her while she is within 30 ft —
    no ally needed. Free flanking pressure on every prey. Build-aligned with
    Outwit Edge + 10-ft guisarme reach (Off-Guard widens hit AND crit window).
    Canon retconned: melee pack hunter (wolf companion), not WoW archer.
  LORE — Windrunner sister who touched the void and is still managing the conversation.

**[65] Sylvanas Windrunner** — Ranger (Precision Edge)
  POWER — *Banshee Queen's Will*: On kills, Sylvanas may Demoralize all enemies
    within 30 ft as a ⏵ (they witness the death).
  MOVE — *Black Arrow* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Precision shot; on kill, target rises
    as skeleton under her command until encounter ends (1 HD per level).
  LORE — Ranger-General turned Warchief; death is not an end she intends to honor.

## 🗡️ ROGUE (#69, 70)

**[69] Imoen** — Rogue (Opportunistic Thief / Thief Racket)
  SIGNATURE — *Flank Caller* (Passive): When Imoen and an ally flank an enemy,
    the ally's Strikes against that enemy gain +1d6 precision damage. Stacks
    with normal flanking Off-Guard. Build-aligned with Gang Up (her L1 feat —
    auto-flanking with any ally) and Dread Striker. Canon: BG Imoen as the
    partner who has the PC's back — she works better paired.
  LORE — Candlekeep's problem child; was Bhaal's daughter before she was anyone else's friend.

**[70] Yor Briar (Yor Forger)** — Rogue (Elusive/Precision)
  POWER — *Thorn Princess*: Yor Briar's first Strike each encounter deals +2d6 precision
    (already in position). She cannot be Grabbed or Restrained by mundane means.
  MOVE — *Clean Strike* (◈, 1/encounter): Single Strike; on kill, no one in the
    scene can identify who struck (DM: kill registers as unseen/unattributed).
  LORE — Assassin wife and mother; the cover stopped being cover some time ago.

## ✨ SORCERER (#72, 73)

**[72] Rachel Roth (Raven)** — Sorcerer (Demonic Bloodline/Soul-Self)
  POWER — *Azarath's Shield*: Once per encounter (↻ reaction): one ally within 60
    ft takes half damage from one incoming attack (Rachel Roth absorbs the rest).
  MOVE — *Soul Self* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Project soul; 30-ft range; Will DC or Stunned
    2 rnds + one secret revealed. Can also pull an ally free of a mental effect.
  LORE — Trigon's daughter; controlled her feelings to protect others. The feelings have been waiting.

**[73] Ultimecia** — Sorcerer (Arcane Bloodline/Time)
  POWER — *Time Compression (Fragment)*: All enemies within 20 ft act at −2
    initiative (continuous aura). Ultimecia cannot be Slowed.
  MOVE — *Apocalypse* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft burst; 5d10 arcane force; Ref DC half.
    Crit fail: Confused 1 rnd (temporal disruption). She ages 5 years each use (cosmetic).
  LORE — Sorceress who hated humanity for ten centuries; forgot grief was the whole of it.

**[75] Yuna** — Summoner (Fayth-Bonded)

    (4d6 cold blast, 30 ft), Bahamut (6d10 force, 60-ft line).
  LORE — High Summoner who walked the pilgrimage knowing the end and chose it anyway.

## 🔱 THAUMATURGE (#78, 79)

**[78] Ryuko Matoi** — Thaumaturge (Regalia / Aura King, Whip-as-Scissor-Blade)

## 🧹 WITCH (#82, 83)

**[82] Medusa Gorgon** — Witch (Twisted Love Patron)
  POWER — *Vector Control*: Once per encounter (↻ reaction): reverse the direction
    of any single-target attack that hits Medusa — misses and targets attacker instead.
  MOVE — *Madness Wavelength* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; Will DC or Confused
    1 rnd + Frightened 1. Already-Frightened targets: Confused 2 rnds. Crona immune.
  LORE — Witch who sharpened herself into a weapon; still deciding if Crona was reason or first casualty.

**[83] Morrigan** — Witch (The Resentment Patron / Hex-Bound Shepherd)
  SIGNATURE — *Cackle of the Wilds* (Passive): When Morrigan uses Cackle
    (free-action hex sustain), she may also extend one additional active hex
    on a different target. Maintains TWO hexes per turn instead of one.
    Direct amplification of her curse-shepherd identity. Stacks with Greater
    Lesson chains at higher levels. Canon: Flemeth taught her to layer curses
    — never release one before the next is set.
  LORE — Flemeth's daughter who refuses every chain — including the ones she makes herself.

## 📚 WIZARD (#87, 88)

**[87] Megumin** — Wizard (Evoker/Spellmaster)
  POWER — *Crimson Demon's Gift*: Megumin's Explosion spell deals maximum damage
    (no roll required; auto-maximum). She will not alter this or accept alternatives.
  MOVE — *EXPLOSION!* (◈◈◈, 1/day, only): 60-ft burst; 8d10 fire; Ref DC half.
    After casting: Slowed 2 until Long Rest. Cannot be quickened or heightened.
    This is her only 3-action slot. She will not change this.
  LORE — Crimson Demon wizard who chose mastery of one spell over competence in many. Correct choice.

**[88] Liliana Vess** — Wizard (Necromancer/Lorebook)
  POWER — *Chain Veil's Focus*: Liliana's necromancy spells have +1 DC. Undead
    she raises act on her initiative and receive one extra attack per round.
  MOVE — *Death's Caress* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft range; 4d10 void + Fort DC or
    Doomed 1. On kill: target rises immediately as undead under Liliana's control
    (until encounter ends).
  LORE — Planeswalker who broke her demonic contracts one by one; still asking who she is without the debt.
