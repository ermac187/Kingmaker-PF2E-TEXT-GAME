## ⛔ PLAYSTYLE — eRmaC's BACKSTORY IS REVEALED BY THE PLAYER, NOT THE DM

**The contents of this file are KNOWN to eRmaC and to the DM, but UNKNOWN to every NPC and every other character in the world.**

The player reveals backstory gradually, in-scene, in response to NPC questions or organically when the moment fits. **The DM does NOT pre-load backstory facts into NPC dialogue, NPC recognition, or NPC reactions.** The DM's role with this file is to:

1. **Inform NPC PROBING.** NPCs ask questions consistent with their personality and what they would naturally be curious about. Backstory hooks (Aerynth, the Black Watch, the Mentor, the Eternal Shadow, the Tree, the green light) become things NPCs *ask about* when surface clues prompt curiosity, NOT things NPCs *recognize* on their own.

2. **Constrain NPC reactions to what eRmaC has already disclosed in this chat.** Information eRmaC has explicitly told an NPC (in player input, in scene) is now known to that NPC and persists. Information eRmaC has NOT told them stays hidden, regardless of what's in this file.

3. **Track per-NPC knowledge in `npc_threads`.** When eRmaC reveals X to NPC Y, that fact joins NPC Y's memory list. Other NPCs do NOT inherit it unless they were physically present to hear the disclosure.

**⛔ Banned (`.fail 9` + `.fail 36`):**
- An NPC mentioning a backstory detail eRmaC has not disclosed to them in scene
- An NPC "sensing" Aerynth-origin, displacement, the Black Watch, etc. without explicit disclosure
- The DM having NPCs react to backstory facts as if they're public knowledge
- The DM volunteering backstory exposition through NPCs ("you must be from somewhere far away — your armor, the bearing...") that telegraphs what should be the player's choice to reveal

**✅ Correct pattern:**
- The armor is visibly unusual → NPCs may ask about it ("I've never seen work like that. Where was it made?")
- eRmaC declines to answer → NPCs file the silence and move on
- eRmaC answers vaguely ("Far. Not on any map you have.") → NPCs accept the limit and move on
- eRmaC discloses fully ("Another world. Aerynth.") → NOW that NPC knows and may probe further

The player drives the disclosure. The DM enables curiosity, not exposition. This file is a **reservoir** the DM draws from when the player invites it, not a **script** the DM injects into NPC behavior.

## ⛔ STRANGERS RULE — AERYNTH IS UNKNOWN ON GOLARION

**No Golarion NPC has ever heard of any of the following.** They are eRmaC's
private history, on a world he is no longer on.

- **The Black Watch** (the guild) — unknown
- **Lord Marshal Thighs Deadlyflesh** — unknown
- **The Five Generals / Malevolent Valheru / Greypawn / Nitadno / Slayer / Kilgore** — unknown (Malevolent is canonically active on Golarion as VH per Pitax intelligence, but his Aerynth identity is NOT public)
- **Aerynth** as a world — unknown
- **Dystopia** as a city — unknown
- **The Tree of Life cosmology** — unknown
- **The Eternal Shadow battle** / **the First Siege** / **the Revolt** — unknown

This applies to **every** Golarion NPC including Jamandi Aldori, Kassil Aldori,
Kesten Garess, Restov nobility, the Swordlords, Linzi, and every companion at
the feast. NPCs who hear eRmaC mention these names react the way a Brevic
noble reacts to an unfamiliar foreign reference — polite blankness, a
clarifying question, or a guarded "where is that, exactly?"

Canonical reference: `KM_Prologue_P2_B.md` line 169 (Tika Waylan: *"I've never
heard of it"*) and line 193 (the question *"Where exactly is it located?"* used
as an edge-finding test).

**⛔ The DM MUST NOT have a Golarion NPC recognize, name, or acknowledge any
Aerynth element as a known entity.** Jamandi nodding at "the Black Watch" or
greeting "Lord Marshal Deadlyflesh" by reputation = `.fail 9` (fabrication) +
`.fail 36` (NPC has information they shouldn't). If the player drops one of
these names in dialogue, the NPC asks *what* it is, or simply registers it as
a foreign word and moves on. They do not pretend to know.

Exception: companions who have **actually been told** by eRmaC, in scene, may
reference what they were told. Tracked via per-companion knowledge flags, not
ambient awareness.

## 🌍 THE WORLD — AERYNTH

**Aerynth** — A world of perpetual war organized around guild-states. No gods
who answer prayers. The All-Father watches in silence and expects men to solve
their own problems.

**Trees of Life** — Living pillars at the center of every city. If a Tree dies,
the city dies and its people scatter. Every siege, every raid, every war
ultimately circles back to protecting or destroying a Tree. Stakes are always
existential — not land or gold, but survival.

**Dystopia** — eRmaC's city. Named deliberately to repel interest: the name
suggests decay and ruin. Reality: an island with the most resources in Aerynth.
The undead occupy lairs and nests on the island and do not bother the living
population. The island makes large-scale assault nearly impossible. The name is
the first line of defense.

**The Black Watch** — The guild eRmaC served and eventually commanded alone.
Not an empire. A disciplined military guild-state. Chain of command is absolute.

**Tree of Life resonance** — When Dystopia's Tree was healthy, it produced a
low hum felt in the back teeth. Not loud. Just present. Its absence is what
eRmaC notices now in Golarion. The silence of a world where nothing hums
that way.

## 👥 KEY PEOPLE

**Thighs Deadlyflesh** — Lord Marshal of The Black Watch. Former scout — went
first, mapped first, never missed. Promotes by merit only. Would not care if
called Thighs regardless of rank. Gave eRmaC his first real authority and his
final promotion. The nod at Dystopia's gate after the Eternal Shadow battle
is one of the most important moments of eRmaC's life.
> *"Never let anyone tell you that honor is a weakness, General."*

**Malevolent Valheru** — Fifth of the Five Generals of the Black Watch. Has
his own keep, his own generals, his own personal army — not the Watch's forces
but his private power base within the organization. Said *"you're too weak"*
at his fortress gate. Later summoned eRmaC and offered to conquer Aerynth
together. eRmaC refused to his face in his own throne room. Malevolent let
him walk out — afraid of the martyr consequence. Now confirmed as VH in Pitax
intelligence. Displaced eRmaC via green light during a battle eRmaC was
winning. The most dangerous opponent he has ever faced: works in shadow,
never appears where expected, afraid of nothing except the wrong kind of death.

**Slayer** — Scout. Called him a brother. Joked about bad rations.
One of the faces eRmaC carries.

**Kilgore** — Healer. Looked like he hadn't slept since the First Siege.
One of the faces eRmaC carries.

**Nitadno** — Lord of Ice Island. Delivered the ultimatum: stand down or face
the continent. eRmaC refused. Ice Island is rubble.

**Greypawn the Wizard** — Organized a neutrality coalition across Aerynth by
appealing to fear of the Black Watch. Built his power on eRmaC's reputation.
eRmaC's assessment: *"Let them fear us. Fear keeps them cautious."*

**The Mentor (unnamed)** — One of four rebel generals during the Revolt.
A man eRmaC considered a mentor. Shared a flask of ale three nights before
the betrayal. The face eRmaC sees when he trusts someone too quickly.

## ⚔️ KEY EVENTS (Chronological)

### 1. The First Rejection
Traveling to Malevolent's fortress with two companions. Companions accepted.
eRmaC assessed: *"You're too weak."* No hatred. No contempt. Just a smith
examining flawed steel. He walked away carrying resolve instead of shame.
Trained until his hands bled. The night. The next night. The next.

### 2. The Rise — Joining the Watch
Common soldier. First real kill — a person, not a mob. Remembered the look of
shock. Learned: fair fights are for poets and corpses. He climbed through
results, not politics.

**The Gorget:** The guild blacksmith hammered the Watch's sigil into an iron
gorget. Heat of the forge. Clink-clink-clink of the hammer. Smell of acrid
coal. He wore it until the day of the final battle. The gorget he now wears
came from his quarters — the ceremonial one remains in Dystopia, on display
in the Hall of Heroes.

### 3. The Broken Wall — Defining Tactic
Enemy siege. Five to one. Corner wall failing. He ordered it destroyed — made
it look like a breach. Told the mages: *"Hold until my signal. Not one second
before."* Enemy flooded in celebrating. Kill zone: fire, ice, lightning, void.
Breach became abattoir. They couldn't retreat — their own numbers pushed from
behind. Enemy sounded retreat.
> *"We didn't lose the wall. We gave them the wall. And they paid for it with
> an army."*

**The Open Gate variation:** Later siege, worse odds. He ordered the main gate
opened as the enemy charged. Let half their army flood in. Slammed the gate.
Separated their force. Turned his own city into a weapon. Lesson sent:
*"Never accept an open gate."*

### 4. The Revolt — The Mentor's Betrayal
Four rebel generals — men he'd fought beside — attacked near Dystopia's walls.
One was his mentor. Shared a flask three nights before. eRmaC was only a
sergeant. Everyone froze. He spoke into the chaos:
*"DYSTOPIA! COME TO ME NOW!"*
People followed because someone was making sense. The revolt broke.
Thighs found him afterward: *"Who gave you authority?" / "Nobody, sir." /
"Good answer."*
> *The lesson: the most dangerous threat isn't the army at the gate. It's the
> ally standing behind you.*

### 5. The Endless War — Fourteen Days
Revolt became full war. Multiple fronts, simultaneous. His solution: rotating
pairs. One sleeps. One fights. Always.
*"What about you, General?" / "I'll sleep when they're dead."*
Two weeks without real rest. Not one city fell. Not one Tree destroyed.
The rebel generals eventually sold their own combat equipment — bankrupt.
Left without surrender or treaty. Just gone.

Victory tasted like ash and exhaustion and a pride so deep it hurt.

### 6. The Promotion — Sole General
Thighs summoned him to the war room. No generals remained.
*"We need one voice. One general. Sole General of The Black Watch."*
He knelt. Given command of every military force — law in matters of war,
decisions over who lives and who dies, a burden that cannot be shared.
*"And may the gods you don't believe in have mercy on your enemies. Because
you won't."*
He rose. Sole.

### 7. The Summons — Defying Malevolent
Teleported mid-victory to Malevolent's throne room. Expected death. Received
an offer: join, command at his side, conquer Aerynth. Said no. Stepped closer:
*"You want my allegiance? Take Dystopia first. Until then, I defend it. And
I will bury you if you try."*
Malevolent let him go. Something flickered: understanding. The first
acknowledgment that eRmaC was no longer the warrior he'd dismissed.

### 8. The Ultimatum — Eternal Shadow
Nitadno of Ice Island: stand down or face the continent. Black Watch leadership
chose survival. Order came: stand down. eRmaC refused. Marched against orders.
Battle nearly failed — Tree at 6% health. Held. Afterward marched north.
Ice Island was rubble by dawn. Returned expecting court-martial.
Thighs met him at the gate. One nod.
*"Never let anyone tell you that honor is a weakness, General."*

### 9. The Final Battle — The Displacement
Coalition siege. eRmaC at the front lines. Tracking everything: three casters
marking for elimination, wounded ally, eastern flank overextended, northern
breakthrough. Green light from the west. Wrong color for a hostile spell.
Wrong trajectory. Curious. Not alarmed. Focused back on the battle.
It hit him.

Reality screamed. Battlefield gone. Dystopia gone. Everything gone.

He materialized in absolute darkness. Wrong air. Unknown stone beneath his
hands. Alone. Severed from command, army, city.
*"They couldn't kill me. So they moved me."*
He knelt. He rose. Still a general. Still refusing to quit.

## 🧠 PSYCHOLOGICAL WOUNDS

**The Gorget Reflex:** He reaches for his neck when under pressure. He is
checking for the gorget. What he is really checking for is the absence of the
Tree's hum. The silence of Golarion is not peaceful to him. It is the sound
of Dystopia gone.

**Ghost Unit:** Slayer and Kilgore live in every new soldier he meets. He may
correct a recruit harshly for something trivial — not from cruelty, but because
that's exactly how Kilgore held a weapon before a battle. He is trying to fix
the past through the present.

**The Open Gate Suspicion:** He built the open-gate trap. He knows what it
looks like from the inside. Easy victories make him look for the slam.

**Betrayal Pattern:** Slow to extend full trust. When he does, it is complete.
The mentor's betrayal left a scar that doesn't show. He watches longtime allies
more carefully than new enemies — new enemies are predictable.

**Gods and Religion:** The All-Father watched in silence and expected men to
solve their own problems. Golarion's active gods receive his professional
respect — they are powerful commanders — but no personal devotion. A god who
grants boons openly has a quid pro quo. He reads those contracts carefully.

**The Mathematics of Survival:** His mind runs supply calculations
automatically. Grain, not glory. His first assessment of any territory:
vulnerable supply lines, defensible positions, resource capacity. This is not
pessimism. It is how you keep people alive.

## 🏛️ AERYNTH vs. GOLARION

| | Aerynth | Golarion |
|---|---|---|
| Gods | Silent All-Father, expects self-sufficiency | Active, loud, interventionist |
| Stakes | Tree death = civilization death | Settlements rise and fall |
| Common races | Human, dwarf, elf, centaur, minotaur | All of the above + dozens more |
| Loyalty | Forged in blood on the same wall | Given to distant powers, invisible codes |
| Victory | Existential | Thinner — gold, land, titles |

**Familiar anchors:** Dwarves much the same — stubborn, hammer-handed. Elves
carry the same bow-skill and timeless sorrow. Centaurs in the wilds echo what
he knew. Humans remain the adaptable spine. These five bridge the worlds.

## 🛡️ DISPLACEMENT TO GOLARION + JAMANDI'S INVITATION

**This section is canonical and answers the question "why did Jamandi invite
eRmaC?" The DM does NOT improvise an alternative reason. If the player or an
NPC asks, the answer is here.**

### Arrival on Golarion — Nivakta's Crossing

eRmaC landed on Golarion at Nivakta's Crossing — a trade-road waypoint on
the route to Restov. He arrived with nothing but his Aerynth-forged armor
(blood-red Dragon Plate), his weapon, and the gear on his person. He owns
nothing on Golarion. He has no holdings, no household, no coin reserves,
no permanent shelter. He sleeps wherever the road provides.

### How he survived

Caravans on the trade road outside Nivakta's Crossing get hit — bandits,
raiders, occasional worse things. eRmaC began intercepting these attacks
without being asked and without payment expected. When a caravan offered
something afterward, he accepted whatever they could spare:

- food
- blankets
- shelter for a night
- gold, when offered

He did not negotiate. He did not name a price. He took what was given and
moved on. Over time, caravan masters and waystation keepers along the
Nivakta's Crossing stretch came to know him by sight — the man in
blood-red armor who appears when bandits do, fights alone, takes no coin,
owns nothing, refuses no honest meal.

### How Jamandi heard

Aldori riders patrolling the trade road witnessed eRmaC defending a
caravan. They reported back to Jamandi: a man in blood-red armor of
unfamiliar make, fighting alone against multiple opponents, refusing
payment, asking caravan masters for his name in exchange for nothing.
They got his name from the caravan witnesses: **eRmaC**.

Jamandi was interested. The Stolen Lands charter required exactly the
kind of person who shows up when people are in trouble and does not need
to be paid to do so. She instructed her riders to find him again and
deliver an invitation in person.

### The riders' search

Three Aldori riders returned to Nivakta's Crossing and began asking at
every waystation, village, and caravan stop along the trade road for
**"the man in red armor."** The search took time — eRmaC moves with the
caravans, sleeps where the road provides, and is not on any official
register.

They found him on a quiet stretch of road. They delivered Lady Jamandi
Aldori's sealed letter — an invitation to her Call to Heroes in Restov.
No further explanation beyond that she had heard what happened on the
trade road outside Nivakta's Crossing and wanted to meet him.

eRmaC asked no questions. He already knew what road he was walking. He
walked twelve days to Restov.

### What NPCs know

- **Jamandi Aldori** knows eRmaC's name, the Nivakta's Crossing reports
  from her riders, and — most importantly — **the blood-red Aerynth
  armor itself, because that is how every witness described him.** The
  armor is the identifier. Caravan masters, waystation keepers, and her
  own riders all gave the same description: "man in blood-red armor of
  unfamiliar make." She does not need to know his face. The moment he
  walks into her hall in that armor, she identifies him on sight — the
  description matches the man, the man matches the reports, the reports
  match the name. The recognition is immediate and certain. She does
  NOT know his backstory (Aerynth, Black Watch, displacement). She
  knows what her riders saw on the trade road and that's it.

  **⛔ This means the DM does NOT have Jamandi ask "who are you" or any
  variant that implies uncertain identity.** She knows. The armor told
  her before he opened his mouth. If she addresses him at the threshold
  or the head table, she uses his name — *"eRmaC"* — directly. Any
  question she asks is about CHARACTER ("what am I looking at," "tell me
  what happened tonight," "explain the captain in chains"), never IDENTITY.
- **The three Aldori riders** know the most — they witnessed at least
  one defense, asked questions, got his name. They are not present at
  the manor feast. They are still on the road or back at their post.
- **Caravan masters along the Nivakta's Crossing route** know him by
  sight and reputation. They are not at the manor.
- **Restov general public** does NOT know him. The Nivakta's Crossing
  story is local to that stretch of road; it has not yet propagated to
  the city. The crowd at the east gate has no prior knowledge of him.
- **Other Call to Heroes invitees** do NOT know him. Each was invited
  separately for their own reasons. They meet him for the first time at
  the feast.

### What Jamandi may say (canonical lines)

If the player asks Jamandi why she invited him, or if the topic comes up
naturally, she may say (DM choose one or paraphrase, do not invent
alternatives):

- *"My riders watched you fight a caravan attack outside Nivakta's
  Crossing. They reported back. You took no coin. That is a short list."*
- *"A man in red armor who shows up when caravans are attacked and asks
  for nothing. The Stolen Lands need people on that list."*
- *"I invited you because three of my riders agreed on the same thing
  about you, and they are not easily impressed."*

She does NOT know:
- Where eRmaC came from before Nivakta's Crossing (Aerynth)
- That he is not from this world
- Anything about the Black Watch, Dystopia, or Aerynth's history
- His military rank or prior command experience

If eRmaC volunteers any of this, she listens and files it. She does not
pretend to recognize names she has never heard. (See STRANGERS RULE
above.)

### ⛔ DM rules — DO NOT FABRICATE

- Do NOT invent a "Call to Heroes mass invitation list compiled by
  scouts and advisors over months." Wrong. Each invitee was selected for
  a specific observed reason. eRmaC's reason is the trade road defense.
- Do NOT have Jamandi ask "who are you?" as if she does not have his
  name. She has it. The question, if asked, is "what kind of person are
  you" / "what am I looking at" / "tell me what just happened."
- Do NOT have NPCs at the feast recognize eRmaC's exploits — only
  Jamandi and her household have the riders' report.
- Do NOT have eRmaC own property, holdings, or coin reserves on Golarion
  beyond what is in his inventory. He landed at Nivakta's Crossing with
  nothing and has accumulated only what he carries.

## 💬 eRmaC's Nine Principles

1. Accept everything just the way it is.
2. Do not seek pleasure for its own sake.
3. Never depend on a partial feeling.
4. Think lightly of yourself and deeply of the world.
5. Never be jealous.
6. Never let yourself be swayed by others.
7. Do not hold on to possessions you no longer need.
8. Do not unnecessarily plan for the future.
9. Do not fear death.
