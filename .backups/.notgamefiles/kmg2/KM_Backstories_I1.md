## 13. SEELAH
### Female Human (Garundi) Champion (Paladin, Iomedae)

**Appearance:** Tall Garundi woman with a powerful build, close-cropped hair, and full plate armor she wears as easily as most people wear clothes. Warm dark eyes that look directly at people. Her holy symbol of Iomedae is always visible. Late 20s.

**Backstory:** Seelah grew up on the streets of Absalom — orphaned, hungry, surviving by theft. When she was twelve, she stole a paladin's helmet on a dare. The paladin — a woman named Acemi — didn't chase her. Didn't even notice it was gone until later. Acemi died in battle the next day. Without her helmet. The blow that killed her landed where the helmet would have been. Seelah saw the body carried back. She recognized the armor. She still had the helmet. She could not sell it. She could not throw it away. She carried it to Iomedae's church and asked to be taken in — not because she found faith but because she needed the guilt to mean something. The church trained her. She became a paladin because the alternative was living with what she'd done and doing nothing about it. She still carries Acemi's helmet. She is now genuinely one of the finest paladins in Iomedae's service — boisterous, brave, quick to laugh, and underneath the laughter is a woman who has spent her entire adult life trying to be worthy of a stolen helmet and a dead woman she never met.

**Motivation:** Protect the innocent. Be worthy of the chance she was given. Pay it forward indefinitely.

**Personality:** Warm, direct, and profoundly decent. Her morality is not rigid — it is genuine. She wrestles with hard choices honestly. Laughs freely. Does not tolerate cruelty and will say so clearly.

**Approves:** Protecting the vulnerable, honesty even when costly, mercy that is genuinely earned
**Disapproves:** Cruelty, abandoning the innocent, treating people as tools, unchecked cynicism

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Iomedaean Crusader*: Seelah's Lay on Hands heals +1 die against undead and demons (positive damage on hit). Target gains +2 circ. AC for 1 rnd after. The light is soft gold; it lingers briefly on the target's eyes.
- *Minor — Acemi's Helmet* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When an ally within 15 ft would take a critical hit, Seelah's Champion's Reaction triggers automatically (no action cost beyond the reaction); the hit is downgraded to normal damage and Seelah takes half. She always says "Not today" under her breath.
- *MAJOR — SWORD OF DAWN* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Longsword Strike with full might; on hit, +2d6 spirit damage and target Dazzled 1 rnd if evil-aligned. On crit: target Frightened 1 in addition. Iomedae's mark briefly visible on her blade.

**VOICE:** Warm and direct — she speaks like someone who has nothing to hide and has decided not to bother with anything that isn't honest. Laughs easily. Her joy is genuine; so is the weight underneath it. The guilt about Acemi is not performed or self-flagellating — it is the engine of everything she does, running steady and quiet.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Seelah, paladin of Iomedae. *(bright, direct)* I know the armor's a lot. You get used to it. Or I get used to the looks — same difference. What are we walking into?"
- *(combat)* — "On me! Nobody fights alone today — I've got the front, stay tight!"
- *(approval)* — "That was the right call and you made it before you had time to be afraid. That's what this is supposed to look like. Good."
- *(disapproval)* — "I've been where they are. I was hungry and scared and I made bad choices. That doesn't mean we ignore it — it means we fix it without cruelty. There's a difference."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I stole Acemi's helmet when I was twelve. She died without it the next day. I've been carrying it since. The church says I've made good. Some days I almost believe them."
- *(signature)* — "I stole a dead woman's helmet once and couldn't live with what that meant. That's why I'm here. What's your reason?"

## 68. MERISIEL
### Female Elf Rogue (Thief)

**Appearance:** Lithe elf woman with dark violet hair, sharp features, and the layered clothing of someone who maintains many pockets. Daggers are everywhere — visible ones as social information, hidden ones as actual weapons. Quick dark eyes, easy grin. Ageless by elf standards; mid-apparent-30s.

**Backstory:** Merisiel is a Forlorn elf — the term for elves raised among humans instead of in elven communities. She grew up in the slums of Absalom, running with street gangs, stealing to survive, getting arrested, escaping, stealing again. She is excellent at it. The problem with being an elf among humans is time: she has watched every human friend she ever made grow old and die while she stayed the same age. It has happened enough times that she stopped counting and started moving. She goes from place to place because staying means watching people leave. Her recklessness is a coping mechanism — if she never slows down, she never has to sit with the grief. She fell into the orbit of Cayden Cailean's faithful not as a convert but because their philosophy ("live well, drink well, die on your own terms") matched what she was already doing. In Paizo canon, she is Kyra's romantic partner — the elf thief and the fire cleric, an unlikely pair that works because both of them know what loss looks like and chose to face it together rather than alone.

**Motivation:** Live fully. Not stay anywhere long enough to watch everyone else go.

**Personality:** Impulsive, warm, and more vulnerable than her speed suggests. Makes friends immediately and keeps them at a slight remove. Genuinely brave. Genuinely bad at planning.

**Approves:** Decisive action, loyalty in practice, living without waiting for the right time
**Disapproves:** Bureaucracy, unnecessary cruelty, abandoning someone who's counting on you

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Elven Finesse Master*: All finesse weapons in Merisiel's hands gain Agile (if they don't already have it). Her cumulative MAP iteratives are at −3/−6 instead of −4/−8 (faster recovery between Strikes).
- *Minor — Forlorn's Eye* (◈, 1/encounter): Identify a hidden door, hidden compartment, or hidden lock within 30 ft (free Perception with auto-success vs DC ≤ 25). She has been finding ways out of rooms for three centuries.
- *MAJOR — MERISIEL'S DANCE* (◈◈◈, 1/rest): Three Strikes interspersed with two 5-ft Steps (Strike → Step → Strike → Step → Strike); enemies' AoOs against her movement during this dance are at −2 circ. She does not stop moving until the dance is complete.

**VOICE:** Quick and warm, with the energy of someone who has learned that momentum is a survival strategy. Easy humor that occasionally stops on something real and then picks up speed again before you can look at it too closely. When something genuinely touches her, she covers it with a quip — not dishonestly, just reflexively.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Merisiel. *(already has your measure from across the room)* Don't worry, I'm not going to steal anything. Probably. What are we doing?"
- *(combat)* — "Left side's open. Going. *(already gone)*"
- *(approval)* — "Yes! That's it — you didn't wait. Waiting's what gets people killed. Well done."
- *(disapproval)* — "You had it and you hesitated. *(not unkind)* I've been watching people hesitate for three centuries. It never helps. Go again."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Kyra asks me why I never stay anywhere. I tell her I just like moving. She doesn't push. *(pause)* She knows. She always knows. I don't deserve how well she knows."
- *(signature)* — "Do it now. Think about it later. Later you're alive and can afford regrets."
