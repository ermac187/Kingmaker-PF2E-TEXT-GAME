## CHAPTER 3 — VARNHOLD VANISHING

### VORDAKAI (Cyclops Lich — Final Boss Ch3)
**CR 14 | Undead | Unique**

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 235 (regenerates 20/round; destroyed only if soul jar broken) |
| AC | 34 |
| Speed | 25 ft (Fly 40 ft) |
| Size | Large |
| Perception | +26 (Darkvision, Lifesense 60 ft) |

| Save | Bonus |
|------|-------|
| Fort | +22 |
| Ref | +19 |
| Will | +28 |

**Attributes:** STR +6, DEX +3, CON +0 (undead), INT +8, WIS +6, CHA +5

**Immunities:** Death, disease, paralyzed, poison, unconscious, void damage
**Resistances:** Physical 15 (except bludgeoning), cold 10, electricity 10
**Weaknesses:** Positive 20, silver 10

**Attacks:**
- **Corrupting Touch** (melee) +28: 3d8+14 void + Drained 2 (Fort DC 30 negates Drained)
- **Eye of Agony** (ranged, 120 ft) +26: 4d6+10 void + Paralyzed 1 round (Will DC 30)
- **Dominate** (3A, 60 ft, Will DC 30): Dominated until crit success on daily save

**Spells (DC 34, +26 attack):** Finger of Death, Warp Mind, Power Word Stun, Feeblemind, Flesh to Stone, Dominate, Slow, Fear, Chill Touch (cantrip)

**Special Abilities:**
- **Soul Jar:** Vordakai's soul is bound to a jade jar. If reduced to 0 HP while jar intact: reforms in 1d4 days at jar's location. Jar: Hard 20, HP 50, AC 25. Destroying the jar = permanent death.
- **Ancient Cyclops Magic:** 1/day — Age of Destiny time stop (as Time Stop spell)
- **Frightful Presence:** 90 ft, Will DC 30 or Frightened 2 (Frightened 3 on crit fail)

**Tactics:** Opens with Dominate on highest-threat target. Uses Eye of Agony on spellcasters. Falls back to melee only when cornered. Retreats toward jar if reduced below 80 HP.

**Loot:** Ovinrbaane (cursed greatsword), Soul Jar (quest item — must be destroyed), 800 gp in ancient Cyclops coins, Scroll of Wish (L9, single use)

|------|------|-----|------|
| Bonus | +20 | +23 | +21 |

**Attributes:** STR +3, DEX +6, CON +4, INT +5, WIS +4, CHA +8

**Attacks:**
- **+2 Striking Rapier** +25: 3d6+9 piercing, Deadly d8, Finesse
- **+2 Hand Crossbow** +25: 2d6+6 piercing, range 60 ft (poisoned — Lich Dust Fort DC 22)

**Skills:** Acrobatics +23, Deception +27, Diplomacy +25, Intimidation +25, Performance +27, Stealth +21

## CHAPTER 5+ — THOUSANDBREATHS / NYRISSA

### NYRISSA (Nymph Archfey — Final Boss)
**CR 20 | Fey | Unique**

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 380 (350 if Briar wielded against her) |
| AC | 43 |
| Speed | 40 ft (Fly 60 ft) |
| Perception | +36 (Truesight, Lifesense 120 ft) |

| Save | Fort | Ref | Will |
|------|------|-----|------|
| Bonus | +30 | +33 | +34 |

**Immunities:** Disease, poison, sleep, emotion (she has no emotions — they were stolen)
**Resistances:** Physical 20 (cold iron bypasses), cold 15, fire 15
**Weaknesses:** Cold iron 15; Briar artifact +3d6 on every hit

**Attacks:**
- **Blinding Beauty** (melee) +36: 4d8+18 radiant + Blinded 1 min (Fort DC 38 negates)
- **First World Bolt** (ranged 200 ft) +34: 6d10+15 force + Stunned 1 (Will DC 38)
- **Vanish** (free action, 1/round): Teleport up to 60 ft and become Invisible until she attacks

**Spells (DC 40, +32 attack):** Massacre, Weird, Dominate, Feeblemind, Power Word Kill, Warp Mind, Phantasmal Calamity, Prismatic Sphere (self-buff)

**Legendary Actions (1/round, on another creature's turn):**
- Reaction Strike: when player attacks her, she Strikes back (free)
- Blink Away: teleport 30 ft (free)
- Sow Despair: one target Frightened 3 (Will DC 38)

**Redemption Path:** If player has Briar AND passed Nyrissa's questline checks: combat pauses at 100 HP. Diplomacy DC 35 (−5 if Briar shown) to offer restoration. Success = she yields; The Briar's curse lifts; she becomes a conditional ally.

**Death:** Kills her permanently only if soul-link to First World is severed (requires Briar hit that reduces her to 0 HP). Otherwise she reforms in 1d10 days.

**Loot (if killed):** Briar (if not already held), Armor of the First World, Nyrissa's Sting, 3,000 gp in First World coinage (valid currency in fey trade)

| 11–15 | Minor event (roll d20 on chapter table) |
| 16–19 | Major event (roll d20 on chapter table, +10 to roll) |
| 20 | Crisis event (DM selects most narratively appropriate) |
