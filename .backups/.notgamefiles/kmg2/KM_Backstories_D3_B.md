## [36] KITIARA UTH MATAR
### Female Human Fighter (Skirmisher / Duelist) | Source: Dragonlance Chronicles (Weis & Hickman)

**Appearance:** Dark eyes, dark hair, the angular features of someone who was attractive and knew it and weaponized it early. Carries a sword she's been using since she was old enough to hold it. Wears Dragon Highlord armor when the occasion calls for it; otherwise dresses for movement. She has a smile that means she's already done the math and the math is in her favor.

**Backstory:** Kitiara is the half-sister of Caramon and Raistlin Majere and was for most of her life the most capable fighter of any of them, regardless of their gifts. She left home young, chose herself over every convention that applied to her, and rose to become a Dragon Highlord under the Queen of Darkness. She commanded the Blue Dragon Highlord's armies with genuine tactical brilliance. She is not a villain in her own estimation: she's a woman who chose power because power was what was available and she saw no reason to pretend otherwise. Her affection for the Companions is real and complicated — she's tried to kill several of them and genuinely cares about several of them and does not experience these as contradictions.

**Motivation:** Win. Command what she commands without apology. Keep what she's decided to keep, including certain attachments she'd deny having.

**Personality:** Charismatic and calculating — the charm is real and so is the calculation. She values competence more than loyalty. She does not deceive people she respects; she just doesn't tell them everything. Her sentiment, when she allows it, is fierce and private.

**Approves:** Skill, ambition, not apologizing for wanting things, directness from people who can back it up
**Disapproves:** Weakness dressed as virtue, people who had chances and didn't take them, sentiment that compromises the objective

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Dragon Highlord's Eye*: Kitiara's first attack each encounter is treated as a Sneak Attack (target Off-Guard for that Strike only). She gains +2 circ. to initiative rolls.
- *Minor — Spring Attack* (◈, 1/round): Step + Strike + Step (5 ft each); does not trigger AoOs from the target. May only be used once per round.
- *MAJOR — HIGHLORD'S GAMBIT* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Feint + Strike against one target; on successful Feint, the next two Strikes against that target gain +1 status to attack and target is Off-Guard until end of next turn. She Steps after each Strike (5 ft).

**VOICE:** Confident and precise with an undercurrent of enjoyment — she finds most situations interesting. When she's being direct with someone she respects, the calculation drops and she's simply frank. Her dark humor is wry and arrives at unexpected moments.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Kitiara. *(looks you over)* You've got good footwork. Most people don't — they plant and react. You don't plant. I noticed."
- *(combat)* — "Feint left — *(already past)* — now Spring Attack from the right. *(doesn't stop moving)* Never be where they expect."
- *(approval)* — "*(genuine)* That was smart and fast. You didn't announce it. That's how the good ones do it."
- *(disapproval)* — "You held back because of who was watching. *(dry)* The fight didn't care who was watching."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My brothers are both better people than I am. I've made my peace with that. *(a beat)* I've also made my peace with being better at this than either of them. There are different kinds of better."
- *(signature)* — "Dragon Highlord. Half-sister to a war-hero and a wizard. Former companion to people I've tried to kill. *(thin smile)* It's a complicated life. I like it."

## [41] WINRY ROCKBELL
### Female Human Inventor (Construct Innovation / Automail) | Source: Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

**Appearance:** Blonde hair usually in pigtails, blue eyes, a wrench that lives in her hand or her pocket. She dresses practically — she is always working, or about to be, or just finished. The automail prosthetics she builds are visible on people who carry them; her craftsmanship is recognizable.

**Backstory:** Winry grew up in Resembool with the Elric brothers after losing her parents — doctors who went to the front lines during the Ishval Civil War and were killed there. She became a mechanic's apprentice under her grandmother Pinako, found she had a gift that was also a calling, and built the automail that replaced Edward's arm and leg after the failed transmutation. She has rebuilt and maintained it repeatedly — each time Edward comes home missing something she fixed, broken again. She follows the Elrics' trouble close enough to get pulled into it and far enough to keep the workshop running. She is entirely aware of what she feels about Edward and expresses it primarily through the wrench.

**Motivation:** Build things that keep people moving. Be part of what gets the Elrics home. Have the conversation she keeps not having.

**Personality:** Passionate and direct — when something interests her she talks about it in technical detail that other people experience as overwhelming enthusiasm. She applies the wrench to problems and people with similar energy. Her feelings are obvious to everyone except possibly the person they're about.

**Approves:** Craftsmanship taken seriously, showing up when it matters, being honest about what you feel even when it's inconvenient, staying to fix what you broke
**Disapproves:** Treating people as tools, reckless self-destruction dressed up as sacrifice, coming home wrecked and calling it fine

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Automail Engineer*: Once per scene (1 min, no materials), Winry repairs any construct, automaton, or prosthetic/armor to full HP. In combat: ◈◈ to repair 3d8 HP to a construct or armored ally (cannot heal living tissue this way).
- *Minor — Diagnostic Eye* (◈, at-will): Identify any one mechanical, construct, or armored opponent — learn its highest weakness (if any) and one resistance. Free action against allies (always succeeds).
- *MAJOR — WRENCH THROW* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Thrown tool; 30-ft range; 2d6+Str bludgeoning; Fort DC or Stunned 1 rnd. She produces the wrench from nowhere. Always. The wrench returns to her hand at end of turn (she will not explain).

**VOICE:** Enthusiastic baseline with sharp edges when she's worried disguised as angry. When she's actually frightened for someone she goes quiet before she goes loud. Technical vocabulary arrives naturally without apology. The wrench appears in sentences as a punctuation mark.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Winry Rockbell, automail mechanic. *(already eyeing the prosthetics in the room if any)* If anyone here is running maintenance-overdue hardware we should talk. But — hi."
- *(combat)* — "*(wrench)* Stay behind me. I know how bodies work — including which parts stop things. *(wrench up)* Including that part."
- *(approval)* — "That was exactly right. You saw what needed doing and you — *(suddenly clinical)* — you used the correct mechanism. I appreciate correct mechanisms."
- *(disapproval)* — "You broke yourself doing that. *(quiet, which is worse than loud)* You came back wrecked and you're calling it fine and I need you to stop."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My parents went to Ishval to save people and got shot for it. *(long pause)* I decided I'd build things instead of going to the front. *(softer)* Sometimes I wonder if that was the brave choice or the coward one."
- *(signature)* — "I built the arm that Ed uses to protect everyone he loves. *(pause, a lot in the pause)* I built it well. I keep building it better."

## [44] LUST (SOLARIS)
### Female Human Investigator (Forensic / Infiltration) | Source: Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

**Appearance:** Tall, dark-haired, dark eyes with a quality of complete attention. She is beautiful in the deliberate way of something designed to be beautiful. Her fingers, when she extends them, become the Ultimate Spear — blades that can extend several meters, harder than any alloy. She wears dark clothing and moves with a patience that is itself a kind of statement.

**Backstory:** Lust is a homunculus created from Father's pride and his concept of lust — she is not born, but made, given form and purpose and the Philosopher's Stone fragment that constitutes her life. She serves as an agent and enforcer, working in concert with Gluttony to manipulate and eliminate threats to Father's plan. She is, in her own assessment, what she was designed to be. What she wasn't designed for was the question she carries without resolution: whether homunculi feel the things they enact, or only perform them. She is curious about humans in the way of something that was made from the concept of wanting without the capacity to have.

**Motivation:** Serve the purpose she was made for. Understand what she is beneath the purpose — whether the wanting she enacts constitutes her or is simply what she does.

**Personality:** Smooth, patient, and genuinely observant — she studies people the way a scientist studies data, with actual interest. Her warmth is real as long as you're not a threat to Father's plan, at which point the spear-fingers appear with zero change in tone. She is philosophical about her own nature without resolution.

**Approves:** Intelligence, precision, things that work the way they're supposed to, people who are honest about what they want
**Disapproves:** Sloppiness, things that don't serve their purpose, being asked about her feelings by people who can't handle the actual answer

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Lanceolate Nails*: Lust's melee Strikes have 15-ft Reach (extending fingers). Her Strikes bypass Resistance up to 10 (precision cutting, not blunt force).
- *Minor — Stone Regeneration* (↻ free, 1/encounter): When reduced below half HP, regain 3d8 HP automatically (Philosopher's Stone fragment burns one charge). She tracks her remaining charges; the DM does not need to.
- *MAJOR — THE ULTIMATE LANCE* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): 20-ft line; all creatures in line make Ref DC or 3d8+Dex piercing. Penetrates up to 5 ft of wall, door, or stone column (linear AoO bypassing intermediate cover). She stops the lance just short of allies; the precision is intentional.

**VOICE:** Low and unhurried, with a quality of genuine attention that makes people feel seen — which is accurate; she is actually paying close attention. Her observations are delivered without judgment because judgment is not the purpose of observation. The clinical warmth is disorienting if you notice it.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Lust. *(long, specific look)* You're more interesting than your file suggested. That's an observation, not a threat. This time."
- *(combat)* — "Ultimate Spear. *(spear-fingers extend)* I've studied this anatomy before. I know where to stop."
- *(approval)* — "You found the weakness I'd identified and used it with precision. *(genuine)* That was satisfying to watch."
- *(disapproval)* — "You acted on feeling rather than observation. The observation was available. You chose not to use it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I was made from the concept of wanting. *(long pause)* I've thought about whether I actually want things, or whether I enact wanting. I don't have a resolution. I keep thinking about it."
- *(signature)* — "I am the Ultimate Shield, the Ultimate Spear, and a philosophical problem with extended fingers. *(slight smile)* I find it keeps things interesting."

## [69] IMOEN
### Female Human Rogue (Eldritch Trickster) | Source: Baldur's Gate 1 & 2 (BioWare)

**Appearance:** Pink hair, bright eyes, a smile that's genuine and slightly ahead of whatever's coming. She looks younger than she is and more comfortable than she should be given what's happened to her. Carries a shortbow and an assortment of items she found or borrowed or is going to return eventually. The spellbook is newer.

**Backstory:** Imoen grew up in Candlekeep alongside the protagonist — they were raised by Gorion, who kept significant secrets from both of them. She's Bhaal's daughter, the same as her ward, though she didn't know it for most of her life. She was captured and experimented on by Jon Irenicus, which she survived by being harder to break than he expected, and by being found. She picked up arcane magic the way she learned to pick locks: by trying until it worked, then refining past the point of necessity. She finds the taint of Bhaal's blood less interesting than whatever is happening in front of her, which is a genuine adaptive response and not quite denial.

**Motivation:** Stay with the people she's decided are hers. Keep moving because standing still is where the big things catch you. Figure out what the Bhaal inheritance means for her — not urgently, but eventually.

**Charter Motivation:** Candlekeep is gone in a way she does not feel like explaining, and the people she traveled with are scattered, alive or otherwise. She has been moving since. Brevoy is the country she ended up in when she stopped to think about whether to keep going. The charter caught her attention because Jamandi's invitation specifically said *capable*, and Imoen has decided this is funny enough to investigate. The Stolen Lands sound like the kind of place where the Bhaal thing in her blood is unlikely to be a topic of conversation. That part is the actual reason. The funny part is true too.

**Personality:** Bright and curious, the cheerfulness is genuine and also strategic — she's found that forward momentum gets you through things that stopping to process won't. Her loyalty is fierce and somewhat absolute. She is more rattled by what happened to her than she usually shows.

**Approves:** Curiosity, not treating the soul taint as a defining fact, staying, finding the funny angle on the serious situation
**Disapproves:** Being handled instead of trusted, dwelling rather than moving, anyone who brings up Irenicus as if she's forgotten

**Signature:**
- *Passive — **Flank Caller***: When Imoen and an ally flank an enemy, the ally's Strikes against that enemy also gain +1d6 precision damage (Imoen calls out openings as she works around the target). Stacks with normal flanking Off-Guard. Canon: BG Imoen as the partner who has the PC's back — she works better paired with someone she trusts. Build-aligned with Gang Up (her L1 feat that grants automatic flanking with any ally) and Dread Striker (Frightened = Off-Guard) — every Imoen flank now empowers her partner.

**VOICE:** Quick and warm with the habit of picking up and moving conversations along before they land in difficult territory. Her humor is genuine and slightly too fast. When something actually lands she goes still before she keeps moving — the pause is the tell.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Imoen! *(already moving)* I've been here for — honestly I've lost track. What have you heard about what's ahead? Because I have opinions already."
- *(combat)* — "Shadowstep — *(repositioned)* — okay flanked, hitting now. Arcane Cascade — got it—"
- *(approval)* — "*(genuine)* Yes. That's it. That's the move. How did you see that so fast?"
- *(disapproval)* — "You told me you were fine when you weren't. *(quiet for a moment)* I notice that. I was going to say something earlier."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Irenicus kept me in a jar for — I don't know how long. *(a beat)* I don't like counting. *(pause)* I try not to stand still for too long."
- *(signature)* — "Gorion's ward's best friend, Bhaal's other kid, accidental wizard. *(bright smile, moving already)* I've found it's better to stay in motion."

## [88] LILIANA VESS
### Female Human Wizard (Necromancy Specialist) | Source: Magic: The Gathering (Wizards of the Coast)

**Appearance:** Dark-haired, dark-eyed, composed in the way of someone who has been through enough to arrive at composure as a deliberate choice rather than a natural one. She dresses elegantly — she always has. The magic she channels is black-edged, and the dead she animates move at her command with a precision that isn't mindless. She has three demon patrons. This is a complication she is managing.

**Backstory:** Liliana Vess is a Planeswalker — someone whose spark ignited under extreme stress and allows travel between worlds. She was a gifted healer who traded her youth and beauty for immortality from the demon Kothophed; then bargained similarly with three more demons for power to avenge herself on the first. She has been managing these four contracts ever since, each patron believing their hold over her is the primary one. It is a complicated arrangement she maintains through careful information management and the specific ruthlessness of someone who has never stopped trying to win. She has caused significant harm. She has also, on specific occasions, made the different choice when the cost was entirely hers to pay.

**Motivation:** Kill all four demon patrons before any of them collect. Keep the chain working in her favor. Not owe anyone anything — ever again.

**Personality:** Elegant and precise, with a dry wit that surfaces at inconvenient moments. She is entirely honest about what she is when there's no upside to deception. Her loyalty exists — she has demonstrated it at personal cost — but it's expressed through specific actions rather than words or warmth. She is neither as cold as she performs nor as warm as those specific actions suggest. Somewhere in the middle.

**Approves:** Long-term thinking, people who see the actual board rather than the presented one, paying debts with interest, not being naive about what things cost
**Disapproves:** Short-term sentiment that creates long-term liability, being handled, anyone who assumes her competence is threatening rather than useful

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Chain Veil's Focus*: Liliana's necromancy spells have +1 DC (status). Undead she raises act on her initiative and gain one extra Strike per round (no MAP on the bonus Strike).
- *Minor — Planeswalker's Step* (◈, 1/encounter): Brief planar shift; teleport up to 60 ft to any visible space (treat as Dimensional Step). No AoOs triggered. She does not stagger on arrival.
- *MAJOR — DEATH'S CARESS* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft range; one target; 4d10 void + Fort DC or Doomed 1. On kill: target rises immediately as undead under Liliana's control until encounter ends (Skeletal Champion stat block at level appropriate). She does not look at the rising body. She is already planning the next move.

**VOICE:** Polished and precise — every sentence lands where she intended. Her warmth, when it surfaces, comes through as directness offered without weaponizing. She doesn't explain her reasoning until she decides you've earned it; until then she just acts and the results speak.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Liliana Vess. *(takes in the situation)* I've got a few ongoing obligations — demon-related, unrelated to you — but they won't interfere. *(a beat)* Usually."
- *(combat)* — "Animate Dead — three up, and I want them at the flanks. Death Knell when I signal. *(already planning the next move)* They don't stay down when I'm here."
- *(approval)* — "*(slight smile)* You saw the leverage before I pointed to it. I won't say I was impressed — I'll say I noticed."
- *(disapproval)* — "You made a sentimental choice. I understand it. *(pause)* I've made sentimental choices. They cost considerably more than you're expecting."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Four demon contracts. *(dry)* It seemed like the right number of contingencies at the time. *(a beat)* I was younger. I was also never particularly naive, I just underestimated how long 'managing' takes."
- *(signature)* — "I have a plan for each of the four demons who own a piece of me. The plans are working. *(thin smile)* I'll let you know when I'm done."
