## BUILD — WATER STANCE CONTROL
**Elf (Nimble Elf) | Monk (Wave Stance → Water) | Background: Hermit**
*Wave Stance: push 5 ft on hit, AC bonus vs. reach. Water-element Ki adds cold damage and Slow 1 on crit. Mountain Quake at L9 clears the board. Battlefield lockdown control.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 14 | 16 | 14 | 10 | 18 | 10 |
| Mod | +2 | +3 | +2 | +0 | +4 | +0 |

**HP** 18 | **AC** 16 (Unarmored + WIS) | **Fort** +6 | **Ref** +7 | **Will** +8 | **Speed** 35 ft
**Weapon:** Wave Hand (1d6+2 B/cold in Wave Stance, push 5 ft on hit)
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Acrobatics +7, Nature +7, Survival +7
**Features:** Flurry of Blows, Wave Stance (push 5 ft on hit, +1 AC vs reach), Ki Strike focus
**L1 Feats:** Nimble Elf (heritage, auto) + Otherworldly Magic (ancestry feat) + Wave Stance | **Boost:** WIS → DEX → CON → STR

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Elf | Nimble Elf | 35 ft speed lets the Wave Stance controller push enemies and immediately reposition for a second push — maintaining the knockback chain without chasing. |
| 2 | Human | Natural Ambition | Bonus feat picks up Stance Savant at L1 — free-action Wave Stance entry from round 1 means control starts two levels earlier than normal. |
| 3 | Catfolk | Nimble | +5 ft speed matches Nimble Elf; Cat's Luck DEX reroll covers the Reflex saves a controller drawing focused melee fire can't afford to fail. |
| 4 | Tengu | Dogtooth | Powerful bite adds a third agile unarmed attack that still applies the 5 ft push on every hit in Wave Stance — more pushes per Flurry turn. |
| 5 | Half-Elf | Elf Atavism | 35 ft Nimble Elf speed via Elf Atavism at full 8 HP — all the mobility benefit without the d6 HP base that hurts sustained Wave control. |
| 6 | Halfling | Gutsy | Frightened crit-fail immunity keeps the WIS/control build focused — fear effects force defensive positioning that breaks the push rhythm. |
| 7 | Goblin | Unbreakable | +4 max HP on a controller that never gets defensive feat investment; free HP from ancestry directly offsets the gap. |
| 8 | Fetchling | Liminal | Shadow Step repositions the wave controller to the perfect angle for a chained knockback, bypassing terrain that blocks ground movement. |
| 9 | Android | Artilect | Nanite Surge +2 to the Wave Crash (Slow 1 on critical) Athletics/save DC 1/day — guaranteeing one Slow proc per fight is a significant action tax on the enemy. |
| 10 | Gnome | Obsessive | 25 ft speed is slower than ideal but Gnome's 8 HP base and Obsessive's skill bonus rounds out the WIS-focused support kit without any feat investment. |

## BUILD — JOTUNBORN GIANT STANCE
**Half-Orc (Jotunborn) | Monk (Dragon Stance) | Background: Gladiator**
*Jotunborn heritage treats wielder as one size larger — Dragon Stance reach 10 ft becomes 15 ft effective. Massive reach DPR with 1d10 fists and Dragon Roar free Demoralize aura.*

|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 18 | 12 | 18 | 10 | 14 | 8 |
| Mod | +4 | +1 | +4 | +0 | +2 | -1 |

**HP** 24 | **AC** 14 (Unarmored + WIS) | **Fort** +10 | **Ref** +5 | **Will** +8 | **Speed** 25 ft
**Weapon:** Dragon Tail (1d10+4 B, reach 10 ft / 15 ft Jotunborn, in Dragon Stance)
**Skills:** Athletics +6 (Jotunborn: +2 to Grapple larger), Intimidation +1, Gladiatorial Lore +2, Survival +4
**Features:** Flurry of Blows, Dragon Stance (1d10 B reach), Dragon Roar (free Demoralize 15 ft in stance), Jotunborn (size Large for maneuvers)
**L1 Feats:** Jotunborn + Dragon Stance | **Boost:** STR → CON → WIS → DEX

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Half-Orc | Jotunborn | Jotunborn counts as Large for maneuvers — Dragon Stance's 10 ft reach becomes effectively 15 ft, enabling hits on flying targets and over obstacles. |
| 2 | Orc | Hold-Scarred (Diehard) | STR 18 + Diehard + 10 HP base: a reach brawler that trades hits at 10 ft range against multiple enemies needs maximum staying power. |
| 3 | Lizardfolk | Frilled | Frilled Intimidation expertise makes Dragon Roar's free-action Demoralize hit more reliably — Frightened 1 aura from round 1 in stance. |
| 4 | Human | Natural Ambition | Bonus feat grabs Titan Wrestler at L1 instead of L2 — Grappling Large creatures online immediately, combining with Jotunborn's size advantage. |
| 5 | Tiefling | Pitborn | STR/Athletics boost + void resist; Pitborn's Athletic synergy raises the Grapple/Shove DCs this reach-brawler relies on at L2 onward. |
| 6 | Dwarf | Mountain's Stoutness | Extra HP/level + 10 base; the dragon-stance wall that fights at reach 10 ft still takes AoO counterattacks — more HP per level matters. |
| 7 | Catfolk | Clawed | Natural claws as a second agile attack type; Dragon Stance tail + claw covers two damage types and two reach distances in a single Flurry. |
| 8 | Goblin | Unbreakable | +4 max HP on a melee brawler with no shield; free ancestry HP is the cheapest mitigation available for a reach attacker. |
| 9 | Half-Elf | Multitalented | Multitalented at L9 adds Exemplar dedication free — Weapon Ikon on handwraps stacks with Dragon Stance reach for a reach+Ikon nova turn. |

## BUILD — PURE SUPPORT MONK
**Gnome (Fey-Touched) | Monk (pure, no archetype) | Background: Acolyte**
*Stunning Fist + Wholeness of Body + Ki Blast + Resounding Cascade = party utility engine. High WIS drives saves and focus spell DCs. Stuns bosses, heals allies, ranged ki finisher.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 14 | 16 | 14 | 10 | 18 | 12 |
| Mod | +2 | +3 | +2 | +0 | +4 | +1 |

**HP** 20 | **AC** 16 (Unarmored + WIS) | **Fort** +6 | **Ref** +7 | **Will** +8 | **Speed** 25 ft
**Weapon:** Fists (1d6+2 B, agile, finesse) / Ki Blast (2d6+4 force, 30 ft, 2 actions)
**Skills:** Medicine +8, Nature +7, Diplomacy +5, Religion +7
**Features:** Flurry of Blows, Powerful Fist, Ki Strike focus, Ki Blast focus, no archetype
**L1 Feats:** Fey-Touched (heritage, auto) + Animal Accomplice (ancestry feat) + Ki Strike | **Boost:** WIS → CON → DEX → STR

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| # | Ancestry | Heritage | Why It Fits |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Halfling | Hillock | Hillock HP/rest + Halfling Luck reroll on a support Monk who stays near allies — free daily HP and a reroll on the Stunning Fist attack both directly serve the party. |
| 2 | Gnome | Wellspring | Wellspring primal cantrip (Tanglefoot or Gouging Claws) adds an off-Flurry control option before Ki Blast comes online at L2. |
| 3 | Leshy | Leaf | Leaf Leshy Stealth lets the support Monk stay hidden between ki bursts — an invisible Stunning Fist is far harder to counter. |
| 4 | Kitsune | Scholar | Scholar's innate arcane/occult cantrip gives a second damage-type cantrip alongside ki, filling cascade turns with varied elemental control. |
| 5 | Half-Elf | Elf Atavism | Elf Atavism (Ancient Elf feat) grants a free dedication at L1 — support Monks who want Bard or Cleric can pick it up without the full feat cost. |
| 6 | Elf | Ancient Elf | Ancient Elf takes Medic Dedication at L1 — Battle Medicine + Wholeness of Body turns this Monk into a full off-healer with no feat slot spent. |
| 7 | Fetchling | Resolute | Resolute Will save bonus covers the highest-priority defensive gap: a support Monk who fails Will is Mind Controlled and actively harms the party it buffs. |
| 8 | Aasimar | Angelkin | Angelkin Diplomacy bonus + fire resist 2 enhances the support flavor; high WIS already drives divine-adjacent saves and Stunning Fist DCs. |
| 9 | Android | Artilect | Nanite Surge +2 to the Quivering Palm attack roll 1/day — a WIS DC stun on a boss from range is the highest-value action in any encounter. |
| 10 | Human | Natural Ambition | Bonus L1 feat takes Ki Blast at L1 instead of L2 — ranged support fire available in the very first fight, before the party even has their second level abilities. |
