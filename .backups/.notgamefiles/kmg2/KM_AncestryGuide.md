## 🧬 LEGAL ANCESTRIES (PF2e Remaster)

**Core:** Human · Elf · Dwarf · Halfling · Gnome · Goblin. **Uncommon:** Half-elf · Half-orc · Hobgoblin · Leshy · Orc · Ratfolk · Tengu. **Rare:** Kobold · Catfolk · Lizardfolk · Shoony · Fetchling · Strix · Conrasu · Nagaji · Vanara · Azarketi · Kashrishi · Fleshwarp · Ghoran · Goloma. **Kingmaker:** Goliath.
**Versatile Heritages** (any ancestry, free): Nephilim (Celestial/Fiendish) · Changeling · Dhampir · Duskwalker · Beastkin · Aiuvarin · Dromaar.

## [1] ALCHEMIST — Primary: INT · Secondary: DEX, CON

**Role:** Bomb-thrower + consumable engine. INT sets item DCs and prep count; DEX improves thrown-bomb accuracy and AC; CON tanks mutagenist frontline.
**Poor picks cost:** Goliath throws off bomb splash math (Large size = scatter geometry). Leshy has no CON-primary option for frontline mutagenist variants.

```
★ Dwarf       — +CON, steel stomach vs poison, Clan Dagger bomb sight
 2 Goblin     — +DEX+CHA, Burn It! scales bomb damage, speed 25
 3 Gnome      — +CON+CHA, First World Magic (primal cantrip)
 4 Halfling   — +DEX+WIS, Keen Eyes, Halfling Luck reroll
 5 Human      — +Any, Natural Ambition extra L1 feat
OK : Elf (speed 30, fragile Fort) · Orc (Ferocity save-slot) · Catfolk (escape crit fall)
AVOID: Goliath (throws off bomb splash math), Leshy (no CON primary option)
```

## [2] ANIMIST — Primary: WIS · Secondary: CON, CHA

**Role:** Spirit-channeling hybrid. WIS sets spell DC and apparition attacks; CON for HP and frontline Spirit Blade variants; CHA for social secondary.
**Poor picks cost:** Goblin (−WIS flaw cripples your primary DC by 2). Hobgoblin (no WIS-primary heritage default — builds around −WIS flaw).

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition unlocks key apparition feat early
 2 Dwarf      — +WIS+CON, steady Fort, Clan Dagger
 3 Elf        — +DEX+INT, low-light, extra skill (or +WIS heritage)
 4 Halfling   — +WIS+CHA, Keen Eyes, Halfling Luck
 5 Goliath    — +STR+CON for melee Spirit Blade builds
OK : Orc (Ferocity) · Gnome (Primal innate) · Hobgoblin (Remorseless Lash)
AVOID: Goblin (no WIS synergy — pure flavor pick)
```

## [3] BARBARIAN — Primary: STR · Secondary: CON

**Role:** Raw damage + HP wall. STR adds to attack rolls, melee damage, and Athletics (grapple/trip/shove); CON adds HP per level (barbarians have d12 — CON compounds). DEX matters only for AC outside of medium armor.
**Poor picks cost:** Gnome / Halfling / Leshy = STR cap 16 (−1 hit, −1 damage every Strike for life).

```
★ Orc         — +STR+CON, Orc Ferocity (1/day death save reroll)
 2 Goliath    — +STR+CON, Large throws, Giant Instinct natural fit
 3 Dwarf      — +CON+STR, Stonewalker for Spirit Instinct, anti-fear heritage
 4 Human      — Natural Ambition = rage feat at L1
 5 Hobgoblin  — +CON+INT, Remorseless Lash stacks with Raging Intimidation
OK : Catfolk (Mobility for Dragon Instinct) · Half-orc · Lizardfolk (tail Strike)
AVOID: Gnome, Halfling (STR ceiling), Leshy (no STR primary)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Giant Instinct (builds 1, 7) → Goliath or Hold-Scarred Orc STRONGLY preferred
  - Dragon Instinct (build 5) → Half-elf (Dragon Sorcerer dedication synergy)
  - Spirit Instinct (build 4) → Dwarf (Stonewalker ghost sight)
```

## [4] BARD — Primary: CHA · Secondary: CON, DEX

**Role:** Party buffer + Occult caster + social face. CHA sets spell DC and Inspire Courage scaling; CON for frontline Warrior Muse variants; DEX for AC and finesse.
**Poor picks cost:** Goblin has no CHA heritage default — +DEX is wasted on a caster. Goliath / Orc MAD (STR vs CHA) splits you and caps your spell DC at 16 forever.

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition = extra composition at L1
 2 Halfling   — +DEX+WIS+CHA heritage, Keen Eyes, Luck reroll
 3 Gnome      — +CON+CHA, innate primal magic reinforces Occult
 4 Half-elf   — Aiuvarin — +CHA flexible boost, Elf ancestry feats
 5 Half-orc   — Dromaar — +STR+CHA for Warrior Muse frontline
OK : Elf (speed 30, fragile) · Tengu (language god) · Vanara (Prehensile Tail)
AVOID: Goblin (no CHA heritage default — works but +DEX wasted)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Warrior Muse (2, 10) → Half-orc/Orc/Human for STR frontline
  - Polymath Wizard dip (7) → Ancient Elf REQUIRED
```

## [5] CHAMPION — Primary: STR · Secondary: CON, CHA

**Role:** Holy tank + reaction god. STR adds to attack + damage + Athletics; CON for HP (critical — you're drawing hits for allies); CHA scales Lay on Hands, Cause focus spells, and reaction power.
**Poor picks cost:** Gnome / Goblin = STR cap 16 → weaker Retributive Strike / Smite + Lay on Hands CHA ceiling low if you also want CON. Pure damage and reaction quality both drop.

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition + Versatile Heritage (Aasimar free)
 2 Dwarf      — +CON+WIS, Mountain Stoutness shield stack
 3 Half-elf   — +CHA flex, Nimble Elf for mobility build
 4 Orc        — +STR+CON, Ferocity for reaction-heavy tank
 5 Goliath    — +STR+CON, Large size for Glimpse of Redemption reach
OK : Elf (Ancient for L1 feat) · Halfling (fragile, Luck reroll) · Leshy (Seedpod)
AVOID: Gnome, Goblin (low STR ceiling clashes with Lay on Hands investment)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Liberator Mobile (5) → Nimble Elf STRONGLY preferred for +5 speed
  - Tyrant Cause (9) → Orc/Hobgoblin (evil-aligned flavor fit)
```

## [6] CLERIC — Primary: WIS · Secondary: CON (Warpriest) / DEX (Cloistered) / CHA

**Role:** Divine caster + Font healer. WIS sets spell DC and Heal scaling; CON for Warpriest frontline; DEX for Cloistered AC; CHA enables some deities' charm/inspire tools.
**Poor picks cost:** Goblin (no WIS-primary heritage). Any ancestry missing WIS primary caps spell DC at 16 and halves Font heal efficiency at higher levels.

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition + Versatile Heritage for deity flavor
 2 Dwarf      — +WIS+CON, Clan Dagger, Forge Dwarf fire resist
 3 Halfling   — +WIS+CHA, Keen Eyes, Halfling Luck
 4 Gnome      — +CON+CHA, Umbral or Fey heritage for deity fit
 5 Elf        — +WIS, speed 30 for cloistered mobility
OK : Goliath (Warpriest only, lumbering) · Hobgoblin (Warpriest) · Leshy (nature cleric)
AVOID: Goblin (no WIS primary heritage option)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Warpriest chassis (1, 3, 7) → Dwarf or Goliath for frontline
  - Cloistered chassis (2, 6) → Halfling/Gnome/Elf for caster fragility is fine
```

## [7] COMMANDER — Primary: CHA · Secondary: CON, STR (if frontline)

**Role:** Battlefield tactician + aura buffer. CHA scales tactic DCs and Banner effect radius; CON for tanking the banner position; STR if you're a front-line commander using a weapon.
**Poor picks cost:** Catfolk (CHA works but no commander synergy heritage). Goblin (−WIS flaw hurts Will saves and no CHA primary default). Gnome = small size limits banner visibility flavor (no mechanical penalty).

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition = tactic feat at L1
 2 Half-elf   — Aiuvarin — +CHA flex, extra language
 3 Halfling   — +WIS+CHA, Halfling Luck, inspire-prone
 4 Hobgoblin  — +CON+INT, Agonizing Rebuke for intimidate-commander
 5 Dwarf      — +CON+WIS, steady Fort for Banner bearer
OK : Orc (Banner Champion = Ferocity) · Leshy (nature commander) · Gnome (small)
AVOID: Catfolk (CHA works but no commander synergy feat), Goblin (no CHA heritage)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Vanguard Fighter/Melee (4, 9) → Orc/Human/Half-orc frontline
  - Strategist Wizard (3) → Ancient Elf REQUIRED
```

## [8] DRUID — Primary: WIS · Secondary: CON

**Role:** Primal caster + shapechanger + pet handler. WIS sets spell DC and Wild Shape form DC; CON for HP (important for frontline Wild Shape and Storm forms). STR matters only inside Wild Shape forms.
**Poor picks cost:** Hobgoblin (no WIS heritage default). Any −WIS ancestry caps your spell DC and Wild Shape attack rolls by 1–2.

```
★ Leshy       — +CON+WIS, Seedpod innate, Verdant Weapon, perfect flavor
 2 Human      — Natural Ambition = Wild Shape feat at L1
 3 Dwarf      — +CON+WIS, Clan Dagger, Stone Druid synergy
 4 Gnome      — +CON+CHA, Umbral Gnome for wild-shape nightvision
 5 Halfling   — +WIS+CHA, Mouser or Nomad heritage
OK : Goliath (Storm Wild Shape builds) · Goblin (Razortooth for wild jaws) · Catfolk (shifter flavor)
AVOID: Hobgoblin (no WIS heritage default)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Storm Wild Shape + Monk (1) → Human or Goliath for STR/WIS/DEX balance
  - Animal Companion Pure (7) → Halfling (Mount Ancestry Feat cat/mouse)
```

## [9] EXEMPLAR — Primary: STR or CHA · Secondary: CON

**Role:** Ikon-channeling divine warrior. STR for weapon-ikon melee; CHA for charm-ikon or social-leaning builds; CON for Ikon of the Eternal + Sky Chariot survivability.
**Poor picks cost:** Gnome = Large-ikon builds clash with Small size mechanics. MAD ancestries (STR vs CHA) split stats — pick one axis.

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition + Aasimar heritage for divine flavor
 2 Orc        — +STR+CON, Orc Ferocity stacks with Ikon of the Eternal
 3 Dwarf      — +STR+CON, Stonewalker for Gaze of the Ancestors
 4 Goliath    — +STR+CON, Large size for Sky Chariot
 5 Halfling   — +DEX+WIS for Sky Chariot mobility variant
OK : Kobold (Ancestor Echoes flavor) · Tengu (ancestral mystique) · Nagaji (oracle overlap)
AVOID: Gnome (works but Large-ikon builds clash with small)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Gaze of the Ancestors (10) → Orc or Kobold for ancestor flavor synergy
  - Sky Chariot (3, 8) → Nimble Elf or Halfling for +5 speed stack
```

## [10] FIGHTER — Primary: STR or DEX · Secondary: CON

**Role:** Best-in-class weapon accuracy (Master L5, Legendary L13) + most feats. STR = heavy weapons + heavy armor + big melee damage; DEX = finesse + light armor AC + ranged precision; CON = HP wall for frontline.
**Poor picks cost:** Gnome (STR/DEX cap 16 = −1 to hit and damage for your career). Goblin (no STR/DEX primary heritage default, and −WIS hurts Will saves). On Fighter, the primary-stat penalty compounds harder than any other class — you rely on it every attack.

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition = fighter feat at L1, any weapon
 2 Orc        — +STR+CON, Ferocity reroll for crit-focused builds
 3 Dwarf      — +CON+STR, heavy armor mastery heritage (Mountain)
 4 Elf        — +DEX+INT, Ancient Elf for Magus/Wizard multiclass
 5 Half-elf   — +CHA flex, Aiuvarin Elf feats for precision builds
OK : Hobgoblin (intimidate fighter) · Goliath (two-handed) · Halfling (sniper)
AVOID: Gnome (works but STR/DEX ceiling low for weapon crit)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Archer Pure / Sniper (2, 8) → Elf (Ancient) or Halfling (Keen Eyes)
  - Flurry/Dual (3, 6) → Human or Half-elf (2 Ancestry feats via Adopted)
  - Shield Tank (4, 9) → Dwarf (Mountain Stoutness +HP)
```

## [11] GUARDIAN — Primary: CON · Secondary: STR

**Role:** Dedicated tank + reaction interceptor. CON is everything — HP, Fortress Stance, Retaliatory Strike uses. STR for weapon output (you still hit things); DEX is wasted (heavy armor caps your DEX).
**Poor picks cost:** Gnome / Halfling = low CON ceiling fights the tank role. You'll die mid-fight to attrition because your HP pool is 2–3 levels behind. On a class whose entire point is staying up, this is catastrophic.

```
★ Dwarf       — +CON+WIS/STR, Mountain Stoutness +HP, Stone Dwarf resist
 2 Goliath    — +STR+CON, Large size for Fortress Stance reach
 3 Human      — Natural Ambition = Shield Warden at L1
 4 Orc        — +STR+CON, Ferocity save reroll
 5 Leshy      — +CON+WIS, Plant Guardian flavor, Seedpod reach
OK : Half-orc (Dromaar) · Hobgoblin (fortress discipline) · Lizardfolk (Thick Scales)
AVOID: Gnome, Halfling (low HP ceiling fights the tank role)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Mountain Pure HP (6) → Dwarf (Stone) REQUIRED for Mountain Stoutness
  - Fortress Terrain (8) → Stone Dwarf for ghost-boundary heritage
```

## [12] GUNSLINGER — Primary: DEX · Secondary: CON, INT (spellshot)

**Role:** Reload-and-shoot precision. DEX adds to attack + ranged damage via deadly/fatal traits + AC. CON for Vanguard frontline. INT for Spellshot hybrid.
**Poor picks cost:** Goliath (Large-size clash with pistol form factor, cultural dissonance). Leshy (no DEX-primary default). Orc / Goliath MAD (STR-primary wasted on a DEX class).

```
★ Human       — Natural Ambition = gunslinger feat at L1
 2 Elf        — +DEX+INT, speed 30, Ancient Elf for Spellshot dedication
 3 Halfling   — +DEX+WIS, Keen Eyes, Halfling Luck reroll
 4 Half-elf   — +CHA flex for Pistolero + Swashbuckler
 5 Dwarf      — +CON+STR/WIS, Forge Dwarf for firearm affinity
OK : Catfolk (mobility) · Hobgoblin (CON+INT for Vanguard) · Gnome (tinker flavor)
AVOID: Goliath (Large = size mismatch on pistols), Leshy (no DEX primary default)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Spellshot Hybrid (1, 10) → Ancient Elf REQUIRED (Wizard dedication)
  - Vanguard Bombardier (5, 8) → Dwarf or Orc for frontline CON
```

## [13] INVENTOR — Primary: INT · Secondary: CON, DEX

**Role:** Gadget-engineer + Overdrive burst DPS. INT sets class DC and innovation potency; CON for armor innovator frontline; DEX for weapon innovator light-armor finesse.
**Poor picks cost:** Leshy (no INT-primary default). Orc (STR-primary wasted on an INT class). Gnome works but small size limits Construct Innovator flavor.

```
★ Gnome       — +CON+CHA, innate primal, First World Magic for gadgets
 2 Goblin     — +DEX+CHA, Razortooth or Irongut for bomb innovator
 3 Human      — Natural Ambition = class feat at L1
 4 Dwarf      — +CON+STR/WIS, Forge Dwarf for armor innovator
 5 Halfling   — +DEX+WIS, Keen Eyes, Luck reroll
OK : Kobold (Dragonscale flavor) · Ratfolk (Tinkering Fingers stacks) · Tengu (tool heritage)
AVOID: Leshy (no INT primary), Orc (STR ceiling wasted on Int caster)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Explosive Blaster (4) → Goblin or Gnome for bomb-damage synergy
  - Armor Tank + Guardian (2, 7) → Dwarf (Mountain Stoutness + Forge)
```

## [14] INVESTIGATOR — Primary: INT · Secondary: DEX, WIS

**Role:** Devise-Stratagem precision striker + skill god. INT powers Stratagem accuracy and signature skills; DEX for AC and finesse weapons; WIS for Perception and Sense Motive.
**Poor picks cost:** Goblin / Goliath (MAD pressure clashes with INT-primary — your Stratagem bonus caps low). Orc (STR wasted). Leshy/Halfling work but miss the +2 INT.

```
★ Halfling    — +DEX+WIS, Keen Eyes negates concealment, Luck reroll
 2 Human      — Natural Ambition = methodology feat at L1
 3 Elf        — +DEX+INT, Ancient Elf for Wizard dip, forensic skills
 4 Gnome      — +CON+CHA, Empathetic Plea for interrogation flavor
 5 Tengu      — +DEX+WIS, Tengu Lore for investigation skill god
OK : Half-elf (Aiuvarin for Elf feats) · Ratfolk (tinker detective) · Hobgoblin (Remorseless Lash)
AVOID: Goblin, Goliath (MAD pressure clashes with Int primary)
BUILD EXCEPTIONS:
  - Empiricist + Wizard (7) → Ancient Elf REQUIRED
```
