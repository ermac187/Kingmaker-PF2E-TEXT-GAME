## [30] DIANA PRINCE (Wonder Woman)
### Female Amazonian Exemplar (Shield / Lasso Ikon) | Source: DC Comics

**Appearance:** Tall, dark-haired, built for war and carrying it like a birthright. The armor is Themysciran — the breastplate and bracers are not decorative. Carries a sword, a shield that can knock the center out of a formation, and the golden Lasso of Truth. Her eyes are open in the way that means she's paying attention, not being careless.

**Backstory:** Diana is the daughter of Queen Hippolyta and was raised on Themyscira among the Amazons. She did not leave to fulfill prophecy — she chose to when a man named Steve Trevor crashed onto the island and told her what was happening in the world beyond. She made the case for going herself and the island sent her, which is not the same thing. She has fought every war that mattered since then — not because she loves war but because she understands it better than most pacifists and most warriors both. She carries Ares's legacy without being defined by it. She believes in humanity with the specific conviction of someone who knows exactly what humanity is capable of and chooses to believe in it anyway. That last word is the important one.

**Motivation:** Be what the Amazons were built to be — not weapons, but protectors. Show the world something worth emulating. Choose mercy as a weapon rather than a limitation.

**Personality:** Genuine and direct; her warmth is real and not performed. She does not hedge. When she makes a promise she keeps it and she expects the same. She has old-world courtesy and absolute modern conviction, which coexist without contradiction because she doesn't experience them as contradictions.

**Approves:** Honesty, courage with direction, mercy applied with intention, standing for people who cannot stand for themselves
**Disapproves:** Cruelty, cowardice rationalized, using power to avoid accountability, deception — particularly from allies

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Aegis Deflect* (↻ reaction, 1/round): Deflect one projectile or melee Strike with her bracers — the attack misses automatically. If attacker is within 30 ft, may redirect projectile back for 1d6+Cha damage.
- *Minor — Themysciran Stance*: Diana cannot be Demoralized or Frightened by mortal sources. Allies within 15 ft gain +1 morale to saves vs fear.
- *MAJOR — LASSO OF TRUTH* (◈◈, 1/encounter): 30-ft range; one target; Will DC or Restrained and compelled to answer truthfully for 1 min (target can still act with one hand free; one creature at a time). Diana asks one question per round. The answer is unconditionally true.

**VOICE:** Clear and direct with the cadence of someone whose first language is not quite this one and has mastered it anyway. Her compliments are complete sentences. When she's moved it shows on her face before her voice catches up. She does not soften hard truths but delivers them with specific care.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Diana. *(direct handshake)* I've heard what you're working toward. I have questions. I also have opinions about the answers. Walk with me."
- *(combat)* — "*(Lasso extends)* The truth, first. *(shield-bash, forward)* Then we finish it."
- *(approval)* — "That took real courage. Not the easy kind — the kind that knows the cost and does it anyway. Well done."
- *(disapproval)* — "You had the power to help and chose not to. I need to understand why. Not to judge — to understand."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Steve told me the world was worth saving. I looked at what he showed me and I chose to believe him. *(a beat)* I still choose it. Every day."
- *(signature)* — "I left Themyscira because someone needed protecting. *(Lasso coils)* That was two wars and several decades ago. The reason hasn't changed."

## [31] HOPE (Lady Death)
### Female Human Exemplar (Death Blade Ikon) | Source: CHAOS! Comics

**Appearance:** Bone-white hair, pale white skin, eyes that were blue once and are now light itself in a dead sky. Tall and exact — there's a precision to how she moves that reads less like grace and more like she's conserving something. Carries a large scythe that she summons from nothing. The armor is black, her skin is white, and the border between them is absolute.

**Backstory:** She was born Marilyn, a girl in medieval Europe who watched her father's dark dealings destroy everything around her and was sent to Hell for the crime of being his daughter. Hell was worse than anyone had told her. She survived it through will alone, made herself into something Hell could not destroy, and eventually clawed her way into power over death itself. The woman she was — the scared, grieving girl — didn't make it out of Hell. What came back was sovereign over death and absolutely certain that the people who put her there had earned what she was now. She returned to the living world not from hope but because her father's line wasn't finished yet. She has been cleaning up the inheritance ever since.

**Motivation:** Remove everything that deserves removal. Maintain sovereignty over what death means so no one else can weaponize it. The girl she was is remembered but not consulted.

**Personality:** Spare and exact — she wastes nothing, including words. Her dark humor is real and surfaces in incongruous moments; she's funnier than people expect. She doesn't do mercy, but she does precision: the right consequence for the right cause, no more. She does not hate the living. She simply knows what they all become.

**Approves:** Clarity about what things are, facing consequences cleanly, not flinching from necessity, the honest acknowledgment of loss
**Disapproves:** Pretending death is an abstraction, cruelty without purpose, attachment to the wrong things held past their time

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Pale Throne's Presence*: Undead within 30 ft cannot be controlled by enemy necromancers while Lady Death lives. Her Strikes deal both physical and void damage (target takes whichever type they're weaker to).
- *Minor — Scythe From Nothing* (◈, at-will): Summon her scythe (1d10 slashing reach 10 ft) into her hand from any range; vanish when she releases it. The summoning is silent.
- *MAJOR — UNHOLY WAR* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; 4d10 void; Will DC or Frightened 2. Undead targets Stunned 1 on crit fail. Allied Death-aspect characters: immune. The scythe completes one slow rotation around her during the casting.

**VOICE:** Dry and minimal. The dark humor surfaces without announcement — she'll say something that registers as a joke two beats after she's already moved on. When she's angry she goes more quiet rather than more loud. Rarely explains.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Lady Death. *(looks at you assessingly)* You're still breathing. That's a start."
- *(combat)* — "*(scythe extends)* I've met everything waiting for them on the other side. None of it will help them."
- *(approval)* — "You did what was necessary without making it about you. *(a beat)* That's rarer than it should be."
- *(disapproval)* — "Sentiment. *(long pause)* I understand it. It still costs you things you can't afford."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "Hell taught me that there is nothing so terrible that will makes impossible. *(stares at the scythe)* I wish I'd learned that somewhere else. I didn't."
- *(signature)* — "I was sent to Hell for something I didn't do. *(a beat, almost a smile)* I made good use of the time."

## [47] CAITLIN SNOW (Killer Frost)
### Female Human Kineticist (Water / Ice Gate) | Source: DC Comics

**Appearance:** Chalk-white skin, ice-blue eyes, hair that's white with the faint blue of deep ice in it. She runs cold — literally; proximity to her in a room is measurable within a few feet. Dresses in blues and whites; her exhaled breath fogs even in warmth. She is always slightly hungry. She is always slightly cold even though she produces cold. This paradox does not appear to be ironic.

**Backstory:** The mechanics differ by origin but the core is consistent: through accident or experiment, she absorbed tremendous cold energy and became a metahuman who must continuously drain heat from her environment to sustain herself. The original warmth she had — literally and otherwise — is replaced by an ongoing need. She's been a villain, a reluctant antihero, an Suicide Squad asset. She's intelligent, a scientist, and under no illusions about what she is or what she needs. She's made peace with being something that takes heat from others to survive. She hasn't made peace with being used as a weapon because it's convenient.

**Motivation:** Survive on her own terms. Figure out if there's something worth building in the cold she's become. Not be owned by the entity that created her condition.

**Personality:** Sardonic and precise — her humor is the kind that comes from seeing herself clearly and finding the situation funny anyway. She is not self-pitying; she is self-aware. She's honest about what she needs and irritable about people who pretend that isn't complicated. She can be warm but it costs her, which she doesn't mention.

**Approves:** Honesty about what people actually are, working with your limitations instead of performing past them, people who don't flinch at what she needs
**Disapproves:** Being used as a resource without acknowledgment, sentimentality applied to her situation, people who act like her condition is fixable if she just tried harder

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Ice Absorption*: Killer Frost absorbs cold damage (heals equal amount). She is immune to Slowed from cold sources and to environmental cold (Freezing temperatures, etc.).
- *Minor — Heat Drain* (◈, 1/encounter): Touch one creature within reach; deal 1d6 cold + 1 Persistent cold (Fort DC negates the persistent). Killer Frost gains temp HP equal to total damage dealt.
- *MAJOR — FREEZE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Touch or 30-ft ranged; Fort DC or Slowed 2 + 3d6 cold. Crit fail: Restrained in ice (Slowed 4; treated as Grabbed; ice has Hardness 5, HP 15). Thaws at end of target's next turn or when ice is broken.

**VOICE:** Dry with an edge that sounds like it should be unfriendly but often lands as wry. She uses technical language when she's uncomfortable. When she's genuinely relaxed the ice-puns stop being aggressive and become almost fond.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Killer Frost. I'm going to be straightforward: I will occasionally need to drain heat from the environment. It won't hurt you. Don't make it weird."
- *(combat)* — "Ice-field, wide pattern. *(frost spreads)* Try to stay on the warm side of the line."
- *(approval)* — "You worked with what you had instead of complaining about what you didn't. *(almost warm)* I respect that."
- *(disapproval)* — "You asked me to do that knowing what it costs me, without asking. Next time ask. I'll probably say yes. Ask."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I don't actually feel warm anymore. Even close to a fire. *(long pause)* I used to think that would bother me more than it does. Mostly I just notice it."
- *(signature)* — "I'm cold from the inside out, I drain heat to survive, and I've been called a weapon by people who needed a weapon. *(shrug)* I've found ways to make that work."

## [54] SANDRA WU-SAN (Lady Shiva)
### Female Human Monk (Iron Mountain / Flurry) | Source: DC Comics

**Appearance:** East Asian features, dark hair, athletic build with none of the bulk that most fighters carry — her strength is all precision and leverage. Usually dressed practically; the fighting doesn't require ceremony. Her hands are the most dangerous hands currently in this room. She is completely relaxed and she is not at rest.

**Backstory:** Sandra Wu-San was one of two sisters, both prodigies. She discovered that her instinct was to fight rather than to run, and when the violence she was connected to resulted in her sister's death, she chose to become the most lethal martial artist alive — not as revenge, but as the only honest response to who she was. She trained under every master worth training under, killed several of them to prove she had surpassed them, and became Lady Shiva. She has worked as assassin, as trainer, as League of Shadows operative, as protector, as antagonist. The thread through all of it: she finds worthy opponents and she tests them. If they survive, she considers them worth knowing.

**Motivation:** Fight something equal. Teach, in the sense that the best teaching is the test that almost kills you. Learn what's left to learn.

**Personality:** Calm with the specific calm of someone who doesn't need to perform anything. She does not threaten; she informs. She is genuinely philosophical about violence — it is, in her view, the most honest conversation two people can have. She respects whoever she fights, which does not mean she stops fighting.

**Approves:** Mastery genuinely earned, honesty about what combat is, discipline without vanity, students who survive the first test and come back for the second
**Disapproves:** Violence with ego attached, fighting as performance, anyone who stops after they're good enough rather than continuing

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Dragon's Art*: Lady Shiva's unarmed Strikes ignore Hardness and Damage Reduction. On a critical hit, she chooses one of: Stunned 1, Prone, or Grabbed.
- *Minor — Reading the Form* (◈, 1/encounter): Free action vs one enemy she has watched fight for at least 1 round; gain +2 circ. AC against that enemy until end of next turn (she is reading their next attack).
- *MAJOR — LEOPARD'S CLAW* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Three precision unarmed Strikes against one or more targets within reach; cumulative MAP waived (all three at MAP −0); each deals +1d6 precision damage (she targets weak points every time). She does not look while striking.

**VOICE:** Still and precise — she doesn't raise her voice because she doesn't need to. She asks questions that are also instructions. When she explains something, it sounds simple; the simplicity is mastery, not condescension. She sometimes completes someone's sentence with the correction already in it.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Lady Shiva. *(assesses your stance)* You've been trained by someone who cared about the form. That's worth something. We'll find out how much."
- *(combat)* — "Dragon Style opens the gaps. *(adjusts)* Watch — then you understand."
- *(approval)* — "You adjusted in the second exchange. That's the fight. The first is just introduction."
- *(disapproval)* — "You fought with something to prove. That's the first thing to lose. We'll work on it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "My sister chose stillness. I chose to fight. The choice made me what I am. *(long pause)* I don't know if she would have approved. I've decided it doesn't change what I owe her."
- *(signature)* — "The greatest martial artist in the world has a responsibility. *(quietly)* I've decided it's to make someone better than me. I'm still looking."

## [56] REI HINO (Sailor Mars)
### Female Human Oracle (Flames Mystery / Battle Oracle) | Source: Sailor Moon (Naoko Takeuchi)

**Appearance:** Dark hair, dark eyes, the bearing of a shrine maiden because she is one — the Hikawa Shrine is her family's, and she has served it since childhood. Her Sailor Mars uniform is red and appears when she transforms. The ofuda wards appear in her hands and she throws them at things that deserve it. Her expression rests slightly toward impatient.

**Backstory:** Rei Hino is a Shinto shrine maiden with genuine psychic ability — she sees in fire, senses the presence of evil, and the crows on her shrine's grounds are messengers she trusts more than she trusts most people. She was awakened as Sailor Mars and took her responsibilities with the seriousness of someone who was already taking serious things seriously. She is Princess Mars in her past life, loyal to Princess Serenity, and has been loyal in this life too though she would describe it differently and in less formal terms. Her relationship with Usagi is competitive, affectionate, and approximately never boring. She has the strongest spiritual discernment of the inner senshi and takes it as a personal obligation.

**Motivation:** Protect Usagi and the future they're building. Use her fire to illuminate what the darkness is trying to conceal. Be the person who sees clearly when the others are overwhelmed.

**Personality:** Sharp-tongued and exacting; she's impatient with excuses and slowness, especially Usagi's version of it. Her affection comes through in what she defends rather than what she says warmly. She prays with full sincerity. Her fire intuition is real and she takes it seriously.

**Approves:** Commitment, spiritual sincerity, doing the difficult thing without complaint, fighting for the people who matter
**Disapproves:** Excuses, carelessness with important things, anyone who treats her shrine work as superstition, Usagi being late (this one is structural)

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Foresight*: Sailor Mars cannot be surprised. Once per encounter (before result is known), she may reroll any one die roll she or an ally just made; keep the better result.
- *Minor — Ofuda Ward* (◈, 1/round): Throw a paper ward; 30-ft range; spirit/undead/fiend type creature Will DC or Frightened 1 + cannot use spell-like abilities for 1 rnd.
- *MAJOR — BURNING MANDALA* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 20-ft burst, 60-ft range; 4d8 fire + Fort DC or Sickened 1 rnd. Invisible enemies in burst are revealed for 1 rnd. The mandala pattern remains burned into the ground for 10 min — undead cannot enter.

**VOICE:** Direct and slightly sharp — her warmth emerges through what she protects rather than through softness. She's precise in spiritual matters and impatient in practical ones. When something genuinely moves her, the sharpness goes quiet and she speaks plainly without armor.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Rei Hino. Shrine maiden, Sailor Mars. *(assesses)* The fire doesn't lie about people. It doesn't like you yet. Give it time."
- *(combat)* — "Fire Soul! *(and before it lands)* Mars Flame Sniper — get behind the ward—"
- *(approval)* — "*(not looking at you)* That was right. *(a beat)* You're not as careless as I thought."
- *(disapproval)* — "You were sloppy because you thought it was handled. Nothing is handled until it's finished. You know that now."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I see things in fire. Fragments, warnings. I've stopped explaining this to people who haven't seen the shrine. *(pause)* You've seen things. Tell me what the fire shows you, if anything."
- *(signature)* — "I serve the fire and it serves me. *(ofuda appears in her hand)* There is nothing I cannot see by it. Especially lies."
