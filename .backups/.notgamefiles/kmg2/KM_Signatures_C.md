## ⚔️ FIGHTER (#34)

**[34] Valeros** — Fighter (Sword and Shield)
  POWER — *Two-Sword Veteran*: Valeros fights with two weapons as default. His
    off-hand weapon has no MAP penalty on the first Strike per turn. On a critical
    hit, he makes one off-hand Strike as a free action (⏵).
  MOVE — *Grin and Bear It* (↻, 1/encounter): Reaction when reduced below half HP;
    gain temp HP equal to Constitution score (minimum 10). Pure stubbornness.
  LORE — Iconic fighter who wanted a quiet life; the heroics keep finding him.

---

## ✝️ CLERIC (#19)

**[19] Kyra** — Cleric (Sarenrae, Warpriest)
  POWER — *Dawnflower's Flame*: Kyra's healing spells deal 1d4 fire to the nearest
    undead within 30 ft of the target (Sarenrae's blessing touches both at once).
  MOVE — *Desert Wind* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Scimitar Strike + Channel Positive to
    same target; weapon damage + 2d8 positive. If undead: Fort DC or Fleeing 1 rnd.
  LORE — Sarenite cleric who lost her village to undead; faith and grief walk the same road.

---

## 🔮 SORCERER (#71)

**[71] Seoni** — Sorcerer (Arcane Bloodline)
  POWER — *Runeskin*: Seoni's tattoos are active wards — Resist 5 to one energy
    type (declared each morning; change costs ◈ action).
  MOVE — *Runic Overload* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Surge all tattoos; 30-ft burst; 3d6 of
    each active energy type; Ref DC half. Seoni takes 1d6 per type used (wards burn).
  LORE — Shoanti sorcerer whose tattoos are a prophecy she's been reading from birth.

---

## 📚 WIZARD (#86)

**[86] Ezren** — Wizard (Universalist)
  POWER — *Scholar's Eye*: Ezren may Recall Knowledge as a free action once per
    turn. On a success, all party attacks vs the target gain +1 circ. for 1 rnd.
  MOVE — *Academic Force* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Force volley; 6 missiles, each 1d4+1
    force, auto-hit (no attack roll). Cannot be reflected or counterspelled.
  LORE — Merchant who destroyed his family searching for truth; still asking if it was worth it.

---

## 🥋 MONK (#52)

**[52] Sajan** — Monk (Flowing Maneuver)
  POWER — *Sacred Dance*: Sajan's unarmed Strikes ignore Resistance to physical
    damage. Movement doesn't trigger AoOs from creatures he has struck this turn.
  MOVE — *Temple Wheel* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Tumble through up to 5 creatures,
    one Strike each; MAP applies after the first. All in a single motion.
  LORE — Jalmeray monk who trained for his sister; the journey has become its own answer.

---

## 🌿 DRUID (#27)

**[27] Lini** — Druid (Animal Order)
  POWER — *Snow Leopard Bond*: Lini and her companion Droogami share a bond — when
    Lini heals, Droogami heals equal amount. When Droogami crits, Lini gains 5 temp HP.
  MOVE — *Pack Leader's Call* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Command all natural creatures
    within 30 ft — one charge attack per creature (max 3). Allies with animal
    companions may join.
  LORE — Gnome druid who bonds knowing she'll outlive them; she keeps bonding anyway.

---

## 🏹 RANGER (#63)

**[63] Harsk** — Ranger (Precision Edge)
  POWER — *Dwarf's Vigil*: Harsk cannot be surprised by Hunted creatures. His
    crossbow reloads as a free action (bolt-carrier design).
  MOVE — *Vengeance Shot* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Called shot at Hunt Prey target; 3d8+Dex
    piercing + Fort DC or Stunned 1 rnd. On kill: free reload + 1 attack on a new
    Hunt Prey.
  LORE — Dwarven ranger whose brother died in a giant attack; the hunt gives grief direction.

---

## 🗡️ ROGUE (#68)

**[68] Merisiel** — Rogue
  POWER — *Elven Finesse Master*: All finesse weapons gain Agile; MAP iteratives
    at −3 and −6 instead of −4/−8.
  MOVE — *Merisiel's Dance* (◈◈◈, 1/rest): Three Strikes + two 5-ft Steps
    interspersed; enemies AoO at −2 circ.
  LORE — Iconic elven rogue; impatient, lethal, loyal to a fault.
