*Stacking Will saves vs magic. Anti-caster frontliner. Warded Mind at L12 grants mental immunity while raging.*

|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|

| Mod | +4 | +1 | +4 | -1 | +2 | -1 |

**HP** 26 | **AC** 17 (Scale Mail) | **Init** +7 | **Perc** +7 | **Speed** 15 ft (Dwarf 20 − Scale Mail 5)
**Fort** +9 | **Ref** +4 | **Will** +7
**Weapon:** Dwarven Waraxe (1H S, 1d8+4, Sweep) | **Armor:** Scale Mail
**Skills:** Athletics +7, Intimidation +0 (CHA −1), Survival +4, Warfare Lore +2 (Trained via Warrior bg)
**Class Features:** Rage (Superstition — +2 dmg/die vs divine/occult/arcane/primal, +1 vs all magic saves), Deny Advantage, Raging Resistance (mental)

**Boost Priority:** STR → CON → WIS → DEX

**Race Options** *(ranked best-fit — pick any)*

| 6 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | Diehard pairs well with WIS 14 / high Will — this build regularly enters melee against powerful spellcasters and needs the safety net. |
| 7 | Goblin | Unbreakable | +4 max HP compensates for the CON 18 build already being high HP — more total buffer against the heavy-hitting casters this build hunts. |
| 8 | Android | Artilect | Nanite surge (+2 trained skill 1/day) on Warfare Lore or Athletics fits thematically as an arcane-resistant constructed warrior. |
| 9 | Tiefling | Grimspawn | Void resist 5 and Intimidation bonuses complement the anti-undead/caster role Superstition plays in Kingmaker's later acts. |
| 10 | Lizardfolk | Frilled | Intimidation bonus reinforces the Demoralize-on-hit loop; combined with high Fort this build doesn't fear the melee range needed. |
