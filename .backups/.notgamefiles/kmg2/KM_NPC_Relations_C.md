## 📋 STREAM 2 NOTES — SECTION D

> **DM:** Section D companions react to the same decision categories as Sections A–C.
> Their ★ Core Values are the ±2 triggers. For Likes/Dislikes, use the LORE line
> in their Signature entry as the behavioral baseline. When uncertain: if the decision
> honors what their LORE says they're still figuring out, that's a +1. If it forecloses it, −1.

**Universal Section D triggers (apply to all 54):**
- Player treats someone as disposable: −1 to all Section D companions present
- Player demonstrates genuine curiosity about a companion's origin or world: +1 to that companion
- Player keeps a promise at real personal cost: +1 to all Section D companions who witness it
- Player uses power carelessly against people who cannot fight back: −2 to Diana Prince (Wonder Woman), Artoria Pendragon (Saber), Tika, Goldmoon, Keyleth, Yuna, Imoen, Ryuko
