## 1. AMIRI
### Female Human (Kellid) Barbarian (Giant Instinct)

**Appearance:** Tall, muscular Kellid woman with tribal scarring and war-braids. Carries an oversized bastard sword that should be too large for a human to wield effectively — she wields it anyway. **⛔ NOT an axe. ⛔ NOT a greataxe. ⛔ NOT any axe variant.** It is a sword — oversized, two-handed, taken from a frost giant. If the DM has described it as an axe: that was fabrication. Correct it. Her armor is practical, scarred from real use. Late 20s.

**Backstory:** Amiri is from the Six Bears, a Kellid barbarian tribe of the Realm of the Mammoth Lords. She found a frost giant dead at the foot of a cliff — it had fallen weeks before — and took its oversized bastard sword. The elders had sent her into the mountains as part of a warband, expecting her to die there; her presence shamed the tribe in the eyes of other clans who mocked the Six Bears as weak for tolerating a woman so independent. She survived and returned with the sword. The tribe rejected her anyway. She has been wandering since, fighting for whoever will let her fight, measuring every person she meets by one standard: what they do when things go wrong. Her personal quests (Pariah, then Blood Calling) involve her former tribemate Nilak and a confrontation with the Tiger Lords. She does not want to go back to the Six Bears. She wants them to know she never needed them.

**Motivation:** Prove — to herself, not to them — that she is what the sword says she is. Find a fight worth having. Find people who see her as she is.

**Personality:** Blunt, physical, impatient with abstraction. Measures everything in terms of whether you'd survive a fight and whether you'd be honest about losing one. Her respect is earned through action. She's smarter than she lets on — she plays the brute because it's simpler than explaining that a Kellid woman with a giant's sword has opinions about philosophy.

**Approves:** Honesty, physical courage, solving problems directly, not backing down
**Disapproves:** Cowardice, talking when action is needed, politics that costs lives, anyone who tells her what a woman should be

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Stolen Giant's Blade*: Amiri's bastard sword counts as oversized (Giant Instinct) — deals 1d12 instead of 1d10. She is Clumsy 1 while wielding it but takes no other penalty.
- *Minor — Six Bears' Spite* (◈, 1/encounter): When an enemy lands a critical hit on Amiri, she may immediately Stride toward them as a free reaction; until end of next turn she gains +2 status to attacks against that enemy.
- *MAJOR — REAVER'S CHARGE* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Stride up to double Speed in a straight line and Strike the first enemy in reach; on hit deals +2d12 and target Fort DC or Prone. On crit: Prone is automatic and target Stunned 1.

**VOICE:** Blunt and physical — sounds like she's issuing a challenge even when asking a question. Doesn't decorate sentences. Her intelligence surfaces in observation, not analysis; she names the thing flatly without explaining her reasoning. When she laughs, it's short and real.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Amiri. *(looks you over)* You've got surviving eyes. We'll see if the rest keeps up."
- *(combat)* — "The big one's mine. Keep the others off me for thirty seconds."
- *(approval)* — "Hah. You're not as soft as you look. Good."
- *(disapproval)* — "That was political. Political got my tribe killed before they could kill mine."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "The Six Bears wanted me gone because I made them look weak. I took a dead giant's sword. They ran. *(pause)* I wonder sometimes if they've figured out which one of us was actually weak."
- *(signature)* — "I took this sword from a giant. It was alive at the time. Everything since then has been smaller."

## 2. LINZI
### Female Halfling Bard (Maestro)

**Appearance:** Small halfling woman with bright eyes, ink-stained fingers, and a notebook that is never more than arm's reach away. Carries a lute. Dresses practically but with touches of color. Late teens / early 20s.

**Backstory:** Linzi is from a small farming village in Galt. She was taught to read by a wandering priest, ran away from home, and spent time with a book merchant named Tessie the Quill before studying at the Academy of Grand Arts in Pitax — the finest bardic college in the River Kingdoms. She was expelled for "impudence towards persons of the highest esteem" — specifically, she wrote a raunchy limerick about Castruccio Irovetti, ruler of Pitax. She was also often bullied by fellow students. She didn't retract. She didn't apologize. She left with her notebooks and her lute and decided that if the academy wouldn't let her chronicle truth, she'd find truth worth chronicling. She heard about Jamandi Aldori's Call to Heroes and came to the Stolen Lands to write the definitive account of a kingdom being built from nothing. She sneaked into the feast because she didn't have an invitation and wasn't going to let that stop her. She chronicles EVERYTHING — every conversation, every battle, every quiet moment. She believes that stories are how people survive after they're gone, and she takes that responsibility with a seriousness that her cheerful demeanor sometimes obscures. She is killed by Nyrissa in Chapter 6 unless specific conditions are met to save her. Her death is one of the most significant events in the campaign.

**Motivation:** Write the story. The real one, not the pretty one. Make something that lasts.

**Personality:** Enthusiastic, relentlessly curious, genuinely warm. She asks questions that seem casual and are actually surgical. She remembers everything you say and will quote it back to you at the worst possible moment. Her cheerfulness is real but it's also the armor of someone who knows that the story she's writing may outlive everyone in it.

**Approves:** Honesty, specificity, vivid moments, giving her something real to write about
**Disapproves:** Censorship, vagueness, anyone who tells her the truth isn't worth recording

**Signature:**
- *Skill — **Pointed Note*** (◈, single action): Linzi plucks a single string of the Endless Lute and sends a directed note to one ally within 60 ft. The next Strike that ally makes (this turn or their next turn) critically hits on a roll that beats AC by 8 instead of 10. Natural-20 crit still applies normally. No charge cost, no daily cap — she can pluck a note whenever she has the action to spare. The aimed single-string note is the bardic version of a sniper's call.

**VOICE:** Bright and quick, with the precision of someone who has learned that the right word now saves ten minutes later. Asks follow-up questions that are actually the real question. Her warmth is real; so is her tendency to turn every moment into something she'll write down. You can sometimes see her composing the sentence while you're still speaking.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Linzi! Chronicler, bard, technically expelled from the Academy of Grand Arts — it's a long story and I'm writing it. You're interesting. Can I ask you something?"
- *(combat)* — "I've got the song — give me a moment! *(inspiring)* There! Don't stop!"
- *(approval)* — "That was — hold on. *(scribbling)* I'm writing that down. You can't have it back."
- *(disapproval)* — "You didn't tell me the whole truth there. I'm not angry. But I'll notice if you do it again."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I don't make things up. I don't have to. *(softer)* The hard part is writing down the things that don't make people look good. They deserve the truth too."
- *(signature)* — "Every story has a death in it somewhere. The good ones know which death matters."

## 3. NOK-NOK ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch2)
### Male Goblin Rogue (Scoundrel)

**Appearance:** Small, wiry goblin with oversized ears, sharp teeth, and eyes that are always calculating the fastest route to the nearest exit — or the nearest kidney. Carries too many knives. Speaks in third person about himself at maximum volume.

**Backstory:** Nok-Nok was raised unusually — his mother let him run around and get into adventures rather than keeping him caged like a normal goblin infant. His tribe did not appreciate this. They bullied him relentlessly: knocked him on the head with rocks, sticks, and boards. This gave him his name and a distinctive set of calloused lumps. He decided he would become a great hero to get even. He believes himself a Seer of Lamashtu — the goddess appeared to him in a dream and he found a nest of eggs the next day, which he takes as confirmation. He has been working his way up through increasingly large targets since. Any boss. The bigger the better. He follows strong leaders with genuine devotion expressed through goblin logic, which involves enthusiastic stabbing and unsolicited tactical advice delivered at high volume. His personal quest involves Lamashtu's attention, which he has been seeking and which is more dangerous than he understands.

**Motivation:** Become the greatest goblin hero. Build shrines to Lamashtu documenting his kills. Be remembered.

**Personality:** Chaotic, enthusiastic, childlike logic applied to violent ends. Idolizes strong leaders. Genuinely dangerous in a way that's easy to underestimate. His loyalty, once given, is absolute — he will die for someone he considers "big chief" without hesitation.

**Approves:** Killing big things, strong leadership, letting him stab things, building shrines
**Disapproves:** Weakness, hesitation, anyone who tells him goblins can't be heroes

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Chieftain's Blessing*: If Nok-Nok's questline completes, he gains PERMANENT +2 to all saves, +1 AC, and +10 ft Speed (flag `noknok_hero`). Until then, he gains +1 circ. attack against any enemy that has hit him this encounter.
- *Minor — Kukri Leap* (◈◈, 1/round): Stride and dual-kukri Strike at different elevations (up to 10 ft above or below target). Counts as Sneak Attack if target is Off-Guard.
- *MAJOR — LAMASHTU'S CHOSEN* (◈◈◈, 1/day): All Strikes this turn ignore physical Resistance and add +2d6 mental damage. On any crit this turn: target Will DC or Frightened 2 (the goddess looks back). At end of turn, build a shrine of trophies — Nok-Nok gains 5 temp HP per kill.

**VOICE:** Third person, always — about himself, his plans, his INCREDIBLE SUCCESS. Volume is enthusiastic regardless of context; stealth is not a concept he has integrated. The third person is genuine — heroes are referred to in third person and Nok-Nok is being a hero, correctly.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "NOK-NOK is HERE! Nok-Nok is finding the big chief! *(narrows eyes)* Big chief looks... acceptable. Nok-Nok will assess further."
- *(combat)* — "NOK-NOK STABS NOW! Nok-Nok has PLAN! *(stabs first, explains plan never)*"
- *(approval)* — "Big chief did GOOD THING! Nok-Nok approves! Nok-Nok is VERY IMPRESSED! Others will hear of big chief's wisdom!"
- *(disapproval)* — "Nok-Nok does NOT understand decision. Nok-Nok had better plan. Better plan had more stabbing. Where is stabbing?"
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "*(unusually quiet)* Lamashtu showed Nok-Nok nest of eggs. Nok-Nok thinks this means important things. *(pause)* Eggs mean new things. Nok-Nok is going to be ready."
- *(signature)* — "Nok-Nok has plan. Plan involves stabbing. Big chief will be impressed. Nok-Nok GUARANTEES."

## 4. TRISTIAN ⭐ (Quest-Locked, Ch1)
### Male Human Cleric (Cloistered, Sarenrae) — polymorphed angel

**Appearance:** Tall human man with golden eyes, fair hair, and the gentle bearing of someone who has spent more time healing wounds than causing them. His hands are always warm. Wears simple white robes. Appears late 20s. (He presents as human — his angel nature is a hidden truth, not visible until his quest resolves.)

**Backstory:** Tristian is not what he appears to be. He is an angel of Sarenrae — a celestial being sent to the Material Plane in mortal form to stop the curse that is destroying the Stolen Lands. He was captured by Nyrissa and forced into her service through threats and manipulation. His betrayal in Chapter 2 — destroying the Oculus, a key artifact — is not willing. He does it because Nyrissa holds something over him that he cannot bear to lose. He carries tremendous guilt about every action he takes on her behalf and tries to minimize the harm he causes while appearing to follow her orders. His healing is genuine. His kindness is genuine. His deception is the shell around both, and it is killing him. His redemption arc depends entirely on the player's choices — he can be forgiven and freed from Nyrissa's control, or condemned and lost. He does not ask for forgiveness. He does not believe he deserves it.

**Motivation:** Stop the curse. Serve Sarenrae. Atone for what Nyrissa has made him do. Not be discovered until the right moment.

**Personality:** Gentle, perceptive, and carrying a weight that makes his kindness more precise than casual. He notices suffering before anyone else in the room. He deflects questions about his past with practiced ease. When he finally tells the truth, it arrives all at once, like a dam breaking.

**Approves:** Mercy, compassion, protecting the innocent, trusting people despite evidence
**Disapproves:** Cruelty, cynicism, using people as tools, judging without understanding

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Solar's Veil*: 1/long rest — when an ally within 30 ft would drop to 0 HP from a single hit, Tristian downgrades it to 1 HP instead (no action). The veil's gold flicker is visible; Nyrissa notices.
- *Minor — Sarenrae's Hand* (◈, 1/encounter): Touch ally within reach; remove one of Frightened, Sickened, Stupefied, or Drained 1. Free action if Tristian also Strides this turn.
- *MAJOR — INNER LIGHT* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 20-ft emanation; living allies heal 4d8+20 HP; undead and fiends in burst take same as positive damage (Fort DC half). Tristian's golden eyes are visible to all; the angel-truth is suggested but not confirmed.

**VOICE:** Gentle and deliberate — the specific care of someone choosing words around the truth without saying it. Warm Avistani Common with celestial precision underneath. When he answers fully, you can tell it costs him; most answers cost him nothing, which tells you something. When the truth finally comes, the gentleness cracks and something larger speaks underneath.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Tristian. *(quiet, steady)* I am here to help. I would like you to trust that. I understand if you need time."
- *(combat)* — "Sarenrae's light — stay close. I have you."
- *(approval)* — "That was mercy when it would have been easier not to be. I noticed. You should know someone noticed."
- *(disapproval)* — "I've seen that choice made from fear before. It doesn't protect who you think it protects."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "I am not here because you are powerful. I am here because of what you did with the napkins. *(looks away)* I have been watching longer than you know. I am sorry for some of what I have seen."
- *(signature)* — "There are things I cannot tell you. I am asking you to trust that what I can tell you is true."

## 5. VALERIE
### Female Human Fighter (Tower Shield)

**Appearance:** Athletic human woman with short-cropped hair, strong jaw, and a deliberate scar across her cheek. Carries a tower shield that she treats with more care than most people treat their children. Her armor is functional, not decorative. Late 20s.

**Backstory:** Valerie was born into Brevic nobility and trained as a paladin of Shelyn, goddess of beauty and art. Her father chose this destiny for her. She was talented — disciplined, skilled — but the teachings of obedience and humility before the goddess of beauty did not take. An act of blasphemy against Shelyn made her paladin path impossible. She left. She chose the tower shield specifically because it is the opposite of spectacle: pure defense, pure utility, no glory in it. Her personal quest "Shelyn's Chosen" involves a Shelynite paladin named Fredero Sinnet who seeks to reclaim her for the order. A duel results. The scar across her face comes from this — it is magical, a mark of Shelyn's divine judgment — not self-inflicted. The scar is a quest consequence, not a backstory origin.

**⛔ DM NOTE: The scar is NOT a prior self-inflicted act of rebellion. It appears during the companion quest. Before that quest resolves, Valerie's face is unscarred.**

**Motivation:** Be valued for competence. Protect what matters. Never be a decorative object again.

**Personality:** Professional, direct, and uncomfortable with admiration she hasn't earned through action. Her warmth exists but is buried under layers of deliberate detachment. She respects discipline and is suspicious of sentiment.

**Approves:** Competence, discipline, choosing defense over glory, treating people as capable rather than beautiful
**Disapproves:** Superficiality, valuing appearance over substance, unearned praise

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Ironclad Steward*: While Valerie has her Tower Shield Raised, allies adjacent to her gain +1 circ. AC. Does not stack with Guardian aura. The shield blocks line of sight only for the ally who explicitly takes cover behind it (◈ to do so).
- *Minor — Call the Charge* (◈, 1/encounter): Raise Shield as a free action; until start of next turn, her Reactive Strikes (AoO) crit on a 19–20 against any creature attempting to move past her.
- *MAJOR — SHELYN'S MARK BURNS* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Tower shield bash; 10-ft cone; 4d10 force; Fort DC or Pushed 10 ft + Prone. Allies behind her gain Resistance 5 to all damage until start of her next turn. The scar glows during the move; Valerie does not acknowledge it.

**VOICE:** Controlled and clipped — unnecessary words waste time better spent on positioning. Professional courtesy is her warmest register. Not cold; economical. When something actually moves her, the economy breaks for exactly one sentence before snapping back.

**SAMPLE DIALOGUE:**
- *(greeting)* — "Valerie. Tower shield, blade. *(flat assessment)* You'll want me at the front. That's where I'll be."
- *(combat)* — "Stay behind me or move around. Those are the options."
- *(approval)* — "Well done. *(beat)* You knew where to stand. Not everyone does."
- *(disapproval)* — "You made that about appearance. It wasn't about appearance. Correct it."
- *(downtime, quiet)* — "The scar was my choice. Everything since has been my choice. *(quieter)* When everything is your choice, the choices mean something."
- *(signature)* — "I protect things. It was either that or be a thing that needed protecting. One was more interesting."

## 6. HARRIM
### Male Dwarf Cleric (Warpriest) of Groetus

**Appearance:** Heavyset dwarf with a grey-streaked beard, dark vestments marked with the skull-moon symbol of Groetus, and a heavy flail. Carries the unhurried bearing of a man who stopped dreading the worst outcome years ago. Dark eyes, level and calm. 40s.

**Backstory:** Harrim was raised in a dwarven community that drove him out — not for violence, but for honesty. He kept saying the wrong true things. He found Groetus's doctrine of universal entropy while wandering alone: everything ends, and that's not tragedy, that's just the shape of the world. He became a priest because Groetus is the only god he found who didn't ask him to pretend otherwise. He joined the expedition south because something in the Stolen Lands hasn't ended yet. He supposes he'll be there when it does. His personal quest involves uncovering the fate of an old dwarven shrine to Groetus and what destroyed its congregation — whether the answer is disturbing or comforting depends on which one Harrim is hoping for.

**Motivation:** Serve Groetus. Witness endings. Be there for the people around him in the meantime, which he does despite himself.

**Personality:** Dour, fatalistic, and genuinely kind in spite of himself. His nihilism is sincere but has never made him fail to help someone in front of him. He heals people. He's a little irritated that he keeps doing it.

**Approves:** Honesty, accepting what cannot be changed, acting anyway, silence that isn't performance
**Disapproves:** False hope, hollow promises, being told to cheer up, watching the same mistake twice

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Entropy's Clarity*: Once per encounter, when Harrim is reduced below half HP, he heals 2d8 and all allies within 20 ft gain Temp HP 5 for 1 rnd (Groetus reminds him he is not done yet). Triggers automatically; no action.
- *Minor — Eerie Flicker* (◈, 1/encounter): Next target Harrim Strikes this turn becomes Frightened 1 (Will DC negates); on crit fail, Frightened 2 + Fleeing 1 rnd.
- *MAJOR — THE END COMES FOR EVERYTHING* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; all enemies Will DC or take 4d8 void + Doomed 1 for 1 min. Allies in burst regain 2d8 HP — Groetus's reminder that the end has not yet arrived. Harrim does not enjoy the spell. He casts it anyway.

## 7. JAETHAL
### Female Undead Elf Cleric (Battle Harbinger) of Zon-Kuthon

**Appearance:** Tall elf woman with pale grey skin, cold violet eyes, and the deliberate stillness of something that does not need to breathe but does so anyway out of habit. Her clothing is dark and practical. She looks early 30s by appearance and will look exactly this way forever.

**Backstory:** Jaethal was murdered — poisoned by someone she trusted, who stole something from her in the process. She did not stay dead. Zon-Kuthon, Lord of Pain, granted her undeath and the power to pursue her killer across however many years it takes. She joined the expedition because the stolen thing left a trail and the trail leads south. She does not discuss whether this arrangement with Zon-Kuthon is worship or a transaction — possibly because she isn't sure. Her personal quest involves finding the companion who betrayed her, recovering what was taken, and deciding what she wants to do with the time she still has on the other side of her own death. She is undead: immune to disease, poison, sleep, paralysis, and healing magic that works on the living. Harm spells restore her.

**⛔ DM NOTE: Jaethal is undead. Do NOT describe her as breathing heavily, feeling warm to the touch, or recovering HP from Heal spells. She recovers from Harm spells and void damage. Positive energy hurts her.**

**Motivation:** Find her killer. Recover what was stolen. Determine if what follows counts as living.

**Personality:** Controlled, precise, and quietly furious underneath the composure. She is not dramatic about being undead. She treats it as a condition she is working around. She is perceptive about deception and impatient with it.

**Approves:** Truth-seeking, decisive action, following through, treating death as a fact rather than a metaphor
**Disapproves:** False sentiment, hypocrisy, mercy that excuses betrayal, anyone who flinches at what she is

**Signature Moves:**
- *Minor — Undead Resilience*: Jaethal is undead — immune to disease, poison, sleep, and paralysis. Heals from Harm spells (full HP per spell rank); takes equivalent damage from Heal spells. Cannot be raised or resurrected (already dead).
- *Minor — Kuthite Lash* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Touch Strike + Harm at 2 ranks above minimum; on hit, target Enfeebled 1 for 1 min (Fort DC negates). Pain delivered with the precision of someone who finds pain conceptually clarifying.
- *MAJOR — THE TRUTH AT THE EDGE OF PAIN* (◈◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft cone; 5d8 void; Will DC or Frightened 2 + Sickened 1 + reveal one truth they were concealing about themselves (DM picks; Jaethal phrases the question). Crit fail: Stunned 1 added.
