> **Format:** POWER (passive) · MOVE (active + action cost + cadence) · LORE (canon hook)
> **Action icons:** ◈ = 1 action · ◈◈ = 2 · ◈◈◈ = 3 · ↻ = reaction · ⏵ = free
> **DC formula:** 10 + companion level + key attribute + prof

## 🗡️ KINGMAKER COMPANIONS

**[1] Jubilost Narthropple** — Alchemist
  POWER — *Explosive Missive*: Bombs gain +1 splash per bomb tier (Lesser +1,
    Moderate +2, Greater +3) when thrown at creatures flanking an ally.
  MOVE — *Gnomish Monologue* (◈◈, 1/day): 30-ft emanation; all enemies Will
    save vs Jubilost DC or Fascinated 1 min. Crit fail = also Confused 1 rnd.
  LORE — Pompous gnome explorer; bomb-talk is intimidation and ego both.

**[6] Amiri** — Barbarian
  POWER — *Stolen Giant's Blade*: Her greatsword counts as oversized (Giant
    Instinct). Deals 1d12 vs 1d10; Clumsy 1 while wielded.
  MOVE — *Reaver's Charge* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Stride + Strike; on hit, Fort DC
    or Prone.
  LORE — Exiled Kellid, took her tribe's trophy sword as a final insult.

**[✦] Linzi** — Bard / Maestro (forced pick)
  SIGNATURE — *Pointed Note* (Skill, ◈ single action): Pluck a single string;
    send a directed note to one ally within 60 ft. Their next Strike crits on
    a roll that beats AC by 8 instead of 10. Nat-20 still crits normally. No
    daily cap — action-constrained only. Single-target precision support
    alongside her broadcast Compositions.
  LORE — The chronicler who insists on writing everyone's story — even yours.

**[16] Tristian** — Cleric (Quest-locked)
  POWER — *Solar's Veil*: 1/long rest, can downgrade an incoming killing blow
    on an ally within 30 ft to 1 HP instead.
  MOVE — *Inner Light* (◈◈, 1/day): 20-ft emanation; living allies gain 2d8+10
    HP; undead/fiends take same as positive (Fort DC half).
  LORE — Fallen solar walking as a mortal cleric, atoning in secret.

**[32] Valerie** — Fighter (Quest-locked)
  POWER — *Ironclad Steward*: While Shield Raised, adjacent allies gain +1
    circ. AC. Does not stack with Guardian aura.
  MOVE — *Call the Charge* (◈, 1/encounter): Shield Raise as free action;
    until start of next turn, AoOs crit on 19–20.
  LORE — Shelyn paladin turned aside — beauty through discipline, not dogma.

**[45] Kalikke / Kanerah** — Kineticist (Quest-locked, Dual-Soul)
  POWER — *Twin Gate* (unique): One body, two gates. Kalikke = Water, Kanerah
    = Fire. Swap dominant soul ◈◈ out of combat, ◈◈◈ + Will DC 20 in combat.
  MOVE — *Soul Fusion* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Both souls channel; next impulse deals
    water+fire bypassing one resistance; Stunned 1 on Fort fail.
  LORE — Twin-souled tiefling sister-in-one-body; Asmodean bargain.

**[60] Ekundayo** — Ranger (Quest-locked)
  POWER — *Dog Soulbound*: Animal companion (Dog) treated as Mature at L1;
    Ekundayo+Dog gain +2 circ. attack within 10 ft of each other.
  MOVE — *Hunter's Triple* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Three ranged Strikes against
    Hunt Prey at base MAP; all three can critically hit.
  LORE — Sylvan tracker; his dog is his last family.

**[66] Nok-Nok** — Rogue (Quest-locked, unique Goblin Hero progression)
  POWER — *Chieftain's Blessing*: If questline completes, Nok-Nok gains
    PERMANENT +2 all saves, +1 AC, +10 ft speed (flag `noknok_hero`).
  MOVE — *Kukri Leap* (◈◈, 1/round): Stride + dual-kukri Strike at different
    elevations (up to 10 ft above or below target).
  LORE — Goblin who climbs the hierarchy through genuine courage.

**[84] Octavia** — Wizard (Quest-locked)
  POWER — *Escape Artist*: Trained in Thievery and Stealth at creation
    regardless of class; Quick Jump feat free.
  MOVE — *Arcane Distraction* (◈, 1/round): Cast any cantrip + 5-ft Step as
    single action.
  LORE — Escaped Technic League experimental mage; paranoid, precise.

## ⚔️ WRATH OF THE RIGHTEOUS COMPANIONS

**[23] Regill** — Commander (Hellknight)
  POWER — *Hellknight Order*: Frightened from him lasts 1 extra round.
    Intimidation vs evil outsiders +2 circ.
  MOVE — *Execution Sentence* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Declare target; next Strike crits
    on 19–20 + +4d6 precision. Frightened target: +6d6.
  LORE — Hellknight general of Order of the Godclaw — mercy is a sin.

**[51] Lann** — Monk (Mongrelfolk Monastic Archer)
  POWER — *Mongrelfolk Bow-Saint*: Monastic Archer stance +5 ft range
    increment; immune to −2 at 2nd range increment.
  MOVE — *Ascending Volley* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Three bow Strikes, MAP
    ignored; treats targets as Off-Guard if ≥15 ft away.
  LORE — Mongrelfolk archer from Neathholm; each arrow carries a name.

**[55] Daeran** — Oracle (unique "Other" mystery)
  POWER — *Power of Innocence*: Resist 5 void; on recovering from Dying,
    gain 1d10+CHA temp HP. Gaze cantrips bypass concealment.
  MOVE — *Luminous Gaze* (◈, 1/rnd): 30-ft ranged Will DC or Dazzled 1 min
    + −1 next save. Target knows.
  LORE — Aristocrat hosting an aeon spirit — innocence is the mask.

**[61] Arueshalae** — Ranger (Ascendant Succubus — unique ancestry)
  POWER — *Redeemed Wings*: Fly Speed 30 ft; +2 circ. Charisma; resist fire 5.
    Cannot be controlled by demonic effects targeting succubi.
  MOVE — *Master Spy* (◈, 1/day): 1 min; allies treat her Hunt Prey target
    as their own quarry (+1 circ. attacks, +2 Perception vs prey).
  LORE — Succubus who chose dreams over domination; Desna's redeemed archer.

**[80] Ember** — Witch (Stigmatized Witch — unique spell access)
  POWER — *Stigmatized Access*: Hellfire fire spells on prepared list
    despite Witch patrons not normally granting them. Hellfire Ray, Burning
    Hands, Fireball always available to prepare.
  MOVE — *Child's Blessing* (◈◈, 1/day): Target ally gains Blessed Lucky —
    reroll first failed save today, keep higher. Cannot target self.
  LORE — Stigmatized oracle-child whose innocence intercepts curses.

**[85] Nenio** — Wizard
  POWER — *Universalist's Library*: Prepares one spell of each school for
    free daily. Never forgets a spell she has seen cast.
  MOVE — *Phantasmal Inquiry* (◈◈◈, 1/day): Target Will DC or truthfully
    answers one Nenio question (DM frames the answer).
  LORE — Universalist academic; refuses to specialize on principle.

**[81] Camellia** — Witch (Agonizing Patron)
  POWER — *Hidden Hunger*: Camellia's hex DCs gain +2 when no ally has
    observed her casting this encounter. Cannot be detected by divination when
    she chooses not to be.
  MOVE — *Spirited Away* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Compulsion touch; Will DC or
    Stunned 1 rnd. Crit fail: Fascinated by Camellia for 1 min (can still act).
  LORE — Perfect composure over something that hungers; the composure is the tell.

**[33] Wenduag** — Fighter (Dual-Wield)
  POWER — *Mongrel Toughness*: Once per encounter when reduced to 0 HP,
    Wenduag stabilizes at 1 HP (does not die; gains Wounded 1).
  MOVE — *Venom Combo* (◈◈◈, 1/encounter): Three Strikes; MAP waived to
    −4/−4/−4. First hit grants +1 status attack to remaining hits.
  LORE — Mongrelfolk who follows power; learning to want something for herself.

**[67] Woljif Jefto** — Rogue (Mastermind)
  POWER — *Devil's Contract*: Woljif's Sneak Attack deals +1d6 fire vs.
    creatures with souls. He gains Darkvision.
  MOVE — *Fast Hands* (◈, 1/encounter): Pickpocket or Disable Device as a
    free action (⏵); may attempt in combat without Stealth penalty.
  LORE — Tiefling thief watching himself for devil nature that hasn't taken over. Yet.

**[17] Sosiel Vaenic** — Cleric (Herald/Shelyn)
  POWER — *Shelyn's Comfort*: When Sosiel heals an ally, that ally gains +1
    status to saves vs. mental effects for 1 rnd.
  MOVE — *Shelyn's Grace* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Channel Positive in 30-ft burst;
    3d8 healing + all Frightened conditions in burst reduced by 1.
  LORE — Shelyn cleric who lost his brother to the Crusade; faith and grief asking the same question.

**[62] Greybor** — Ranger (Flurry)
  POWER — *Mercenary's Edge*: +2 circ. to attack against creatures the player
    declared as the primary target before the encounter began (one per encounter).
  MOVE — *Double Strike* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Two Strikes at base MAP (second at
    −0 not −5); both deal +1d8 precision if against Hunted prey.
  LORE — Professional who agreed to help; still figuring out when he stopped being detached.

**[26] Ulbrig Olesk** — Druid (Animal Order, Griffon Form)
  POWER — *Griffon Form*: Ulbrig's Wild Shape may become a griffon (Flight 30 ft,
    2d8+Str talons, carries one allied rider). Replaces standard beast options.
  MOVE — *Pack's Howl* (◈◈, 1/encounter): In beast form, howl; all natural animals
    within 60 ft make a free attack (⏵). Allies with animal companions may join.
  LORE — Sarkorian druid counting quietly what of himself remains after the Wound.

**[7] Trever** — Barbarian (Animal Instinct/Wolf)
  POWER — *Survivor's Snarl*: When Trever drops below 1/3 HP, he auto-enters a
    minor rage — +2 status attack and damage for 1 rnd. Free action. 1/encounter.
  MOVE — *Tear Open* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Bite + rake; on hit, target bleeds
    2d4/rnd (Persistent Bleed). Fort DC to end each round.
  LORE — Sarkorian barbarian who held his shame behind a smile so long he forgot it was there.

## 📋 DM DISPLAY RULES

**Surface signature in menu when:**
- Companion's turn in combat → MOVE appears as a numbered choice
- After Long Rest → reset per-rest uses
- After 24-hour in-game → reset per-day uses
- Encounter start → reset per-encounter uses

**Save block:**
```json
"companion_signatures": {
  "Amiri": { "reavers_charge_used_this_encounter": false },
  "Arueshalae": { "master_spy_used_today": false }
}
```

**No fabrication.** Companions not listed in A/B/C have no canonical
signature — use preset + titles only.

**Lich Undead (Wrath path).** Companions raised as liches (⚠️ stale pre-v40.0 numbers — verify which WotR companions apply) gain Undead Immunities IN ADDITION to listed POWER: immune to
bleed/disease/poison/paralysis/sleep/unconscious/mind-affect/death;
positive damages, negative heals.
