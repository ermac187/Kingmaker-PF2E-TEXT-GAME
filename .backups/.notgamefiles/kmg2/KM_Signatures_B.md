## 🗡️ PATHFINDER ICONICS

**[10] Lem** — Bard (Maestro)
  POWER — *Halfling's Cheer*: Inspire Courage extends to allies within 60 ft
    (not 30 ft); allies using his composition gain +1 circ. to saves vs. fear.
  MOVE — *Courageous Surge* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Composition Cantrip free +
    immediate Bon Mot on a target; on success, ally adjacent to target gains
    Inspire Courage bonus doubled for 1 rnd.
  LORE — Halfling bard from Absalom; plays for love of people, not audiences.

**[13] Seelah** — Champion
  POWER — *Iomedaean Crusader*: Lay on Hands heals +1 die vs undead/demons;
    target gains +2 circ. AC for 1 rnd.
  MOVE — *Sword of Dawn* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Longsword Strike; on hit, +2d6
    spirit and target Dazzled 1 rnd if evil-aligned.
  LORE — Iconic paladin of Iomedae; duty before self, always.

**[20] Harrim** — Cleric (Warpriest/Groetus)
  POWER — *Entropy's Clarity*: Once per encounter, when Harrim is reduced
    below half HP, he heals 2d8 HP and all allies within 20 ft gain Temp HP 5
    for 1 rnd (Groetus reminds him he is not done yet).
  MOVE — *Eerie Flicker* (◈, 1/encounter): Next target Harrim strikes this
    round becomes Frightened 1 (Will DC negates); on crit fail: Frightened 2.
  LORE — Dwarven priest of Groetus; nothing lasts, including the fight you are in.

## ✝️ CLERICS (#18, 21)

**[18] Jaethal** — Cleric (Battle Harbinger/Zon-Kuthon)
  POWER — *Undead Resilience*: Jaethal is undead — immune to disease,
    poison, sleep, and paralysis. Heals from Harm spells; hurt by Heal.
  MOVE — *Kuthite Lash* (◈◈, 1/encounter): Touch Strike + Harm at 2 ranks
    above minimum; on hit, target Enfeebled 1 for 1 min (Fort DC negates).
  LORE — Undead elf inquisitor of Zon-Kuthon; seeks truth at the edge of pain.

**[21] Goldmoon** — Cleric Warpriest (Mishakal Grave-Warden, Heal Font)
  SIGNATURE — *Mishakal's Light Lays Low* (Passive): Cast Down (L3 feat) fires
    against ANY enemy target, not just undead/fiends. Heal vs living enemy =
    no damage but Reflex save vs Prone. Auto-prone applies regardless of damage
    compatibility. Stacks with Improved Cast Down (L8, Prone on success too),
    Fast Channel (L7, free-action Heal/rnd), Echoing Channel (L10, hits
    adjacents free). Battlefield-control healer.
  LORE — Plains princess, first mortal cleric of returned gods of Krynn.
