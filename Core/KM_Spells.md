# KINGMAKER — SPELL LISTS BY BUILD
## KM_Spells.md | Referenced by: KM_Builds.md, KM_BuildGuide.md, KM_Spells.md, KM_Spells.md

> **DM:** Use this file when a spellcasting build levels up or asks what spells they have.
> Each build lists: cantrips (always available), spell slots per level, and the full
> recommended prepared spell list at each level tier.
>
> **Prepared casters** (Cleric, Druid, Witch) choose spells daily from their list.
> **Spontaneous casters** (Oracle, Psychic, Summoner) know their listed spells permanently.
> **Magus** prepares from arcane list; slots are small but Spellstrike multiplies impact.
>
> **Spell DC = 10 + proficiency + key ability mod.**
> **Spell attack = proficiency + key ability mod.**

## BUILD 3 — DIVINE GUARDIAN (Paladin Champion)
**Tradition:** Divine (Focus Spells only) | **Key Ability:** Charisma (+3) | **DC L1:** 15

### Cantrips (always available via devotion)
Guidance, Divine Lance, Shield, Stabilize

### Focus Spells (Refocus = 10 min prayer/meditation)

Champions do NOT prepare full spell lists. They gain focus spells and devotion spells through class feats. All casting uses Focus Points.

| Char Lvl | Focus Spells Available | Focus Points |
|----------|----------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Lay on Hands (1d6+4 heal, touch, 1A) | 1 |
| 3 | Lay on Hands scales to 2d6+4 | 1 |
| 5 | Lay on Hands 3d6+4; deals equal vitality damage vs undead | 1 |
| 7 | Lay on Hands 4d6+4 | 2 (via feat) |
| 9 | Litany of Righteousness (1A, 30 ft, enemy weakness 7 to all damage for 1 rnd) | 2 |
| 11 | Lay on Hands 6d6+4 | 2 |
| 13 | Litany against Wrath (enemy takes penalty to attacks) | 3 (via feat) |
| 15+ | Lay on Hands scales +1d6/2 lvls; Litanies scale with proficiency | 3 |

**Priority:** Lay on Hands (ally HP recovery) > Champion Reaction (Shield of the Devoted / Retributive Strike) > Litany feats (L9+).
**Champion Devotion (feat-granted):** Some Champion feats grant prepared divine spells (e.g., Deity's Domain). These are rare — default to focus spells above.
