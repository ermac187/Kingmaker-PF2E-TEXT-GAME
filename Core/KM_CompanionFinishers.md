# COMPANION FINISHING MOVE RESULTS
## DM: calculate tier, read the result for that companion's weapon, write the surrounding scene. The RESULT is locked — do not soften it.

Tiers: Clean (1–49%) / Brutal (50–99%) / Savage (100–199%) / Annihilation (200%+)

---

## JAETHAL — Carrion Scythe (scythe, slashing / reaping)
*Style: precise, economical, death treated as a matter of fact; she reaps*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | The curved blade catches the throat on the pull and opens it in one clean reap. Target folds. |
| Brutal | The point hooks under the jaw and the draw tears it open to the ear; the jaw detaches on the far side. Skull visible through the wound. |
| Savage | The reap takes the head — the curve catches the neck and the pull-through separates it at the second cervical vertebra. The head goes with the swing. Body stands one beat before dropping. |
| Annihilation | The scythe catches at the hip and the full reap opens the body diagonally to the opposite shoulder — she harvests him like a stalk of wheat. Two unequal pieces, joined at the spine until they aren't. The near half stays upright a moment against whatever is behind it. |

## REVY — Twin Flintlock Pistols (piercing, ranged)

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|

## YOR — Thorn Stilettos (piercing, paired, finesse)
*Style: professional, silent, from behind when possible — the target does not know it is over until it is over*

**Backstab / Sneak Attack approach (Off-Guard or flanked):**

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Blade enters the side of the neck from behind, drawn forward through both carotids in one motion. The throat opens. Target's hands come up too late and find nothing. |
| Brutal | Blade in at the base of the skull, second blade drawn across the throat simultaneously. Spine severed at C1. Head drops forward, attached by the front of the neck only. |
| Savage | Cross-draw from behind — both stilettos enter at either side of the throat, drawn toward center. They meet at the trachea. The front of the neck opens. Head unsupported, tilts back. The spine is the only thing holding it on and the spine is severed. |
| Annihilation | From behind: first blade enters the skull base and is left there. Second blade opens the throat full width, continues into the clavicle on the left side, severs the subclavian artery. Yor steps back. The target takes three steps forward on momentum before registering that none of the systems required to continue living are still intact. Falls face-first. |

**Frontal approach:**

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Stiletto through the eye socket at the angle that finds the brain. Fast. Silent. The target folds. |
| Brutal | Paired entry: one blade through the throat, one through the eye socket. Both hands clear of weapons before the body decides to fall. |
| Savage | Seventeen entry wounds up the centerline — sternum, throat, jaw, eye. The face is a different shape than it was. |
| Annihilation | Both stilettos enter and are withdrawn faster than the eye tracks. Thirty-one entry wounds. The pattern covers chest, throat, and skull. The target is not recognizable as the person who entered the room. The wounds are precise enough that a physician could name each organ struck. |

## BELLATRIX — Wand hexes (magic: void/mental/necrotic/force)
*Style: theatrical, gleeful, loud about it — she does not kill quietly and she does not want to*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | The curse stops the heart. Target's face locks on the last expression they had — usually surprise. They sit down and stay sitting. |
| Brutal | Void burst detonates the chest cavity outward. Ribs exit through the back. The sternum is elsewhere. The scream is cut off because the lungs are no longer intact enough to produce one. |

## USAGE
1. Calculate overkill tier from the COMBAT CHECK math.
2. Find the companion's section.
3. Read the Result line for that tier aloud to yourself.
4. Write the scene — the companion's movement, the moment, the aftermath. Your prose.
5. The Result is in there, verbatim or in substance. Do not replace it with softer language.

Softening the Result = `.fail 9`.
Skipping the finishing move = `.fail 9`.
Using a lower tier's Result than the math earned = `.fail 9`.
