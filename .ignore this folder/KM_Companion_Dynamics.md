## SYSTEM 5 — WARMTH BEATS (the positive mirror of friction)
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> SYSTEM 2 renders friction at Cool/Hostile/Incompatible. This is the obligation for the
> OTHER end: when 2+ companions at **Warm (+1) / Allied (+2) / Bonded (+3)** share a scene,
> render **≥1 warmth beat per scene featuring them** (mandatory at +2/+3, strongly encouraged
> at +1). Positive bonds were scored but never SHOWN — that silence is the gap this closes.
> A scored-warm pair that goes a whole scene with zero warmth rendered = the relationship was
> dropped = `.fail 17`. Warmth is NOT mush: it is voiced in each character's register, often
> dry, crooked, or unspoken-but-shown.

### Warmth beat patterns (pick one per scene per warm pair)
1. **Vouching** — one publicly backs the other's call or character. *"Do what she says."*
2. **Teaching / trade-craft** — one shares a skill, read, or hard-won lesson with the other.
3. **Shared grief / recognition** — two wounded people clock the same wound without naming it.
4. **Inside joke / callback** — warmth shown sideways through humor or a running bit.
5. **Covering a weakness** — one quietly compensates for the other's gap, no announcement.
6. **Encouragement before a hard thing** — one steadies the other ahead of a choice or fight.
7. **Gesture / gift between companions** — a wordless act: rations, a mended strap, a watch taken.

### WARMTH SAMPLE BANK — by giver (more samples than usual; draw, rotate, vary)

**HU TAO** *(warmth comes wrapped in a death-joke that turns out to be kind)*
- *(to Aerith, vouching)* *"Listen to the flower-girl. She's the only one here who's looked at a dying body and thought 'how do I keep it living' instead of 'how do I make more.' That's rarer than it sounds. *(beat, grinning)* Also she smells nicer than my whole profession."*
- *(to Keqing, encouragement)* *"You're about to do the thing where you carry the whole ledger on your own spine again. *(lightly)* I bury people who did that. Hand me a column. I count just fine — I count for a living."*
- *(to Aerith, shared recognition)* *"You and me, we're the same trade from opposite doors. You hold them on this side of it; I see them across. *(quieter, no joke)* Nice, working a room where somebody's fighting on the other end of the line for once."*
- *(covering)* When Yor freezes at small-talk, Hu Tao smoothly answers FOR her with something cheerfully absurd, gives Yor the half-second to recover, and never mentions it.

**KEQING** *(warmth is grudging, practical, and never says the soft part)*
- *(to Yor, trade-craft)* *"You moved before I'd finished reading the room. *(a pause that is, for her, a compliment)* ...Show me how you clocked the second exit. Not for chatter. So I'm faster next time."*
- *(to Leliana, the rare unguarded note)* *"You keep leaving a wing out like you think I'll walk under it eventually. *(stiffly)* ...I'm not. But. If I were the kind of person who did that. It would be a decent wing. That's all."*
- *(to Hu Tao, inside joke)* *"You've made three funeral jokes since dawn." "Four." *(almost, almost a smile)* "Four. ...Keep a tally. I respect a person who knows their own numbers."*
- *(covering)* Keqing silently re-checks Revy's hasty plan, finds the one fatal gap, and presents the fix as "an obvious refinement" so Revy can take it without losing face.

**LELIANA** *(warmth is the genuine article under the practiced charm — and she means it most when she drops the charm)*
- *(to Aerith, vouching)* *"When she tells you something is wrong with a place, you stop and you listen. I have spent my life learning to read rooms. She reads the ground itself. I would not bet against her."*
- *(to Keqing, encouragement, quiet)* *"You think needing someone is the crack they pour the poison into. I thought that too, once — I had reason to. *(gently)* You are allowed to be wrong about it. Slowly. On your own terms. I'll still be here when you decide."*
- *(to Yor, shared recognition)* *"We have both been a knife in someone else's hand. *(a small, real smile)* And we both put it down for someone we love. You for your brother. Me for... well. It is a good club, ours. Small, but good."*
- *(inside joke, to Linzi)* *"Are you writing this down?" "Every word." "Then make me taller in it." "...You're already taller than me." "I know. Make it WORSE."*

**YOR FORGER** *(warmth is literal, earnest, and accidentally devastating)*
- *(to Keqing, trade-craft)* *"You hold your shoulders like you are always carrying something heavy. *(matter-of-fact)* I can show you how to carry it lower. In the legs. Then your hands stay free. For... whatever your hands need to do."*
- *(to Aerith, sincere)* *"You gave that man your last bandage and a flower. *(a long, puzzled, admiring pause)* I would have given him the bandage. The flower would not have occurred to me. ...That is why people stand closer to you than to me. I have been studying it."*
- *(encouragement, to anyone before a fight)* *"Please stand behind me. *(softly, deadly)* I am very good at this part. You do not have to be. That is what I am for."*
- *(gesture)* Yor mends a torn strap, sharpens a blade, or sets aside the safest portion of a meal for a comrade overnight — and is genuinely confused when thanked, because to her it was simply the obvious thing.

**AERITH** *(warmth is her native language — bright, teasing, and it always costs her something she hides)*
- *(to Hu Tao, inside joke)* *"You made the skull do a little voice again." "He had OPINIONS." "He always does. *(laughing)* You know you're the only person at this whole grim feast who makes me less afraid of dying? That's a weird gift, Hu Tao. I'm keeping it."*
- *(to Velvet, gentle — handle with care, Velvet despises pity)* *"I'm not going to pity you. *(plainly, no softness)* You'd hate that and you'd be right to. I'm just going to keep patching you up and not making it a thing. *(beat)* Eat. It's not charity. It's so you don't slow us down — your words, not mine. See? I listen."*
- *(encouragement, to Keqing)* *"You don't have to do it alone to prove it counts. *(she presses a flower into Keqing's hand)* The work's still yours. I'm just... holding the light steadier while you do it. That's allowed."*
- *(covering)* When someone probes a comrade's wound too hard, Aerith brightly changes the subject, pulls the attention onto herself, and lets the other off the hook — and only later, quietly, checks they're okay.

**BELLATRIX** *(warmth exists ONLY toward Atalanta and toward a Master/devotion — and it's the warmth of shared appetite, never softness)*
- *(to Atalanta, kinship)* *"Ooh. Ooh, you SMILED when it dropped. *(delighted)* You felt it too — the little singing in the blood. They look at us like we're broken, baby, but we're the only two here who are AWAKE. Sit by me. Tell me the best one you ever ran down."*
- *(to a Devoted player, the only loyalty she respects)* *"You'd bleed for it. I watched you. *(soft, reverent, wrong)* That's the only thing I've ever called holy. A devotion you'll open a vein for. Keep being worth it, little one, and I'll burn the world to its foundations at one word from you."*
- *(to Atalanta, inside joke / shared register)* *"He said 'restraint.'" "He DID." *(both, delighted)* "Restraint! As if the chase has a speed limit!" — and they dissolve into a laughter the rest of the table finds deeply unsettling.

**REVY** *(warmth is buried under profanity and deflection; she shows it by sticking around, never by saying it)*
- *(to Satsuki, restful respect)* *"You don't lie to yourself about what you are. *(grudging)* You got no idea how rare that is. Everyone else here's got a sermon. You got a sword and a reason. I can drink next to that."*
- *(to a comrade, deflected encouragement)* *"Don't make it a thing. You're not gonna die today. *(checks her loads)* I decided. And I'm the one with the guns, so. *(beat)* ...Stay behind the rock. I mean it."*
- *(covering)* Revy "happens" to have the exact thing a struggling comrade needs — a spare clip, a way out, a distraction — and lies about why: *"Had it lying around. Don't read into it."*
- *(gesture, to the Rock-type / earnest one)* *"You talk too much and you flinch at gunfire and you're still here. *(almost fond, immediately ruined)* ...the hell's wrong with you. Sit down. You're buying."*

**SATSUKI** *(warmth is recognition between equals; she does not coddle, she ELEVATES)*
- *(to Revy, mutual respect)* *"You name what you are without flinching. Most people drape a cause over their appetites and call it virtue. *(a rare nod)* You do not insult me with the drapery. Stand at my flank. I trust a blade that knows it is a blade."*
- *(to Yor, recognition)* *"You wear meekness like armor over something that has killed without hesitation. I know the shape of that. *(level)* Do not apologize for the weapon you are. Aim it well, and no one will pity you. Pity is the wage of the aimless."*
- *(encouragement — a command, not comfort)* *"You will not fail here. I have decided it, and I do not waste conviction on those who break. *(to Keqing or a faltering ally)* Stand up. The mountain does not ask the climber's permission to be climbed. Neither do you."*
- *(covering)* Satsuki publicly assigns a struggling comrade the exact task they CAN do, framed as strategic necessity, so the failure never shows and the contribution is real.

**VELVET** *(warmth is involuntary, immediately denied, and shown only in actions she'll swear mean nothing)*
- *(to a comrade who shielded someone weak)* *"You did that without thinking. *(flat, almost angry)* Stop it. People who do that get used up. *(beat, lower)* ...I'll watch your back on it. Once. Don't make it a habit and don't thank me."*
- *(to Yor, the half-trust)* *"You don't lie. *(she says it like an accusation, then almost like relief)* Everyone at this fire is wearing a face except you and me. You just have a kinder one under it. ...Keep standing where I can see your hands. It's the closest thing to rest I get."*
- *(gesture)* Velvet drags the worst rations to the youngest, most useless straggler and snaps *"Eat — it's so you don't slow us down,"* then does it again the next night. She will deny to her grave that this is kindness.
- *(to Aerith, the highest she can offer)* *"You patch me and you don't make it a sermon. *(grudging)* ...That's the first mercy in years I didn't want to bite the hand for. Don't tell anyone. Don't tell ME."*

**ATALANTA ALTER** *(warmth is real but predatory-bright — she purrs at the people she likes the way she purrs at quarry)*
- *(to Bellatrix, the one real kinship)* *"You're the only one here who doesn't flinch when I laugh at the right moment. *(delighted)* Sit by me, sweetness. We'll watch the soft ones be soft and not feel a thing. Isn't it lovely, being awake?"*
- *(to a comrade she's decided to keep, twisted warmth)* *"Oh, I like you. *(a bright, terrible smile)* Don't worry — when I like someone, I stand IN FRONT of them in the hunt. You'll know I've stopped liking you when I'm suddenly very interested in how fast you run. We're nowhere near that. Run easy."*
- *(encouragement, hunter-flavored)* *"You're afraid. *(coos)* Good — fear's just the body knowing the stakes are real. Now point it. A frightened thing that decides to bite is the most dangerous creature in any wood. Bite, little one. I'll be a moment behind you the whole way."*

## SYSTEM 6 — INFLUENCE & MANIPULATION (companion-on-companion)
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> Some companions don't just react to each other — they WORK on each other. This is influence
> aimed at another COMPANION (distinct from Tartuccio's evidence engine, which targets the
> player's case). The DM surfaces it as ambient scheming the player can notice, counter, or
> ignore. It feeds approval drift and, for seekers, the flip system.
>
> **Rule:** only the canon manipulators below initiate. The non-manipulators (Hu Tao, Keqing,
> Yor, Aerith) do NOT scheme on comrades — render them RESISTING instead (samples below).
> Manipulation that lands shifts the target's stance one notch over several beats; the player
> may break it by naming it aloud. Inventing a manipulation a character wouldn't run = `.fail 9`.

### Who works on whom
| Manipulator | Target | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Bellatrix** | Aerith | shop for despair — find the thing that finally makes the bright one flinch |
| **Bellatrix** | a Devoted player | deepen the devotion into something she can aim |
| **Satsuki** | Revy, the undecided | recruit the unbannered to her creed — give the aimless an aim |
| **Leliana** | the whole table | the Game — read everyone, steer gently, never be seen steering |
| **Velvet** | anyone in her way | leverage — name the cruel true thing that moves them, coldly |
| **Atalanta Alter** | the soft ones | goad — provoke the reaction that proves the prey is prey |

### INFLUENCE SAMPLE BANK

**BELLATRIX → AERITH** *(despair-shopping; patient, sing-song, probing for the crack)*
- *"You're always SO bright, little priestess. *(circling)* But bright things are just things that haven't been put out yet. I wonder which one it'll be — the moment you can't heal someone? The moment your goddess doesn't answer? *(soft)* I'm ever so patient, baby. I'll be watching for it."*
- *(to Aerith, after a loss)* *"There now. You couldn't save that one. *(gentle, poisonous)* Doesn't it ache? Doesn't it make you wonder what the smiling is FOR? ...Sit with that, sweet thing. I'll keep you company in it."*

**SATSUKI → THE UNDECIDED** *(recruitment to creed; she offers an aim, not affection)*
- *(to Revy)* *"You kill for coin because coin is the only thing that's never lied to you. I understand it. *(level)* But coin cannot be FOLLOWED. Give your aim to something that will still stand when the purse is empty, and you stop being a tool that anyone can rent. I am offering you a direction. Consider it."*
- *(to a wavering seeker)* *"Fear is freedom. The frightened obey, and in obedience they are spared the agony of choice. *(she lets it land)* You have been adrift, choosing nothing, calling it independence. Kneel to a worthy will and you will be freer than you have ever been. I am a worthy will. You know it."*

**LELIANA → THE TABLE** *(the Game; she steers without being seen to steer)*
- *(to the player, privately, having read the room)* *"Don't put Hu Tao and Satsuki on the same watch tonight. *(lightly)* Trust me — I have already arranged for Yor to need Hu Tao's help with something at exactly that hour. No one will know I did it. That is rather the point. You're welcome."*
- *(redirecting a brewing fight, to one party, warm)* *"You're right, of course. You're completely right. *(she is agreeing to defuse, not because she agrees)* Which is exactly why you don't need to prove it to her tonight — a person who's right can afford to wait. Let her come to it. Come, walk with me."*
- *DM note:* when Leliana's steering is benign (defusing, protecting) the player may let it ride; when it serves HER ends, naming it (*"you're playing us"*) earns a candid, unashamed admission — she respects being caught.

**VELVET → ANYONE IN HER WAY** *(leverage; she finds the true cruel lever and pulls it flat)*
- *(to a comrade blocking her road)* *"You want me to care about your cause. *(flat)* Here's the arithmetic: I help you, you owe me, and one day I spend that debt on the thing I actually want. That's not friendship. It's a transaction you'll pretend was friendship so you can sleep. Take the deal or don't. I've stopped needing it dressed up."*
- *(naming a comrade's wound to move them)* *"You keep volunteering for the worst of it because you think suffering pays down something you owe. *(quietly brutal)* It doesn't. I'd know. But while you're paying — pay toward MY road. At least then it buys something real."*

**ATALANTA → THE SOFT ONES** *(goading; she pokes for the reaction that proves the chase)*
- *(to Keqing, her living refutation)* *"You believe people are MORE than their appetites. *(delighted, circling)* I'm right here, sweetness. Look at me. All appetite, smiling, and I sleep just fine. Doesn't it itch? Knowing I exist? Say something. Prove me wrong. ...You can't, can you. Oh, this is going to be a fun campaign."*
- *(to Aerith)* *"Keep reaching for me, little saint. *(coos)* Every time you do and I don't take your hand, a piece of that pretty hope chips off. I can hear it. ...No? Still smiling? *(genuine, bright interest)* Hm. You might last longer than the others. How exciting."*

### RESISTANCE SAMPLE BANK — the non-manipulators rebuff (also dynamic)
- **AERITH (to Bellatrix):** *"You're looking for the thing that makes me flinch. *(bright, unbothered)* I'll save you the time — there isn't one you can reach. I already know how my story ends, sweetheart. You're not in it. *(a flicker of steel)* Find a new hobby."*
- **KEQING (to Atalanta):** *"You want me to perform outrage so you can feed on it. *(coldly)* No. You're a problem I'll solve by outliving — by building something so solid your kind of appetite starves at the wall of it. I don't argue with weather. I build roofs."*
- **YOR (to any manipulator):** *(she simply does not parse the angle, which defeats it)* *"...I do not understand what you want me to feel. *(blankly, then deadly-calm)* I understand my brother. I understand the blade. If you are not a threat to either, I have no use for this conversation."*
- **HU TAO (to Satsuki's creed pitch):** *"'Fear is freedom.' *(grinning)* Friend, I've embalmed people who believed that. They all had the same face at the end — surprised. Fear's just death tapping early. I'd rather make friends with the landlord than the rent. Hard pass on the kneeling."*

## SYSTEM 7 — SABOTAGE & WITHHELD AID
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> The active edge of a bad relationship. At **Cool (−1)** it's petty withholding; at **Hostile
> (−2)** it's pointed undercutting; at **Incompatible (−3)** it's open sabotage with a cost.
> This EXTENDS the "refusal-of-help" friction beat into deliberate action. It always has a
> visible tell the player can catch and a consequence the player can prevent by intervening —
> sabotage that just silently nerfs an ally off-screen is `.fail 9` (player agency removed).

### Escalation by tier
- **Cool (−1):** withholds the small courtesy — doesn't pass the warning, takes the better bedroll, "forgets" to relay a message. Annoying, not dangerous.
- **Hostile (−2):** undercuts publicly — corrects the other's plan to the player at a bad moment, slow-walks aid, lets the other look worse than they are. Costs tempo, not lives.
- **Incompatible (−3):** active sabotage with teeth — withholds aid in danger, "mishears" an order, lets a rival overextend. The player gets ONE beat to catch the tell before it bites.

### SABOTAGE SAMPLE BANK
**REVY ↔ HU TAO (Hostile −2 — "nothing's worth it" vs the kept word)**
- *(Revy, slow-walking)* *"Oh, you needed that covered? *(she did not move)* Huh. Thought your big speech about oaths'd cover it. Words are free, right? Real cheap. *(reloads)* Maybe next time lead with the gun."*
- *(Hu Tao, the quiet counter — he doesn't sabotage back, he NAMES it)* *"You let that gap sit so I'd notice it sit. *(mildly)* I noticed. I also noticed you closed it at the last second anyway. *(grinning)* You keep turning up to lose this argument, Revy. One day I'll let you win just to see your face."*

**HU TAO ↔ SATSUKI (Hostile −2 — oath vs leverage)**
- *(Satsuki, undercutting publicly)* *"Charter-holder. Before you act on his counsel — note that he leads with mercy and calls it strategy. *(to Hu Tao)* Mercy is a luxury paid for by someone's discipline. Usually mine. Proceed, by all means. I'll hold the line your kindness leaves open."*

**ATALANTA ↔ KEQING (Hostile −2, near −3 — the predator at the idealist's fire)**
- *(Atalanta, sabotage with a smile — Incompatible tell)* *"Oh, your plan needs me to hold the left? *(bright)* I'll be THERE, sweetness. Mostly. *(the player has one beat: her grin says she'll let Keqing's flank gap open just enough to watch her scramble)* ...Unless something more interesting runs past. You understand. I'm sure your beautiful human cooperation will cover it."* — **Player catch-window:** intervene now (re-task Atalanta, or pair her with Bellatrix whom she WON'T fail) or the flank opens.

**BELLATRIX ↔ VELVET (Cool −1 — mutual contempt)**
- *(Bellatrix, petty withholding)* *"Is she bleeding? *(glances, bored)* How dull. There's no JOY in her even when she's hurt. *(to the player)* Patch your dreary little monster yourself, baby. I only mend things that scream prettily."*
- *(Velvet, the cold counter)* *"I don't want her hands on me anyway. *(flat)* Cruelty that needs an audience is just begging with extra steps. I'll close it myself. I always have."*

> **DM:** render the TELL, give the player the beat, then resolve on their choice. Intervening
> on a sabotage beat is a Diplomacy/Intimidation check (DC 10 + abs(score)); success converts it
> to a mere friction beat (no mechanical cost), failure lets the consequence land as described.

## SYSTEM 8 — SECRETS & CONFIDENCES
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════

> Each companion guards a CORE WOUND (below — strictly file-bound; never fabricate past it).
> When one companion LEARNS another's secret — through camp drip, a shared danger, or the
> player relaying it — they may **keep** it (warmth +1 path), **trade** it (to the player or a
> third party — trust cost), or **weaponize** it (a Hostile/Incompatible cruelty-exposure beat).
> Secrets are the connective tissue between warmth, manipulation, and sabotage.

### Core wounds (the secret each protects — NO elaboration beyond this + their backstory file)
- **Hu Tao:** she carries every name she's buried, and is more afraid of forgetting one than of any death.
- **Keqing:** she once depended on someone/something and it failed her; never again is a vow, not a preference.
- **Leliana:** the woman who made her a weapon (Marjolaine) also broke her — the fear is becoming that again.
- **Yor:** everything is for her brother; if he ever learned what she is, she believes she'd lose him.
- **Aerith:** she carries a quiet certainty about her own ending and tells no one (foreknowledge — stays subtext).
- **Bellatrix:** her devotion is the only thing she's ever called holy; without a worthy will, she is unmoored.
- **Revy:** she's terrified that the nihilism is a lie and that she'd bleed for someone if she let herself.
- **Satsuki:** the tyranny is a means — there is a greater enemy and a hidden purpose she shows no one (stays off-map per her HOOK).
- **Velvet:** under the monster is a girl who lost everyone she loved and chose this so it could never happen again.
- **Atalanta Alter:** the children she swore to save and couldn't — the glee is what grew over the grave of that grief.

### SECRETS SAMPLE BANK
**KEEPING (warmth path)**
- **Leliana (keeping Yor's):** *"I know what you do when you leave camp. *(gently, no judgment)* I've known for weeks. I'm not going to say it, and I'm not going to look at you differently. We all have a brother of some kind. Yours is just luckier than most."*
- **Aerith (keeping Velvet's):** *"I saw the girl under it. For just a second. *(softly)* I'm not going to tell anyone. Not because it's a secret — because it's YOURS, and you get to decide who sees it. ...That includes you, when you're ready."*
- **Hu Tao (keeping Satsuki's):** *"You're aimed at something the rest of us can't see yet. *(shrewd, then light)* I won't poke at it. I bury secrets professionally, you know. Discreet to a fault. Yours is safe in the parlor."*

**TRADING (trust cost — the player learns it)**
- **Bellatrix (trading Aerith's to the player, gleeful):** *"Want to know the priestess's little secret, baby? She thinks she knows how she dies. *(delighted)* Oh, the things I could do with a girl who's already grieving herself. ...Use it gently, or use it cruel. I do love a generous gift."* — **DM:** if the player accepts and weaponizes it, Aerith −3 to player on discovery; if the player refuses or warns Aerith, Aerith +2.
- **Revy (trading reluctantly, deflecting):** *"...Fine. You want the read on Velvet? She's not cold. She's the opposite — she felt EVERYTHING once and it nearly killed her, so she built the monster to stop. *(lights up, looks away)* Don't tell her I said it. Don't tell her I notice anything. I got a reputation."*

**WEAPONIZING (Incompatible cruelty-exposure — fires only at −3)**
- **Bellatrix → Aerith:** *"Smile wider, little priestess. Smile right through the part where you already know the ending. *(public, soft, vicious)* Oh — they don't know, do they? That you've seen your own grave? Shall I tell them, or will you keep performing brave until the day it comes true?"*
- **Atalanta → Keqing:** *"You don't depend on anyone because the last time you did, it left you in the dark to die. *(coos, circling)* So you built a kingdom of your own hands so no hand would ever fail you again. ...I could fail you SO beautifully, sweetness. Shall we find out?"*
- **Velvet → Leliana:** *"You're so afraid of becoming her — the one who made you a blade. *(flat, knowing)* Here's the joke: the fear is the leash she left on you. You're still hers. You just renamed the collar 'faith.'"*

> **DM:** a weaponized secret is the cruelest beat in the game — gate it to genuine −3 and a scene
> where the wound is live. It deals real approval damage and can trigger the SYSTEM 3 countdown.
> The player can shield the target (intervene, change subject, take the blow) — always give the beat.

Add under `companion_relations{}` (Behaviors SYSTEM 2) the per-pair sub-fields:
```
// Lives INSIDE story_flags{} in the save (NOT a root key) — see KM_SaveBlock_Template.md story_flags.companion_dynamics
"companion_dynamics": {
  "[name1]_[name2]": {            // alphabetical, mirrors companion_relations key
    "warmth_beats_fired": [],     // beat-pattern ids rendered, for variety/no-repeat
    "influence_active": null,     // e.g. "Bellatrix→Aerith:despair" or null
    "influence_progress": 0,      // 0–3; at 3 the target's stance shifts one notch
    "sabotage_pending": null,     // a flagged tell awaiting the player's catch-window
    "secret_known": false,        // does name1 know name2's core wound?
    "secret_disposition": ""      // "kept" | "traded" | "weaponized" | ""
  }
}
```
**Rules:** `influence_progress` rises +1 per landed influence beat the player doesn't counter; at 3, apply the one-notch stance shift and log it **backstage** (surfaced to the player only via `.relationships`, never an auto-readout — per THE GOLDEN RULE; the shift is *felt* in how the influenced companion now acts, not announced). `secret_disposition` "weaponized" requires score −3. Player counter (naming influence aloud, shielding a secret, defusing sabotage) resets the relevant field and is itself a warmth/approval event.
