# KINGMAKER — COMPANION SIGNATURES
## KM_Signatures.md | Referenced by: KM_Companions_Behaviors.md, KM_LoadRules_B.md, KM_SceneFiles.md
## v5.0 (2026-05-22): Merged from KM_Signatures_A/B/C/D/E.md. Single source of truth.

> **DM:** PF2e-translated canonical signature abilities per companion.
> Load when a companion is in the active party. Numbers match KM_CompanionIndex.md.
>
> **Format:** POWER (passive) · MOVE (active + action cost + cadence) · LORE (canon hook)
> **Action icons:** ◈ = 1 action · ◈◈ = 2 · ◈◈◈ = 3 · ↻ = reaction · ⏵ = free
> **DC formula:** 10 + companion level + key attribute + prof
> **Use scaling:** L1–5 as listed · L6–10 +1 · L11–15 +2 · L16–20 +3 (unless fixed)
> **No fabrication.** If a companion # is not listed across Parts A–E, they have no
> canonical signature — use preset + titles only.

> **⚙️ ICONIC WEAPON ADVANCEMENT — flagged `[ICONIC]` weapons only.**
> A weapon tagged `[ICONIC]` is bonded to its wielder and CANNOT become obsolete. Its
> *fundamental* progression — to-hit (potency), damage dice (striking), and flat damage —
> advances automatically and for free on the schedule below, so the wielder never has to
> trade away their signature arm to keep pace. It STILL accepts *property* runes (flaming,
> frost, ghost touch, returning, etc.) found or bought, slotted normally up to the
> property-slot count its current potency tier allows.
> ⛔ Do NOT install a *fundamental* rune (potency/striking) on an `[ICONIC]` weapon — it
> already auto-holds the best available; a found fundamental rune is salvaged or moved to
> other (non-iconic) gear. No double-dipping. (A found *property* rune slots in normally.)
>
> | Lvl | Auto (free) | Property slots |
> |----|----|----|
> | 3  | +1 to-hit (potency I) · **2 damage dice (striking)** | 1 |
> | 5  | +1 flat damage | 1 |
> | 7  | +1 flat damage | 1 |
> | 9  | +2 to-hit (potency II) | 2 |
> | 11 | **3 damage dice (greater striking)** | 2 |
> | 13 | +1 flat damage | 2 |
> | 15 | +1 flat damage | 2 |
> | 17 | +3 to-hit (potency III) | 3 |
> | 19 | **4 damage dice (major striking)** | 3 |
>
> Flat-damage bumps are cumulative (+4 total by L15). Dice jumps land at **3 / 11 / 19**,
> to-hit at **3 / 9 / 17**. Applies to any weapon line tagged `[ICONIC]` (companion or PC);
> untagged weapons follow normal loot/shop rules.
>
> **⛔ WHAT QUALIFIES AS `[ICONIC]`:** a weapon is iconic — and auto-advances — only if it is
> **(a)** a **named signature weapon** (Stolen Giant's Blade, Bakuzan, the Thorn
> Stilettos, Tauropolos, etc.), OR **(b)** **granted by the character's Title** (KM_Companions_Titles.md
> § Item — a title that bestows a weapon, e.g. Jaethal's Carrion Scythe). A **plain, unnamed weapon
> with no title-granted weapon** (a generic longsword, a generic flail) is **NOT iconic** — it follows
> normal loot / shop / rune rules. Do NOT tag generic gear `[ICONIC]`.

    keeps). *The Long Memory*: once per major arc she may supply one piece of true historical
    lore about the current threat — drawn from the songs, reports, and confidences she has
    gathered over the years (narrative; resolve on her proficiency).
    The ballad-cycle is the chronicle, not a copy of it.

    deception, diplomacy, and the reading of people are one trained discipline. PASSIVE, always on

    • *Read the Mark*: +2 status bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, and Perception-to-Sense-Motive.

      her (privately, or as a quiet aside to the player) the real read on an NPC who is performing,
      bluffing, or concealing, when a lesser companion would take it at face value.
    • *Hold the Mark*: when the player TASKS her to delay, charm, distract, extract from, or feed a
      false read to ANY NPC — not just Tartuccio — she runs it at her full elite bonus as an opposed
      social contest (her Deception/Diplomacy/Performance vs the target's Perception/Will), sustained
      and re-contested each round per § CADENCE rule 8 (KM_Prologue_Systems.md). This passive is the
      engine behind that mechanic; it generalizes to any interaction (occupying a guard, working a
      merchant, stalling a noble, drawing out a suspect).
    • *The Long Con* (1/major arc): she may plant or maintain a sustained deception on an NPC across
      scenes — a false allegiance, a fed narrative, a cultivated trust — resolved narratively on her
      proficiency; the mark acts on what she made them believe until something external cracks it.
    ⛔ Not mind-control: targets still roll, a crit-fail on her part is read as handling, and she
    cannot manufacture knowledge an NPC doesn't have. She is the best in the room at this — not omnipotent.
