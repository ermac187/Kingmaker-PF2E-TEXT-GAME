# WEAPON-TYPE FINISHING MOVE RESULTS
## Applies to: any attacker (enemy NPC, unnamed soldier, creature) whose weapon type matches below.
## DM: calculate tier, match weapon type, write the scene around the locked result.

Tiers: Clean (1–49%) / Brutal (50–99%) / Savage (100–199%) / Annihilation (200%+)

---

## DAGGER / AGILE BLADE (piercing, light)
*Assassins, thugs, opportunists. Short entry wound, close range.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Blade enters under the jaw or behind the ear. Death immediate. Target sits down. |
| Brutal | Blade enters the throat and is drawn sideways. The throat opens. Target's hands find the wound and find nothing useful to do with it. |
| Savage | Multiple entry wounds in a tight cluster at the throat or base of skull. The neck no longer supports the head at the correct angle. |
| Annihilation | Eleven entry wounds. Face, throat, both eyes. What is left is not a face. The dagger is still in one of them. |

---

## SHORTSWORD (piercing/slashing, agile)
*Light fighters, melee rogues, corridor guards.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Thrust through the lung from behind. Target goes forward onto hands and knees and does not get up. |
| Brutal | Entry at the armpit, angled down through the chest. Lung collapses. Ribs visible through the wound on withdrawal. |
| Savage | Blade enters the throat, exits the back of the neck. Head drops forward. Attached by the front of the throat only. |
| Annihilation | Blade through the eye socket, withdrawn, entered again at the other temple. Crown of the skull fractures between the two entries. What comes out of the ear is not blood only. |

---

## LONGSWORD (slashing, one-hand or two)
*Standard soldiers, bandits, fighters.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Draw-cut across the throat. Clean line. Target kneels, then goes flat. |
| Brutal | Diagonal cut opens the chest from shoulder to sternum. Collarbone visible. Arm drops. |
| Savage | Horizontal cut at neck height. Head leaves in the direction of the swing. Body stands one beat. |
| Annihilation | Two cuts. First diagonal shoulder-to-hip. Second opposing. Four sections. The point of crossing is center mass. |

## RAPIER (piercing, finesse)
*Duelists, assassins, elite rogues (Pitax Assassin).*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Thrust through the heart. Entry wound small. No exit. Target steps backward once, then folds. |
| Brutal | Thrust enters the chest, is withdrawn, enters again at the throat on the riposte. Two wounds, one mortal before the second. |
| Savage | Three thrusts in rapid sequence — chest, throat, skull base. Third one does not come back out. Target is pinned to whatever is behind it, briefly, before gravity settles. |
| Annihilation | The duelist's full phrase: seven entry wounds tracking from sternum to throat to jaw to eye. The eye is the last entry. The rapier exits the back of the skull. The face is a different geometry than it was. |

|------|--------|

| Savage | Horizontal sweep at neck height. Decapitation. Head goes in the direction of the swing. Body stands. |

## FIRE (elemental — spell, bomb, or weapon)
*Who uses it: Hu Tao (Genshin ghost-flame — blue-white, colder-looking than it is), Bellatrix (fiendfyre — alive, serpentine, eats what it touches), any fire evocation caster.*
*Narration lean: Hu Tao's fire leaves pale marks and smells like incense. Bellatrix's fire sounds like something enjoying itself. Generic fire is orange and loud.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Ignition at the chest. Heart stops from the thermal shock before the flame spreads. Target drops. Still burning. |
| Brutal | Fire takes the face. Skin gone, cartilage visible, no hair, no brow. The scream lasts exactly as long as the lungs can manage it. |
| Savage | Flash-ignition of the torso. Sternum visible through what the flame took. Ribs blackened. The body collapses inward at the chest before going down. |
| Annihilation | The body burns through structurally. Upper body separates from lower at the waist as the connective tissue burns out. Both halves continue burning separately. What remains is ash and bone, still in the shape they were in when it finished. |

## LIGHTNING (elemental — spell or Spellstrike)
*Who uses it: Keqing (Genshin Electro — violet arcs, internal discharge, leaves scorch-trace along blood vessels), Regongar (storm-tempered, loud, external, blunt).*
*Narration lean: Keqing's lightning is precise — it finds the path through the body and follows it. Regongar's is brutal — it hits all at once and doesn't navigate.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Discharge stops the heart. No visible wound. Target drops mid-step with no warning. The smell of ozone arrives after. |
| Brutal | Lightning tracks through the blood vessels from entry point outward. Exit burns at both wrists and the back of the neck. Ribs carbonized along the interior. The wound smells like burning. |
| Savage | Discharge enters the skull. The inside of the skull has nowhere to put that energy. Crown of the head fractures outward. What was inside maps the radius of the exit. |
| Annihilation | Full body discharge. Eight exit points — wrists, ankles, shoulder blades, crown, jaw. Each one visible. What holds the body together afterward is unclear. It stops holding together as the attacker steps away. |

## ICE / COLD (elemental — spell or frost bomb)
*Who uses it: Kalikke (Water kineticist — flash-freeze, precise), frost bomb (Jubilost — splash-radius, less controlled).*
*Narration lean: Kalikke freezes exactly what she means to. Jubilost's bombs freeze the whole splash zone at once.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Flash-freeze at the chest. Heart stops inside ice. Target falls rigid, shatters on impact with the floor. The pieces are still the shape they were. |
| Brutal | Arm or shoulder flash-frozen, then struck. The frozen section separates at the joint. The rest of the body goes down. The separated piece is still intact — just cold and no longer attached. |
| Savage | Upper body frozen to the neck. One blow. Upper half separates from lower at the freeze line. Lower half stays where it was. Upper half is in pieces on the floor. |
| Annihilation | Full freeze in under a second. One blow. The body does not break in two — it fragments. The pieces are scattered in the direction of the strike and they are very cold and they are very many. |

## ACID (elemental — spell or acid bomb)
*Who uses it: Jubilost (acid bombs — splash, sizzling sound, smoke), any acid caster.*
*Narration lean: Jubilost's acid comes in a flask that breaks on impact — splash radius, eating sound, smoke. Spell-delivered acid is a stream or burst and hits harder at point of impact.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Acid contacts the throat or face. Dissolves what it touches in seconds. Target cannot make sound. Goes down. |
| Brutal | Acid splash across the face. Facial features dissolve in sequence — eyes first, then the nose, then the architecture of the cheekbones. The scream requires a throat and the throat is dissolving. |
| Savage | Direct hit to the torso. Ribs visible as the tissue between them liquefies. The structural support of the chest dissolves. The body collapses inward and then down. |
| Annihilation | The body does not hold its shape. What was solid becomes liquid at the impact site and spreads outward. What is left on the floor is not a body — it is what a body becomes when the things holding it together are dissolved. Bones remain. Everything between them does not. |

## VOID / NECROTIC (spell, hex, or divine harm)
*Who uses it: Jaethal (Urgathoa — precise, clinical, death is unremarkable), Bellatrix (Cruciatus / AK — theatrical, the Killing Curse is instantaneous and leaves no mark), Harrim (Groetus — everything ends, the energy that unmakes).*
*Narration lean: Jaethal's void is quiet and exact. Bellatrix's is bright green and the target is simply not alive anymore. Harrim's makes the target look like they were always going to end.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | The life goes out. No wound. Face holds whatever expression it had. Target sits down and does not get up. The body is intact. It is simply no longer in use. |
| Brutal | Void energy collapses the tissue at the point of contact. Skin blackens and pulls inward. The wound is an absence — the flesh didn't tear, it withdrew. |
| Savage | The skull collapses inward. Eyes void first, then the structure behind them. The face inverts. The skull becomes a different shape. The body follows it to the floor. |
| Annihilation | The body unravels from the impact point outward. Tissue goes first, then cartilage, then bone, all withdrawing into the void in sequence. What remains is a diminishing outline of what stood there, and then nothing. The floor is clean. |

## CURSE / MENTAL (psychic spell, hex, mental debuff kill)
*Who uses it: Bellatrix (Cruciatus extended — the curse that breaks minds, the body fails after), Jaethal (Truth at the Edge of Pain — void + mental), any mental caster.*
*Narration lean: Bellatrix's curse makes the body destroy itself from terror and pain before the mind fully goes. Psychic kills leave no wound — the skull is intact, the face is wrong.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Aneurysm. No external mark. Target drops mid-sentence. Face has an expression but the expression stopped making sense partway through. |
| Brutal | Nose and ears bleeding. Eyes wrong — one pupil fixed, one blown. Target convulsing on the floor. Then not. |
| Savage | Skull deforms from inside — no external wound until the pressure inside resolves it. The crown separates from the skull in a line rather than an impact pattern. What exits the ears tells the story. |
| Annihilation | Every blood vessel in the skull ruptures simultaneously. What exits every opening — eyes, ears, nose, mouth — arrives at the same moment. The face is still recognizable. Everything behind it is not. |

## POISON (injury, ingested, or contact)
*Who uses it: Yor (Thorn Stilettos can carry contact poison — professional, the target doesn't know), Nok-Nok (kukri, chaos), generic assassin poison.*
*Narration lean: Yor's poison is chosen for clean stop — fast-acting, no spectacle. Assassin poison is slower and worse.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Convulsions, brief, then stop. Target folds. The poison found the heart and that was enough. |
| Brutal | Convulsions tear muscle — the body fighting itself. Foam at the mouth, wrong color. The sound is involuntary and it goes on too long before it stops. |
| Savage | The toxin reaches the heart while the target is still standing. Every muscle fires simultaneously. The body goes rigid mid-motion — whatever position they were in, they hold for one beat — then the muscles fail all at once and they go down in a shape no one falls in voluntarily. |
| Annihilation | Complete systemic cascade. The poison shuts down systems in the wrong order. Target remains conscious through more of it than they should. What the body does involuntarily in the last thirty seconds is not something to describe neutrally. Then it stops, all at once, in the middle of whatever it was doing. |

## BOMBS — FIRE (alchemist, Jubilost)
*Thrown flask. Breaks on impact. Sticks and burns.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Flask breaks across the chest. Alchemical fire sticks to clothing and skin. The burning reaches the heart before anyone can help. Target drops still on fire. |
| Brutal | Direct hit to the face. Alchemical fire on the face does not come off. Facial features gone within seconds. The sound is very loud and then isn't. |
| Savage | Flask to the torso at close range. The explosion pressure alone moves the target back two feet. The fire takes care of the rest. Charred structure visible where the chest was. |
| Annihilation | Multiple flasks. The target is on fire and coming apart simultaneously. What is left when the fire dies is in pieces and charred to the bone. The floor is scorched in the shape of what they were. |

*Thrown flask. Splash radius. Eating sound.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Splash across the face and throat. Acid does not care what it contacts. Target goes down before they finish registering it. |
| Brutal | Direct hit center mass. The eating sound is audible. Clothing gone, then the skin beneath it, then what is beneath the skin. Ribs visible in the splash zone. |
| Savage | The acid dissolves the structural tissue of the torso. The body collapses inward at the chest before collapsing downward. What pools on the floor is not only acid. |
| Annihilation | Full splash coverage. The body dissolves in zones — fastest where the concentration is highest. What remains when it finishes eating is the skeleton, clean, and the floor beneath it, also eaten. |

## BOMBS — FROST (alchemist, Jubilost)
*Thrown flask. Flash-freeze on contact. Brittle.*

| Tier | Result |
|------|--------|
| Clean | Frost bomb to the chest. Heart freezes mid-beat. Target drops rigid. Shatters partially on impact with the floor. |
| Brutal | Flash-freeze at the shoulder and arm. One strike. The frozen section separates clean. The rest of the body falls. The separated arm is intact on the floor, just no longer attached, and very cold. |
| Savage | Frost bomb to the torso. Upper body frozen solid. One strike at the freeze line. Upper half in pieces. Lower half remains standing briefly. |
| Annihilation | Multiple bombs. Full-body freeze. The impact that follows does not produce two pieces — it produces many. They scatter in the direction of the strike. They are cold. They are numerous. Jubilost counts them later. |
