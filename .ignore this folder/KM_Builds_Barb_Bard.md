## BUILD — TACTICAL REACH KING (GIANT INSTINCT, HEAVY PLATE VARIANT)
**Human (Versatile Human) | Barbarian (Giant Instinct) | Background: Warrior**
*Become Large during Rage. Guisarme + Large rage = 15 ft reach at L1, 20 ft at L7, 25 ft at L13. The "God Combo": Heavy Plate at L1 via Versatile Human → Sentinel Dedication scaling at L2. Battlefield controller, tank, and high-DPR striker.*

| | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Score | 18 | 10 | 16 | 10 | 12 | 10 |
| Mod | +4 | +0 | +3 | +0 | +1 | +0 |

**HP** 23 | **AC** 19 (Full Plate, DEX cap 0, Trained Heavy) | **Init** +6 | **Perc** +6 | **Speed** 20 ft
**Fort** +8 | **Ref** +3 (Bulwark — treats failed Reflex save vs area as success) | **Will** +4
**Weapon:** Guisarme (2H S, 1d10+4 / +1d10+10 Large+Rage, Reach, Trip) | **Armor:** Dragon Plate (homebrew, Resist fire 5) or Full Plate (Bulwark, Heavy)
*Alt: Maul (1d12 B, Shove, Backswing) — trades 5 ft reach for max single-hit damage.*
**Skills:** Athletics +7 (Trained, → Expert L3), Acrobatics +1, Intimidation +3 (Trained via Warrior bg), Medicine +4, Warfare Lore +3 (Trained via Warrior bg), Crafting +1, Perception +6
**Class Features:** Rage (Giant — Large size, +6 damage, 10 ft reach, TempHP = level+CON), Deny Advantage, Raging Resistance (vs physical from chosen alignment damage type per Instinct rules)
**Key Feats:** L1 Heritage Versatile Human → bonus general feat: Armor Proficiency (Heavy via campaign homebrew armor menu) | L1 Ancestry → Natural Ambition (grants Sudden Charge as bonus class feat) | L1 Class → Raging Intimidation | L1 Skill (Warrior bg auto) → Powerful Leap | L2 Class → Sentinel Dedication | L3 General → Toughness | L3 Class → Titan Wrestler
**Boost Priority:** STR → CON → WIS → DEX

**The "God Combo" (L1–L3 stack):**
- **Versatile Human** grants Armor Proficiency (Heavy) at L1 → wear Full Plate immediately.
- **Sentinel Dedication (L2)** auto-scales Heavy Armor to Expert at L7, Master at L15.
- **Bulwark trait** (Full Plate) → DEX is irrelevant for Reflex; use +3 flat. Frees the stat to dump.
- **Toughness (L3)** patches Barbarian's mid AC with massive HP pool (16 + level HP, +1/level from Toughness).
- **Titan Wrestler (L3)** lets you Trip/Shove enemies up to TWO sizes larger than you. While Raging Large, that's Gargantuan.

**Race Options** *(ranked by reach synergy. ★1 Human/Versatile Human is the canonical L1 Heavy Armor route — pick anything else if you want to trade L1 Full Plate for a different chassis. Heritages that EXTEND reach, ADD reach unarmed strikes, or provide alternate AC paths without burning the L1 general feat are ranked above pure HP picks.)*

|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| ★1 | Human | Versatile Human | REQUIRED for Heavy Armor proficiency at L1 — the canonical chassis. Full Plate, AC 19, Bulwark. The entire L1 build hinges on this. |
| 2 | Goliath | Great | Reach-weapon optimization + Large-size synergy with Giant Instinct. Already +STR+CON. When raging the Large+Goliath stacking opens reach feats other ancestries can't take. |

| 6 | Vanara | Tail Swipe | Prehensile tail (baseline) + Tail Swipe L1 = bonus reach unarmed strike. Free third-action attack that doesn't require the Guisarme. +DEX +WIS helps initiative for "act first, control reach bubble." |
| 7 | Dwarf | Ancient-Blooded | Native Heavy Armor proficiency. Alternate route to Full Plate WITHOUT spending Versatile Human — frees the L1 general feat. +CON survivability stacks with Toughness. |
| 8 | Orc | Hold-Scarred | Original carousel chassis. Diehard free + 10 base HP for Giant rage 0-HP drops. Hide Armor instead of Full Plate (AC 18, no Bulwark) — trades AC for huge HP pool. |
| 9 | Lizardfolk | Cliffscale | Natural armor bonus + STR 18 synergy. Hide Armor variant. Alt Bakuwa heritage (+4 natural AC) is also strong for the tank role. |
| 10 | Azarketi | Spined | Spine ranged unarmed attack (1d4 poison, **10-ft range increment, 20/30/40 increments**). Reach alternative for when the Guisarme can't reach — flying targets, ledge gaps, mounted enemies above. |
