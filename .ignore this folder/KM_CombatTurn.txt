─── KILL RESOLUTION ─────────────────────────────────────────

If target HP ≤ 0 and player did not declare non-lethal:
  → Target is DEAD. Write the word DEAD or equivalent. Not "he does not move."
  → FINISHER TRIGGER? crit kill / overkill / Stage Fatality / boss-or-named kill.
  → IF YES — FINISHER: Overkill% = (damage dealt − HP remaining) ÷ target max HP × 100
       Tier: 1–49% Clean / 50–99% Brutal / 100–199% Savage / 200%+ Annihilation
       REQUIRED: tier label in COMBAT CHECK + a Finishing Move narration that **MATCHES the
       tier's register** and **ENDS on a decisive, visceral, unmistakably-dead image.**
       ⛔ THE REGISTER SCALES — under-rendering it is the failure:
         · Clean (1–49%)  = clean, immediate, final.
         · Brutal (50–99%) = bloody, hard, the body broken.
         · SAVAGE (100–199%) = DEVASTATING and TOTAL — chest caved, the man wrecked and
           discarded, the kill emphatic; a Savage tier is NOT a quiet slump.
         · ANNIHILATION (200%+) = catastrophic, theatrical destruction.
         A muted/generic death on a Savage/Annihilation tier = under-rendered finisher = `.fail 7`.
       SOURCE BY ATTACK:
         · Guisarme kill → KM_GuisarmeFinishers.md (pick A–E) — it CUTS; narrate the blade.
         · UNARMED MELEE kill (fist, heel into a body or wall) → brutal PHYSICAL finisher at
           the tier register directly (body-mechanics, not a blade). ⛔ Do NOT load the guisarme
           file for a kick — a heel is not a guisarme.
         · ⛔ **FALL / THROW / DEFENESTRATION / ENVIRONMENTAL kill = SPECIAL — RUN THE § FALLS
           CALCULATION, do not one-line it.** The death is the IMPACT WITH THE GROUND, not the
           shove. The finisher MUST show its work (KM_Combat_Systems.md § FALLS + KM_PR_04
           window-hazard):
             1. **FALL DISTANCE** — state the height (manor upper floor ≈ 25–30 ft, not a flat 15)
                and the damage: half the distance in bludgeoning (~12–15) + GLASS if through a pane
                (+2d6 slashing + 1d6 persistent bleed).
             2. **SURFACE STRUCK** — name it and let it decide lethality: grass/soil/hedge = softer
                (broken but maybe alive at low tier); STONE / FLAGSTONE / MARBLE = hard, lethal.
             3. **HOW HE LANDS (orientation)** — head/neck/back-first onto hard surface = killed;
                feet-first / a roll / into soft = survivable-but-broken. A thrown, surprised body
                defaults to a BAD landing — never a clean one.
             4. **INJURIES** — name the specific damage from that surface+orientation (dislocated
                shoulder, sprained ankle/knee on soft; fractured spine, broken ribs, shattered
                skull/bones on hard) — as the FINISHER imagery if dead, or as the persistent
                DEBUFFS (Enfeebled/Slowed/lost action) if it was a survived drop (§ FALLS injury table).
             5. **SELF-IMPALEMENT check** — if the faller has a DRAWN blade in hand, roll **d6 (§ FALLS):
                5–6** = he comes down on his own weapon (+the weapon's PIERCING damage, a grisly
                self-impalement at the Savage register — his own knife standing out of him). Occasional,
                not every fall; drawn blades only (a bow/blunt just snaps).
           Render the DESCENT and the catastrophic LANDING at the tier register — the wet
           compression, the wrong angles. The environment is the weapon; the fall is its own
           special finisher, NOT a generic "he hits the ground and doesn't move."
  → IF NO — REGULAR KILL: no tier label, no finisher. Pull a one-sentence result from
       KM_WeaponKills.md (guisarme → HALBERD/POLEARM; unarmed → bludgeoning/unarmed line) →

  → ⛔ END ON THE KILL, NOT A FADE. BANNED endings (any of these under-sells a death): "he does

       breathing" / "barely conscious" / any quiet fade-to-still standing in for the kill. The
       LAST image is the DEATH itself — emphatic, physical, done. A Savage kick that ends on
       "he does not move again" is exactly the pathetic non-ending this bans.
  → BANNED: any NPC line implying survival

       KILL / GHOST / ELIMINATE / SILENCE, the companion KILLS — Jaethal least of all
       hesitates. ⛔ NO "flat of the blade, for incapacitation not execution" substitution
       for a lethal order (= `.fail 39` reassigned action + `.fail 9` softening). A
       surprise/stealth lethal strike usually KILLS OUTRIGHT — do not nerf damage to leave
       the target at 1 HP "alive."
  → GHOST / STEALTH-KILL = SILENT *LETHAL* takedown (throat / base of skull / covered-mouth
       thrust) — the target DIES without raising alarm. A non-lethal flat-blade/pommel
       knockout is NOT a ghost: it is LOUDER (grunt, struggle, dropped gear) and leaves a
       live enemy who can wake and shout. "Ghost / kill quietly / eliminate" = lethal +
       silent; only an explicit "knock out / take alive" order is non-lethal (and harder to
       keep quiet). See KM_ClaudeInstructions § GHOST / STEALTH-KILL.
  → ⛔ GHOSTING IS ROLLED, PER TARGET — never an auto-sweep. EACH target = TWO ROLLS:
       (1) ASSASSINATION = a DEXTERITY check (armor-modified) — does the deft kill LAND?
           • SUCCESS = INSTANT ASSASSINATION: the throat/heart/base-of-skull strike is lethal
             REGARDLESS of the HP total — mark DEAD even if the damage roll alone wouldn't reach 0
             (10 dmg on a 12-HP target, throat slit → DEAD, not "2 HP"). ⛔ Do NOT compute an
             overkill % or a Clean/Brutal/Savage tier on a stealth kill — those are for HP-depletion
             kills; an assassination has a severed artery, not "overkill." Narrate the anatomical
             kill, mark DEAD, then roll (2).
           • FAILURE = BOTCHED KILL: she screwed it up — the target SENSES her a half-beat early,
             twists away, or BREAKS OUT of the grip before the blade goes home. The target is
             ALIVE and ALERTED, and the struggle/cry makes ⛔ ALL OF THEM REALIZE SHE'S THERE —
             full ALARM, silent run OVER, roll initiative. (This is the worst outcome: no kill AND
             the room is up. A failed assassination does NOT just make noise — it leaves a living,
             warned enemy and blows the whole approach.)
       (2) NON-DETECTION = STEALTH vs the OTHER enemies' PERCEPTION (armor-modified) — rolled ONLY
           if (1) succeeded. Did anyone ELSE notice the act / the dropping body / the sudden silence?
           • SUCCESS = unseen; she may continue to the next target (with escalation).
           • FAILURE = the kill landed (target DEAD) but someone CLOCKED it → ALARM, silent run over.
       STAT SPLIT: DEXTERITY lands the kill (roll 1); STEALTH keeps it unseen (roll 2). A deft killer
       in noisy plate can kill clean yet be spotted; a quiet sneak with shaky hands can botch the kill.
       ESCALATE each successive ghost (cumulative penalty to the assassination and/or bonus to the
       others' Perception). Chain them; one failure of EITHER roll ends the silence.
       (2b) SILENCE WITNESS — if the target was an ACTIVE TALKER in ongoing enemy banter (just
            addressed, or his turn to reply), the others get a BONUS to the non-detection Perception
            (roll 2) — they're waiting on a voice that never comes ("Renno? ...Renno."). Killing
            right AFTER his line, or killing a quiet/peripheral/leaving man, gives NO such bonus;
            killing him ON his turn to answer is the loudest gap. Reward timing, punish greed; roll
            it, never auto. Full rule in KM_Combat_Systems.md § PRE-COMBAT (UNAWARE ENEMIES TALK).
       ⛔ ARMOR MODIFIES BOTH: its Dex Cap limits the DEXTERITY assassination (roll 1); its Check
       Penalty hits the STEALTH non-detection (roll 2); HEAVY armor (plate) additionally CLATTERS
       (penalty to both — fumbles the kill AND betrays position). Cloth/light = ideal ghoster; plate
       = doubly poor. (KM_ClaudeInstructions § GHOST.)
  → JAETHAL'S KILL STYLE = CLINICAL ANATOMICAL LETHALITY — a blade across the throat/carotid
       (slit neck), driven into the heart, a kidney, the liver, up under the ribs, or into the
       base of the skull. Cold, exact, an executioner's economy — never vague "strikes him
       down" or a bloodless drop. (KM_Backstories §7; vague Jaethal kill = `.fail 7`.)
