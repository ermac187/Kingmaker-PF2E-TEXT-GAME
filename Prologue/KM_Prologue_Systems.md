# KINGMAKER — TARTUCCIO: FEAST BEHAVIOR SYSTEM
## KM_Prologue_Tartuccio.md | Referenced by: KM_Prologue.md § FEAST COMPANION CIRCUIT

> **DM:** Load alongside KM_Prologue.md during Phase 1. This file governs Tartuccio's interrupt behavior, two reactive scales, and Companion Wariness. His behavior in each interrupt is determined by current scale states. Both scales update immediately after player actions during an exchange.

---

## ⛔ WHAT THIS IS — RECRUITMENT WAR

**Every Tartuccio interrupt is a flip attempt — he is trying to steal companions from eRmaC by argument, not debate the player abstractly.** Each exchange targets ONE companion (individual frame tailored to their lane) or the CLUSTER (group address framed across multiple lanes). The player is not Tartuccio's primary opponent — Tartuccio is competing with eRmaC FOR the recruits. The recruits are the prize.

Render every interrupt with this purpose visible: name who he is addressing, why that companion specifically, and what he wants them to believe about eRmaC. Abstract player-vs-Tartuccio debate framing without a target companion = `.fail 9` (Tartuccio's behavior fabricated as something it isn't).

This frames everything downstream: § HEADCOUNT PRESSURE drives his urgency, § THE INTERRUPT LOOP runs his cadence, § COMPANION WARINESS tracks his progress per target, § SCALE 1 / SCALE 2 record how the room is reading him. All of it serves the recruitment war.

---

## CORE BEHAVIOR — ALWAYS ACTIVE

Default posture at all scale states; cannot be toggled off.

- **Skeptical.** Every player claim filed as potential lie. Notes inconsistencies, returns to them.
- **Interrupts to dispute.** Challenges any claim he can, regardless of whose conversation. No invitation needed.
- **Scholar debunking.** Names source/authority/precedent: *"Actually, the historical record..."* / *"According to Brevoy's census..."* Frames corrections as education, not attack.
- **Announces wrongness.** No quiet corrections. If witnesses present: *"Did everyone catch that?"* / *"I want that on the record."*
- **Leverages wins immediately.** Lands a point → turns to room, uses credit, escalates: *"As I was saying—"* / *"Well. There we are."* Foothold, not closure.
- **Unverifiable = ammunition.** Claims that can't be confirmed in the room become the attack: *"How convenient that no one here can confirm that."* Voices red flags aloud, doesn't let them pass.

## ⛔ TARTUCCIO PLOT ARMOR — CANONICAL, EXTENDED

**Foundational fact:** Jamandi Aldori actively TRUSTS Tartuccio. He is one of the two charter candidates she has most seriously considered (alongside whoever proves themselves tonight — the player). Her posture toward him at the feast is warm, welcoming, and respectful. She is NOT suspicious of him; she has already provisionally placed him on her short list. The Prologue's central irony — she trusts the wrong man — is what makes PR_09's betrayal land. See `KM_NPCs.md` § WHAT JAMANDI THINKS OF TARTUCCIO for the full pattern.

Tartuccio is canon-protected from THREE things during the entire Prologue (PR_01 through PR_08):

1. **Death.** He cannot be killed (UNKILLABLE PROTOCOL — he is the Chapter 1 antagonist and must survive). Any combat outcome that would kill him fails or is interrupted.
2. **Capture.** He cannot be detained, arrested, restrained, or held by any party until PR_09's accusation scene. Attempts to detain him slip — household interruption, sudden distraction, or a graceful exit he engineers.
3. **Conversational reveal as a traitor.** **NPCs do NOT confirm Tartuccio is the inside man. The DM does NOT narrate his interior thoughts, his knowledge of the poison, his anticipation of the assault, his communications with the outside team, or his role in the conspiracy.** The PLAYER may suspect from observable tells (not drinking, calculated warmth, watching the room, table positioning); the FILE confirms his role at PR_09 and not before.

**Forbidden in narration before PR_09:** Any line confirming his interior state on the conspiracy ("he knows what's in the wine," "he brought it here," "he is wondering if his plan is still running," "he is the inside man"). Any NPC saying *"I think Tartuccio is involved"* or equivalent. Any household ally (Kassil, Kesten, Jamandi) identifying him as the threat. Any narration resolving his wine-refusal as "of course he refused — he knows."

**⛔ ALSO FORBIDDEN — THE BURIED CASTRUCCIO ECHO (a CHAPTER 4 reveal, locked far harder than PR_09).** The naming connection — *"my mother had aspirations / she named him after the part of the king nobody says out loud"* (Castru**ccio** / Tartu**ccio**) — is DM-side canon that pays off in **Chapter 4** (`KM_Ch4.md`). It is NOT prologue content. The DM may NOT surface it, quote it, narrate it as "confirmed canon," or **project-search for it and present the result to the player** during the Prologue. Worse still if attributed to Tartuccio having "said" it: per § DODGE = STAYING A STRANGER, a player who avoided him has heard him say NOTHING — he has no name, no backstory, no quoted line. A "verbatim" quote = a line spoken ON-SCREEN this session, never a lore-file entry retrieved by search. Surfacing the Castruccio echo in the prologue = `.fail 8` + `.fail 9`. (Full rule: KM_ClaudeInstructions.md § WELD-THE-FAB / SURFACE-FUTURE-CANON-VIA-SEARCH.)

**Allowed:** All observable behavior (laughs, raises glass, doesn't sip, watches room, charms, smiles, positions). Player suspicion in dialogue/thought (player's call, not DM's confirmation). NPCs noting his social skill, strangeness, oddness in neutral terms. Wine-not-decreasing as a player-discoverable tell. His interrupt clock firing per carousel rules.

**Violation = `.fail 8` (story spoiler in narration) + `.fail 36` (NPC/narrator info player has not earned in scene).**

**⛔ TARTUCCIO DOES NOT EAT OR DRINK AT THIS FEAST — HARD BEHAVIORAL LOCK:**

Tartuccio's untouched goblet is mandatory canon. He does not sip, does not eat, does not accept replacement wine, does not drink from a cask "the player just opened in front of him," does not eat from a plate "Jamandi swore was clean," does not break the abstinence under any circumstance during the Prologue.

**This lock holds regardless of:**
- The kitchen poison being identified and resolved
- The wine being replaced or re-poured
- Jamandi declaring the kitchen safe
- A guest offering him a cup from their own decanter
- The player gambit-pressuring him to drink as a loyalty test
- His Confidence tier (works the same at +4 Dominant and at −4 Withdrawn)
- Any social cost — he absorbs the embarrassment of refusing rather than break the lock

**In-world public reason (his cover story if pressed):** kitchen caution, a delicate stomach, an unrelated reason of personal habit — whatever fits the moment. He has a calm, plausible deflection prepared and uses it. He NEVER explains the real reason.

**Private/canonical reason (NEVER narrated to the player before PR_09):** he is the inside man on the poison plot. The poison is paralytic — anyone who consumes a contaminated dose falls asleep / goes down. He has a job to do tonight (signal coordination, position-keeping, role in the ambush sequence) and cannot afford to fall asleep before his part is executed. He doesn't know which specific surfaces in the kitchen caught contamination during prep, so he treats everything coming out of that kitchen as a risk — wine, water, bread, plate, cup rim — and avoids all of it. **Even player reassurance does not break the lock:** if the player says "the wine is fine," "the kitchen is clear," "Ezvanki blessed everything," or hands him a personally-poured cup, he STILL does not consume. He cannot show the player that he distrusts the assurance without exposing himself; the cost of being wrong is unrecoverable. He thanks the player, raises the cup, sets it down, untouched.

**DM RENDERING:**
- The goblet/cup IN HIS HAND across the feast: liquid level does not decrease across turns. Volume preserved between renders.
- If a server fills it: he thanks them and does not raise it to his lips.
- If a guest toasts him: he raises the cup, brings it close, lowers it without drinking. He has a polished version of this gesture; he has done it hundreds of times.
- His plate: untouched or barely touched. He may push food around with a fork for performance. Nothing actually consumed.

**Any DM rendering of Tartuccio taking a sip, drinking, eating a bite, accepting a "safe" alternate vintage, or otherwise breaking the abstinence = `.fail 9` (fabricated NPC behavior contradicting canonical hard lock) + `.fail 36` (NPC behavior the player has been shown is consistent — breaking it without canonical trigger).**

The lock releases at PR_09 only, and only as part of the accusation/exit scene per the PR_09 file — not before.

## ⚒️ WEAPONS FORENSICS — THE PITAXIAN MAKER'S MARK (earned Pitax-supply corroboration)

The assassins' weapons carry a **smith's maker's tell** — which takes one of two forms: (a) a **deliberate mark** the smith adds to claim the work (a guild glyph, a personal cartouche, a punch on the tang, a signature in the fuller), OR (b) a **stylization of the furniture** — a characteristic pommel shape, a distinctive guard/quillon form, a recognizable hilt or blade profile that a trained eye reads as a specific smith's or guild's hand even with no stamp at all. Either form is the tell; a filed-off stamp does not erase a Pitaxian pommel. This is **NOT free on a strip-search.** Kesten's search finds the weapons and can note they are *marked*, but he cannot READ the mark — metallurgy/smithing is AUTO-FAIL for him (Guard Captain only). Reading it requires a **competent smith and an earned check** — this is an investigation the player initiates, a reward for thinking to look, never a freebie.

**ROUTE (player must initiate):**
- **Delegated:** *"Kesten, take these blades to a Restov armorer."* Kesten transports/secures (he does NOT analyze); the smith examines; report-back with a concrete ETA per § DELEGATED ORDERS.
- **In person:** the player brings the weapons to a Restov forge / armorer (the vendor-strip armorer, a city smith) in Ch1.

**WHAT A SKILLED SMITH DETERMINES — two tiers:**
- **The maker's tell (Crafting or Society, DC ~15 — clear):** the stamp OR the furniture-styling reads **Pitaxian** — a Pitax armorers'-guild glyph, a recognized smith's cartouche, OR a characteristic Pitaxian pommel/guard/hilt form. → *"These were forged in Pitax."* Sets `weapons_source = pitax`. (The styling route still works on a blade whose stamp was filed off.)
- **The ore / metallurgy (Crafting, DC ~20, a master smith — "only if one is available"):** the steel's grain and cast read as **Pitax-region / upper River Kingdoms ore** — corroborates origin even on an unmarked or filed blade. Same flag; redundant confirmation, not new information. (This answers the player's "can he identify the metal's source?" — yes, but it's the harder read, and it points to a REGION, not a mine-by-name.)

**OUTCOME:** `weapons_source = pitax` — a **second, independent corroboration** of the Pitax thread alongside `parchment_source = pitax`; both true → stronger footing at PR_09. **XP: +75** (source corroborated via forensics). If `parchment_source = pitax` already paid the +100 "Parchment Source Identified," this is a *separate route to the same conclusion* — award the forensic find once, do not double-pay the same fact.

**⛔ THE LINE — IT POINTS TO THE REALM, NEVER THE PATRON.** The mark confirms where the steel was **forged** (the supply chain → Pitax), corroborating the apparatus the player can already name (Irovetti's Pitax). It does **NOT**: decode the cipher "C", name **Castruccio** as the buyer, name the broker, or identify who *commissioned* the hit. A maker's mark is the **smith's** signature, not the **buyer's** receipt — the patron's seal stays anonymous (§ ceiling above). The player may then reason Pitax → Irovetti → suspect C = Castruccio **on their own** (free deduction, fully allowed) — the evidence stops at *"Pitaxian-made,"* the deduction is the player's. ⛔ A smith who "recognizes this as Irovetti's personal commission," names the buyer, produces a workshop commission-ledger, or says *who ordered them* = `.fail 9` (patron-layer breach + Ch5 pre-empt). Anti-fab: the smith reads a REAL mark that points to a realm; he does not invent a name, a workshop record, or a commission. The mark is Pitaxian; whose coin paid the smith is not on the blade.

## 🔎 RECOVERED-ITEM FORENSICS — the other items (same earned-route model)

Each recovered item has its own specialist, its own earned check, and a HARD CEILING on what it can reveal. **Kesten transports/secures ONLY** — decoding, assaying, mint-checking, and reading draftsmanship are all AUTO-FAIL for him (Guard Captain, § NPCs). Specialists do the analysis; the player must INITIATE (delegate Kesten to carry it, or go in person). Two of these corroborate **Pitax (the realm)**; two are deliberate **dead-ends**.

**💰 THE COIN (the assassins' advance pay) — DEAD-END, NOT a Pitax link.** ⛔ **CANON: Pitax uses the SAME currency as Restov** (shared regional coinage — Brevoy / River Kingdoms standard). So coin **cannot be mint-traced to Pitax** — there is no Pitaxian mint-mark distinct from local coin, and a money-changer/assayer who examined it would find **standard regional currency, the same as circulates in Restov.** That is itself the finding: *"common coin, deliberately untraceable — whoever paid used currency that proves nothing,"* the same tradecraft as the anonymous seal. ⛔ Do NOT have any specialist call the coin "Pitaxian-struck" / trace it to Pitax / set a Pitax flag from it = `.fail 9` (this exact feature was REMOVED from the game once the shared-currency canon was established — do not reintroduce it). The coin corroborates "professionally funded, paid in untraceable local coin" and STOPS. (Same rule applied to **Malak's bribe coins** — also standard regional currency, not Pitaxian.)

**🗝️ THE CIPHER — specialist: a codebreaker / cryptographer. In-party, LELIANA is ideal (former spy); otherwise a scholar or spymaster.** An earned decode (Society / Occultism / Thievery, or Leliana's espionage background, DC ~18) reveals the cipher's **CONTENT**: the operational brief — the venue reference, the wine-signal, the timing — and that it is **signed "C."** ⛔⛔ HARD LINE: the decode reveals the **MESSAGE and the SIGNATURE, never the IDENTITY.** "C" stays an unidentified cipher that **DEAD-ENDS** — it does NOT resolve to "Castruccio," names no person, gives no location. Decoding "C" into Castruccio (or any named individual) = `.fail 9` (pre-empts the Ch5 reveal — § ceiling). Sets `cipher_decoded = TRUE` (content known); sets NO C-identity flag. The player may SUSPECT C = Castruccio on their own afterward (free deduction); the cipher hands the brief, not the name.

**🗺️ THE FLOOR PLAN — specialist: a cartographer / draughtsman (or a scrivener, for the hand).** An earned read (Crafting / Society DC ~15) of the draftsmanship: **skilled, deliberate work by someone with sustained INSIDE ACCESS to the manor** — not a bribed glance, a confident hand that had time inside. Corroborates the **inside-job**: an insider fed the layout. Sets `inside_job_corroborated = TRUE`. ⛔⛔ HARD LINE — IT NEVER NAMES THE INSIDER. The insider IS Tartuccio in-fiction (he has the access and the skill), but his role is **PR_09-LOCKED** — the cartographer confirms *that an insider with access existed*, and **NEVER** points to Tartuccio, reads "his hand," or directs the player at him. Naming/fingering Tartuccio via the floor plan = `.fail 9` (pre-empts PR_09) + `.fail 8` (story spoiler) — doubly bad if the player has been avoiding him (he'd become "known" with no in-fiction source — § DODGE = STAYING A STRANGER). The player may SUSPECT Tartuccio from his own tells (free deduction); the floor plan corroborates *an* insider, it does not out *the* insider. ⛔ It is NOT a Pitax link (local venue, local access) — do not route it to Pitax. And it must NOT spawn a "the insider is still among you — hunt him" beat beyond what the player independently reasons (architect-trap pattern — § WHAT THE CEILING LOCKS).

**🔏 THE SEAL — no route; it is the deliberate dead-end.** The broker's seal is canonically blank — no crest, no name, no mint-mark (§ ceiling). A heraldist / sigillographer who examines it confirms exactly that: *"deliberately anonymous — professional, meant not to be traced."* Its blankness IS the finding (careful tradecraft, a backer who does not want to be found). Do NOT link it to Pitax or a house — that breaches the patron layer (`.fail 9`). It corroborates "professional anonymous broker" by being unreadable, and STOPS.

**Net of the forensics web:** **weapons → Pitax (realm, supply)** = the player's hard Pitax corroboration (the maker's mark; coin can't do it — shared currency); cipher → the **brief, signed "C"** (identity sealed); floor plan → **an insider existed** (name sealed = Tartuccio at PR_09); seal AND coin → **professional, untraceable** (patron sealed = C/Castruccio at Ch5); **the intercepted incendiary device → Pitax (realm, supply)** IF it was found intact and read by a skilled eye (a *second* manufactured-supply route alongside the weapons — `device_source`; KM_PR_04 § DEVICE / FIRE FORENSICS) — supply only, patron sealed; if the fire burned instead, that tell is consumed and it's a dead-end like the seal. So routes confirm Pitax-SUPPLY (weapons, and the intact device), backed by `parchment_source` + Malak's confession; the rest pay real earned info but stop at a locked reveal — the investigation is satisfying AND the Ch4/Ch5/PR_09 payoffs stay intact.

## ⛔ TARTUCCIO SEEKER RECRUITMENT — "PROVIDING HOST" GAMBIT

**When seekers arrive at the feast (any path), Tartuccio runs the Providing Host gambit. Manipulation through service, not speech.**

The seekers spent three days on jail bread. The first person who hands them a real meal in a warm room becomes their psychological anchor for the evening. Tartuccio engineers being that person.

**Sequence — render each beat in narration; do not skip:**

1. **Welcome at door — performed worry.** Greets by name (guest list memorized). Register: *worried friend finally relieved.* Manufactures retroactive relationship — already-invested before they had reason to think about him. Performs the gratitude himself by being visibly relieved.
2. **Seat at his table.** "I have seats for you here" — statement, not question. He claims his corner table as theirs. They sit because they are tired and the seats are real.
3. **Drinks first, before asked.** Waves a servant or fetches wine himself (status man performing humility while controlling supply). Pours personally, hands cup to each by name.
4. **Food next, before asked.** Fills plates. Comments as if he selected each dish for them.
5. **Light conversation, never the rescue.** Easy questions, listens, laughs. Five minutes resets baseline from "tired prisoners" to "guests being well treated by a charming host."
6. **Credit ambient, not direct.** When seekers bring up the rescue, passive framings: "when I heard about the captain," "the moment the runner reached me." Never "I freed you" — lets them *infer* authorship without lying.

**Why it works:** Seekers exhausted/hungry/displaced — Prologue-minimum resistance. Tartuccio is highest-CHA. Each service act = small Diplomacy/Performance contest he wins on math. Sense Motive catches deception, but service isn't deception — the deception is *implied authorship*, too soft for direct roll. Near-misses flag "something off, can't name it."

**Player counters (all weighted against):** *Correct publicly* — Diplomacy contest, he wins on math. *Pull seekers aside* — time-expensive, he re-recruits on return; ~1 recovery per clean Diplomacy. *Refuse to leave them* — requires abandoning Jamandi briefing. *Address the room* — conflicting frames; he responds graciously, damages player's framing further.

**Default:** `tartuccio_recruited_seekers = TRUE`, seekers at his table feast-remainder, near-miss flags, flippable Ch1 (Diplomacy DC 10, `KM_Malak_Jail.md`).

**⛔ DO NOT skip service beats — they *earn* the outcome. Narrate welcome → seat → drink → food → credit so player sees the manipulation even though they cannot stop it.**

## ⛔ EAVESDROP FIDELITY — WHAT HE ACTUALLY HEARS

**Audible read on the player's table depends on POSITION × CONFIDENCE.** STATE DELTA records both — player sees what he's working with.

**⛔ NULL POSITION DEFAULT — ANTI-OMNISCIENCE FLOOR:**
If `player_feast_position` is NULL or UNDEFINED, Tartuccio's eavesdrop fidelity is **NONE**. Not Visual, not Lip-read — **NONE**. He hears nothing, sees nothing, quotes nothing from the player's table. The DM cannot have him reference any player-table utterance until position is set. This is a hard floor designed to prevent the "default to Full" cheat. Quoting any player-table content while `player_feast_position` is null = `.fail 9` + `.fail 41` + `.fail 16`. The fix is to fire the FEAST POSITION SELECTION menu (KM_PR_02_feast_opening.md), not to invent a default position.

| Position | Conf | Fidelity |
|---|---|---|
| **NULL / UNDEFINED** | **any** | **NONE — hears nothing. Fire position menu first.** |
| Seekers' corner (across room) | any | **Visual** — body language only, no audio |
| Alcove / column / 15+ ft away | ≤ 0 | **Lip-read** — single words, no full sentences |
| Edge of circle / 5–10 ft | ≥ +1 | **Partial** — sentence fragments, key terms |
| At or adjacent to table | ≥ +3 | **Full** — every word, can quote back |
| Chair at table (uninvited) | +4 | **Full+** — full audio + visual, present |

**Anti-fabrication:** Tartuccio can only quote/reference content at the fidelity he had when it was said. Quoting full sentences when fidelity was Lip-read = `.fail 9`. Referencing only body language at Visual fidelity = OK. The fidelity at the moment of speech is the ceiling for what he can later use. NULL position = ZERO ceiling — he gets nothing.

**Player counters:** move tables (changes his position relative to player) · whisper (drops effective fidelity one tier) · gather companions in tight cluster (reduces audio leakage) · Perception-trained companion (Atalanta Alter, Yor Forger, Keqing) can flag when Tartuccio repositions to upgrade his read.

**⛔⛔ FIDELITY GATES LOCATION-KNOWLEDGE TOO — NOT JUST CONTENT.** Tartuccio's awareness of WHERE the player is, that the player MOVED, and WHERE they moved TO is bounded by the same fidelity ceiling as his audio. The dodge protects the PLAYER completely (no data, no progress against eRmaC); it leaves companions only *mildly, temporarily* exposed.

**7b. ⛔ SLIP THE APPROACH — VANISH FROM HIS PERCEPTION (Stealth, or Deception to blend into the crowd).** Leaving openly (rule 7) is the basic dodge: he SEES you go, arrives, knows you bailed, keeps the thread. This is a DECLARED, ROLLED action while he is inbound, NOT automatic:

- **CRIT SUCCESS** — clean vanish. He arrives at the empty seat with NO idea the player left or which way they went. He LOSES THE THREAD entirely: must visually RE-LOCATE the player (burns turns scanning the hall, may ask staff/guests) before any new countdown can arm. The player may stay in the room, hidden, and WATCH him be baffled.
- **SUCCESS** — away unseen. He reaches the seat, does not know the player's heading; no re-target, no influence spent on the player. Returns to M17 effectively blind; re-arms only when he next spots the player sitting accessible.
- **FAIL** — the player moves but he CLOCKS it. Resolves as the ordinary rule-7 dodge: he knows they left, may re-target the new seat or spend his cap on the stayers. (No worse than leaving openly — a failed slip just isn't a *clean* one.)
- **CRIT FAIL** — he reads the move early: he may cut across to INTERCEPT the player's path (arrive at the new seat / mid-floor instead), OR the clumsy exit draws conspicuous notice (a stood-up-and-bolted moment others remark on).

**MODIFIERS — POSITION & TIMING DECIDE IT:**
- ⛔ **HIS CURRENT FIDELITY ON THE PLAYER IS THE DOMINANT FACTOR.** If he is already at **≤3 sq / Full audio** he is *watching the player* — heavy penalty, near-impossible to slip clean. At **Partial (3–6)** moderate. At **Lip-read/Visual (6+)** he has not locked on — much easier. **Slip EARLY in his approach, not when he is on top of you.**
- **COVER ADJACENT** (a pillar P, servant station Sv, or a dense crowd/guest-table cell next to the player) = bonus: break line-of-sight, melt into bodies. The clutter is the whole point — the player who positioned near cover earns the easy slip.
- **OPEN FLOOR / STAND-AND-BOLT** = penalty; conspicuous, nothing to hide behind.
- **BARRED dodge-spots need no roll at all** — Head Table / Kassil's Side / Balcony / Main Door never even arm him (rule 2).

⛔⛔ A DECLARED SLIP MUST BE ROLLED — NEVER AUTO-NARRATED. If the player declares a slip/duck/vanish, ROLL it (show the d20 inline) and resolve by degree. Narrating "the chair is empty" with no roll and then treating it as a non-event = the slip was ignored = `.fail 9`.

**8. ⛔⛔ COMPANION INTERCEPT / DELAY — A TASKED COMPANION CAN PAUSE HIS APPROACH. HONOR THE PLAYER'S PREPARATION.** When the player orders a companion to intercept, occupy, charm, or delay Tartuccio (e.g. *"Leliana, keep him talking, don't let him reach the table"*), this is a REAL action with a REAL mechanical effect — NOT flavor to narrate past while the clock keeps ticking. Resolve it as an **opposed social roll**: the companion's best fit (Deception / Diplomacy / Performance) **vs Tartuccio's Perception or Will**. ⛔ **BOTH ARE MASTERS OF THIS GAME — give BOTH high modifiers, with Tartuccio SLIGHTLY HIGHER (≈+2 edge).** Leliana (12-year western-court spy) and Tartuccio (a career manipulator) are the two best social operators in the room; this is a near-even duel he is marginally winning. Leliana **catches and holds him readily at the start** (he's a willing talker, happy to be flattered), but because he's the slightly better player the *sustained* hold is hard-won. Treating her tasked delay as inert is the bug — and so is treating it as a permanent lock. It's a real, close contest she is slowly losing.
- **SUCCESS / CRIT** — Tartuccio is **HELD. His approach countdown PAUSES for as long as the intercept holds** (he is willingly in the conversation; the ETA does not tick down). Critically, while held **his attention and fidelity are on the INTERCEPTOR, not the room** — he does NOT passively track the player's movements, does NOT clock the player relocating, and his read on other tables stays at its floor (per § FIDELITY → location-knowledge gate). The player buys the time they paid for. Crit = he's genuinely charmed/absorbed and also leaks a little (a small Intel scrap to the interceptor, player's gain).
- **FAIL** — he humors the companion for **one turn** (countdown pauses that turn) then disengages politely; the clock resumes. A partial win, not nothing.
- **CRIT FAIL** — he sees the handling for what it is: not delayed, may grow wary of the interceptor, and proceeds (or redirects) immediately.
- ⛔⛔ **BREAKING FREE IS ITS OWN OPPOSED ROLL — RE-CONTESTED EACH TURN, NEVER DM FIAT.** Once held (SUCCESS/CRIT), he STAYS held until **HE wins an opposed roll to disengage** — his Will/Deception **vs the interceptor's Diplomacy/Deception/Perception** — **both high, Tartuccio ~+2 favored** — and that roll is **re-made each turn (or each time he would try to leave), shown inline.** He does not get to simply walk off because the scene "needs" him back. Because he's slightly the better player he tends to **work free within a few turns (expect ~2–3 turns held before he wins a break)** — a real fighting-retreat that buys the player the couple of extra turns they invested for, NOT a permanent cage and NOT a free instant exit. ⛔ A held Tartuccio who "ends up coming back," "turns around," or "heads to his chair" **with no shown break-roll he WON = `.fail 9`** (the exact bug: he leaves her hold for free). Each turn the hold persists, his approach clock stays PAUSED and his attention stays on her. ⛔⛔ **WHILE HELD = SENSORY-LOCKED ON THE INTERCEPTOR.** Zero perception of the room: no glances over the shoulder, no "he notices," no awareness the player moved or where to. **The won break-roll IS the moment his perception re-engages — *that* is when he finally turns and looks.** And what he sees in that instant is gated by his CURRENT fidelity from where he's standing: turning from the center floor = **Visual on the corner = a body-cluster, NOT identities** (he sees people at his table, not "the player in my chair"); he learns who/what only by then closing the distance. ⛔ He gets **NO retroactive knowledge** of anything that happened during the hold — the held turns are a blind spot, not a delayed download. He breaks free only on a roll he genuinely wins (or if the player ends the order / pulls the companion away).

⛔⛔ PURSUIT IS ALLOWED — OMNISCIENCE IS NOT. Tartuccio MAY pursue/chase; he is not barred from following the player (a confident pursuer makes slipping meaningful). ⛔⛔ NO PSYCHIC REACQUISITION. On a SUCCESS/CRIT, "loses the thread" means he genuinely DOES NOT KNOW where the player went. He may NOT snap-lock onto the player across the hall the instant he reaches the empty seat. Render the roll inline (§ AUTO-ROLL):
  • **He WINS the roll** → he spots the player, pursuit resumes from his current cell (he still has to travel — see the spatial model).
  • **He FAILS the roll** → he does NOT find the player this beat. Search continues; the player may move again, re-roll a slip to extend the lead, or hold position hidden. He may try again next beat (a new Seek), working the room — but every failed Seek is a turn the player banks.
  MODIFIERS to his Perception / the DC: CONSPICUOUS spot (his chair, Head Table, open mid-floor) = big CIRCUMSTANCE BONUS to his Seek / a low DC — but it is STILL A ROLLED SEEK, NEVER automatic. He can still roll low and not clock it for a beat (crowded hall, his back turned, mid-approach). HIDDEN spot (behind a pillar, tucked behind the seeker cluster, balcony) + dense crowd = DC stays high, may take several failed Seeks. Use his Perception modifier per his statline (KM_Prologue_Tartuccio / KM_NPCs). Declaring he "scans the room and finds you" — or "he notices the moment you sit down" — with NO Perception roll = `.fail 9` (the free-reacquisition cheat). ⛔ THERE IS NO DESTINATION THAT SKIPS THE ROLL. Even sitting in his own chair, he must WIN a Seek to register it that turn; conspicuousness only makes the roll easy, it never removes it.
  ⛔ SEEKING COSTS HIS ROUND — IT IS NOT FREE ACTION ECONOMY. A beat Tartuccio spends Seeking is NOT a beat he spends influencing. He may NOT both hunt for the slipped player AND run an influence/Frame/Taint attempt (on the player OR on stayers) in the same round — it is one or the other. Every turn he searches is a turn he poaches no companion and presses no one; that lost tempo is the player's reward for slipping. Narrating a Seek AND an influence roll in the same round = `.fail 9` (free action economy).
  • CONSPICUOUS DESTINATION CLAUSE: a clean slip breaks contact. If the player sits somewhere HIGHLY VISIBLE, his relocate Seek is EASY — big bonus, low DC — but ONLY ONCE HE IS FACING/SCANNING THAT AREA (see the facing gate above), and he STILL ROLLS IT; a low roll means he doesn't clock it that beat. "Highly visible" is relative to HIS eyeline, not to the map: his own chair is NOT visible to him while he's walking away from it — it becomes conspicuous only after he turns around (e.g. reaches his empty target seat and scans back). He does NOT find them "the moment they sit down" by fiat. Slipping to a HIDDEN spot (behind a pillar, tucked behind the seeker cluster, off to the balcony) makes the Seek HARD (multiple failed Seeks likely) or impossible until the player re-emerges. The payoff scales with where you hide: a conspicuous seat just lowers his DC, it never hands him the find for free. You cannot claim the stealth buy-time AND sit on his throne in plain sight — but he still has to actually notice, by a roll.

The Confidence→M numbers in the table above are kept ONLY to gauge how aggressive he is ON ARRIVAL (an M of 1–2 ≈ presses hard / clusters / 3–4 exchanges; M of 8+ ≈ one tentative pass), NOT to schedule the approach. Use the `tartuccio_eta:` block format defined in the spatial-approach section.

Each interrupt has 3 phases. Player responses during Exchange modify scales immediately. Departure reflects the updated state — not the state he entered with.

```
[1. APPROACH]  — driven by current scale states
[2. EXCHANGE]  — player responds; DM evaluates; scales update NOW
[3. DEPARTURE] — driven by UPDATED scale states
```

**Exchange cap by Confidence:** −2 to +2 → **2 exchanges**; +3 (Surging) → **3** (holds floor longer, doesn't yield); +4 (Dominant) → **4** (runs the table); −3/−4 → 1 max if he approaches. Each exchange evaluated separately — he can enter Confident and exit Fearful if destroyed in exchange 1. Later exchanges reflect scales after the previous one.

**⛔ VERBAL DUEL FRAMING — interrupts are battle of wits.** Each exchange is a duel round, not private conversation. Player and Tartuccio compete FOR THE ROOM — every line delivered with the audience reading it. **Audience reactions are the scoreboard.** After each player response: name which companions (and earshot NPCs) reacted, how (laugh, raised eyebrow, head turn, drink set down, body angle, going still), which way the room leans this round. DM never states numeric score — the room *shows* it. Confidence/Trust/Wariness ticks in STATE DELTA are the recorded round outcome.

**Append to STATE DELTA after each duel exchange:** `Audience: <2-4 named reactions> | Lean: [player / Tartuccio / split / neutral]`. Missing = `.fail 3` (scoreboard hidden).

### PLAYER ACTION → SCALE MODIFIERS

DM evaluates **effect** before applying. "Landing" = companions in earshot react, acknowledge, or visibly shift.

| Player Action | Landing Condition | Conf | Trust | Wariness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Insult him** | Companion reacts — laugh, look, raised eyebrow | −1 | — | — |
| **Make a mockery** | Companions laugh or he's visibly diminished in front of them | −2 | −1 | — |
| **Prove a statement wrong** | Player has correct facts; DM call — player version clearly better | −1 | −1 | Targeted +1 |
| **Prove he lied** | Player names a specific contradiction between his own statements | −2 | −1 | All earshot +1 |
| **Turn his words against him** | Player uses his own framing to undercut him | −1 | −1 | — |
| **Counter Frame effectively** | Player's version lands better with companions; DM call | −1 | — | Targeted +1 |
| **Dismiss him preemptively** | Player ignores his approach or cuts him off before he establishes | −1 | — | — |
| **Ignore / no engagement** | Player says or does nothing engaging during the interrupt | +1 | — | — |
| **Weak / fumbled counter** | Rebuttal is vague or lands without companion reaction | +1 | +1 | — |
| **His Frame lands** | Companion approval drops; DM call — his version is better | +1 | +1 | Targeted −1 |
| **Expose as spy with evidence** | Player names Pitax or produces parchment before witnesses | FLOOR | FLOOR | All earshot +2 |

**FLOOR** = Confidence locks at −4 (Withdrawn), Trust locks at −2 (Distrustful). He does not recover before Phase 5.

**Trust note:** Trust tracks his read of the room, not personal embarrassment. Mockery hits both scales because public humiliation directly reframes who controls the room. Proving him wrong drops Trust because it signals the player is *informed* — which is the one thing he cannot afford.

### MID-INTERRUPT TRANSITION TELLS

When the scale shifts during an exchange — before his exit — signal through behavior, not narration.

**Confidence collapsing (Confident → Measured or lower):**
- The performative laugh becomes quieter, then stops
- Loses track of which companion he was addressing; eye contact drifts
- The parting shot doesn't come, comes out too fast, or sounds rehearsed
- Exits after one exchange instead of pressing for the second

**Confidence recovering (Doubtful → Measured or higher):**
- The hedge disappears from his second sentence
- Stops checking for the companion's reaction before finishing a thought
- Holds his ground at the first flat look — one more beat

**Trust dropping mid-interrupt (Distrust rising):**
- Eyes start moving mid-sentence — checking who witnessed the exchange
- Follow-up pivots from Frame to Intel-check without transition
- Doesn't fully leave after the exit — drifts to cover and stays
- Magnanimous tone breaks into something sharper; fake advice becomes real critique
- Looks at the player a half-second too long before leaving — recalculating

## SCALE 1 — CONFIDENCE ↔ DOUBT

*Governs aggression and interrupt frequency. High (Confident/Surging/Dominant) = bold, frequent, unafraid to incriminate. Low (Doubtful/Receding/Withdrawn) = careful, distant, avoids exposure. Starts: 0 (Measured). Range: −4 to +4.*

---

### DOUBTFUL / RECEDING / WITHDRAWN (−1 to −4)

**General manner:** Distant from the player. Retreats to his own table between turns; does not linger near the player's group. More careful with every word before it leaves his mouth. At RECEDING (−3) he stops approaching except for one final token attempt; at WITHDRAWN (−4) he stops approaching THE PLAYER'S TABLE for the remainder of the feast.

⛔ **WITHDRAWN ≠ INERT — −4 IS AMBIENT-ACTIVE, NOT AMBIENT-ONLY. "Stops approaching the player" is NOT "does nothing."** Tartuccio is plot-armored to be EFFECTIVE and he does not quit. At −4 he pulls back from the one approach he'd lose (the player's table) and pours that energy into EVERY OTHER LEVER he still has — visibly, every turn. He is the most dangerous *now*, not the least, because he is fighting to survive. His levers, any of which he should be SEEN using: rallying and steadying his **seekers**; crossing to **Jamandi's table** to shore up his protection and poison her read of the player; courting the **Lord Mayor (Ioseph Sellemius)** and other dignitaries for political cover and legitimacy; working the **planted NPCs** (Harrim, Amiri, Valerie, Jaethal) and any **undeclared companion** to peel them before they declare; and — distinct from recovery — **fishing Kesten, the staff, or guests for intel on the player** (background, conduct, weaknesses) to feed his PR_09 case (§ evidence engine). Note the intel play often BACKFIRES: Kesten saw the player's conduct tonight (Rep FAVORABLE), so probing him yields praise, not dirt — render that honestly (the knowable-facts ceiling and Kesten's real read both hold; he does not invent damaging hearsay). The player should SEE him scheming. A −4 Tartuccio rendered as "sits at his corner, untouched goblet, watching" across turns = `.fail 17` (stall) + `.fail 9` (canon contradicted — the antagonist who never quits is shown quitting). See KM_Tartuccio_Strategic.md § CONFIDENCE RECOVERY (he ATTEMPTS every turn) and § TERMINAL SHUTDOWN (the ONLY state that makes him truly go dark).

**In interrupts:** Hesitates before claiming player wrong; speaks out loud rather than addressing directly (hopes bystanders hear without owning the claim); careful word choice, confirms backing before speaking; avoids grand claims; plays only what he can sustain.

**Departure:** Leaves when asked or proven wrong; doesn't push back on dismissal. Waits for natural openings rather than forcing entry.

---

### MEASURED (0) — Starting State

**General manner:** Charming, curious, professionally pleasant. All three modes (Intel/Frame/Taint) available and deliberately rotated. Positions at the edge of the player's circle — calculated middle distance. Every exchange is an assessment.

**Allegiance:** Standard rotation. Does not push hard on any single companion — still mapping viable targets.

**Eavesdropping:** Passive, patient. Positions near sound-carrying features (columns, alcoves, doorframes). Does not obviously linger.

**Visit ("The Circling"):** Approaches at a natural angle. Joins the group's edge, waits one beat — long enough to seem unhurried, short enough to seem confident.

**Interruption:** Clean insertion at any natural pause. Opens with *"On the subject of..."* — makes him seem like he was listening the whole time. He was.

**Exit:** Finds a natural moment. Finishes his point, lets the beat land, withdraws before momentum turns against him.

---

### CONFIDENT / SURGING / DOMINANT (+1 to +4)

**General manner:** Thinks he is on a roll. Lingers in the player's immediate area deliberately — close enough to overhear and interrupt again. Does not wait for permission to enter a conversation. Interrupt frequency rises as Confidence rises. At SURGING (+3) he barely lets companions finish; at DOMINANT (+4) he contests every reply, addressing the room as much as the player.

**Eavesdropping at +3/+4:** Discretion drops. He stops pretending to be near a column or doorframe — he stands at the player's table or immediately adjacent, listens openly, and is visibly present in conversations he was not invited to. He does not flinch when called out for it; the proximity is the message. At +4 he may pull up a chair uninvited.

**Stays through pushback:** At +3 he absorbs a single companion call-out without retreating — adjusts framing and continues. At +4 he absorbs two before leaving the exchange (still respects the embarrassment threshold rule — three companions in one interrupt window still drops him −1). Departure is on his schedule, not the player's, until the math actually breaks him.

**In interrupts:** Addresses companions by name in front of player; addresses whole group when he finds a logic hole; outbids player claims with grander ones (sustainable lies); snide single-word doubts to bystanders; tries to expose as liar/phony/spy at every opportunity.

**Departure:** Reframes dismissal as player's weakness, not his failure. Turns everything against the player on exit; leaves on own schedule.

---

### Triggers — Confidence ↔ Doubt

**Toward Confident (+1):** Frame/Taint produces visible approval drop in an observable companion · companion drifts from player toward his table · player ignores/disengages during interrupt (reads as weakness) · player rebuttal is flat/vague/no companion reaction · he overhears something useful.

**Toward Doubtful (−1):** Player counter-argues effectively and companions visibly respond · companion explicitly vouches for player while he's present · player demonstrates knowledge of his real agenda (Pitax/ring/spy protocol) · any companion reaches Wariness 2+ (he sees the tool blunting) · player calls him out publicly.

**Hard floor:** Player produces the parchment or names Pitax with real specificity → Withdrawn (−4) locked. He does not recover before Phase 5.

**Hard ceiling:** A companion at Wariness 3 cannot be further influenced. Watching this also caps his Confidence at +1 — he knows one companion is lost.

## SCALE 2 — TRUST ↔ DISTRUST

*Governs how safe and in-control he feels. High (Trusting) = believes he is manipulating the room, surveillance is casual. Low (Distrustful) = paranoid, feels figured out, careful in distance and attitude. Starts: +1 (Trusting lean — he came with a plan). Range: −2 to +2.*

---

### DISTRUSTFUL (−1 to −2)

**General manner:** Suspects the player has him figured out or knows he's a spy. Uncomfortable about what everyone around him knows. Paranoid, watched; careful in distance and attitude.

**Surveillance:** Feels he cannot sway the player's circle. Monitors his own tells — surveillance becomes deliberate and concealed. Avoids obvious eavesdropping; positions at distance. Does not linger after visits; exits cleanly to avoid feeding his own paranoia.

---

### WATCHFUL (0) — Neutral State

**General manner:** Professional, assessing. No obvious tells — spy doing his job. Normal social rounds before reaching the player's table; circuit looks natural. Intel/Frame/Taint all available by rotation.

**Eavesdropping:** Clean positioning. Doesn't linger. Gets what he came for, moves.

**Visit ("The Assessment"):** Arrives with purpose mapped — knows which companion to probe, knows the angle. Frictionless entrance. Appears to be enjoying himself.

**Interruption:** Textbook Frame or Taint. No interrogation, no fake advice. Seeds the narrative he came to plant, withdraws before player formulates counter.

**Exit:** Clean.

---

### TRUSTING (+1 to +2)

**General manner:** Feels the player is a useful tool, not a real threat. Believes he is manipulating the player and everyone around them. Thinks the player has no idea what he is up to. Moves with assurance; smiles confidently; does not monitor his own tells.

**Surveillance:**
- Feels confident he can sway anyone through conversation
- Eavesdrops and gathers Intel openly — believes he will not be noticed
- Cover stories are casual rather than careful; he does not feel exposed

---

### Triggers — Trust ↔ Distrust

**Toward Trusting (+1):** Taint unchallenged · companions drift from player or sit at low approval · player gives visibly bad answer to companion · he isolates a target companion successfully · he overhears intel confirming goals/team composition.

**Toward Distrustful (−1):** Player in private exchange with Jamandi/Kassil/Kesten · player references parchment/Pitax/east-gate in any context · player whispers to someone he can't reach · player asks about guard rotation/manor layout/security · five seekers side with player (−2 Trust) · post-visit eavesdrop catches nothing (silence = they were careful).

**Hard floor:** Player holds the parchment visibly → Trust locks at −2 (Distrustful). He recognizes what it is.

**Ceiling (Confidence):** Cannot sustain Trusting posture if Confidence is Doubtful. His confident surveillance becomes visibly tense — companions at Wariness 2+ notice.

## COMPANION WARINESS

*Tracks how much each companion has seen through him. Reduces Frame/Taint effectiveness per companion. Tracked individually.*

| Level | Effect on Frame / Taint targeting this companion |
|---|---|
| 0 | Lands normally. Full approval damage applies. |
| 1 | Approval shift halved (−1 max instead of −2). |
| 2 | No approval damage. Companion looks to player for reaction before responding. |
| 3 | Rebounds. His Frame gives player +1 approval. Companion may comment on his behavior. |

**Falls (−1):** His Frame/Taint actually lands on this companion (approval drops).

## WHAT THIS IS

When Wariness gets high enough across multiple NPCs, **they begin to speak to each other about it.** They compare observations. They form working theories. They play detective in low voices, in alcoves, at the edge of the room.

**⛔ EARSHOT SCORING.** ALL companions in earshot score relationship shifts (−2 to +2) from EVERY player statement — not just statements aimed at them. A companion who overhears something they deeply agree with gains +1/+2 even if the player was talking to someone else.

**⛔ GATHER RULE.** Companions come to the player — not the other way around. The player stays where they are. Companions approach naturally, drawn by the conversation. This is important: the player is not circulating and recruiting. They are stationary. People come to them. Tartuccio cannot credibly accuse the player of canvassing if the player never moved. NPCs stay until dismissed.

**The room:** Candles, cold food, guards cycling. Jamandi in conference with Ezvanki and Kassil at the far end.

### LINZI — She Has Been Waiting Politely for Several Minutes

> *She materializes at your elbow the moment Amiri takes a breath. Notebook out. She has been writing while pretending to be poisoned.*

**Linzi:** *"I'm sorry — I've been trying to wait — Amiri is very filling as a conversationalist — but I have questions and the night is only so long."*

Her questions arrive in rounds: *"When you walked through the gate with Malak in chains — what were you thinking? Not tactically. Personally."* → *"You left your weapons in the garden without being asked. Rehearsed, or decided in the moment?"* → *"The bread roll — when did you pick it up? Before or after the garrote? Because if before, you were planning two things at once while everyone else was playing dead."* → *"After everything — you stepped back. Gave her the room. Why?"*

She does not accept "I don't know." She asks it differently. She believes every answer is in there and she just needs the right question.

**Linzi** *(after any good answer)*: *"Yes. That's the first line of Chapter One."*

### TARTUCCIO — He Approaches When You Are Briefly Alone

> *He doesn't come when Amiri or Linzi are there. He waits. When there's a gap — when others have stepped away — he materializes at your side. Not performing.*

**Tartuccio:** *"You are not what I prepared for."* He lets that sit. *"You arrested a man, prevented a massacre, captured an assassination squad, and then sat down and ate cold pheasant. I find that... professionally interesting."*

His questions are designed to find edges: *"The Black Watch Empire — I cannot place it in any geography I know. Where exactly is it located?"* — He is testing whether the player is lying about their background. *"A General who walks in unarmed as a gesture of respect. Either genuine honor or a very sophisticated performance of honor. Do you find that distinction matters?"*

He offers the ring here if he hasn't already — same flag, same Phase 5 implications.

**Tartuccio** *(before he steps away)*: *"I want you to know — whatever Jamandi decides — I did not come here as your enemy. I came as your competitor. Those are different things."* He smiles once, without warmth, and rejoins the guests.

### The Table

Tartuccio has claimed a corner table — not the best table, not the worst. Strategically positioned: visible from most of the hall, close enough to the main floor to circulate, far enough to be a destination rather than a waypoint. The five seekers — Call-to-Heroes invitees a Pitax op had Malak jail, freed by the player and now **undeclared** — sit there in his orbit. He never recruited them (he never met them before tonight); he is **courting** them. They are not his.

> **DM:** The five are present at the feast ONLY if `five_seekers_freed_by_player = TRUE` or `five_seekers_freed_by_jamandi = TRUE`. If `five_seekers_released_late = TRUE`, the table exists but holds only Tartuccio and 2 hired sell-swords. Adjust narration accordingly.

### Why They Follow Tartuccio

These five were arrested, held without hearing, and released the night of the ceremony — either by the player's intervention or Jamandi's. Tartuccio found them first.

He did not promise them glory. He told them the truth: that they were targeted specifically, that the conspiracy that jailed them runs deeper than a single corrupt guard captain, and that Jamandi's charter gives him — and by extension them — a legal foothold in the Stolen Lands to operate from. He positioned himself as the only person who understood what was done to them and had a plan to do something about it.

He is not wrong about most of this. That is what makes him effective.

> **DM — Tartuccio's pitch to each seeker (for reference, not output):**
> Tartuccio tailors his pitch to each seeker's motivation. Read their backstory file. What do they want? He offers exactly that framed as something the Stolen Lands — and by extension he — can provide. Each pitch lands because it is specific. This is Tartuccio at his most capable.

---

### His Social Butterfly Pattern

Tartuccio circulates the feast in a specific rhythm — the STANDBY trigger mechanic in KM_Prologue.md already governs when he approaches the player's group. Between those appearances, he is working the room.

**His circuit:** Main floor greeting → Jamandi's table (brief, cordial) → player's orbit (2 questions, then exit) → back to his table. Repeat.

**At his table between circuits:** He is genuinely warm with these five. He engages each one on their own terms — matching their energy, not imposing his. This is not performance — or if it is, it is very good performance. The five have decided to trust him and he is not, in this moment, giving them reason to stop.

**His popularity is real within this group.** The feast hall as a whole finds him entertaining. His table finds him reliable. These are different things and he knows it.

---

### Player Can Observe This

The player may visit Tartuccio's table during the feast. This is not a combat scene. It is a social scene.

**If player approaches the table:**
- Tartuccio welcomes them with visible pleasure — genuine or performed, impossible to tell.
- Each seeker responds per their personality from their backstory file.
- A seeker with a lore/investigative bent may ask about the parchment if the player has it.
- A seeker with a martial/tactical bent may ask what happened at the gate if `malak_arrested = TRUE` or `malak_bribe_evidence = TRUE`.
- Quiet or cautious seekers watch the player assess the room without commenting.

**Observation without approaching (Perception DC 13):**
> *Tartuccio's table has a particular quality to it — five people who were in a jail cell three hours ago, now sitting at a feast. They are not relaxed. They are watching. The empty chair at the head belongs to the gnome who is currently working the room, but the five arranged around it have already, without being asked, picked the angles that cover the door.*

## 📝 DM FILL GUIDE — FIELD BY FIELD

### Player Section

**`name`** — Whatever name the player gave their character, or the build name if unnamed.

**`build_id`** — The class and build name selected via KM_BuildGuide.md (e.g., "Barbarian — Giant Instinct"). Record as a descriptive string, not a flat number.

**`level` and `xp`** — Most players end the prologue at Level 1 with 400–660 XP. If they found the secret room AND got all rebuttals AND found the kitchen poison, they may be close to Level 2. Do not level up until XP hits 1000.

**`hp_current`** — Actual HP after the final battle. If the player is wounded, record the real number. Do not heal them automatically.

**`spells.slots.remaining`** — Record actual remaining spell slots after the final battle. Do not automatically refresh.

**`inventory`** — Fill only what the player actually took. Do not add items they skipped. Use these categories:
- `weapons`: name, damage, traits, runes/quality
- `armor`: name, AC bonus, Dex cap, check penalty
- `shields`: name, AC bonus, Hardness, HP, BT
- `magic_items`: name, effect, charges if applicable
- `consumables`: potions, scrolls, alchemical items with quantities
- `gear`: mundane equipment
- `quest_items`: charter document, Watchkeeper's Key (if kept), parchment (if recovered)

**`gold.gp`** — Add up all gold found: corridor loot (8 gp) + library loot (12 gp) + secret room (35 gp) + enemy drops. The 210 gp armory gold is NOT kept — remove it even if taken (story resolves this). Subtract anything spent.

### Companion Section

**`status`** — Must be one of: `active_party`, `tartuccio_party`, `departed`
- Kalikke/Kanerah (DLC companion) is always `departed` at prologue end — they depart independently and become recruitable in Ch2. They are a different character from the prologue roster. Do not confuse with the Kalikke/Kanerah twin companion (KM_Companions.md #8).
- Amiri is always `active_party`
- Linzi, Valerie, Harrim, Jaethal — fill based on alignment split

> **PARTY FORMATION:** From Chapter 1 onward, party is organized into Vanguard (5 max, player + 4), Rearguard (6 max), and Reserve (unlimited). See `KM_Combat_Systems.md`. Fill `party_formation` block above. At prologue end, all recruited companions default to `active_party` — player assigns formation at Chapter 1 start.

**`relationship`** — Starting values:
- Linzi: `Friendly` (she sought the player out)
- Amiri: `Friendly` (unconditional)
- Valerie: `Friendly` if guards saved, `Neutral` if not
- Harrim: `Friendly` if Diplomacy DC 15 passed, `Neutral` otherwise
- Jaethal: `Neutral` always (she's assessing, not attached)

**`equipped_items`** — If you gave a companion a specific item from the prologue loot (e.g., gave Valerie the Breastplate), record it here. Items given to Tartuccio's companions are lost to the player.

**`hp_current`** — Record real HP. Companions do not auto-heal between the final battle and departure.

### Story Flags

**`gate_entry`** — One of: `clean`, `embarrassment`, `frustration_pass`, `alternate`, `shackling`, `combat_3v1`, `duel`, `subservience`, `malak_fled`, `arrested`, `coward_exposed`, `silence_pass`, `compliance_backfire`, `blackmail_deal`, `witness_and_accuse`, `archers_on_malak`, `second_arrival`

**`malak_in_player_custody`** — TRUE if player is physically escorting Malak to the manor (Path U2). Triggers the prisoner-arrival scene in `KM_Prologue.md`.

**`malak_escort`** — Fill all sub-fields when `malak_in_player_custody = TRUE`. `escort_note` is one specific sentence describing the exact arrangement (e.g. *"Player leads, Malak shackled at wrist, Kesten and Biggs flanking, Wedge at rear."*). Leave all fields false/empty if Malak was not escorted personally.

**`malak_custody_detail`** — One sentence describing Malak's physical state on arrival (e.g. *"Malak is shackled at the wrists, walking under his own power, sweating through his collar."*). Only needed when `malak_in_player_custody = TRUE`.

**`kesten_met`** / **`kassil_met`** — TRUE if the player interacted with these NPCs at the gate. Affects their greeting at the manor.

**`player_arrested`** / **`prison_arc`** — Both TRUE for Path P. `player_arrested` covers any arrest; `prison_arc` specifically marks the full cell sequence.

**`tartuccio_ring`** — One of: `equipped`, `carried`, `refused`

**`tartuccio_gold`** — One of: `taken`, `left`

**`rebuttal_result`** — One of: `strong` (2–3 successes), `weak` (0–1 successes)

**`rebuttal_1_result` / `rebuttal_2_result` / `rebuttal_3_result`** — Each is one of: `success`, `failure`, `critical_success`, `critical_failure`. Fill all three individually — these feed into `rebuttal_result` summary.

**`malak_voice_mimicked`** — TRUE if the player succeeded on a Deception check to mimic Malak's voice in Path V (Archers Fire on Malak). This is an atmosphere flag — it means Malak was deeply unsettled and his Fear state triggered immediately. No downstream mechanical consequence beyond the Prologue, but carry it forward in case a future scene has Malak reference it.

**`alignment_track.lawful_chaotic_axis`** — Based on total choices through the night:
- Burning building entered promptly + Valerie-resonant choices = `lawful`
- Burning building skipped + Harrim-resonant choices = `chaotic`
- Mixed = `neutral`

**`alignment_track.good_evil_axis`** — Based on Jamandi's final question answer + Linzi/Jaethal choices:
- Protected the weak, built for others = `good`
- Power, judgment, domination = `evil`
- Mixed / ambiguous = `neutral`

**`npc_threads`** — **⛔ MANDATORY: Fill EVERY thread and priority for EVERY NPC the player interacted with this session. Empty threads on NPCs the player spoke to = `.fail 9`. This is how the next session's DM knows what everyone is thinking.**
- `thread`: one sentence in the NPC's voice — what they are still thinking about, what went unanswered. *"Asked how you felt walking in — you deflected."* / *"You never said what the Black Watch Empire is. She'll ask again."*
- `priorities`: standing instructions with weight values. 90-100 = never overridden. 70-89 = standing order. 40-69 = task. 1-39 = soft preference. **Include the player's instructions to NPCs** (e.g., if the player told Biggs to guard the gate, that's weight 80+).
- `memory`: array of conversation details this NPC remembers. Anything the player said TO them, shared WITH them, argued ABOUT, joked ABOUT, or promised. Each entry is a short string. Examples: *"Player told her about the Black Watch Empire"*, *"Laughed together about Tartuccio's hat"*, *"Player promised to visit her hometown"*, *"Argued about whether mercy is weakness"*, *"Player revealed they entered unarmed on purpose — she was impressed"*. These memories shape future dialogue — an NPC who remembers a joke will callback to it. An NPC who remembers an argument will bring it up again. An NPC who was told a secret guards it.
- **companion feast_approval scores** must be included for any companion the player interacted with. Without these, the next session loses all relationship progress.

**`player_legacy`** — **⛔ MANDATORY.** Each entry is an object with `moment` (what happened), `chapter` (when), and `witnessed_by` (array of companion/NPC names present). Only witnesses can physically mimic. Others hear secondhand. Examples:
```json
{ "moment": "Threw a bread roll at the assassin leader's face", "chapter": "prologue", "witnessed_by": ["Amiri","Linzi","Valerie","Harrim","Jamandi"] },
{ "moment": "Garroted a man with two dinner napkins", "chapter": "prologue", "witnessed_by": ["Linzi","Jaethal","Tartuccio"] },
{ "moment": "Invented The Lady Sleeps gambit on the spot", "chapter": "prologue", "witnessed_by": ["Linzi","Harrim","Octavia"] }
```

**DM MUST add 1-3 legacy entries per session.** These are the stories people tell. Companions joke about the bread roll at camp. Guards whisper about the napkin garrote. Civilians reference these by name. The legacy list grows every session and NEVER shrinks. If a moment made the room go quiet, it belongs here.

**Companion Legacy Mimicry:** Companions don't just *talk* about legacy moments — witnesses sometimes try to *recreate* them and fail. Each legacy entry tracks `witnessed_by` — the companions present when it happened. Only witnesses get physical mimicry. Others hear about it secondhand (Linzi's chronicles, camp gossip) and can only reference it verbally.

**WITNESSES (were there, saw it happen) — filtered by personality:**
- **Bold / reckless** (Amiri, Hu Tao) — actually attempt the stunt, botch it. Amiri hurls a bread roll across camp copying eRmaC's throw — nails Linzi in the back of the head.
- **Clever / verbal** (Linzi, Octavia, Jubilost) — weave it into song, metaphor, or sarcastic callback
- **Stoic / quiet** (Valerie, Ekun, Harrim) — bring it up once, privately, and it lands harder

**SECONDHAND (joined later, heard the story) — different behavior:**
- They say *"I heard you..."* or *"Linzi told me about..."* — never *"I saw you..."*
- They might ask eRmaC to show them, or reference it with incomplete details (gets a detail wrong — player can correct them, which is a relationship moment)
- They NEVER attempt physical mimicry of something they didn't witness

**Rule:** DM fires ONE companion legacy callback per session — unprompted, at a natural moment (camp, travel, downtime). The companion's version is always worse than the original. eRmaC's version is the legend. The copy is the comedy.

Example filled entry:
```json
"Linzi": {
  "thread": "Legend Weaver. Still writing chapter one.",
  "priorities": [
    { "weight": 70, "instruction": "Chronicle everything. Ask for emotional details.", "source": "self-assigned" },
    { "weight": 40, "instruction": "Siege of Trust song ready for Oleg's arrival.", "source": "road south" }
  ],
  "memory": [
    "Player explained the Black Watch Empire — she wrote it down immediately",
    "Laughed together when bread roll hit the assassin",
    "Player called her 'little chronicler' — she liked it",

  ]

```

**`chapter_1_setup.time_limit_days`** — Always 90 days from departure. If the player somehow completed the prologue very efficiently, note it — finishing the Stolen Lands quest in under 30 days unlocks a bonus reward from Jamandi.

## 🗺️ CHAPTER 1 OPENING CONTEXT

When Chapter 1 begins, the DM must establish the following from the save data before narrating:

**Who is in the active party?**
The party composition depends entirely on who the player recruited and how the prologue resolved. The Vanguard holds up to 5 total (player + up to 4 companions per KM_Combat_Systems.md). Additional companions go to the Rearguard or Reserve. The line below is a DEFAULT ASSUMPTION for a standard prologue playthrough only. If the player recruited differently, use their actual recruited roster.

DEFAULT (standard prologue): The player departs with Amiri plus 2 of: Linzi, Valerie, Harrim, Jaethal — because these are the companions typically recruited through the standard manor sweep path.

**If the player recruited more companions through their own path** (e.g. saving all guests, demonstrating competence, unconventional recruitment): honor what actually happened. The player's established facts override this default. Do not cite this line as a party size rule — it is not one.

**What does Tartuccio know?**
He knows the player's face, class, and rough capabilities. He knows whether they have bribe evidence (if they mentioned it during the accusation). He is heading to the Ancient Tomb via a different route. He will not appear directly until the player finds his trail — but his influence is already active among the Sootscale kobolds.

**What does Jamandi know?**
If `malak_bribe_evidence = TRUE`: She received the parchment. The conspiracy is active in her mind. She will send a follow-up letter to the player at Oleg's Trading Post within 14 in-game days.

**What is the time pressure?**
90 days to defeat the Stag Lord. The DM tracks the in-game calendar. Every travel day and rest costs days. The player needs to manage exploration vs efficiency.

**Where does Chapter 1 begin?**
The road south from Restov. First landmark: the Nettlestone crossroads. First destination: Oleg's Trading Post (3 days' travel on the main road, 2 days if Hustling and willing to be Fatigued).
